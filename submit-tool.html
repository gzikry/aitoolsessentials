Vendor submissions

Submit your AI tool for editorial review.

One short form. No login. Submission is free—but listing, ranking, review timing, and placement are not guaranteed.

Editorial rankings cannot be purchased. Sponsored placements, when offered, are labeled and kept separate.

What happens after submission

  1. Identity check: we verify the product URL and basic company information.
  2. Editorial fit: we check whether the tool solves a repeated workflow covered by the directory.
  3. Evidence review: we look for official pricing, documentation, privacy information, and applicable benchmark evidence.
  4. Decision: accepted tools enter the review queue. We reply only when clarification or follow-up is needed.

What submission does not buy

  • No guaranteed listing or review date
  • No guaranteed rank, badge, backlink authority, traffic, clicks, or votes
  • No ability to edit the editorial score
  • No hidden sponsored placement

What helps

Send direct links to pricing, documentation, privacy/security details, and any independent benchmark submission that names the exact model or version.
