    Related AI tools to compare

    Productivity · Pay-as-you-go credits, one-time purchase, no subscription: 1,000 credits USD 19, 5,000 USD 79 (marked most popular), 10,000 USD 149, 20,000 USD 249. Every account's first purchase is discounted to USD 9.90 / 49 / 98 / 196 for the same four packs, once per account. New accounts get 100 free credits. Credits never expire and work in the web app and the API. Humanizing costs 100 credits per 1,000 words actually rewritten; citation-format correction is the cheapest operation at 5 credits per 1,000 words. Checkout runs through Waffo (the Privacy Policy also names Creem), each acting as merchant of record; prices are USD and no recurring plan is sold - the Terms state each purchase is a one-time charge.

    HumanPen

    HumanPen is a document toolbox that works on files rather than on a text box. You upload a DOCX or PPTX and get the same format back, edited: an AI-phrasing rewriter with two strengths that can import your own Turnitin or iThenticate report and touch only the flagged passages, a citation-format converter covering 12 styles, a shortener that hits a target word count, and a translator for 12 languages that keeps layout, charts and formulas. Billing is metered - you are charged for words actually rewritten, not for the whole file - and credits do not expire, so a 500-word job costs cents instead of a month's subscription. The humanizer is English-only and the vendor says so. There is also a documented REST API, an MCP server and an agent skill. The site is candid to an unusual degree about what the tool is for: the /ethics page states plainly that the humanizer is used to lower detector scores, declines to promise a pass, declines to run its own detector, and the Terms forbid submitting work as your own where an assessment requires independent work.

    Best for: Writers and researchers who already have a finished Word or PowerPoint file and need the file itself changed - AI phrasing reduced, references converted to a target style, a document cut to a word limit, or a layout-preserving translation - then downloaded again as an editable DOCX or PPTX. The unit of work is a whole document, not pasted text, so it suits a 50,000-word thesis with cross-references and figure numbering. Not a fit for anyone who wants a draft written for them (there is no generation entry point), for non-English humanizing, for assessed work where the institution restricts AI assistance, or for a buyer who needs a named company, country, postal address or a governing law that names a jurisdiction.
