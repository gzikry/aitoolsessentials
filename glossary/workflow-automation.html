    AIVane

    AIVane turns described or recorded browser and desktop tasks into editable automation workflows. It offers a browser app, Windows and Mac downloads, Hosted AI via DeepInfra and optional bring-your-own model configuration. Sam Luo publicly identifies as its independent operator; Creem separately sells purchases. Workflow execution, installers and checkout were not independently tested.

    Best for: People who need editable AI-assisted browser or desktop workflows, including cloud runs and optional local execution, and can test with non-sensitive sample data first.

    Best for: Visual automation scenarios and multi-step operational workflows

    TinyCommand

    TinyCommand is one platform holding eleven connected products - Discovery and Enrichment, Forms, Tables, Workflows, AI Agents, an email designer, Email & campaigns, Portals, SEO and AI visibility - so a form, the record it creates, the workflow that reacts to it, the agent that researches it and the email that follows all live in the same place instead of in six subscriptions. Forms are dynamic rather than static: they can verify a field, look up a person mid-fill, branch, and take payment through Stripe, Razorpay or UPI, and forms and responses are unlimited on every plan including the free one. Tables behave like a light database with six views, 37 field types and AI columns that fill themselves; Workflows run on a visual canvas with 60+ node types, seven trigger types and human sign-off steps; AI agents use six built-in tools and can be embedded on a site or run inside a workflow; email design and broadcast campaigns reuse the same table data. Credits are the meter for automated work rather than for data collection - a workflow step costs 1 credit, an AI agent step about 10, a company lookup 20 and a person lookup 10, with no charge when a lookup misses. Vision and AI agent features run on TinyCommand's own self-hosted models, and the Privacy Policy says customer data processed by those features is not sent to third-party AI providers. The company is TinyCommand LLC of Sheridan, Wyoming, the terms are governed by Wyoming law, and the legal set includes a DPA covering the EU and UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act 2023 and CCPA/CPRA with SCC and UK IDTA transfer mechanisms. Buying runs through the vendor's own signup and checkout; the free tier needs no card.

    Best for: Teams replacing a stitched-together stack - Typeform or Jotform plus Airtable or Notion plus Zapier or Make plus Mailchimp plus a Clay-style enrichment tool plus a chatbot - with one login, one bill and one data model: forms that call apps mid-fill, tables that hold the records, workflows with 60+ node types across 458 integrations, AI agents that read and act on your own data, email campaigns sent from those tables, and login-gated client portals. It fits solo operators on the free-forever tier, growing teams that want the agent builder at $49/mo, and agencies that need many workspaces and seats at $149/mo. Not a fit if you need the single best tool in any one category (the point here is breadth, not depth), a native mobile or desktop app (the platform is browser-only), a published sub-processor list to hand to a compliance reviewer today, a longer execution history than 15 days on Free and Basic, or if the Black Friday and lifetime offers matter to you - both are real but neither is mentioned on the pricing page.
