Layout was measured in Chrome with device metrics at 1280×900 and 390×844 (the layout viewport, not the window size) on `/`, `/newsletter/`, `/newsletter/2026-w42.html`, `/newsletter/2026-w35.html`, `/subscribe/`, `/premium/`, `/sponsor/`, `/tools/`, `/tools/chatgpt/`, `/pricing-watch/`, `/stack-audit.html`, `/search/`, `/updates/`, `/updates/2026-09.html`, `/benchmarks/`, `/articles/learn.html`, `/comparisons/chatgpt-vs-claude.html`, `/guides/switch-guides/`, `/legal/privacy.html`, `/premium/welcome/`, `/services/ai-stack-audit.html`, `/articles/claude-code-vs-cursor-vs-copilot.html`, and `/articles/claude-code-vs-copilot-vs-cursor.html`.
