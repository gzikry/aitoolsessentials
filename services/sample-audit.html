    Sample

    What a finished audit looks like.

    Redacted structure from a real delivery. Client identity, systems, and figures are replaced with placeholders — we do not publish a client's stack or their costs.

    Section 1

    Direct answers to the goals you named

    Each workflow you asked about gets its own recommendation. Example shape:

    Your stated goalRecommendationWhy
    Handle more inbound callsDo it differently, not with an AI receptionistYour booking system already collects requests and requires human confirmation. Adding a second booking path creates conflicts. Automate the follow-up message instead.
    Reduce time on patient repliesDo it — narrow and fencedTemplate drafting for non-sensitive messages is worth it. Anything identifying a customer stays out of a general AI workspace.
    Get better at schedulingDo not do it yetScheduling is owned by an existing system with a live calendar. Until we know which one and who confirms, a new tool adds a second source of truth.

    Note the shape: two of three are "yes, like this" rather than refusals. That was the main failure of an earlier version of this audit.

    Section 2

    Keep / cut / defer, with your numbers

    SystemMonthlyOwnerRenewalDecision
    General assistant (seat 1)$20OwnerMonthlyKEEP — narrow non-sensitive drafting lane
    General assistant (seat 2, unowned)$20unknownunknownCUT — no owner, no defined weekly job
    Meeting recorder$18unknownunknownCUT — not used on sensitive calls, unused for the stated workflow
    Specialist documentation toolunknown——DEFER — only if the ranked bottleneck is documentation

    Where a field was left blank it stays "unknown". We do not fill in a plausible figure to make the table look complete.

    Section 3

    The ranked bottleneck, worked in depth

    Options compared on fit, cost, effort, and risk, ending in a single recommendation and the tradeoff you are accepting. Not a list of possibilities.

    Sections 4-5

    Monday actions and a 30-day sequence

    Every finding carries a specific next action with a named owner. The sequence covers weeks 1-4 and ends with a cancel rule so the stack does not drift back.
