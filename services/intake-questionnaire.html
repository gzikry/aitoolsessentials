Audit intake

AI Stack Audit intake questionnaire

Answer these questions before requesting an audit. The better the input, the more useful the recommendation report.

Email intakeBack to audit

Business context

  1. What type of business or role is this for?
  2. How many people will use the AI tools?
  3. What tools do you currently pay for?
  4. What tools are you considering?
  5. What is your monthly budget range?

Workflow context

  1. What are the 3 most repetitive tasks you want to improve?
  2. Which tasks take the most time each week?
  3. Which tasks create the most errors or bottlenecks?
  4. Which outputs matter most: emails, reports, videos, designs, research, code, meetings, sales follow-up, customer support?

Risk and constraints

  1. Will sensitive client/customer data be involved?
  2. Are there compliance, legal, or privacy constraints?
  3. Do you need team controls, approvals, or admin features?
  4. Are there tools you cannot use?

Success criteria

  1. What would make the audit successful?
  2. What would you like to stop doing manually?
  3. Do you want fewer tools, better tools, or a new workflow?
  4. What timeline are you working with?
