    01Router vs Ai Genjutsu Io

    Developers and small product teams adding AI video or image generation to an app who would otherwise need an account, API key and billing setup with several vendors, and who want to see each upstream provider's price before choosing a route. It also suits creators who want one credit balance across video, image, audio and chat in a browser studio, and agencies that want to resell the same models to their own users at a markup they set. It is not a fit if you need a named company, a governing-law clause, a refund policy or a training-use statement before you pay, if you need to buy more than $5,000 of credit at once, or if client media has to be covered by a processor you can name in a data-processing agreement. · Creators who want a specific trend effect from one photo without writing a prompt - pick a template, see its credit price and example, upload the photo it asks for, and download a short vertical clip. Good fit for pet dances, glow-up transformations, meme scenes and the lion-story trends, and for occasional users who would rather buy a $1.99 pack than subscribe. Not a fit for anyone who needs a free trial (there is none), for sound on a classic template (they are silent), for work where an exact likeness is contractual, or for anyone unwilling to upload a portrait to a four-week-old service.
