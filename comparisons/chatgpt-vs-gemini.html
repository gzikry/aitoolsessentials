Evidence status

ChatGPT

OpenAI officially lists ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Free is $0; paid self-service plans are billed per user per month, while Enterprise pricing is sales-assisted. Exact localized prices were not consistently exposed by the official pricing page during verification, so no unverified amounts are reported.

Gemini

For US personal accounts, Gemini Free is $0 per month, Google AI Pro is $19.99 per month, and Google AI Ultra starts at $99.99 per month, with a $199.99 option offering higher limits. For organizations, Gemini is included as a core service in eligible Google Workspace editions; official US Workspace rates are $8.40/$7 per user per month for Business Starter, $16.80/$14 for Business Standard, and $26.40/$22 for Business Plus on flexible/annual plans respectively. Enterprise pricing is sales-assisted.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gemini included with Google Workspace?

Gemini capabilities are bundled into Workspace plans at different tiers, and Google sells Gemini as a standalone subscription too. Check Google's current Workspace pricing page for what your plan includes.

Which has better benchmark scores?

Both models appear high on public leaderboards like Arena Text, but ranks shift weekly and apply only to exact model versions tested. See our benchmarks hub for dated snapshots rather than treating either as "the winner."
