Evidence status

Adobe Podcast

Adobe Podcast's official plans page lists a Free plan and a Premium plan with a 30-day free trial. Free Enhance Speech is audio-only, 30 minutes / 500 MB per file, 1 hour per day. Free Studio allows 30-minute projects, 2 downloads per day, no original-recording download, and branded audiograms. Premium adds video, bulk upload, strength adjustment, 4 hours/day, files up to 1 GB / 2 hours, and unlimited Studio downloads. No self-serve USD Premium price was published on that official page during this check.

Udio

Free includes 100 monthly credits and a 10-credit daily limit. Standard is $10/month with 1,200 monthly credits; Pro is $30/month with 4,800 monthly credits. Top-ups are $3 for 100 credits or $25 for 1,000 credits.
