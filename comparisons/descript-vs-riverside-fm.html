    Evidence status

    Descript

    Free $0 includes 1 media hour/month and 100 one-time AI credits. Hobbyist is $24/month or $16/person/month billed annually, with 10 media hours and 400 AI credits/month. Creator is $35/month or $24/person/month billed annually, with 30 media hours and 800 AI credits/month. Business is $65/month or $50/person/month billed annually, with 40 media hours and 1,500 AI credits/month. Enterprise has custom pricing, media minutes, AI credits, retention, legal terms, and AI controls.

    Official sources checked 2026-09-18

    Open verification questions
    • The terms establish user ownership of protectable Output but do not provide a single universal commercial-license clause covering every generated feature and bundled asset. Commercial use therefore remains subject to third-party media licenses and applicable rights.
    • The privacy policy's general language allows AI training and service improvement, while newer Help Center guidance says in-house training is opt-in and production models use no user data. The exact default for a particular non-Enterprise account should be confirmed in its current Share Data setting.
    • AI-generated output may not qualify for copyright protection and may resemble output generated for other users.

    Terms last updated February 25, 2025 say Descript claims no ownership of User Content. Inputs and AI-generated Outputs are User Content, and as between the user and Descript, the user owns them to the extent protectable under applicable law. Descript warns that outputs may not be unique or copyrightable and makes the user responsible for third-party rights and legal suitability. Descript may use Inputs and Outputs for model and service development unless the user opts out; its agreements prohibit third-party AI providers from using those Inputs and Outputs to train their own models. Current Help Center guidance is more specific: production models use no Descript user data, internal R&D uses only data from users who opted into sharing, and Enterprise sharing is disabled by default. Descript requires explicit recorded authorization from the depicted speaker before creating a custom voice clone; it refuses deceased, non-consenting, non-authorizing, or artificial-source voices. Consent source: https://help.descript.com/ai-speech/custom-speaker. Data-controls source: https://help.descript.com/account-and-app-settings/data-privacy. Premium Storyblocks media has a commercial-use license under its EULA, but other libraries may require separate clearance; GIPHY assets cannot be used commercially.

    Riverside

    Riverside's official pricing page lists Free at $0/month with 2 hours of one-off multi-track recording, 720p, a watermark, 44.1 kHz audio, and unlimited single-track recording. Official compare/FAQ copy lists Pro at $24/month billed annually or $29 monthly ($288/year) with a 14-day trial; Grow at $34/$39 ($408/year); Webinar at $79/$99; and Business as custom. Official FAQ states annual billing saves up to 20% and that recording is unlimited while separate-track download hours are capped (15 on Pro, 20 on Grow, 25 on Webinar).
