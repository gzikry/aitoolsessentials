    Descript

    Free $0 includes 1 media hour/month and 100 one-time AI credits. Hobbyist is $24/month or $16/person/month billed annually, with 10 media hours and 400 AI credits/month. Creator is $35/month or $24/person/month billed annually, with 30 media hours and 800 AI credits/month, scaling to a team of three billed separately. Business is $65/month or $50/person/month billed annually, with 40 media hours and 1,500 AI credits/month, scaling to a team of five billed separately. Enterprise has custom pricing, media minutes, AI credits, retention, legal terms, and AI controls. Annual billing saves up to 35%.

Descript warns that outputs may not be unique or copyrightable and makes the user responsible for third-party rights and legal suitability. Descript may use Inputs and Outputs for model and service development unless the user opts out; its agreements prohibit third-party AI providers from using those Inputs and Outputs to train their own models. Current Help Center guidance is more specific: production models use no Descript user data, internal R&D uses only data from users who opted into sharing, and Enterprise sharing is disabled by default.     Riverside

    Riverside's official pricing page lists Free at $0/month with 2 hours of one-off multi-track recording, 720p, a watermark, 44.1 kHz audio, and unlimited single-track recording. Official compare/FAQ copy lists Pro at $24/month billed annually or $29 monthly ($288/year) with a 14-day trial; Grow at $34/$39 ($408/year); Webinar at $79/$99; and Business as custom. Official FAQ states annual billing saves up to 20% and that recording is unlimited while separate-track download hours are capped (15 on Pro, 20 on Grow, 25 on Webinar).
