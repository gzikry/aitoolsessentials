    Adobe Firefly

    Official US monthly pricing checked 2026-11-14: Firefly Standard US$9.99/month with 2,000 generative credits, Firefly Pro US$19.99/month with 4,000 credits (promotional US$9.99 for the first 6 months, ends 2026-12-06), and Firefly Pro Plus US$49.99/month with 10,000 credits (promotional US$19.99 for the first 6 months, ends 2026-12-06). Pro includes unlimited image generations on select models for the first year and Pro Plus adds unlimited video generations on select models for the first year. All paid tiers add Firefly Boards and access to partner models such as Google and OpenAI. The Firefly Premium tier recorded at US$199.99 with 50,000 credits on 2026-10-01 was not displayed in the individual-plans block on 2026-11-14.
