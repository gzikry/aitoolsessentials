Microsoft Dragon Copilot

Microsoft's official Dragon Copilot product page does not publish a self-serve consumer price list; next steps route to a Microsoft representative. Microsoft Learn documents Physician Per User and Physician Flex licenses sold through a direct customer agreement or a Microsoft certified partner. Flex ambient and generative AI features can require linking an Azure subscription, and Microsoft publishes a consumption-unit description for Flex AI-assisted sessions.

  • No official self-serve USD list price for the Physician Per User license was published on the product page during this check.
  • Consumption-unit rates and included Flex sessions can change and should be read from current Microsoft licensing guidance.
  • Availability by country and care setting is not fully enumerated on the marketing page.

Official product materials describe physician, nurse, and radiologist experiences and a migration path from Dragon Medical One. Do not copy third-party reseller dollar figures as official Microsoft list prices. Confirm current SKUs and quotes with Microsoft or a certified partner.

Heidi Health

Heidi's official pricing page lists Free, Clinician, and Teams plans and a feature comparison covering transcription, templates, Ask Heidi, evidence features, team controls, EHR integration, SSO, and custom hosting. A free starting plan is advertised. Exact regional paid dollar amounts were not consistently exposed on the official pricing page during this verification, so no unverified USD figures are reported.
