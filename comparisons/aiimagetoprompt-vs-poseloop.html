    AI Image to Prompt vs PoseLoop (poseloop.app)

    Creators and small teams who already have a reference image and need a written brief for it: illustrators testing an art direction, designers producing concept variants, marketers fixing a campaign's visual language from a moodboard, and hobbyists learning how image prompts are structured. It fits the free-and-anonymous case well - no account, eight output languages, model-specific formatting for Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux and Nano Banana - and it fits someone who wants one editable paragraph rather than four variants at once. It is a poor fit if you need to know who operates the site, what law governs it, or what anything costs: there is no company, controller, postal address or named jurisdiction anywhere, the only address published is a personal Gmail, the pricing page belongs to a different product, and six five-star testimonials from named people carry no evidence they are real. · Creators and small social teams who want to post a trend clip while the format is still climbing, starting from one or two photos rather than a prompt or a shoot - one photo for most trends, two for a duet, up to five friends, or one shot of a car. It suits people who will read the per-render credit price on the Generate button before committing and who are happy to buy a one-time credit pack (credits never expire) rather than subscribe. It is a weaker fit for a buyer who needs a registered street address or a named human counterparty to contract with, who wants every data processor named as a company before uploading faces, who wants a flat per-clip price list on the pricing page, or who wants a tool with a third-party track record - the domain is about two months old and no independent review of it exists.
