When Zapier is the better fit

Zapier is often the fastest starting point for business users who need simple app-to-app automations and a very broad integration catalog.

When Make or n8n is the better fit

Make is strong for visual multi-step scenarios. n8n is stronger when technical teams want deeper control, self-hosting options, and developer-oriented workflow design.

Quick recommendation

Choose Zapier AI when its strengths match your repeated workflow. Choose Make when its ecosystem, interface, or specialist capability reduces review time. For revenue-oriented buyers, test both with a real task before clicking into a paid plan.
