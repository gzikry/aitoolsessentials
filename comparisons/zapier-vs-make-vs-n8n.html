Evidence status

Zapier AI

Core Zapier plans: Free at $0 with 100 tasks/month; Professional starts at $19.99/month when billed annually; Team is $69/month when billed annually; Enterprise is custom. AI steps, code, MCP, SDK, and Zap workflows draw from a shared task pool. In AI by Zapier, Standard model steps consume 1 task per run, Advanced 3 tasks, and Premium 5 tasks; tool calls add task usage. The pricing page separately lists Agents Free with up to 400 automated activities/month and Agents Pro with up to 1,500 activities/month, but did not expose a dependable Agents Pro price in the retrieved official page text.

Make

At the displayed 10,000-credit tier: Free is $0 with up to 1,000 credits/month; Core is $12/month; Pro is $21/month; Teams is $38/month; Enterprise is sales-led. Most module actions use 1 credit, while AI features using Make's AI Provider can consume an operation credit plus token-based credits. Make's AI Provider is available on all plans; custom AI-provider connections are available on paid plans. Credits normally expire at the end of the subscription term, and extra-credit bundles are available on eligible paid plans.

n8n

Starter: €20/month billed annually for 2,500 workflow executions with unlimited steps and 2,300 AI credits/month. Pro: €50/month billed annually for 10,000 executions and either 5,700 or up to 13,700 AI credits/month depending on plan size. Business: €667/month billed annually for 40,000 executions and self-hosted deployment. Enterprise: custom. Starter and Pro trials include 800 AI Assistant credits. AI-credit balances refresh monthly and unused credits do not roll over; additional AI credits cannot currently be purchased.

When Zapier is the better fit

Zapier is often the fastest starting point for business users who need simple app-to-app automations and a very broad integration catalog.

When Make or n8n is the better fit

Make is strong for visual multi-step scenarios. n8n is stronger when technical teams want deeper control, self-hosting options, and developer-oriented workflow design.

Quick recommendation

Choose Zapier AI when its strengths match your repeated workflow. Choose Make when its ecosystem, interface, or specialist capability reduces review time. Test both with a real task before clicking into a paid plan.
