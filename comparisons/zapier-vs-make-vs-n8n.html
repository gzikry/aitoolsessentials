    Zapier AI

    Core Zapier plans: Free at $0 with 100 tasks/month; Professional starts at $19.99/month when billed annually; Team is $69/month when billed annually; Enterprise is custom. AI steps, code, MCP, SDK, and Zap workflows draw from a shared task pool. In AI by Zapier, Standard model steps consume 1 task per run, Advanced 3 tasks, and Premium 5 tasks; tool calls add task usage. The pricing page separately lists Agents Free with up to 400 automated activities/month and Agents Pro with up to 1,500 activities/month, but did not expose a dependable Agents Pro price in the retrieved official page text.

    Make

    At the displayed 10,000-credit tier: Free is $0 with up to 1,000 credits/month; Core is $12/month; Pro is $21/month; Teams is $38/month; Enterprise is sales-led. Most module actions use 1 credit, while AI features using Make's AI Provider can consume an operation credit plus token-based credits. Make's AI Provider is available on all plans; custom AI-provider connections are available on paid plans. Credits normally expire at the end of the subscription term, and extra-credit bundles are available on eligible paid plans.

    Official sources checked 2026-10-01

    n8n

    Starter: €20/month billed annually for 2,500 workflow executions with unlimited steps and 1,600 Assistant credits/month. Pro: €50/month billed annually for 10,000 executions with either 4,000 or up to 9,600 Assistant credits/month depending on plan size. Business: €667/month billed annually for 40,000 executions, self-hosted. Enterprise: custom. Free trials include 800 Assistant credits plus 100 AI credits that expire when the trial ends. Assistant-credit balances refresh monthly and unused credits do not roll over; additional AI credits cannot currently be purchased. The page advertises saving 17% on annual billing.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which automation tool is cheapest?

    It depends on your workflow shape. Zapier bills per task ($19.99+/mo annual for Professional), Make bills credits (~$12/mo at the 10k tier), n8n bills executions (€20/mo for 2,500 runs). Complex multi-step workflows are usually cheapest on n8n because steps inside a run are unlimited.

    Can I self-host any of these?

    Only n8n. It offers a free self-hosted community edition. Zapier and Make are cloud-only.

    Which is best for AI-powered automations?

    Check credit/task costs of AI steps specifically: Make charges more credits for AI modules, and Zapier counts AI steps as tasks. Model your actual scenario volume in each platform's calculator before choosing.
