Evidence status

DeepSeek

DeepSeek's official website describes its consumer web chat as free. No official paid consumer or team subscription schedule was located. API access is separately usage-priced, with current model and peak/off-peak rates published in DeepSeek's API pricing documentation.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25
Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
Open verification questions
  • No official consumer team subscription or enterprise-seat price was found.
  • Current API prices are model-, token-, cache-, and peak/off-peak-dependent and are not summarized as a single price.
  • Output ownership does not establish that every output is copyrightable or free of third-party rights.

DeepSeek's privacy policy says Inputs and related personal data may be used to improve services and train machine-learning models and that collected personal data is directly processed and stored in the People's Republic of China. Its terms state that users retain any rights in Inputs and that DeepSeek assigns to users any rights it may have in Outputs, subject to applicable law and the terms; the terms expressly mention lawful uses including personal use, research, derivative-product development, and training other models.

Mistral Le Chat

Le Chat is now Mistral Vibe. Free plan available; Pro is $14.99/month excluding taxes; Team is $24.99/user/month excluding taxes; Enterprise pricing is custom.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25
Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
Open verification questions
  • No blanket commercial-use conclusion was made for consumer subscriptions because the consumer terms limit access to personal use and direct business integrations to separate commercial terms.
  • Taxes and final localized prices vary.

Official documentation confirms the rename to Vibe and three modes: Work, Code, and Chat. Free-plan conversations may be used to improve models with an opt-out; Pro, Team, and Enterprise conversations are not used for training by default. Connectors and uploaded library documents are not used for training. Consumer terms assign Mistral's rights in outputs to the user, but consumer access is expressly personal; business integrations and products distributed to third parties are directed to Mistral's commercial terms.
