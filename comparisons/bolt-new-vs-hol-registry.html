    Bolt.new

    Free: $0, public and private projects, 300,000-token daily limit, 1 million tokens/month, 10 MB uploads, Bolt-branded hosting, up to 333,000 web requests, and unlimited databases. Pro: $25/month, no daily token limit, starts at 10 million tokens/month, 100 MB uploads, token rollover, custom domains, private sharing, and up to 1 million web requests. Teams: $30/member/month with Pro features plus centralized billing and administrative controls. Enterprise is custom-priced.

    HOL Registry

    Free. Browsing the plugin catalogue and submitting a public plugin repository cost nothing - stated in the catalogue's own FAQ ("list your public repository for free") and in the live https://hol.org/plugins/llms.txt, which sells nothing. No paid tier exists for the catalogue itself; paid credits apply to other HOL registry operations, and HOL Guard is a separately priced product with its own plans. Verified 2026-10-14 from the live /plugins/llms.txt plus the archived catalogue page, because live catalogue HTML is served behind a Cloudflare challenge and returned 403 to every scripted client.
