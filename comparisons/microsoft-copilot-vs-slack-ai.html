    Evidence status

    Microsoft Copilot

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is available for work or education without an additional Copilot license and supports metered pay-as-you-go agents. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business is displayed at a promotional $18/user/month, paid yearly, versus a $21 list price. Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot is $23.50/user/month paid yearly, and Business Premium with Copilot is $32/user/month paid yearly. Prices are USD, annual subscriptions that auto-renew, and may vary by market.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25
    Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
    Open verification questions
    • The general pricing page does not expose one comprehensive price for every Microsoft Copilot product or enterprise licensing scenario.
    • Pay-as-you-go agent consumption depends on the selected agent and metering arrangement; no single universal usage limit or price is stated on the overview page.
    • The $18 Microsoft 365 Copilot Business amount is promotional; the checked pricing page does not state its expiration directly.

    This verification is scoped to Microsoft 365 Copilot for business, not every consumer or product-specific service branded Copilot. Microsoft 365 Copilot works in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, Loop, and other Microsoft 365 surfaces; it uses Microsoft Graph data the user is permitted to access and includes Copilot Chat, Copilot Search, agents, and app-specific drafting, summarization, analysis, and meeting capabilities. Prompts, responses, and Microsoft Graph data are not used to train foundation LLMs. Interaction content is stored under the customer's Microsoft 365 contractual commitments, encrypted at rest, manageable through Microsoft Purview, and deletable through Copilot activity history controls. Microsoft does not claim ownership of service output but does not determine whether output is copyright-protected or enforceable. Its Copilot Copyright Commitment can defend qualifying commercial customers against certain copyright claims when required guardrails and content filters were used. The official privacy and overview documentation was updated July 9, 2026.

    Slack AI

    Free: $0. Pro: $7.25/user/month when paid annually or $8.75 when paid monthly. Business+: $15/user/month annually or $18 monthly. Enterprise+ uses sales-based pricing. The pricing page labels Free and Pro as including Basic AI and Business+ as including Advanced AI; the exact feature set depends on plan and admin settings. The page was also displaying a 50%-off-for-three-months promotion for eligible Pro and Business+ purchases.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25
    Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗
    Open verification questions
    • Slack's AI Principles page says AI features are included in paid plans, while the current pricing page labels the Free plan as having Basic AI. The exact production availability and limits of Free-plan AI could not be reconciled from the official pages.
    • Slack does not publish a single numeric usage allowance for all AI features; some capabilities state that plan limits apply.
    • No explicit, AI-output-specific ownership statement was located in the checked official Slack sources.

    Official pages list capabilities including Slackbot, conversation and thread summaries, daily recaps, AI search, file summaries, workflow generation, huddle notes, translation, and enterprise search, with availability varying by plan. Slack states that Customer Data never leaves Slack-controlled infrastructure and is never used to train LLMs. Third-party LLMs are hosted within Slack's cloud environment; providers do not have access to Customer Data. AI only uses content the requesting member is authorized to access. Conversation summaries and search answers are ephemeral; recaps are stored for 90 days. Workflow-generated summaries stored in messages or canvases follow the organization's retention settings. Admins can disable or restrict native AI features.
