    Change Text In Image vs Layergrab

    Creators, e-commerce sellers and small marketing teams who need to change words already baked into a finished image - a typo on an exported ad, a price on a product shot, a date on an invitation, a label in a screenshot - without the source file, priced per edit at 10 credits with a one-time pack instead of a subscription. Also worth a look if you want a text-blur tool that provably runs on your own device. Not a fit if you need a named company and jurisdiction before paying, if you need account password recovery, if you need a committed deletion deadline for uploaded images, or if you are editing IDs, receipts, bank statements or other evidence (banned in its own acceptable-use policy). · Designers, ecommerce sellers and social-media editors who need to pull one element off a flat image - a product, a poster's text, a sticker - as a clean transparent layer without opening desktop software. The Chrome extension (right-click any image on the web) is the fastest version of the job. Not a fit for anyone who needs a named company and a refund policy before paying, or for bulk API automation (no developer API is offered).

    Change Text in Image

    One-time credit packs only, USD, server-rendered under /#pricing: Starter $14.90 for 400 credits, Pro $26.90 for 1,000 and Studio $39.90 for 1,800, each card converting its allowance into text-edit generations and a per-edit price - '400 credits - $0.37 USD / edit - 40 text-edit generations', '1,000 - $0.27 - 100', '1,800 - $0.22 - 180'. The footnote reads 'Each generation uses 10 credits. New accounts receive 10 free credits. One-time purchase, no recurring charges', and the Terms add that credits do not expire while the account remains available and failed generations return the credits spent. The arithmetic reconciles on every card (400/10 = 40 at 14.90/40 = $0.3725, 1,000/10 = 100 at $0.269, 1,800/10 = 180 at $0.2217). The Chinese mirror /zh carries the identical price tokens and the same three packs. No subscription, no yearly plan, no add-ons; checkout labels every card 'Pay with Waffo' and /api/config/public returns waffo_enabled:'true'. Refunds are a Terms clause, not a separate page: full refund within 7 days only if none of that purchase's credits have been used. Verified 2026-11-18 on /#pricing, /zh and /api/config/public.
