    Edit Pic Text vs Pawheadshots

    Creators, ecommerce and small marketing teams who need to change words that are already baked into an image - fixing a typo on an exported flyer, updating a price on a product shot, relabelling UI copy in a screenshot - without the original design file, at 5 credits per edit with the cost shown before you send. Also worth a look if you need text extraction (1 credit) or image translation in the same place. Not a fit if you need the vendor to name the company you are paying, if you are editing identity documents or payment screenshots (banned in its own terms), or if you want a vendor whose pricing page describes only the product it sells. · Cat and dog owners who want a comedy headshot pack out of one phone photo - 30 fixed looks plus 6 styles you choose, with a matching Employee ID, PawkedIn profile and printable Pet Resume - as a single $19.90 one-time purchase; a $29.90 'Me & My Human' pack does 30 portraits of you and your pet together. Not a fit if you need to see the results before paying (there is no preview), if you expect the files to be stored for you (they are deleted 7 days after purchase), or if you need a company address and governing-law clause before you buy.
