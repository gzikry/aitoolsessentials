    Design comparison

    MiniMax H3 Image (minimaxh3image.com) vs SharkFoto

    Creators, marketers and small teams who want several current image and video models behind one account and one credit balance instead of an account per model - product photos, portraits, social visuals and short clips assembled in the browser, with a credit estimate shown before every generation. It fits light use on the Lite tier, regular creation on Pro and heavier production on Ultra, and a one-time pack suits buyers who do not want a subscription. Not a fit if you need a named company to contract with or a governing-law clause to review, a published retention period or training statement, a dedicated refund page, or the single best model for one job - the pitch here is breadth across a shared model catalogue, and the same platform is already listed here as 01Router. · Small teams and solo creators who want one browser workspace that generates and edits images, video, audio and 3D from text or an uploaded photo, plus a genuinely free file-tool layer (conversion, compression, resize, embroidery) that needs no account. It suits a buyer who values a named operator and a real governing-law clause, disclosed Google Analytics and AdSense IDs, and an explicit no-training statement on uploads. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs every model tier-guaranteed, who objects to an ad-supported free tier that sets analytics and advertising cookies on first visit without a shown banner (from the US), or who wants a published registered street address rather than the entity and the city alone - and a weaker fit for anyone who would rely on the '4.7/5 based on 4,644+ ratings' badge, which the site does not source.
