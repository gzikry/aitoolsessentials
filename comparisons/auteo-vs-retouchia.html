    Auteo (auteo.ai) vs Retouchia

    Solo creators and small teams who want one subscription that turns a prompt into video, images, voice and music instead of buying each medium from a different site, and who want the credit cost of every render shown before they commit. It suits a buyer who values a named operating entity (Sigmify LLC, Wyoming) with a real governing-law and venue clause, processors named as companies, an explicit no-training statement, and a storefront that advertises no false urgency. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs an operating (not registered-agent) address, retention committed to fixed periods, a free or one-off route that avoids a subscription entirely, or a track record - the domain is about two months old and the legal set was rewritten the same week. · Ecommerce sellers, creators and small marketing teams who need occasional one-off photo edits - background removal, object removal, upscaling, old-photo restoration, colourising - and would rather buy a $9.99 credit pack than add another monthly AI subscription. Not a fit for anyone who needs a named legal counterparty, a contractual jurisdiction, or a guarantee about which specific model runs their image.

    Retouchia

    Free: 5 credits on sign-up, advertised as ~1 image, no card required, full-resolution downloads on the free tier. Paid: two one-time packs only, no subscription - Basic $9.99 for 350 credits (shown against a $12 anchor, "Save 16%") and Pro $29.99 for 1,200 credits (against a $39.90 anchor, "Save 24%"). The cards advertise ~70 images from 350 credits and ~240 from 1,200, i.e. 5 credits per image, while the Terms state "Each public output costs 4 credits". Paid credits are valid 365 days from purchase (Terms section 6, Refund Policy section 1, and the "Credits valid for 1 year" line on each card); the Terms state no coded expiry reminder is sent. No monthly or yearly plan is sold. Payment card data is processed by Waffo Pancake. Prices in USD and identical on /pricing, /zh/pricing, /zh-tw/pricing and llms.txt.
