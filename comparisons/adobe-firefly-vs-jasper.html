Adobe Firefly

Official US monthly pricing lists Firefly Standard at US$9.99 with 2,000 generative credits/month; Firefly Pro at US$19.99 with 4,000 credits/month; Firefly Pro Plus regularly US$49.99 with 10,000 credits/month; and Firefly Premium regularly US$199.99 with 50,000 credits/month. On the verification date, Pro Plus and Premium had first-year 30% promotions of US$34.97 and US$139.91 per month, ending 2026-10-21. Standard image features are unlimited; credits cover premium features such as video, audio, and partner-model generations. Promotional unlimited access to selected models applies for the first year under the offer terms.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Open verification questions
  • The exact free-plan credit allocation was not exposed consistently on the public pricing page and is therefore not stated.
  • Promotional unlimited-generation model coverage can change and is governed by Adobe's current promotion page and offer terms.
  • Adobe does not warrant that output is non-infringing, unique, or copyrightable.
  • IP indemnification is plan- and contract-specific, particularly for enterprise offerings, and should not be assumed for every Firefly subscription or beta feature.

Adobe's official Firefly FAQ and policy statements say Adobe does not train Firefly foundation models on customer content; current Firefly models use licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public-domain content. Adobe's General Terms say users retain rights and ownership in their Content and Adobe does not claim ownership. The Generative AI User Guidelines state that output may generally be used commercially; if Adobe specifically designates a beta feature as non-commercial, output from that feature is personal-use only. Credits renew monthly and do not roll over.

Jasper

Pro: $69/month per seat when billed monthly or $59/month per seat when billed yearly, with a seven-day free trial and one included seat. Pro includes two Brand Voices, five Knowledge assets, and three Audiences. Business: custom pricing and includes advanced agents, a no-code Custom Agent builder, Jasper Grid, unlimited Brand Voices/Knowledge/Audiences, API access, enterprise governance, account management, and flexible deployment options.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25
Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
Open verification questions
  • Jasper's public self-service terms permit model improvement using Customer Property; separately negotiated Business or enterprise contracts may contain different data-use restrictions that are not publicly verifiable.
  • No universal word, generation, or token limit is stated on the current public pricing page.
  • The public privacy page does not present a concise AI-training control comparable to the explicit model-improvement clause in the Terms of Service.

Official pages describe Jasper as a marketing-focused platform with Canvas, Jasper IQ, Brand Voice, Knowledge Base, Audiences, agents, content pipelines, AI Studio, Grid, APIs, and more than 30 supported languages. Jasper's public self-service Terms of Service define inputs and outputs as Customer Property, state that Jasper claims no ownership over Customer Property, and say Customer Property remains the customer's. However, those same terms grant Jasper a royalty-free, sublicensable, transferable, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license over Customer Property and state that Jasper uses Customer Property, including inputs and outputs, to provide, maintain, and improve the services, including developing and enhancing Jasper's AI models. Jasper's security page says customer data is retained for the agreement's duration and afterward according to its Data Retention Policy unless a written deletion request is received. The public terms checked were effective May 30, 2025.
