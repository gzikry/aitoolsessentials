    Makifyai vs Unblurfy

    E-commerce sellers, small marketing teams and creators who turn existing product shots into listing images, ads, mockups and social graphics without a designer - one browser workspace over GPT Image 2.5/2.6, the Nano Banana 2.x family, Seedream 5.0 and FLUX 3, with 30 free credits on sign-up and one-time packs from $9.90. A sensible fit if you want per-image credit control instead of a fixed subscription and will read the refund rule before paying: 3 calendar days, 50 paid credits or fewer, minus a 2-10% processing fee. Not a fit if you need to know who the data controller is, how long uploaded product photos are kept, or that your outputs are licensed for commercial use before you upload client material - no privacy notice on the site names a controller, address or retention period, training use is never mentioned, and the Terms decline to guarantee commercial usability even though the marketing copy says a commercial licence is included. · Anyone rescuing an ordinary photo - a shaky family snapshot, a soft product shot, a blurred screenshot of text, a scan from a shoebox - who wants a fix without signing up or installing anything: one free 1K enhance a day with no watermark, then either one-time credits that never expire or a small monthly plan for 2K output and 20-image batches. Also covers old-photo restoration, colourising and video-frame extraction from the same credit pool. Not a fit if you need 4K output or an API, if the company you are paying has to be named before you buy, or if the job is revealing censored, mosaicked or paywalled content - refused in the site's own terms.
