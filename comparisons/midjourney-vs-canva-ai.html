    Evidence status

    Midjourney

    Official plans are Basic at US$10/month or US$96/year with 3.3 Fast GPU hours/month; Standard at US$30/month or US$288/year with 15 Fast GPU hours/month and unlimited Relax image generations; Pro at US$60/month or US$576/year with 30 Fast GPU hours/month, unlimited Relax images and SD video, and Stealth Mode; and Mega at US$120/month or US$1,152/year with 60 Fast GPU hours/month, unlimited Relax images and SD video, and Stealth Mode. Annual billing is a 20% discount. Unused subscription Fast time does not roll over.

    Official sources checked 2026-10-01

    Open verification questions
    • The official Privacy Policy discusses training-data collection and service improvement but does not expressly state, in the reviewed text, whether every user's prompts or generated Assets are used for model training.
    • Midjourney does not guarantee that generated Assets are copyrightable, unique, or free of third-party rights.
    • Commercial ownership for organizations above US$1 million annual revenue depends on maintaining the required Pro or Mega subscription when creating the Assets.
    • Relax Mode is unlimited subject to queues and operational limits; it is not a guaranteed throughput rate.

    The current Terms are effective 2026-05-27. They say users own Assets they create to the fullest extent possible under applicable law, subject to third-party rights and the Agreement. Companies, and employees of companies, with more than US$1 million in annual revenue must use Pro or Mega to own Assets. Midjourney receives a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, sublicensable, royalty-free, irrevocable license over submitted Content and generated Assets. Content is publicly viewable and remixable by default; Stealth Mode is limited to Pro and Mega. The Privacy Policy covers prompts and uploaded content and says Midjourney may use personal data to provide, maintain, and improve the service. Re-verified 2026-10-01: Official plan pages still match the recorded Basic/Standard/Pro/Mega prices, Fast GPU-hour allocations and the 20% annual discount; no drift detected.

    Canva AI

    The official US annual pricing view (checked 2026-10-01) lists Canva Free at US$0/year for one person, Canva Pro at US$144/year for one person, Canva Business at US$250/year per person, and Enterprise through sales; the page advertises saving from 16% on yearly billing. AI allowance as published on 2026-10-01: Canva Free gets up to 20 uses of Standard or Premium AI tools with no Ultra AI access; on paid plans Standard AI tools can be used abundantly and do not draw from the allowance, Premium AI tools draw up to 200 uses, and Ultra AI tools up to 20 uses, all against a single monthly allowance pooled across AI tools. Pro is described as 10x and Business as 20x the Canva Free AI allowance. An optional AI Pass add-on and AI top-ups are sold at extra cost on some plans. Allowances reset monthly.

    Official sources checked 2026-10-01
    Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
    Open verification questions
    • Canva prices are localized and may be personalized by billing region; the summary records the official US annual view observed on the verification date.
    • The official pages do not translate the shared AI allowance into a guaranteed generation count because task complexity changes consumption.
    • Canva does not guarantee that AI output is unique, copyrightable, accurate, or free of third-party rights.
    • Commercial use of output containing Canva library content depends on the separate license for that content.

    Canva states that allowance consumption depends on the tool, model, and task complexity; allowances reset monthly. Canva's privacy page says User Content is not used to improve AI-powered features unless consistent with the user's Privacy Settings, which users can review and change. The AI Product Terms say that, as between Canva and the user and to the maximum extent permitted by law, the user owns Output and may use it for any lawful purpose. Output that incorporates or modifies Canva Licensed Content remains subject to the applicable content license. Outputs may not be unique. Re-verified 2026-10-01: Canva Pro is now US$144/year for one person on the annual view, replacing the US$180/year recorded on 2026-08-25. The published AI allowance has also been restated: Free is now described as up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses (previously recorded as 200 Standard / 20 Premium), and paid plans are expressed as multipliers of the Free allowance rather than fixed Standard/Premium/Ultra counts.
