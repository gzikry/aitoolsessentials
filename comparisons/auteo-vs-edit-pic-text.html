    Design comparison

    Auteo (auteo.ai) vs Edit Pic Text

    Solo creators and small teams who want one subscription that turns a prompt into video, images, voice and music instead of buying each medium from a different site, and who want the credit cost of every render shown before they commit. It suits a buyer who values a named operating entity (Sigmify LLC, Wyoming) with a real governing-law and venue clause, processors named as companies, an explicit no-training statement, and a storefront that advertises no false urgency. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs an operating (not registered-agent) address, retention committed to fixed periods, a free or one-off route that avoids a subscription entirely, or a track record - the domain is about two months old and the legal set was rewritten the same week. · Creators, ecommerce and small marketing teams who need to change words that are already baked into an image - fixing a typo on an exported flyer, updating a price on a product shot, relabelling UI copy in a screenshot - without the original design file, at 5 credits per edit with the cost shown before you send. Also worth a look if you need text extraction (1 credit) or image translation in the same place. Not a fit if you need the vendor to name the company you are paying, if you are editing identity documents or payment screenshots (banned in its own terms), or if you want a vendor whose pricing page describes only the product it sells.
