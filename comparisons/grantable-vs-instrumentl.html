Evidence status

Grantable

Grantable's official pricing page lists Free at $0 with 5 chat messages/day and file imports up to 10 MB; Starter at $50/month; and Pro at $150/month. Qualifying 501(c)(3) organizations with annual operating budgets under approximately $500,000 get 50% off Starter and Pro for one year ($25/month and $75/month). Official docs also describe a Pro+ tier; a public dollar price for Pro+ was not listed on the main pricing page during this check. An Agency Hub add-on is listed at $300/month.

Instrumentl

Instrumentl's official pricing page lists Discover at $299/month paid upfront annually or $349 paid monthly (up to 3 users); Pre-Award at $499/month annually or $579 monthly (up to 5 users); Full Lifecycle at $999/month annually or $1,159 monthly (up to 15 users); and Enterprise as custom pricing. A 14-day free trial with no credit card is advertised on self-serve plans.
