    Indie creators who want one credit-based workspace for AI images and short AI video without juggling multiple subscriptions · Small teams and solo creators who want one browser workspace that generates and edits images, video, audio and 3D from text or an uploaded photo, plus a genuinely free file-tool layer (conversion, compression, resize, embroidery) that needs no account. It suits a buyer who values a named operator and a real governing-law clause, disclosed Google Analytics and AdSense IDs, and an explicit no-training statement on uploads.     Evidence status

    AIBase

    Official /en/pricing lists Free trial credits, plus monthly Basic $29.9/mo (was $39.9), Pro $49.9/mo (was $69.9), and Ultimate $99.9/mo (was $120) with 800, 1,400, and 2,800 credits per month respectively. Yearly billing is 20–40% off (Basic yearly ~$180; Pro yearly equivalent ~$29.9 × 12 × 0.6 ≈ $215; Ultimate $99.9 × 12 with 40% off ≈ $720, saving $238.8 on the Pro tier). Pay-as-you-go credit packs available. Per the public Credits Cost Table, text-to-image runs 12–46 credits (~$0.05–$0.18) and 5-second video runs 150–380 credits (~$0.60–$1.52). Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, Seedream 5.0 Lite, Seedance 1.5 Pro, and Veo 3.1 Fast run on monthly; Veo 3.1 Pro, Seedance 2.0, and HappyHorse 1.0 require yearly or credit packs.

    SharkFoto

    Free plan $0/month with 10 credits and no credit card. Personal $10.00/month, or $9.00/month billed annually at $108.00 (500 credits a month, $0.018/credit). Professional $30.00/month, or $27.00/month billed annually at $324.00 (2,000 credits a month, $0.0135/credit). Each annual total equals twelve monthly payments at the discounted rate (9x12=108; 27x12=324). All paid plans list the same features: AI image, video and music generation, AI Effects, embroidery digitizing, batch conversion up to 30 files, 5 GB / 10 GB storage, no watermark, commercial license. Web checkout is Stripe; in-app purchases are Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple's App Store (iOS). The Free tier's 10 credits and the paid prices were read from the rendered /pricing on 2026-11-26 with the Monthly/Yearly toggle and enabled 'Buy Now'/'Sign Up Free' buttons. The Refund Policy states a 7-day window for subscription credits only, a 50-subscription-credit usage cap, add-on credits non-refundable, and a processing fee up to 6%.
