    Evidence status

    GitHub Copilot

    Copilot Free is free and limited to 2,000 completions per month, auto model selection, and an unspecified allowance of GitHub AI Credits. Copilot Student is free for verified students. Paid individual plans: Pro $10/month with 1,500 total monthly AI credits; Pro+ $39/month with 7,000; Max $100/month with 20,000. Organization plans: Business $19/user/month with 1,900 AI credits per user; Enterprise $39/user/month with 3,900. Additional usage is $0.01 per AI credit; code completions and next-edit suggestions remain unlimited on paid plans.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25

    Open verification questions
    • GitHub does not publish a numeric AI-credit allowance for Copilot Free or Student on the plans page.
    • The public Terms do not give a blanket commercial-use warranty, non-infringement guarantee, or ownership assignment for generated Output; they only state that GitHub does not claim ownership.
    • Starting April 22, 2026, new self-serve Copilot Business sign-ups for organizations on GitHub Free and GitHub Team were temporarily paused; the official page did not publish an end date.

    GitHub's Terms effective April 27, 2026 state that GitHub does not claim ownership of Copilot Input or Output. For individual licenses, GitHub and its affiliates may use Inputs and Outputs to develop, train, and improve AI/ML models unless the user opts out in account settings. The individual-license training provision does not apply when use is governed by a GitHub Customer Agreement or volume licensing agreement; those agreements control data use. GitHub says Inputs and Outputs are not shared with third-party AI model providers for those providers' independent model training. GitHub warns that Output can resemble training data or third-party/open-source code. Users must determine whether a license is required and review, test, and validate Output. GitHub's broader Terms grant GitHub and its affiliates rights to use hosted user content to develop and train AI technologies; the Copilot opt-out does not apply to public-repository content or the broader repository-content license.

    Lovable

    Free includes 5 daily build credits, capped at 30 per month, plus monthly grants of 20 Cloud credits and 4 credits for AI features in user-built apps. Pro is $25/month per workspace, shared across unlimited users, with 100 monthly credits and additional daily grants. Business is $50/month per workspace, shared across unlimited users, with 100 monthly credits and added SSO, roles, internal publishing, security, and workspace controls. Enterprise uses a company-size-based platform fee and volume-based credit pricing. Credit use varies by feature and task complexity; monthly plan credits expire two months after issue, while daily grants do not roll over.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25
    Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
    Open verification questions
    • As of August 21, the effective Terms already grant training rights with a prospective opt-out, while the product documentation says Free and Pro customer-data training begins September 9, 2026. The sources do not fully explain operational use during the intervening period.
    • Lovable does not guarantee that AI Output is unique, free of third-party rights, accurate, or suitable for a particular purpose.
    • Enterprise pricing is not published as a fixed amount.

    Official documentation describes Lovable as generating editable frontend, backend, database, authentication, and integration code from natural language, with GitHub synchronization and shared workspaces. Terms effective August 15, 2026 state that users own Customer Data, including applications, websites, and projects, and own AI Output generated for them, subject to third-party rights in underlying models, training data, or outputs. The Terms permit personal or internal-business use, including creating, deploying, operating, and making built applications available to end users. The Terms grant Lovable a broad license to use Customer Data, including for AI/ML development and training, but users can opt out prospectively for free regardless of plan. The official data-control documentation says Free and Pro customer-data training is scheduled to begin September 9, 2026 and users may opt out before or after that date. Business and Enterprise workspace data is excluded from training by default under organization agreements and DPAs. The April 14, 2026 Privacy Policy says raw or identifiable Personal Data is not used for training, while anonymized or aggregated data may be used.
