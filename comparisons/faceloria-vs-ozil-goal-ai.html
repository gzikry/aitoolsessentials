    Faceloria vs Ozil Goal Ai

    People curious about face-shape analysis who want a structured facial-measurement report rather than a horoscope-style app: upload one front-facing photo, get a face-shape category, width-to-height ratio and vertical-balance measurements for free, then decide whether the $16.99 one-time Full Report (4-week styling action plan and PDF) is worth it. Not a fit for anyone who needs a named company, jurisdiction or refund policy before paying, or for anyone who finds a 0-100 attractiveness score unhelpful - that framing is part of the product. · Anyone who wants one photo of themselves (or of a consenting adult) turned into a short, fixed football clip - a goal celebration, a missed-goal gag, a header jump or a rabona - and would rather buy a one-time credit pack than start another monthly AI video subscription. Good for a single meme clip or social post from a photo you already have, with a free still preview before any payment, and for buyers who value a named operator and a plain-spoken policy. Not a fit for anyone who needs the real player in the clip (the templates use your photo, and uploading a public figure is forbidden), for high resolution or long output (480p or 720p, 9-15 seconds), for anyone unwilling to declare consent for a third party, or for a project where the soundtrack rights matter - the video's audio is the vendor's, not something the purchase grants you.
