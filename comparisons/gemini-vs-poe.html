Evidence status

Gemini

For US personal accounts, Gemini Free is $0 per month, Google AI Pro is $19.99 per month, and Google AI Ultra starts at $99.99 per month, with a $199.99 option offering higher limits. For organizations, Gemini is included as a core service in eligible Google Workspace editions; official US Workspace rates are $8.40/$7 per user per month for Business Starter, $16.80/$14 for Business Standard, and $26.40/$22 for Business Plus on flexible/annual plans respectively. Enterprise pricing is sales-assisted.

Open verification questions
  • Subscription prices, included benefits, and model limits vary by country, account type, taxes, and promotions.
  • Enterprise Workspace pricing requires Google Sales or a reseller.
  • Rights in generated material remain subject to Google terms, third-party rights, and whether applicable law recognizes intellectual-property protection.

Google's official Gemini Apps Privacy Hub, updated August 10, 2026, says Gemini data may be used to maintain and improve services and the machine-learning technologies powering them; users can manage whether activity improves Google AI through Gemini Apps Activity, subject to stated exceptions. Workspace editions provide enterprise-grade security and privacy, with availability and limits varying by edition and region.

Poe

Poe offers free daily points and annual subscriptions of $49.99/year for 10,000 points/day, $199.99/year for 660,000 points/month, $499.99/year for 1.65 million points/month, $999.99/year for 3.3 million points/month, and $2,499.99/year for 8.25 million points/month. Poe displays annual equivalents of $4.17, $16.67, $41.67, $83.33, and $208.33 per month. Taxes, currency conversion, regional availability, and app-store pricing may vary.

Open verification questions
  • Monthly-billing prices were not included in the extracted official pricing page, so only verified annual prices and their displayed monthly equivalents are reported.
  • No blanket commercial-use assurance was found because Poe grants a personal right to use the service and underlying model-provider policies may impose additional restrictions.
  • Privacy and training treatment varies by each bot or app's privacy-shield status.

Poe provides one interface for third-party text, image, audio, and video bots, custom bots/apps, group chats, and shared subscriptions. Fully shaded privacy-shield bots share data with model providers but interactions are not used by those providers for training; half-shaded bots/apps may share data with developers or external resources and may use interactions for training. Poe's terms define prompts and bot-generated results as 'Your Content' and state that users retain ownership, while granting Poe a perpetual license for stated service purposes. Use also remains subject to policies of the underlying model providers.
