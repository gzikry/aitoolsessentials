AIDetector.cx

Free: 5 introductory checks total (1 guest + 4 signed-in), no recurring reset. Pro US$12/month or US$120/year - 300 credits/month. Pro Plus US$29/month or US$290/year - 1,000 credits/month. Business US$79/month or US$790/year - 3,000 credits/month with up to 5 team seats. Annual billing is charged upfront and grants credits monthly on the renewal cycle. Credit costs vary by job: standard text/image/video detection 1 credit, advanced image & deepfake analysis 4 credits/image, forensic video & deepfake 6 credits per 30s, citation verification 1 credit per 5 references, hallucination checking 2 credits per 1,000 words. Verified 2026-10-08 from /pricing plus the shipped /assets/index-*.js monthlyPrice literals (12/29/79), which agree.

Shortwave

Business is $30/seat/month, Premier is $45/seat/month, and Max is $120/seat/month. Business includes standard intelligence (Claude Sonnet 4.6), roughly 150-300 AI requests/day, 5 years AI search history, 50 threads per AI search, 3 AI filters, and AI integrations. Premier adds advanced intelligence, 2x AI usage, unlimited AI search history, 100 threads per search, and 10 AI filters. Max adds expert intelligence (Claude Opus 4.6), 6x AI usage, 150 threads per search, and 50 AI filters. A 14-day free trial is advertised; no permanent free plan is listed on the pricing page.
