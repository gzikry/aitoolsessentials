Airtable AI

Free: $0 and 500 AI credits per editor each month. Team: $20/seat/month billed annually or $24 billed monthly, with 15,000 AI credits per billable collaborator each month. Business: $45/seat/month billed annually or $54 billed monthly, with 20,000 AI credits per paid user each month. Enterprise Scale: custom pricing with 25,000 AI credits per paid user at list price. Additional self-serve AI credits start at 10,000 for $20/month or $200/year and scale at the same published $2 per 1,000-credit monthly rate through 400,000 credits.

Open verification questions
  • The ownership clause does not guarantee that generated output is unique, copyrightable, or free of third-party rights.
  • Third-party providers may retain metadata for billing, safety, and compliance even where Input and Output retention is prohibited.
  • The current AI Terms still mention legacy Pro and Plus workspaces in the retention section even though the current public pricing lineup is Free, Team, Business, and Enterprise Scale.

Airtable's August 10, 2026 billing documentation says AI credits are pooled at workspace or organization level. Credits fund features such as Field Agents, AI automations, deep-research questions, feedback categorization, document analysis, and image generation; consumption varies by input, selected model, and output. The Airtable AI Terms, last updated July 23, 2026, classify Input and Output as Customer Data and state that customers retain all ownership rights in both. Airtable states that neither it nor its third-party AI providers will use Input, Output, or other Customer Data to train generative-AI models. For Business and Enterprise Scale, providers do not log Input or Output for human review or retain it, subject to stated exceptions. For Free, Team, Pro, and Plus workspaces, providers may retain Input and Output for up to 30 days for safety and compliance moderation.

Grammarly

Free: $0/month and 100 generative-AI prompts per month. Pro: displayed at $12 USD and includes 2,000 AI prompts per member per month, unlimited personalized suggestions, full-sentence rewrites, tone controls, plagiarism checking, and AI-generated-text detection. Enterprise: contact sales and includes unlimited prompts per member per month plus enterprise controls. The extracted pricing page did not unambiguously expose the billing cadence attached to the displayed $12 Pro amount.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Open verification questions
  • The billing cadence represented by the $12 Pro price was not rendered unambiguously in the retrieved official pricing text.
  • Grammarly's exact treatment may differ for negotiated Enterprise agreements, which are governed by a separate Customer Business Agreement rather than the public self-service terms.

Official sources confirm AI drafting, rewriting, tone adjustment, fluency suggestions, grammar assistance, plagiarism checking, and AI-text detection. For Free, Premium, and single-user Pro accounts, Product Improvement and Training is enabled by default, but users can opt out; when disabled, user content is not used for model training, validation, or product improvement. Multi-user accounts bought directly online are also enabled by default, with an administrator opt-out. Enterprise-tier, sales-purchased Pro/Business, and Education accounts have training disabled by default. Grammarly says its external LLM service providers may receive prompts and context to provide generative features but may not train their models on user content. Under the October 29, 2025 Superhuman terms, inputs and outputs are User Content, and users own all right, title, and interest in User Content; the terms also grant Superhuman a broad license for operating, improving, customizing, and developing its services.
