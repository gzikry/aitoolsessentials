The Privacy Policy permits the use of "non-identifiable information, including anonymized, aggregated, or de-identified data, for lawful purposes" but does not state whether submitted prompts or support conversations are used to train models. Robylon's robots.txt notes that blocking "training" crawlers "would also block Googlebot, which is multi-purpose" and allows named AI crawlers - a crawler policy, not a statement about customer data.
    • International transfers rest on consent ("By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of such information") rather than a named mechanism. No Standard Contractual Clauses or Data Privacy Framework basis is referenced.
    • Retention is two-tier and partly open-ended: customer and user information is deleted 30-60 days after termination or on request, but usage data is kept only "as long as needed" with no period. Custom retention policies are an Enterprise-only feature.
    • The Privacy Policy's last-updated date (7 April 2024) predates the current product surface, including voice, WhatsApp and the browser-automation section of the Terms, so parts of it may describe an earlier service.
    • Enterprise is quote-only ("Let's talk") and per-conversation cost depends on which model answers, so no single headline price exists for the top tier.
