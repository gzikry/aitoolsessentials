    Accuratescribe vs Meloletter

    Solo transcribers, podcasters, journalists, students and small teams who want one flat unlimited plan instead of a minutes meter - $9.99 a month billed yearly or $19.99 monthly, 134+ languages, speaker identification, subtitles and DOCX/PDF/SRT export, free to try on partial transcripts. A good fit if you transcribe a few hours a week and would rather not track a credit balance. Not a fit if you need a named counterparty or governing law before you pay, if you expect any refund after purchase (the policy is non-refundable even if the service is unused), or if you need a per-minute rate for very high volume - the same operator's WhisperTranscribe.ai and WhisperTranscript.ai brands sell meters, both of which are held pending verification rather than listed. · People buying a one-off keepsake gift - a birthday, an anniversary, a proposal, a thank-you to a parent or friend - who want to hear and read the thing before they pay for it, and who would rather spend $19.90 once than hold a music-generation subscription they will use twice a year
