    Design comparison

    Auteo (auteo.ai) vs Image Text Editor

    Solo creators and small teams who want one subscription that turns a prompt into video, images, voice and music instead of buying each medium from a different site, and who want the credit cost of every render shown before they commit. It suits a buyer who values a named operating entity (Sigmify LLC, Wyoming) with a real governing-law and venue clause, processors named as companies, an explicit no-training statement, and a storefront that advertises no false urgency. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs an operating (not registered-agent) address, retention committed to fixed periods, a free or one-off route that avoids a subscription entirely, or a track record - the domain is about two months old and the legal set was rewritten the same week. · Ecommerce sellers, marketers and small teams who need one word, price, date or label corrected on a finished poster, screenshot, banner or product photo when you no longer have the design file - priced per saved result (1 credit at 1K, 2 at 2K) with one-time packs that never expire or a monthly plan for longer result retention. Also a fit if you want a no-sign-in local demo and a policy set that states its retention windows in days. Not a fit if the edit has to be re-editable afterwards (the download is flattened, with no text layer or removal mask), if you need a named company and jurisdiction before paying, if you need to be sure the AI provider does not train on your uploads, or if the image is a document - IDs, passports, certificates, receipts and bank statements are named as prohibited in its own policies.
