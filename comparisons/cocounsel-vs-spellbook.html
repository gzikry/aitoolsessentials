CoCounsel Legal

Thomson Reuters’ official Westlaw plans-and-pricing page lists Westlaw Advantage with CoCounsel Essentials starting at $415.35/month. Broader CoCounsel Legal packages are sales-quoted and vary by jurisdiction coverage, seats, and term. No unofficial third-party seat figures are treated as official list prices.

  • Standalone CoCounsel Legal seat pricing remains sales-configured.
  • Starting-at Westlaw bundle prices can be personalized by jurisdiction, attorney count, and term.

Official CoCounsel Legal product page describes Westlaw- and Practical Law-grounded research, analysis, and drafting, plus Word/Outlook/Teams integrations and a statement that customer data is not used to train underlying models. The $415.35/month figure is the official starting-at price for the named Westlaw Advantage with CoCounsel Essentials bundle observed on 2026-08-26, not a complete firmwide quote.

Spellbook

Spellbook’s official homepage does not publish a self-serve consumer price list. Official comparison materials describe custom per-seat annual subscriptions. No unverified dollar figure is reported as a list price.

Official sources checked 2026-08-26

Open verification questions
  • Exact per-seat USD pricing is quote-specific and was not listed on the official homepage.
  • Zero-retention and training statements should be confirmed in the current DPA/contract, not only marketing copy.
  • HIPAA/SOC/GDPR resources on the trust center are vendor-published, not independently certified here.

Official homepage describes Word-native contract review and drafting, intake-to-signed-contract search, and a zero-retention statement that LLM providers do not train on or store customer data. A public trust center lists SOC 2 Type II and other vendor compliance resources. Treat those as vendor claims to verify in contract review.
