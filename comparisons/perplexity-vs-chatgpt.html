Perplexity

Official support lists Standard/Free, individual Pro, individual Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max. Max includes everything in Pro plus the highest access to advanced models, Comet Max Assistant, Create files and apps, and Brain. The reviewed public consumer-plan pages did not expose current Pro or Max dollar prices. An official organization-onboarding article lists Enterprise Pro at $40/seat monthly or $400/seat annually.

ChatGPT

OpenAI officially lists ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Free is $0; paid self-service plans are billed per user per month, while Enterprise pricing is sales-assisted. When Perplexity is the better fit

Perplexity is stronger when source discovery and citation visibility are the core job.

When ChatGPT is the better fit

ChatGPT is stronger when you need to transform research into briefs, drafts, plans, or analysis after collecting sources.

Quick recommendation

Choose Perplexity when its strengths match your repeated workflow. Choose ChatGPT when its ecosystem, interface, or specialist capability reduces review time. Test both with a real task before clicking into a paid plan.
