    Khanmigo vs Random Topic Generator

    Free teacher-prep tools from Khan Academy, plus low-cost learner and family tutoring · People practising impromptu speaking on their own terms - language learners, teachers running a class warm-up, Toastmasters-style clubs, and anyone who wants to talk for one minute without freezing. The free path needs no account: draw 1, 3 or 5 topics from a 250-topic library in eight languages, pick a 1/2/3/5-minute timer, and record locally. The paid path is only for the AI speech report, which costs 100 credits a run (about $1 on every pack and plan), so it fits someone who wants grammar, filler-word and pacing feedback and is willing to pay per analysis. It is not a fit if you need a named company, a governing-law clause or a published refund term before you subscribe, if you object to ad-supported pages on a paid product, or if you want a human coach - the report is model output scored on six fixed dimensions.

    Khanmigo

    Khanmigo's official teacher page states the product is completely free for teachers. The official pricing page lists $4/month or $44/year for learners and for families, with sales tax not included, and District Tools as request pricing. Official help documentation describes a Free Teacher Tools subscription for teachers in the United States and other listed locales.

    Random Topic Generator

    Free $0: unlimited topic draws (1, 3 or 5 from a 250-topic library in 8 languages), the 1/2/3/5-minute timer and local recording, no account, no credits, no AI report. Standard $19.9/month for 2,000 credits/month; Pro $59.9/month for 6,000 credits/month; both 'valid for the current month; cancel anytime'. One-time packs, 'one-time payment, no auto-renewal': $5.00/500 credits, $10.00/1,000 credits, $20.00/2,000 credits. AI analysis costs 100 credits a run, so $5 is about 5 analyses and the $10 card itself says 'Up to 10 AI analyses (100 credits each)'; Standard is about 20 a month and Pro about 60, i.e. roughly $1 an analysis on every path. Stripe for card payments, USD. Verified 2026-11-14 on https://randomtopicgenerator.net/pricing, restated identically on https://randomtopicgenerator.net/zh/pricing (the only two price figures on the Chinese mirror are the same 19.9 and 59.9), and confirmed in the shipped app bundle, where PLANS carries standard {creditsPerMonth: 2000, priceMonthly: 19.9, stripePriceIdEnvKey: 'STRIPE_PRICE_STANDARD'} and pro {creditsPerMonth: 6000, priceMonthly: 59.9, stripePriceIdEnvKey: 'STRIPE_PRICE_PRO'}.
