Exact localized prices were not consistently exposed by the official pricing page during verification, so no unverified amounts are reported.

Claude

Claude Free is $0. Pro is $17 per month with a $200 annual prepayment or $20 month-to-month. Max starts at $100 per month. Team supports 2–150 users and lists Standard seats at $20 per seat per month and Premium seats at $100 per seat per month. Enterprise is billed annually at a seat price plus usage at API rates.

When ChatGPT is the better fit

Consider ChatGPT when you need a broad everyday assistant, multimodal help, spreadsheet support, and a large ecosystem of product integrations.

When Claude is the better fit

Consider Claude for long-form writing, editing, document analysis, careful tone, and turning messy notes into structured deliverables.

Quick recommendation

Choose ChatGPT when its strengths match your repeated workflow. Choose Claude when its ecosystem, interface, or specialist capability reduces review time. Test both with a real task before clicking into a paid plan.
