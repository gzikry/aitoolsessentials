    Image Text Editor vs Tiny Deskai

    Ecommerce sellers, marketers and small teams who need one word, price, date or label corrected on a finished poster, screenshot, banner or product photo when you no longer have the design file - priced per saved result (1 credit at 1K, 2 at 2K) with one-time packs that never expire or a monthly plan for longer result retention. Also a fit if you want a no-sign-in local demo and a policy set that states its retention windows in days. Not a fit if the edit has to be re-editable afterwards (the download is flattened, with no text layer or removal mask), if you need a named company and jurisdiction before paying, if you need to be sure the AI provider does not train on your uploads, or if the image is a document - IDs, passports, certificates, receipts and bank statements are named as prohibited in its own policies. · People who want to put themselves and a couple of friends into the 'Outstanding' Tiny Desk meme - a 15-second desk-concert clip for a TikTok, Reel or Short - from photos they already have, and would rather buy a small one-time video pack than start another monthly AI video subscription. Not for anyone needing high resolution, batch output, or a clear answer on what music the finished video carries.
