    Adobe Firefly

    Official US monthly pricing (re-confirmed 2026-10-01) lists Firefly Standard at US$9.99 with 2,000 generative credits/month; Firefly Pro at US$19.99 with 4,000 credits/month; Firefly Pro Plus at US$49.99 with 10,000 credits/month; and Firefly Premium at US$199.99 with 50,000 credits/month and unlimited Firefly Video Model access. All paid tiers include unlimited standard image and vector generations; credits cover premium features such as video, audio, and partner-model generations. The first-year promotional rates recorded on 2026-08-25 (US$34.97 Pro Plus and US$139.91 Premium) were no longer displayed on the official pricing page on 2026-10-01.

    TheFaceRating

    Free on-device scan, then one-time reports: Starter USD 7.99, Pro USD 16.99, VIP USD 24.99, each marked 'One-time - no subscription'. Verified on three independent surfaces that agree - the homepage plan cards, the results-page paywall reached by running a real scan, and Terms section 6 - and confirmed purchasable by opening the checkout, which returned a live Stripe session (cs_live) titled 'TheFaceRating Starter Report' at USD 7.99 with merchant Vban LLC and Apple Pay / Google Pay offered. Studio credit packs are priced only in the Terms: 10 credits USD 9.99, 30 credits USD 19.99, one credit per rating plus render, credits do not expire. Free colour analyses are limited to two per day per hashed IP. No subscription price exists anywhere on the site.
