    Makifyai vs Tiny Deskai

    E-commerce sellers, small marketing teams and creators who turn existing product shots into listing images, ads, mockups and social graphics without a designer - one browser workspace over GPT Image 2.5/2.6, the Nano Banana 2.x family, Seedream 5.0 and FLUX 3, with 30 free credits on sign-up and one-time packs from $9.90. A sensible fit if you want per-image credit control instead of a fixed subscription and will read the refund rule before paying: 3 calendar days, 50 paid credits or fewer, minus a 2-10% processing fee. Not a fit if you need to know who the data controller is, how long uploaded product photos are kept, or that your outputs are licensed for commercial use before you upload client material - no privacy notice on the site names a controller, address or retention period, training use is never mentioned, and the Terms decline to guarantee commercial usability even though the marketing copy says a commercial licence is included. · People who want to put themselves and a couple of friends into the 'Outstanding' Tiny Desk meme - a 15-second desk-concert clip for a TikTok, Reel or Short - from photos they already have, and would rather buy a small one-time video pack than start another monthly AI video subscription. Not for anyone needing high resolution, batch output, or a clear answer on what music the finished video carries.
