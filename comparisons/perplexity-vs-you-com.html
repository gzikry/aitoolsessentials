Perplexity

Official support lists Standard/Free, individual Pro, individual Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max. Max includes everything in Pro plus the highest access to advanced models, Comet Max Assistant, Create files and apps, and Brain. The reviewed public consumer-plan pages did not expose current Pro or Max dollar prices. An official organization-onboarding article lists Enterprise Pro at $40/seat monthly or $400/seat annually.

You.com

Official support says the chat product has a free plan with unlimited Smart Agent access, limited premium-agent/model queries, and paid plans with greater access, but it does not state current chat-plan prices. The current public pricing page primarily lists APIs: Web Search $5/1,000 calls, Contents $1/1,000 pages, Answer $5/1,000 calls, Research from $12/1,000 calls, and Finance Research from $110/1,000 calls, with a $100 introductory credit.

How to decide

Match each platform to your actual workflow constraints: editor preference and model access for coding tools; task-based versus credit-based versus execution-based pricing, self-hosting needs, and technical depth for automation. Run one real workflow in each finalist before paying.
