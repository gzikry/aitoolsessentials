    Evidence status

    Replit AI

    Starter is free with daily Agent credits, free daily usage, one published live project, and a built-in database. Core is $25 monthly or $20/month billed annually, includes two parallel agents and $25 toward the most powerful models. Pro is $100 monthly or $95/month billed annually, includes ten parallel agents, $100 toward the most powerful models, up to 15 collaborators, and premium support. Enterprise is custom-priced. Replit also uses effort-based/pay-as-you-go and usage-based billing for applicable AI, deployment, and database resources.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25
    Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
    Open verification questions
    • The explicit Output Content ownership and no-training language is in the Commercial Agreement. The general consumer Terms do not publish an equally explicit assignment of rights in AI-generated Output.
    • The pricing page labels free and included Agent allowances qualitatively rather than publishing exact numeric Agent-credit limits.
    • Third-party AI services are excluded from the Commercial Agreement's definition of Replit AI Features and can be governed by separate provider terms.

    Official documentation describes Replit Agent as turning plain-language requests into apps, designs, slides, and other artifacts. The Commercial Agreement states that commercial customers retain all rights in Input Content and own Output Content. Under the Commercial Agreement, Replit will not use Customer Content to develop or improve products, train machine-learning models, or create derivative works except as expressly permitted; customer-requested tuning may be performed solely for that customer's use during the agreement term. The consumer Terms state that users retain their rights in content they submit and grant Replit the operational rights needed to provide the service. Replit's Privacy Policy was last updated August 3, 2026 and covers collection of usage, interaction, project, command, payment, and communication data, along with applicable access, deletion, correction, appeal, and sale/sharing opt-out rights.

    v0

    Free: $0/month, $5 monthly credits, 7-message daily limit. Premium: $20/month with $20 monthly credits, but is being sunset and is unavailable to new users. Plus: $30/user/month with $30 monthly credits per user. Business: $100/user/month with $30 monthly credits per user and model-training data opt-out. Enterprise: custom pricing and credits. Unused monthly credits roll over but expire after 65 days; purchased credits expire after one year and require an active paid plan.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25
    Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
    Open verification questions
    • The official page says model token rates can be viewed in the model selector or pricing page, but the extracted live documentation did not consistently expose a complete current rate table.
    • The FAQ does not promise copyrightability, uniqueness, non-infringement, or an IP indemnity for generated code.
    • The effect of deleting content already incorporated into a trained model is limited: the FAQ says existing models will not be updated after such a deletion request.

    Official documentation describes v0 as an AI agent for producing full-stack apps and agents, with Git integration, a full editor, previews, backend connections, and Vercel deployment. v0 may use user prompts or generated content with third-party models and learning systems to improve products. Enterprise content and content from customers who opt out in Vercel settings are not used for training. Vercel platform-service customer data or code is not used to train, improve, or fine-tune v0 models. The FAQ says Vercel does not own code generated from user prompts, while warning that output may resemble other output or third-party IP and must be evaluated by the user. Pricing terminology on the retrieved current documentation uses 'Plus'; some indexed official snippets still use the former name 'Team'.
