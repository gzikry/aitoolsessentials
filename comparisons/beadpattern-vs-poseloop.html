    BeadPattern vs PoseLoop (poseloop.app)

    Turning a photo or a prompt into a fuse-bead grid with per-colour bead counts you can actually shop from · Creators and small social teams who want to post a trend clip while the format is still climbing, starting from one or two photos rather than a prompt or a shoot - one photo for most trends, two for a duet, up to five friends, or one shot of a car. It suits people who will read the per-render credit price on the Generate button before committing and who are happy to buy a one-time credit pack (credits never expire) rather than subscribe. It is a weaker fit for a buyer who needs a registered street address or a named human counterparty to contract with, who wants every data processor named as a company before uploading faces, who wants a flat per-clip price list on the pricing page, or who wants a tool with a third-party track record - the domain is about two months old and no independent review of it exists.
