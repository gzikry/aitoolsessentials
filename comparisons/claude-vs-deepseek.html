    Claude

    Claude Free is $0. Pro is $17 per month with a $200 annual prepayment or $20 month-to-month. Max is $100 per month with 5x Pro usage every 5 hours and every week, and now includes $100/month in Claude Platform API credits. Ultra is $200 per month with 10x Pro weekly usage, no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, and $200/month in API credits. Team supports 2-150 users: Standard seat $20/seat/month billed annually ($25 monthly), Premium seat $100/seat/month billed annually ($125 monthly). Enterprise is US$20/seat/month billed annually with a 20-seat minimum plus usage at API rates. Pro and above also list a usage-credit-billed product called Fable, included up to half the weekly limit on Max and above.
