    Auteo (auteo.ai) vs Unblurfy

    Solo creators and small teams who want one subscription that turns a prompt into video, images, voice and music instead of buying each medium from a different site, and who want the credit cost of every render shown before they commit. It suits a buyer who values a named operating entity (Sigmify LLC, Wyoming) with a real governing-law and venue clause, processors named as companies, an explicit no-training statement, and a storefront that advertises no false urgency. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs an operating (not registered-agent) address, retention committed to fixed periods, a free or one-off route that avoids a subscription entirely, or a track record - the domain is about two months old and the legal set was rewritten the same week. · Anyone rescuing an ordinary photo - a shaky family snapshot, a soft product shot, a blurred screenshot of text, a scan from a shoebox - who wants a fix without signing up or installing anything: one free 1K enhance a day with no watermark, then either one-time credits that never expire or a small monthly plan for 2K output and 20-image batches. Also covers old-photo restoration, colourising and video-frame extraction from the same credit pool. Not a fit if you need 4K output or an API, if the company you are paying has to be named before you buy, or if the job is revealing censored, mosaicked or paywalled content - refused in the site's own terms.
