    Evidence status

    Perplexity

    Official support lists Standard/Free, individual Pro, individual Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max. Max includes everything in Pro plus the highest access to advanced models, Comet Max Assistant, Create files and apps, and Brain. The reviewed public consumer-plan pages did not expose current Pro or Max dollar prices. An official organization-onboarding article lists Enterprise Pro at $40/seat monthly or $400/seat annually.

    Perplexity is documented as an AI-powered answer/search product with citations, premium models, file uploads, Research, Create files and apps, Comet, and Computer features depending on plan. AI Data Retention is enabled by default for Free, Pro, and Max users and can be turned off for future collection; previously collected training data is not removed by a later opt-out. Enterprise data is not used for AI training, and Perplexity says its third-party model-provider agreements prohibit provider training on Perplexity data. Consumer terms warn that output may be subject to third-party model licenses and use restrictions. 2026-09-02 official blog “Optimizing On-Device Inference for Apple Silicon” (https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/optimizing-on-device-inference-for-apple-silicon): Lily is a lightweight local inference engine built specifically for Apple silicon and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B so on-device compute does not bottleneck Hybrid Compute / Perplexity Computer tasks. Official facts from that post: Rust runtime; OpenAI-compatible chat-completions API; custom Metal kernels; neither PyTorch nor MLX in the execution path; standalone demo on GitHub. The official pplx-garden README names the demo at https://github.com/perplexityai/pplx-garden (lily/), Apache-2.0, greedy generation for the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MLX affine 4-bit checkpoint format, Apple GPU family 10 or later (M5 and newer). Lily is not a new paid Perplexity plan SKU. Consumer Pro/Max dollar prices were not re-verified on 2026-09-02. Re-verified 2026-10-01: the official plan-comparison article still routes consumer Pro and Max to the app rather than publishing dollar amounts, and lists Education Pro at $10/mo with SheerID verification. Enterprise Pro is stated as starting at $40/month or $400/year per seat, matching the 2026-08-25 record.

    Gemini

    For US personal accounts, Gemini Free is $0, Google AI Plus is $4.99 per month with double the Free usage limits, Google AI Pro is $19.99 per month with four times the Free usage limits, and Google AI Ultra is $99.99 per month (5x AI Pro limits) with a $199.99 option (20x AI Pro limits). Gemini Enterprise Business edition starts at $21 per seat per month (up to 300 seats) and the Standard/Plus editions start at $30 per seat per month. For organizations, Gemini is also included as a core service in eligible Google Workspace editions, which previously checked at $8.40/$7 per user per month for Business Starter, $16.80/$14 for Business Standard, and $26.40/$22 for Business Plus on flexible/annual plans (2026-09-18 check, not re-exhibited on the 2026-11-14 page).
