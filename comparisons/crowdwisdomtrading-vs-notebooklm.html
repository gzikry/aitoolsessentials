    NotebookLM

    NotebookLM is now Gemini Notebook (the official FAQ states the rename took effect in July 2026). The product remains free for individuals with standard usage limits and up to 50 sources per notebook. Gemini Notebook is also included in Google AI Plus (double the standard usage limits, up to 100 sources), Google AI Pro (four times the standard limits, up to 300 sources), and Google AI Ultra (5-20x the Pro limits, up to 600 sources). The plans page does not publish US-dollar prices; the paid tiers are billed through Google One and are available only in listed regions. Organizations, schools, and enterprise access route through Google Workspace or Google Cloud.
