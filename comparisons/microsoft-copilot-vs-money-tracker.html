    Microsoft Copilot

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is available for work or education without an additional Copilot license and supports metered pay-as-you-go agents. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business is displayed at a promotional $18/user/month, paid yearly, versus a $21 list price. Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot is $23.50/user/month paid yearly, and Business Premium with Copilot is $32/user/month paid yearly. Prices are USD, annual subscriptions that auto-renew, and may vary by market.

Microsoft 365 Copilot works in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, Loop, and other Microsoft 365 surfaces; it uses Microsoft Graph data the user is permitted to access and includes Copilot Chat, Copilot Search, agents, and app-specific drafting, summarization, analysis, and meeting capabilities. Prompts, responses, and Microsoft Graph data are not used to train foundation LLMs. Interaction content is stored under the customer's Microsoft 365 contractual commitments, encrypted at rest, manageable through Microsoft Purview, and deletable through Copilot activity history controls.
