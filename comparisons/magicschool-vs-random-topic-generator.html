    Magicschool vs Random Topic Generator

    K-12 lesson planning, classroom communications, and teacher-facing AI tools with a published free tier · People practising impromptu speaking on their own terms - language learners, teachers running a class warm-up, Toastmasters-style clubs, and anyone who wants to talk for one minute without freezing. The free path needs no account: draw 1, 3 or 5 topics from a 250-topic library in eight languages, pick a 1/2/3/5-minute timer, and record locally. The paid path is only for the AI speech report, which costs 100 credits a run (about $1 on every pack and plan), so it fits someone who wants grammar, filler-word and pacing feedback and is willing to pay per analysis. It is not a fit if you need a named company, a governing-law clause or a published refund term before you subscribe, if you object to ad-supported pages on a paid product, or if you want a human coach - the report is model output scored on six fixed dimensions.
