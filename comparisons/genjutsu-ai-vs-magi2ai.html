    Creators making trend-shaped short video who already have a performance worth copying and photos of the people, pets or products to put in it - the preset library does the prompt work and the workspace shows a credit estimate before you generate. Not a fit for anyone who needs a named company, a stated jurisdiction and a retention period before paying, for work where an exact likeness or an unchanged background is contractual, for text-to-video alone (the AI Video Maker page covers that at a much higher credit cost), or for anyone unwilling to upload photos of real people to an unidentified operator. · Creators making short MAGI-2 video clips with dialogue and native sound from a prompt, first/last frame, or reference media, who prefer a single-model studio to Auteo’s broader multi-model suite.
