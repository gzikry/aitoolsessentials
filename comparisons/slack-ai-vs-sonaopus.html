Evidence status

Slack AI

Free: $0. Pro: $7.25/user/month when paid annually or $8.75 when paid monthly. Business+: $15/user/month annually or $18 monthly. Enterprise+ uses sales-based pricing. The pricing page labels Free and Pro as including Basic AI and Business+ as including Advanced AI; the exact feature set depends on plan and admin settings. The page was also displaying a 50%-off-for-three-months promotion for eligible Pro and Business+ purchases.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25
Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗
Open verification questions
  • Slack's AI Principles page says AI features are included in paid plans, while the current pricing page labels the Free plan as having Basic AI. The exact production availability and limits of Free-plan AI could not be reconciled from the official pages.
  • Slack does not publish a single numeric usage allowance for all AI features; some capabilities state that plan limits apply.
  • No explicit, AI-output-specific ownership statement was located in the checked official Slack sources.

Official pages list capabilities including Slackbot, conversation and thread summaries, daily recaps, AI search, file summaries, workflow generation, huddle notes, translation, and enterprise search, with availability varying by plan. Slack states that Customer Data never leaves Slack-controlled infrastructure and is never used to train LLMs. Third-party LLMs are hosted within Slack's cloud environment; providers do not have access to Customer Data. AI only uses content the requesting member is authorized to access. Conversation summaries and search answers are ephemeral; recaps are stored for 90 days. Workflow-generated summaries stored in messages or canvases follow the organization's retention settings. Admins can disable or restrict native AI features.

Sonaopus

$99 USD one-time per profession-specific product, no subscription and no recurring charge. Five products: HR Management, Executive Assistant, Project Management, Real Estate, Recruiting. Free tier: up to eight generations per profession before purchase, with no signup or card. Lifetime access covers future updates to that profession's tool. Terms state that except where required by law all purchases are final and no refunds are offered, with the free trial given as the reason. A stale third-party mirror (devadex.com) still lists a retired pre-launch $59 price; the mirrored store account sonaopusaiworkflow.gumroad.com now returns 404 and the price on every live vendor page is $99.

Official sources checked 2026-09-12

Open verification questions
  • The homepage FAQ answer “Nothing stored” contradicts the Privacy Policy, which correctly says workflow inputs are kept in your browser's local storage. The vendor agreed in writing on 2026-09-12 to correct the more absolute wording but the homepage copy still carries it as of 2026-09-13.
  • No service address or city/country is published for the seller or data controller; the vendor declined to publish one beyond the Nigeria governing-law clause. The operator name is disclosed.
  • The legal-review disclosure for legally sensitive outputs (ER case documents, offer letters, contracts, HR policies) appears inside workflow prompts rather than as a standalone, findable FAQ entry.
  • No data-export feature exists for locally stored workflow data; the Privacy Policy states this and directs users to contact support.
  • A stale third-party mirror (devadex.com) continues to advertise a retired $59 pre-launch price for the HR product. The mirrored Gumroad account now 404s; the mirror is outside the vendor's direct control.
  • Affiliate program terms are advertised at 40% on the vendor site and via Gumroad, but no written program terms (cookie window, attribution, payment terms) were published for review.
