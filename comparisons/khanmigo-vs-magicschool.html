    Evidence status

    Khanmigo

    Khanmigo's official teacher page states the product is completely free for teachers. The official pricing page lists $4/month or $44/year for learners and for families, with sales tax not included, and District Tools as request pricing. Official help documentation describes a Free Teacher Tools subscription for teachers in the United States and other listed locales.

    Official sources checked 2026-08-26

    Open verification questions
    • Current teacher-eligibility countries and verification rules are completed in the official signup flow and may change.
    • District Tools pricing is quote-specific.
    • The number of children covered by a family subscription should be confirmed on the current official parent/pricing pages at purchase time.

    Official teacher, pricing, and help pages checked 2026-08-26. Teacher-free access is a separate product path from learner/family subscriptions and from district classroom access. Official teacher-page language says teachers cannot grant students Khanmigo access; classroom access is through school or district implementations.

    MagicSchool

    MagicSchool's official pricing page lists Free at $0 Forever Free for individual teachers exploring AI; Plus at $8.33 USD/user/month billed annually or $12.99 USD/month billed monthly; and Enterprise as custom pricing for districts. Official FAQ copy restates Plus as $12.99/month or $8.33/month billed yearly ($99.96 yearly).

    Official sources checked 2026-08-26
