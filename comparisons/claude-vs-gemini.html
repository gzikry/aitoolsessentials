    Evidence status

    Claude

    Claude Free is $0. Pro is $17 per month with a $200 annual prepayment or $20 month-to-month. Max is $100 per month with 5x Pro usage every 5 hours and every week, and now includes $100/month in Claude Platform API credits. Ultra is $200 per month with 10x Pro weekly usage, no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, and $200/month in API credits. Team supports 2-150 users: Standard seat $20/seat/month billed annually ($25 monthly), Premium seat $100/seat/month billed annually ($125 monthly). Enterprise is US$20/seat/month billed annually with a 20-seat minimum plus usage at API rates. Pro and above also list a usage-credit-billed product called Fable, included up to half the weekly limit on Max and above.

    Official sources checked 2026-11-14

    Gemini

    For US personal accounts, Gemini Free is $0, Google AI Plus is $4.99 per month with double the Free usage limits, Google AI Pro is $19.99 per month with four times the Free usage limits, and Google AI Ultra is $99.99 per month (5x AI Pro limits) with a $199.99 option (20x AI Pro limits). Gemini Enterprise Business edition starts at $21 per seat per month (up to 300 seats) and the Standard/Plus editions start at $30 per seat per month. For organizations, Gemini is also included as a core service in eligible Google Workspace editions, which previously checked at $8.40/$7 per user per month for Business Starter, $16.80/$14 for Business Standard, and $26.40/$22 for Business Plus on flexible/annual plans (2026-09-18 check, not re-exhibited on the 2026-11-14 page).

    Frequently asked questions

    Claude vs Gemini: which is better for long documents?

    Claude handles very long single documents with strong instruction adherence, making it a common choice for contract or report analysis. Gemini integrates with Google Drive and handles multimodal inputs. The right pick depends on where your material lives.

    Do Claude and Gemini cost the same?

    Both offer free tiers and paid plans around $20/month, but plan features and limits change often. Check the official pricing pages before deciding — our comparison page links both directly.
