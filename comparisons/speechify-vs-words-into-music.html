    Speechify

    Free: $0, listen at up to 1.5x speed, 10 robotic-sounding voices, text-to-speech features only. Premium: $29/month, includes 1000+ natural voices, 60+ languages, 5x speed, scan and listen, AI summaries and chats, Google Drive/Dropbox/OneDrive integrations, voice typing, AI podcasts, and a voice AI assistant. The official pricing page also offers a yearly billing toggle advertised as 'SAVE 60%'; the captured view showed monthly prices with the note 'Prices shown per month, billed monthly'. No team or enterprise price was exposed on that page.

    Words into Music

    Starter $9.99/month or $59.99 billed annually (30 songs each month); Creator $19.99/month or $119.99 annually (65 songs); Studio $29.99/month or $179.99 annually (100 songs). One-time packs: 20 songs $9.99, 40 songs $19.99, 90 songs $39.99, no subscription required. Annual plans are paid upfront and release the monthly allowance; unused subscription credits expire at the next allowance boundary and do not roll over, while purchased pack credits do not expire. Payments via Stripe; one introductory song for an eligible verified account. The same six figures appear on /pricing/ and in the site's llms.txt.
