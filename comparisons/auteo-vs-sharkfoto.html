    Design comparison

    Auteo (auteo.ai) vs SharkFoto

    Solo creators and small teams who want one subscription that turns a prompt into video, images, voice and music instead of buying each medium from a different site, and who want the credit cost of every render shown before they commit. It suits a buyer who values a named operating entity (Sigmify LLC, Wyoming) with a real governing-law and venue clause, processors named as companies, an explicit no-training statement, and a storefront that advertises no false urgency. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs an operating (not registered-agent) address, retention committed to fixed periods, a free or one-off route that avoids a subscription entirely, or a track record - the domain is about two months old and the legal set was rewritten the same week. · Small teams and solo creators who want one browser workspace that generates and edits images, video, audio and 3D from text or an uploaded photo, plus a genuinely free file-tool layer (conversion, compression, resize, embroidery) that needs no account. It suits a buyer who values a named operator and a real governing-law clause, disclosed Google Analytics and AdSense IDs, and an explicit no-training statement on uploads. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs every model tier-guaranteed, who objects to an ad-supported free tier that sets analytics and advertising cookies on first visit without a shown banner (from the US), or who wants a published registered street address rather than the entity and the city alone - and a weaker fit for anyone who would rely on the '4.7/5 based on 4,644+ ratings' badge, which the site does not source.
