    Image Text Editor vs PoseLoop (poseloop.app)

    Ecommerce sellers, marketers and small teams who need one word, price, date or label corrected on a finished poster, screenshot, banner or product photo when you no longer have the design file - priced per saved result (1 credit at 1K, 2 at 2K) with one-time packs that never expire or a monthly plan for longer result retention. Also a fit if you want a no-sign-in local demo and a policy set that states its retention windows in days. Not a fit if the edit has to be re-editable afterwards (the download is flattened, with no text layer or removal mask), if you need a named company and jurisdiction before paying, if you need to be sure the AI provider does not train on your uploads, or if the image is a document - IDs, passports, certificates, receipts and bank statements are named as prohibited in its own policies. · Creators and small social teams who want to post a trend clip while the format is still climbing, starting from one or two photos rather than a prompt or a shoot - one photo for most trends, two for a duet, up to five friends, or one shot of a car. It suits people who will read the per-render credit price on the Generate button before committing and who are happy to buy a one-time credit pack (credits never expire) rather than subscribe. It is a weaker fit for a buyer who needs a registered street address or a named human counterparty to contract with, who wants every data processor named as a company before uploading faces, who wants a flat per-clip price list on the pricing page, or who wants a tool with a third-party track record - the domain is about two months old and no independent review of it exists.
