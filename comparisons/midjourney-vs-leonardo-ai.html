Leonardo AI

Official individual pricing lists Free with 150 Fast Tokens daily; Essential at US$12/month with 8,500 Fast Tokens/month and a 25,500-token rollover-bank cap; Premium at US$30/month with 25,000 Fast Tokens/month and a 75,000-token rollover-bank cap; and Ultimate at US$60/month with 60,000 Fast Tokens/month and a 180,000-token rollover-bank cap. Premium and Ultimate include unlimited relaxed generation on specified image and video models, subject to fair-use queues and supported-model restrictions.
