    AI Image to Prompt vs SharkFoto

    Creators and small teams who already have a reference image and need a written brief for it: illustrators testing an art direction, designers producing concept variants, marketers fixing a campaign's visual language from a moodboard, and hobbyists learning how image prompts are structured. It fits the free-and-anonymous case well - no account, eight output languages, model-specific formatting for Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux and Nano Banana - and it fits someone who wants one editable paragraph rather than four variants at once. It is a poor fit if you need to know who operates the site, what law governs it, or what anything costs: there is no company, controller, postal address or named jurisdiction anywhere, the only address published is a personal Gmail, the pricing page belongs to a different product, and six five-star testimonials from named people carry no evidence they are real. · Small teams and solo creators who want one browser workspace that generates and edits images, video, audio and 3D from text or an uploaded photo, plus a genuinely free file-tool layer (conversion, compression, resize, embroidery) that needs no account. It suits a buyer who values a named operator and a real governing-law clause, disclosed Google Analytics and AdSense IDs, and an explicit no-training statement on uploads. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs every model tier-guaranteed, who objects to an ad-supported free tier that sets analytics and advertising cookies on first visit without a shown banner (from the US), or who wants a published registered street address rather than the entity and the city alone - and a weaker fit for anyone who would rely on the '4.7/5 based on 4,644+ ratings' badge, which the site does not source.
