Gamma

free: $0; 400 one-time signup credits that do not refill; up to 10 slides per prompt; PDF, PPTX, PNG, and Google Slides export. plus: $9/seat/month equivalent, $108/seat billed annually; 1,000 monthly credits, up to 100 slides per prompt, branding removal, and advanced image models. pro: $18/seat/month equivalent, $216/seat billed annually; 4,000 monthly credits, premium image models, custom branding/fonts, analytics, 10 custom domains, API access, and workspace templates. ultra: $90/seat/month equivalent, $1,080/seat billed annually; 20,000 monthly credits, advanced text/image/video models, 100 custom domains, and early feature access. team: $20/seat/month, $240/seat billed annually; 6,000 monthly credits per seat, centralized billing, company theme, shared folders, admin controls, and advanced data controls. business: $40/seat/month, $480/seat billed annually; 10,000 monthly credits per seat, SSO, advanced models, and advanced data controls. enterprise: Custom annual agreement.

Open verification questions
  • The official page was extracted in its annual-billing state. Monthly individual-plan checkout prices were not independently captured and should not be inferred from the annual discount.
  • Gamma's terms grant a broad training license where training controls are unavailable or left enabled; publications should distinguish individual plans from Team/Business plans.

The repository's 400-credit Free allowance remains supported, but those are one-time starter credits rather than a monthly refill. The repository's Plus $10 and Pro $20 figures do not match the current official annual-pricing view, which shows $9 and $18 monthly equivalents. Paid credits roll over only up to twice the monthly allocation; additional credits can be purchased. Individual Free, Plus, Pro, and Ultra workspaces allow Gamma to use content for AI-feature improvement by default, but users can opt out in account settings. Team and Business workspace content is automatically excluded from training, with the control locked. Gamma does not claim ownership of user content. Users grant Gamma a license needed to operate and provide the service. The pricing page also states that content remains the user's after cancellation or downgrade. Public API documentation is available at https://developers.gamma.app/; the pricing page says API access is available on paid plans and consumes AI credits.

Sonaopus

$99 USD one-time per profession-specific product, no subscription and no recurring charge. Five products: HR Management, Executive Assistant, Project Management, Real Estate, Recruiting. Free tier: up to eight generations per profession before purchase, with no signup or card. Lifetime access covers future updates to that profession's tool. Terms state that except where required by law all purchases are final and no refunds are offered, with the free trial given as the reason. A stale third-party mirror (devadex.com) still lists a retired pre-launch $59 price; the mirrored store account sonaopusaiworkflow.gumroad.com now returns 404 and the price on every live vendor page is $99.
