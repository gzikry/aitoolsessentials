    Individual plans checked 2026-11-14 from gamma.app/pricing: Free $0 with 400 one-time signup credits that do not refill and up to 10 slides per prompt. Plus $9/seat/month equivalent, $108/seat billed annually, with 1,500 monthly credits and up to 100 slides per prompt. Pro $18/seat/month equivalent, $216/seat billed annually, with 4,000 monthly credits, premium image models, custom branding, analytics, 10 custom domains, API access, and workspace templates. Annual billing is advertised as saving up to 28%. Unused paid-plan credits roll over up to 2x the monthly amount.
