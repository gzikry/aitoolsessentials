Perplexity

Official support lists Standard/Free, individual Pro, individual Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max. Max includes everything in Pro plus the highest access to advanced models, Comet Max Assistant, Create files and apps, and Brain. The reviewed public consumer-plan pages did not expose current Pro or Max dollar prices. An official organization-onboarding article lists Enterprise Pro at $40/seat monthly or $400/seat annually.

Official sources checked 2026-08-25
Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
Open verification questions
  • Current public prices for individual Pro and Max were not verifiable from the reviewed official pages.
  • A blanket commercial-use or output-ownership claim was not made because consumer outputs may be governed by third-party model licenses.
  • Enterprise Max pricing was not publicly stated in the reviewed sources.

Perplexity is documented as an AI-powered answer/search product with citations, premium models, file uploads, Research, Create files and apps, Comet, and Computer features depending on plan. AI Data Retention is enabled by default for Free, Pro, and Max users and can be turned off for future collection; previously collected training data is not removed by a later opt-out. Enterprise data is not used for AI training, and Perplexity says its third-party model-provider agreements prohibit provider training on Perplexity data. Consumer terms warn that output may be subject to third-party model licenses and use restrictions.

NotebookLM

NotebookLM has been renamed to Gemini Notebook. The official about page states it is free to use for individuals. The page advertises uploading PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, audio files, Google Docs, and Google Slides; summarization with citations; and an Audio Overview feature. No paid consumer subscription price was published on the about page during verification.

Official sources checked 2026-09-01
Pricing ↗Docs ↗Privacy ↗Rights ↗
Open verification questions
  • The about page does not publish paid plan prices; if a premium tier exists, it was not exposed on the retrieved page.
  • Google's general terms and policies govern use; no product-specific commercial-use or output-ownership clause was found on the NotebookLM page.
  • The rename from NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook may not be fully reflected across all Google surfaces.

Official about page checked 2026-09-01. The page states: 'We value your privacy and never use your organization's data to train Gemini Notebook.' When used as an individual, data is not used for training unless the user shares feedback. The product is powered by Gemini's multimodal capabilities. A support page (support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16179536) provides additional privacy details. Privacy and Terms are governed by Google's policies at policies.google.com.
