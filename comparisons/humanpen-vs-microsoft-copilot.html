    Microsoft Copilot

    Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is available for work or education without an additional Copilot license and supports metered pay-as-you-go agents. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business is displayed at a promotional $18/user/month, paid yearly, versus a $21 list price. Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot is $23.50/user/month paid yearly, and Business Premium with Copilot is $32/user/month paid yearly. Prices are USD, annual subscriptions that auto-renew, and may vary by market.
