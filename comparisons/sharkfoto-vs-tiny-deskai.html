    SharkFoto vs TINY DESK AI (tiny-deskai.net)

    Small teams and solo creators who want one browser workspace that generates and edits images, video, audio and 3D from text or an uploaded photo, plus a genuinely free file-tool layer (conversion, compression, resize, embroidery) that needs no account. It suits a buyer who values a named operator and a real governing-law clause, disclosed Google Analytics and AdSense IDs, and an explicit no-training statement on uploads. It is a weaker fit for someone who needs every model tier-guaranteed, who objects to an ad-supported free tier that sets analytics and advertising cookies on first visit without a shown banner (from the US), or who wants a published registered street address rather than the entity and the city alone - and a weaker fit for anyone who would rely on the '4.7/5 based on 4,644+ ratings' badge, which the site does not source. · People who want to put themselves and a couple of friends into the 'Outstanding' Tiny Desk meme - a 15-second desk-concert clip for a TikTok, Reel or Short - from photos they already have, and would rather buy a small one-time video pack than start another monthly AI video subscription. Not for anyone needing high resolution, batch output, or a clear answer on what music the finished video carries.
