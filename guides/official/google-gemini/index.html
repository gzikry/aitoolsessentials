    Google (Gemini)

    What the official Google AI blog and Gemini resources cover, from multimodal research to enterprise deployments.

    Visit official blogAll vendor guides

    Summary updated: 2026-08-28 · Curation only — we do not reproduce full original content.

    Key takeaways

    • Multimodal is the headline, not the footnote. Google’s AI blog treats text, image, audio, and video as one pipeline. Expect detailed posts on Gemini’s native multimodal training, not just feature announcements.
    • Research papers ship alongside products. Google AI publishes technical deep dives on Gemini model architecture, benchmarks, and responsible AI practices. These are more in-depth than typical vendor release posts.
    • Enterprise integration is a distinct topic. Posts about Google Workspace, Search, and Vertex AI show how Gemini moves from demo to production inside existing Google Cloud and productivity workflows.
    • Benchmarks are usually Google-first. When Google compares Gemini to other models, they run their own evaluations and publish methodology. Read the fine print before using those numbers in a vendor-neutral comparison.
