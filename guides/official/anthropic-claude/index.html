Anthropic (Claude)

What the official Anthropic blog and research hub cover, and why their safety-first framing actually changes how you prompt.

Summary updated: 2026-08-28 · Curation only — we do not reproduce full original content.

Key takeaways

  • Constitutional AI is their signature concept. Anthropic’s blog spends more time explaining self-critique and harm-reduction mechanisms than any other vendor. If your use case requires strict output guardrails, their documentation is worth reading in full.
  • Long context is documented with real limits. Posts break down Claude’s 200K-token context, how it handles long documents, and prompt strategies for reducing hallucination at scale. This is more detailed than typical vendor “it can do long context” claims.
  • Tool use and agent loops get first-class treatment. Anthropic publishes step-by-step guidance on tool calling, multi-step agent workflows, and XML-structured prompts. These are integration guides, not marketing blurbs.
  • Safety benchmarks are public. Red-team results, dangerous-capability evaluations, and alignment research are published alongside product announcements. The blog is as much a research log as a product changelog.
