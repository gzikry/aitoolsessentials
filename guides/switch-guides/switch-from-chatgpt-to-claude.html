    Migration guide · Updated 2026-10-17

    Switching from ChatGPT to Claude.

    Long-document analysis, careful tone, and coding review are Claude's strengths. If your work is long-form writing, contract/document review, or careful analysis, the switch often pays for itself.

    Claude reviewGet a decision brief

    ✓ What carries over

    • Conversation habits (clear prompts work everywhere)
    • Custom instructions map to Claude's Project instructions/styles
    • File uploads work in both

    ✗ What doesn't transfer

    • GPTs/custom GPT builds do not transfer
    • DALL·E image generation has no Claude equivalent
    • ChatGPT memory does not carry to Claude
    Cost reality

    Do the math before cancelling anything

    Claude Pro is $17/mo billed annually ($20 monthly). ChatGPT Plus pricing varies by plan — check both official pages before cancelling anything.

    First-week test plan

    Migrate one task, not your whole workflow

    1. Move ONE real weekly task (not everything) for 5 days.
    2. Compare editing burden and hallucination rate on the same source material.
    3. Check export/retention settings before uploading anything sensitive.
