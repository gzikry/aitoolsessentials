Switching from Midjourney to Leonardo AI.

Leonardo offers a free daily credit tier and API access that Midjourney lacks, making it attractive for product-shot pipelines and budget-conscious teams. The tradeoff is generally lower peak aesthetic quality.

Leonardo AI reviewGet a decision brief

✓ What carries over

  • Prompt-writing skills transfer almost fully
  • Style references re-create with some tuning
  • Upscaling workflows are similar

✗ What doesn't transfer

  • Midjourney style codes (--sref) don't translate — rebuild reference sets
  • Private generation defaults differ by plan
  • Discord-native workflow habits don't apply
Cost reality

Do the math before cancelling anything

Leonardo's free tier gives daily credits; paid tiers undercut Midjourney's base plan for light users. Heavy aesthetic-driven creators often find Midjourney worth the premium — model your monthly image count first.

First-week test plan

Migrate one task, not your whole workflow

  1. Recreate your 10 most-used prompts on identical settings.
  2. Blind-compare outputs before judging — brand aesthetics matter more than specs.
  3. Move production only if quality is acceptable to your audience, not just cheaper.
