Switching from HeyGen to Synthesia.

Synthesia leads for corporate training/enterprise video with stronger compliance posture and templates; HeyGen often wins on avatar realism and social-content speed. Switch when your use case is internal comms at scale.

Synthesia reviewGet a decision brief

✓ What carries over

  • Scripts import as text
  • Brand colors/fonts re-create quickly
  • Both support 100+ languages

✗ What doesn't transfer

  • Custom avatars must be re-recorded on Synthesia
  • HeyGen's viral-style templates have no direct equivalent
  • Video projects do not export between platforms
Cost reality

Do the math before cancelling anything

Synthesia's starter plan targets individual creators; enterprise seats cost more. Compare minutes-per-month against both official pricing pages before cancelling either.

First-week test plan

Migrate one task, not your whole workflow

  1. Rebuild one real training video in Synthesia end to end.
  2. Get stakeholder sign-off on avatar quality before migrating a library.
  3. Export source scripts from HeyGen before cancelling.
