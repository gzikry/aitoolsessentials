Switching from Perplexity to You.com.

You.com emphasizes customizable AI models and apps-style research. Worth testing if you want more control over which model answers your research queries.

You.com reviewGet a decision brief

✓ What carries over

  • Search-and-cite habits translate directly
  • Browser/mobile workflows similar
  • Prompt libraries carry over

✗ What doesn't transfer

  • Perplexity Collections do not transfer
  • Source-quality differences mean re-verifying citations initially
Cost reality

Do the math before cancelling anything

You.com offers free use with paid tiers for advanced models. Perplexity Pro pricing is on their official page — verify both before switching.

First-week test plan

Migrate one task, not your whole workflow

  1. Run 10 real research questions through both in one week.
  2. Grade citation quality and follow-up depth.
  3. Keep whichever reduces verification time — not whichever is newer.
