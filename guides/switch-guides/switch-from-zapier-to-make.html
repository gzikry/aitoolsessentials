Switching from Zapier AI to Make.

Make charges per operation inside visual scenarios; multi-step workflows that burn Zapier tasks often run dramatically cheaper on Make.

Make reviewGet a decision brief

✓ What carries over

  • App connections/auth re-authorize fresh
  • Trigger/action logic maps to Make modules
  • Webhooks supported on both

✗ What doesn't transfer

  • Zaps do not import — you rebuild scenarios visually
  • Filter/path logic differs and needs re-testing
  • Task history does not transfer
Cost reality

Do the math before cancelling anything

Make Core runs roughly $12/mo at the 10k-operations tier. Model YOUR volume: count current Zapier tasks × steps before comparing sticker prices.

First-week test plan

Migrate one task, not your whole workflow

  1. Pick your highest-volume Zap and rebuild it in Make.
  2. Track operations consumed for a week vs tasks burned on Zapier.
  3. Migrate the rest only if the math holds on real usage.
