Switching from Fireflies.ai to Otter.ai.

Otter undercuts Fireflies on entry pricing and is strong for individual note-takers; Fireflies stays ahead for sales teams needing CRM sync and conversation intelligence. Switch only if your usage profile matches Otter's strengths.

Otter.ai reviewGet a decision brief

✓ What carries over

  • Meeting platforms supported overlap heavily (Zoom/Meet/Teams)
  • Transcript search habits carry over
  • Highlight/comment workflows are similar

✗ What doesn't transfer

  • Historical transcripts do not export wholesale between platforms
  • Fireflies' CRM integrations (HubSpot/Salesforce) have no Otter equivalent
  • Soundbites/clips metadata stays behind
Cost reality

Do the math before cancelling anything

Otter Pro runs well below Fireflies' business tiers for individuals. Teams relying on revenue-intelligence features should price the cost of losing them, not just the subscription delta.

First-week test plan

Migrate one task, not your whole workflow

  1. Run Otter alongside Fireflies on one week of real meetings — never switch blind.
  2. Grade transcript accuracy on your industry vocabulary and accents specifically.
  3. Export anything you must keep from Fireflies before cancelling.
