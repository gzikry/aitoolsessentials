## POST 4 — Automation pricing models decoded

Zapier, Make, and n8n bill in three fundamentally different units. Most people pick
wrong because they compare sticker prices instead of their own volume.

- **Zapier — tasks:** every action run counts. Simple, predictable, expensive at scale.
  Professional starts $19.99/mo (annual billing).
- **Make — operations/credits:** most modules cost 1 credit, AI features cost more.
  Core ~$12/mo at the 10k tier. Cheapest if your scenarios are mostly non-AI steps.
- **n8n — executions:** whole workflow runs, unlimited steps inside each run.
  Starter €20/mo for 2,500 executions. Best value for complex multi-step workflows —
  and the only one you can self-host free.

**Rule of thumb:** few-step simple automations → Zapier; heavy AI steps → check Make's
credit costs on YOUR scenario; complex branching logic at volume → n8n wins on math alone.
