How to test before paying

  1. Choose one repeated video format first: tutorial, talking-head, short, or product demo.
  2. Test script quality, edit time, thumbnail iteration, and publishable output quality on the same brief.
  3. Avoid paid annual plans until the tool owns a weekly production step.
