How to test before paying

  1. Read the official commercial-use and licensed-content terms for the exact plan.
  2. Generate the same brief in two tools and compare editability and stock-content mixing.
  3. Do not assume output is unique, trademark-clear, or yours to register.
  4. Keep a dated screenshot of the terms you relied on.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not legal advice and not a copyright or trademark clearance.
  • Plan-specific stock licenses can restrict commercial use even when the model output is otherwise allowed.
  • Do not treat community posts as the current license.
