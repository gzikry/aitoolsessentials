    How to test before paying

    1. Inventory every AI subscription with owner, monthly cost, and last real use.
    2. Group tools by job (drafting, research, meetings, coding, visuals, automation) and mark overlaps.
    3. Run one real weekly task in each contender; demos do not count.
    4. Tag keep / trial / replace / cancel before the next renewal date.
    5. Prefer free trials and monthly billing until one tool clearly owns the job.

    Safety and compliance guardrails

    • Not financial, procurement, or legal advice.
    • Confirm current prices and cancel terms on vendor pages before acting.
    • Do not paste client secrets into tools during evaluation without policy review.
