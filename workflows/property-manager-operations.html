How to test before paying

  1. Start on Landlord Studio Go or a TenantCloud 14-day trial with one sanitized unit.
  2. Keep tenant identifiers, screening reports, and Social Security numbers out of consumer chat tools.
  3. Compare listing publish time, rent-reminder setup, maintenance ticket flow, and export ownership.
  4. Have a human review every listing and screening decision for fair-housing and local-law issues.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not legal, fair-housing, or tenant-screening certification.
  • Do not paste applicant Social Security numbers, bank details, or eviction files into a personal chatbot.
  • Vendor ROI calculators are marketing, not audited results.
  • Listing copy that mentions families, disability, or protected classes needs a human compliance check.
