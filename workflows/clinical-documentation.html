How to test before paying

  1. Use only de-identified or organization-approved encounters until legal, privacy, and IT review is complete.
  2. Compare draft-note edit time, specialty template fit, and whether the note can be placed into the official record without copy-paste risk.
  3. Ask each vendor for current BAA, retention, audit-log, and EHR integration terms in writing. Do not infer those terms from a homepage badge.
  4. Keep diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, billing codes, and the signed medical record under a licensed professional.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not medical advice and not a clinical-decision device certification.
  • No HIPAA, FDA, or EHR-certification claim is implied by listing a tool here.
  • Enterprise ambient tools such as Dragon Copilot are quote-based; do not treat third-party street prices as official.
  • If a tool cannot show contracts and admin controls your organization requires, keep it out of live patient workflows.
