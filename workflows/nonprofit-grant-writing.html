Recommended tools for this workflow

#1 · Nonprofit · Free $0 with 5 chat messages/day; Starter $50/mo; Pro $150/mo; qualifying 501(c)(3)s under ~$500K get 50% off for 1 year ($25/$75)

Grantable

An AI grant-writing workspace with a real free plan. Official pricing lists Starter at $50/month and Pro at $150/month, plus a one-year 50% nonprofit discount for qualifying 501(c)(3) organizations under about $500K operating budget. Docs also describe a Pro+ tier whose public dollar price was not listed on the main pricing page during this check.

  • Best for: free-first AI grant writing and funder research for small nonprofits
  • Trial test: use one real workflow before upgrading.

How to test before paying

  1. Start on Grantable Free or an Instrumentl 14-day trial before paying annual-upfront software.
  2. Use one real program and sanitized org documents, not a generic charity prompt.
  3. Compare funder-match quality, draft edit time, export ownership, and the actual cash outlay including annual-upfront billing.
  4. Keep eligibility, budgets, and anything under an authorized signature with a human grant lead.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not fundraising, tax, or grant-award advice.
  • Vendor ROI, win-rate, and hours-saved figures are marketing claims unless independently verified.
  • Do not paste donor, beneficiary, or Social Security data into a personal chatbot.
  • Grantable's 50% nonprofit price is eligibility-gated; Instrumentl's cheapest official path after the trial is a paid plan.
