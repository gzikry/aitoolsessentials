#1 · Education · Free $0 for individual teachers; Plus $12.99/mo or $8.33/mo billed annually ($99.96/year); Enterprise custom

MagicSchool

An education-specific AI platform for teachers and schools. Official pricing lists a forever-free individual teacher plan, a self-serve Plus plan, and custom Enterprise for districts. Vendor privacy pages claim FERPA, COPPA, and SOC 2 alignment; those are vendor claims, not certifications by this site.

  • Best for: K-12 lesson planning, classroom communications, and teacher-facing AI tools with a published free tier

How to test before paying

  1. Start on official free teacher tiers before paying for Plus or a general chatbot subscription.
  2. Use de-identified class context until your school reviews student-data, retention, and DPA terms.
  3. Compare one lesson plan, one rubric, and one family email across MagicSchool, Khanmigo, and a general assistant.
  4. Keep grades, IEP decisions, and identifiable student records under a licensed educator and approved district systems.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not a grading, IEP, or special-education certification.
  • No FERPA, COPPA, or SOC 2 certification is implied by listing a tool.
  • Khanmigo teacher-free access is not classroom student access.
  • Do not paste identifiable student records into a personal ChatGPT or similar consumer account during a trial.
