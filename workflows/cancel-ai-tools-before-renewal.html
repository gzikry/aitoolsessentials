How to test before paying

  1. List every AI subscription with renewal date and monthly vs annual billing.
  2. Tag keep / trial / cancel using a real weekly job, not a demo.
  3. Export chats or files you still need before you lose access.
  4. Run the weekly job once on the replacement, then cancel in the vendor portal.
  5. Confirm the next statement; some tools bill through app stores.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not financial or legal advice.
  • Confirm cancel terms on the vendor billing page.
  • Do not cancel the only tool that owns a weekly workflow until a replacement has passed one real task.
