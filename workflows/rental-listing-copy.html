How to test before paying

  1. Start from a feature list and photos, not a competitor's listing.
  2. Generate two drafts, then remove any language that could steer protected classes.
  3. Compare Canva or Landlord Studio syndication against a manual Zillow/Apartments post.
  4. Do not publish until a human reads the listing for fair-housing issues.

Safety and compliance guardrails

  • Not a fair-housing or advertising-law certification.
  • Do not include applicant criteria that could discriminate.
  • Keep unit photos and tenant faces out of consumer image generators unless you have rights.
