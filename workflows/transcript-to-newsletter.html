How to test before paying

  1. Start with one real episode or meeting, not a sample file.
  2. Compare cleanup time in Descript or Otter, then drafting time in Claude or ChatGPT.
  3. Verify every quote and proper name against the transcript.
  4. Check guest consent before publishing a recap.
