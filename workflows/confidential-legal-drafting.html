    How to test before paying

    1. Start with de-identified or fully sanitized matter files until ethics, conflicts, and IT have approved the vendor.
    2. Prefer tools with written no-training, retention, access-control, and audit terms that match your bar guidance.
    3. Verify every citation and authority in Westlaw, Lexis, or the primary source before a filing or client advice.
    4. Keep legal judgment, privilege calls, and anything under signature with a licensed professional.

    Safety and compliance guardrails

    • Not legal advice and not a substitute for a licensed attorney.
    • No privilege, confidentiality, or bar-compliance certification is implied by listing a tool.
    • Consumer chat tiers are the wrong place for client-identifiable or privileged material.
    • Harvey and Spellbook are quote-based; the only official dollar figure recorded here is Thomson Reuters’ starting-at Westlaw Advantage with CoCounsel Essentials price.
