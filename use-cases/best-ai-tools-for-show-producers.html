Riverside

A remote recording studio for podcasts and video shows. Official pricing lists a $0 Free plan with a 2-hour one-off multi-track allowance, then Pro, Grow, and Webinar self-serve tiers and custom Business. Official FAQ states annual billing saves up to 20%. Recording itself is described as unlimited; separate-track download hours are the billed cap.

Best for: remote podcast recording, AI editing, clips, and hosting with a published free plan

Adobe Podcast

Adobe's browser podcast tools for Enhance Speech cleanup and Studio recording. The official plans page publishes a Free plan and a Premium plan with a 30-day free trial. Exact Premium US-dollar pricing was not listed on that page during this check, so no unofficial third-party dollar figure is recorded.

Best for: browser speech enhancement and short studio sessions with a published free plan
