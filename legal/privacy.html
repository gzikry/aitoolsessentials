Privacy policy

A plain-language explanation of what this site collects, why, and how to contact us.

Effective 2026-08-21

Information you provide

If you email us, submit a tool, or request an AI Stack Audit, we receive the information you choose to send. This may include your name, email address, company or website, product information, current software stack, workflows, budget range, and questions.

Forms and email delivery

Public forms are transmitted through FormSubmit to contact@aitoolsessentials.com. Do not submit passwords, API keys, payment-card data, confidential client data, health information, or other sensitive material. The AI Stack Audit is strategy-only and never requires account access.

Analytics

The site is configured to load Plausible Analytics on the production domain. Plausible is used for aggregate page and referral statistics and is configured without advertising cookies. We do not use analytics data to build individual advertising profiles.

Local browser storage

The site may use local browser storage to count outbound-link interactions on your device. This data stays in the browser unless a separate analytics event is explicitly enabled.

Payments and memberships

If Premium membership is activated through Whop, payment and membership information is handled by Whop under its own privacy terms. AIToolsEssentials should not receive or store full payment-card details.

How information is used

  • Respond to messages and submissions
  • Prepare requested strategy reports
  • Evaluate products for possible editorial coverage
  • Maintain security and prevent spam
  • Understand aggregate site usage

Retention and deletion

We retain correspondence and submissions only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose provided, operational records, dispute prevention, and legal obligations. To request deletion or correction, email contact@aitoolsessentials.com.

Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the site, including hosting, form delivery, analytics, email, and—when activated—payment/membership processing. We do not sell submitted personal information.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit personal information for a child.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

Contact

Email privacy questions to contact@aitoolsessentials.com.
