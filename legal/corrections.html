    Corrections policy

    Prices, model defaults, features, benchmarks, policies, and product limits change. Corrections should be easy to request and independently check.

    Effective 2026-08-21

    What to send

    • The exact AIToolsEssentials page URL
    • The sentence, table row, benchmark record, or price that appears wrong
    • A primary source such as official pricing, documentation, policy, model card, or benchmark submission
    • The correction you believe is required

    How we handle corrections

    1. Check the cited primary source and its publication/effective date.
    2. Determine whether the claim was wrong when published or became stale later.
    3. Update the source data and generator—not only the rendered HTML—when applicable.
    4. Regenerate and validate affected pages.
    5. Change the page update date when the correction is material.

    Vendor requests

    Vendors may correct factual errors but cannot negotiate ratings, benchmark caveats, pros/cons, comparisons, or rankings in exchange for money or access.

    Benchmark corrections

    Include the exact benchmark release, model/version, harness, reasoning effort, run date, source row or submission record, and metric. We do not transfer a model score to an entire product.
