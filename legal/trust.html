    Trust & methodology

    How AIToolsEssentials works.

    This page answers the questions we get most: how we verify pricing, what our scores mean, how affiliate relationships work, and how to correct something we got wrong.

    Evidence freshness

    79 tools tracked. Pricing snapshots are taken directly from vendor official pages. Each snapshot has a checked date — we never use screenshots, third-party data, or old blog posts. We re-verify weekly and update the date when the official page changes.

    If a tool's "checked on" date is older than 7 days, it's queued for re-verification. We publish the date on every review page so you can judge freshness yourself.

    What our scores mean

    Editorial score (5-point scale)

    This is our product rating based on workflow fit, output quality, and operating cost. It is not a lab benchmark. External benchmark results are displayed separately and apply only to the exact model and configuration shown.

    Affiliate relationships

    We may earn a commission when you click certain links and make a purchase. This is how we fund the free directory, free Stack Audit, and free Keep/Cut Weekly email.

    Affiliate status never changes editorial rankings, scores, or "worth it" verdicts. A tool that pays us commission is treated identically to one that doesn't. We label affiliate links clearly and keep them separate from our recommendation text.

    What we don't do

    • We don't sell rankings or "featured placement" to vendors
    • We don't fabricate test scores, traffic counts, or user numbers
    • We don't use AI-generated prose in our reviews
    • We don't accept payment for coverage or positive reviews
    • We don't set login walls on public reviews

    Corrections

    If we got something wrong — a price, a feature, a policy, a date — tell us. We verify corrections against official sources before publishing.

    Email: contact@aitoolsessentials.com with the page URL and what needs fixing. We reply within 2 business days.

    How ratings are produced

    We do not currently run hands-on tests of the tools we list, so no page claims a test result. Ratings are editorial product scores covering job fit, likely output quality, ease of adoption, and operational cost, drawn from official vendor documentation, dated pricing checks, and published benchmark context. Our method is public at /legal/editorial-methodology.html.

    We keep a hands-on testing protocol on file. If we ever publish a test result, it will meet that standard and be labeled as tested.

    Community reports

    Readers can submit test reports through our community page. We verify each report against our protocol before publishing. Reports identify the reporter's role, tool version, task, and date. Vague endorsements don't clear verification.

    Directory listings and third-party verification

    A directory confirming a listing is not a ranking, a score, or an endorsement, and it never changes anything editorial here. We list the site in independent software directories because it helps buyers find the reviews; we do not buy placement in any of them.
