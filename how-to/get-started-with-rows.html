How to get started with Rows for AI-native spreadsheets

A practical Rows setup path: import data, try AI transformations, connect integrations, and decide if the paid tier earns its seat.

Setup commands, limits, and security terms change. Follow the linked official documentation before running anything.

Keep/Cut Weekly

Before you start

Use a test profile, synthetic data, and no production credentials. This guide is strategy and evaluation guidance, not managed implementation or support.

  1. Import your data

    Start with a CSV import or connect an integration. The free tier offers manual data import and 10 integration accounts.

  2. Try AI transformations

    Use AI to clean, categorize, or enrich your data. The free tier includes 5 AI tasks per month — test whether they save you time.

  3. Connect an integration

    Pull data from an API or service directly into your spreadsheet. Test whether the integration works with your data source.

  4. Collaborate with teammates

    Share your spreadsheet and test real-time collaboration. Decide if the free tier covers your team size.

  5. Decide on the paid tier

    If you need more than 5 AI tasks/month or more integrations, compare paid tiers against your productivity gain.

Completion checklist

  • Data imported
  • AI transformations tested
  • Integration connected
  • Collaboration tested
  • Paid tier decision made
