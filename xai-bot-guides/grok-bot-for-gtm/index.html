Grok Bot for GTM

How to use Grok Bot for Enterprise GTM: a Chief of Staff, prospecting and account prompts, live slides, and the rest of the team.

The team

The GTM setup is built around a Chief of Staff that owns calendar, inbox, and routing. Specialists then own prospecting, customer intelligence, slides, forecasting, and sales coaching.

What’s different

  • Onboard Grok Bot like a new teammate: record yourself doing a task once and turn it into a skill.
  • Have the bot learn your voice from sent mail before it drafts anything.
  • Use routines for daily inbox triage, meeting prep, and weekly account rundowns.
  • Bots can call each other, so the Chief of Staff can spin up slides or prospecting work on demand.

Why it matters

GTM work is fragmented across tools, calls, and follow-ups. A coordinated bot team can own the research, drafts, and updates while the human stays in customer conversations.
