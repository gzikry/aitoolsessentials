Designing Grok Bot with Grok Bot

How a designer uses always-on Grok Bot agents to explore ideas, work with real production assets, and keep judgment in the loop.

The team

The designer runs four named agents. Figma Bro handles production work. Motion God prototypes interaction feel. Experiments makes ideas tangible before they are fully formed. Devbot answers engineering questions.

What’s different
