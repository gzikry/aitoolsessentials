Grok Bot for PMs

For the first time, a PM can have a team that reports to them. Attention lists, shipping software, and the bots I actually run.

The team

The PM runs named agents with separate memory and roles: Chief of Staff for calendar and inbox, Eng mgr for breaking down work, five Eng ICs for Cloud Agents, a data analyst for dashboards, PM Pete for RFCs and research, and a recruiter.

What’s different

  • Named agents with scoped memory beat one omniscient agent for referenceability and parallelism.
  • Agents learn on the job from your past Slack, email, and work artifacts.
  • Ask them to stay quiet unless something needs your attention.
  • Keep humans on external sends, purchases, and destructive actions.

Why it matters

Most PM tools help you manage artifacts. This setup gives the PM a team that can execute, review, and report back, which changes what one person can ship without adding headcount.
