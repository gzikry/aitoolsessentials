Gemini now has a $4.99 plan

Google, OpenAI and xAI all sell a chat plan under $10 now, and the $100 tiers bundle API credits. A second $20 assistant is the easy cut.

This week’s call: AI chat assistants
KEEP One $20 assistant you open daily. Claude Pro is $20 month-to-month or $17 with a $200 annual prepay, checked 14 Nov.
TEST FIRST Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month as your second opinion, checked 14 Nov.
CUT A second $20 chatbot doing the same job.

Count the chat assistants on your card. On our 14 Nov 2026 checks of the official pages, ChatGPT Plus is $20 a month, Google AI Pro is $19.99, Claude Pro is $20 month-to-month, and Grok Premium is $30. They all answer, draft, summarize and read your files. If two of them bill you every month, one is there out of habit.

The sub-$10 rung is the cheapest second opinion

Three of the big four now sell a plan under $10. Google added Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month, between Free and AI Pro. It wasn’t in our 18 Sep record. Google says Plus gets double the Free usage limits and AI Pro gets four times. ChatGPT Go is $8. Grok’s new Plus is $8 too, and it bundles Cursor.

That changes the math on a second subscription. Say you keep the other assistant for one job, like checking a draft or a second read on a contract. A backup rarely needs a high usage cap. At $4.99 that’s $59.88 a year. At $8 it’s $96. A second $20 seat is $240. Move the backup down a rung for one month and see if you notice.

At $100 you’re buying API credits, not just chat

The top of the ladder moved on 14 Nov. Anthropic now sells its $200 tier as a separate plan called Ultra, with 10x Pro weekly usage and $200 a month in API credits. Max stays $100 and now includes $100 a month in API credits. ChatGPT Pro is also $100. On Grok’s page, the old $30 SuperGrok is now Premium and the $100 SuperGrok Plus is now Super.

Those credits only count if you’d spend them. If you already pay a separate API bill for scripts or a coding tool, Max can absorb it. If you don’t, you’re paying $100 for headroom. Look at the last 30 days. Did you hit a usage limit even once? No limit, no upgrade.

If you’re still on SuperGrok, nothing forces a switch. On our 13 Oct check, xAI’s documentation said existing SuperGrok plans continue and switching is optional. If $30 for Premium is what you’d pay anyway, there’s nothing to do today.

Worth a look

Poe puts many models behind one $49.99 plan

Poe runs chat, image and video models from several vendors in one app, with side-by-side answers and custom bots. Its official page, checked 14 Nov 2026, lists annual plans only: $49.99 a year for 10,000 points a day, which Poe shows as $4.17 a month. Bigger plans run from $199.99 to $2,499.99 a year, and there’s a free daily points allowance.

The catch: we found no monthly prices, so you’re prepaying a year, and training rules vary by bot. Fully shaded bots don’t feed provider training. Half-shaded ones may. Use it as the backup, not the main seat.

One thing to do this week

Open the billing page of every assistant you pay for. Write down the plan, the renewal date, and whether you hit a usage limit in the last 30 days. If two $20 assistants show up, keep the one you opened most this week. Move the other to its sub-$10 rung or to free before it renews. Then run the free Stack Audit.
