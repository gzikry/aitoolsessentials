Firefly Pro is $9.99 until 6 Dec
Adobe, ElevenLabs, Slack and TinyCommand all cut the first payment this month. The renewal stays at list, so price the year, not the month.

THIS WEEK’S CALL: BLACK FRIDAY AI DEALS
KEEP: Any plan you’d pay list for. Firefly (https://aitoolsessentials.com/tools/adobe-firefly/) Standard is $9.99 a month with 2,000 credits, checked 14 Nov.
TEST FIRST: Firefly Pro at $9.99 a month for six months, then $19.99. The offer ends 6 Dec.
CUT: A deal you’d only buy because it ends this week.

This is the week every subscription on your card gets a cheaper-looking twin. Most of what we logged isn’t a lower price. It’s a lower first payment. On our 14 Nov checks, Adobe listed Firefly Pro at $19.99 a month, or $9.99 for the first six months if you sign up before 6 Dec. ElevenLabs (https://aitoolsessentials.com/tools/elevenlabs/) showed its $22 Creator plan at $11 for the first month. Slack (https://aitoolsessentials.com/tools/slack-ai/) was running 50% off for three months on eligible Pro and Business+ purchases.

PRICE THE PROMO AT MONTH SEVEN

Do the year, not the month. Firefly Pro on the promo is $59.94 for six months, then $119.94 for the next six at $19.99. That’s $179.88 in year one against $239.88 at list. A real saving, if Pro is the plan you’d want anyway. If Standard at $9.99 already covers you, the promo isn’t a discount. It’s an upgrade with a delayed bill. Slack’s deal works the same way, just multiplied by seats: half off for three months, then full price on every user.

ElevenLabs is the same shape over a shorter window: $11 once, then $22. Its annual billing equals ten monthly payments, which works out to $18.33 a month for Creator. If you’d keep it a year, that’s the lower number, and it isn’t a one-month price. Unused paid credits roll over for up to two months, so one slow month isn’t wasted.

ELEVEN FREE MONTHS STILL RENEW

TinyCommand (https://aitoolsessentials.com/tools/tinycommand/)’s Black Friday offer, checked 21 Nov, is the boldest one we’ve logged. On the yearly plan you pay for month one only: $19 for Basic, $49 for Professional. Months two to twelve are free. From month 13 it renews at the normal yearly price, $199 or $499. The window closes 7 Dec at 11:59 PM ET. For scale, Basic billed monthly is $19 a month, or $228 a year.

That’s a good deal on a tool you’ll use, and a $199 surprise next November on one you won’t. The risk isn’t the first charge. It’s a renewal you set up a year before you’ll think about it. Before you pay for any of these, put the list-price renewal date in your calendar. If you wouldn’t pay list on that date, skip the deal.

WORTH A LOOK
Unblurfy sells credits that don’t expire
Unblurfy (https://aitoolsessentials.com/tools/unblurfy/) rebuilds detail in blurry, pixelated or too-small photos, then shows the result next to the original on a slider. On our 20 Nov check, signed-in users get 2 free enhances a day at 1K with no watermark. Monthly plans run $5.99, $9.99 and $24.99, and those credits reset with no rollover. The one-time packs never expire: $4.99 for 20 credits, $14.99 for 80.

The catch: the site says plainly that AI redraws detail rather than recovering it, so don’t use it where the fix has to be exact. Checkout needs a Google sign-in. A few photos a month? The free tier or a pack beats a subscription.

ONE THING TO DO THIS WEEK
Before Friday, open the billing page of every AI tool you pay for and write down two things: the renewal date and the price it renews at. Any plan on an intro rate gets a calendar reminder a week before the rate ends. If a deal shows up for something you already pay for, check it’s your plan and not an upgrade.
