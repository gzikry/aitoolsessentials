    How this stack is chosen

    Small portfolios should not start on a quote-only enterprise PMS. Landlord Studio publishes a Go plan at $0 for up to 3 units, then Pro at $12/month or $144/year. TenantCloud publishes Starter at $25/month or $180/year, with Growth and Pro self-serve tiers and Business from $100/month. Use ChatGPT or Canva only on de-identified amenity lists. Keep applicant Social Security numbers, screening reports, and bank details out of consumer chat. A human still owns fair-housing review.
