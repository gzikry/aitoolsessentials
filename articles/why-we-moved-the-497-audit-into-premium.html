Why we moved the $497 audit into $12 Premium

28 August 2026

We used to sell the AI Stack Audit as a $497 standalone service. One person bought it. Test users said it didn't solve their problem at the price.

So we moved it into Premium at $12/month — same content, same PDF, same one-pager you can print, same 30-day roadmap — and it's now a member benefit rather than a luxury purchase.

The math

A $497 audit has to convince the buyer before they've seen the deliverable. That's the entire problem. You can't know whether a $497 report is useful until after you've paid $497.

A $12/month Premium subscription has a different trust curve: low risk, 7-day free trial, cancel anytime, and the audit is just the most concrete deliverable among many. If the audit doesn't land for you inside the trial, you've paid nothing.

Who actually needs this

The audit's core user is not an enterprise with a procurement stack. It's:

  • The consultant paying for three overlapping AI subscriptions.
  • The small practice (optometry, law, real estate) using ChatGPT and wondering what else to add, or not add.
  • The agency with a renewal coming up and no time to test five alternatives.
  • The creator earning enough to know their tool sprawl is costing money but not enough to hire a fractional CTO.

None of those people should be paying $497 for a decision document. All of them can justify $12 for a month of structured thinking.

What the audit actually is

You fill out a 6-question intake questionnaire: name, email, business, website, what you're currently running, what problems you're trying to solve. Within 48 hours we send back a PDF.

The PDF has nine pages:

  1. Cover + confidentiality.
  2. Read This First — the one-page decision summary.
  3. Findings — five findings with confidence levels.
  4. Recommended stack — keep / cut / trial per tool.
  5. PHI fence — what never goes into a consumer chatbot.
  6. Phones, scheduling, replies — use what you already pay for.
  7. Print this page — eight templates + the emergency rule.
  8. 30-day roadmap — inventory, fence, no new vendors, scribe trial if needed.
  9. BAA questions + spend logic.

The one-page "Read This First" summary is the part we think is most valuable. Most audit-style reports bury the decision under forty pages of context. Ours front-loads it.

What we didn't change

The scope boundary. We still don't set up software, connect to your EHR, take logins, sign BAAs, or offer ongoing support. The audit is strategy. Implementation stays with you, your IT person, or your agency.

If you tried the $497 pilot

Canyon Optometry received our first paid audit on 28 August 2026. If you're reading this and you tried the $497 version, the audit you got is the same one that becomes a Premium deliverable as of today. If you have outstanding follow-up questions from your report, reply to the delivery email — we'll answer.

Going forward, intake comes through Premium, and the deliverable is what you see in the sample audit page.

The point

The decision "which AI tools do I actually pay for" is not enterprise consulting. It's a $12 question with a 48-hour turnaround. We priced it accordingly.
