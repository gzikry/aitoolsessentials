    How this stack is chosen

    Consultants trade on trust, so data-handling terms matter as much as capability. Before putting client material into any tool, read its training policy: Claude consumer chats may be used for training unless opted out; business tiers differ. Prefer tools with explicit no-training commitments on your plan tier.
