Is Shortwave worth it?

Whether Shortwave's AI email client is worth paying for over Gmail's built-in features plus a separate AI assistant.

Check current pricesMore guides

Short answer: worth it if you live in Gmail and want AI triage, summaries, reminders, and scheduled send without switching tools. Not worth it if you barely process email or if Gmail's built-in AI covers your needs.

What the paid plans actually buy

  • Free tier with limited AI features
  • Business $30/seat/month, Premier $45/seat/month, Max $120/seat/month

Current pricing on the official pricing page.

When Shortwave is worth it

  • You process 50+ emails daily and want AI triage built into Gmail
  • You need AI-powered search across your entire email history
  • You want scheduled send and reminders without a separate tool

When it is not worth it

  • You process fewer than 20 emails a day — Gmail's built-in features may suffice
  • You use Outlook or Apple Mail — Shortwave is Gmail-only
  • You need heavy team collaboration — dedicated email platforms have stronger shared inbox features
