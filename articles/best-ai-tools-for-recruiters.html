    Recruiting AI should reduce bias, not amplify it

    Use AI for drafting and summarizing, then apply consistent criteria when screening. Avoid letting models make rejection decisions, and comply with local hiring and privacy rules.

    Best first workflow

    Create a repeatable role-launch workflow: intake notes into a job description, sourcing message variants, interview question bank, scorecard, and debrief summary templates.

    Suggested first workflow

    1. Choose one repeated job that currently takes more than 30 minutes.
    2. Run the same task through two tools from this shortlist.
    3. Measure review time, quality, privacy risk, and total cost.
    4. Document the winning process so it can be repeated.

    FAQ

    Can AI screen resumes?

    It can summarize and rank against stated criteria, but recruiters should own final decisions and watch for bias and regulatory requirements.

    What should recruiters automate first?

    Interview scheduling notes, outreach personalization, job description drafts, weekly pipeline reports, and debrief summaries are good starting points.
