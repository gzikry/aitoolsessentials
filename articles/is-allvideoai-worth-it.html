Is AllVideoAI worth it?

Whether AllVideoAI's multi-model video workspace earns its subscription over single-tool alternatives.

Check current pricesMore guides

Short answer: worth it if you produce AI video regularly and want multiple models (text-to-video, image-to-video, face swap) in one place. Not worth it for occasional clips or if one model does everything you need.

What the paid plans actually buy

  • Free tier with limited generations
  • Starter $17/month (billed annually, $204/year)
  • Higher tiers with more credits and faster generation

Current pricing on the official pricing page.

When AllVideoAI is worth it

  • You generate video from multiple source types (text, image, reference footage)
  • You want to compare output across models without separate subscriptions
  • You produce video at least weekly for real work

When it is not worth it

  • You only need text-to-video — a dedicated tool may be cheaper
  • You produce fewer than 10-20 clips a month — the free tier may suffice
  • You need commercial rights — verify the license terms before selling output
