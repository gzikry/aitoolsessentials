    What to look for in AI research software

    Prioritize citation visibility, source quality, exportable notes, current information, and the ability to separate facts from interpretation.

    Best workflow for research tools

    Use AI search for discovery, save sources, then use a writing assistant to synthesize findings into a brief with claims linked back to evidence.

    How to test these tools

    1. Pick one real workflow you repeat weekly.
    2. Run the same input through two or three tools.
    3. Score output quality, review time, factual accuracy, collaboration, privacy, and price.
    4. Adopt the winner only if it saves time after human review.

    FAQ

    Are AI research tools always accurate?

    No. They can surface useful sources quickly, but important claims still need source-level verification.

    What is the highest-revenue angle for research content?

    Buyer guides for market research, competitor monitoring, academic summaries, and sales research usually have strong commercial intent.
