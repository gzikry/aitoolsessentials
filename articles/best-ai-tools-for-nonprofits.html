How this stack is chosen

Small nonprofits should not start on a $299/month grant database. Grantable publishes a real free plan (5 messages/day) plus Starter at $50/month and Pro at $150/month, with a one-year 50% discount to $25/$75 for qualifying 501(c)(3)s under about $500K. Instrumentl is the paid discovery and lifecycle platform: official Discover is $299/month billed annually or $349 monthly, with a 14-day free trial. Vendor win-rate and revenue-uplift claims are marketing. Keep eligibility, budgets, and authorized submissions with a human grant lead.
