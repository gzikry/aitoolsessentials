Is Luma AI worth it?

Whether Luma AI's 3D and video generation platform is worth its subscription for product demos, social video, or 3D prototyping.

Check current pricesMore guides

Short answer: worth it if you need photorealistic 3D models or AI-generated video from text prompts for real work. Not worth it for casual use or if you only need one output type (a dedicated tool may be cheaper).

What the paid plans actually buy

  • Plus $30/month or $300/year with 10,000 credits
  • Pro $90/month or $900/year with 40,000 credits
  • Ultra $300/month or $3,000/year with 150,000 credits
  • Team and Enterprise are sales-assisted

Current pricing on the official pricing page.

When Luma AI is worth it

  • You create 3D product prototypes from text or images
  • You generate social video content from reference footage
  • You need both 3D and video output in one workflow

When it is not worth it

  • You only need 2D images — dedicated image generators are cheaper
  • You produce fewer than 20-30 assets a month — the credit cost may not justify a subscription
  • You need production-use rights — verify the commercial license before selling output
