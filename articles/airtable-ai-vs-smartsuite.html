    Airtable AI vs SmartSuite

    Same ops table. Keep one.

    Airtable AI benefits from a larger ecosystem, more template coverage, and strong integration breadth. SmartSuite offers similar AI-assisted tables, forms, and workflows with simpler admin and often lower team pricing. If your team already uses Airtable, AI features are the incremental upgrade. If you are evaluating from scratch, compare SmartSuite's workflow templates and admin overhead before signing.

    Weekly workflow test

    Run the same real task in both tools for one week. Score on: time-to-first-result, corrections needed, export/import quality, and monthly cost at your expected volume. The winner should be obvious by day three.

    Decision summary

    • Pick the tool that fits the weekly job you actually repeat.
    • Keep the other only if it covers a different workflow you cannot do otherwise.
    • Set a 30-day trial for the runner-up; cancel unless usage is weekly and verified.
