Is HtmlSlides worth it?

Whether HtmlSlides' AI HTML presentation maker is worth paying for over traditional slide tools.

Check current pricesMore guides

Short answer: worth it if you create presentations from briefs regularly and want them interactive and web-shareable. Not worth it if you only make slides occasionally or need heavy collaboration features.

What the paid plans actually buy

  • Free: 10 templates, 50MB storage
  • Pro $9.90/month (launch offer): all official templates, more storage
  • Credit packs available for additional generations

Current pricing on the official pricing page.

When HtmlSlides is worth it

  • You create presentations from topic briefs or outlines
  • You want slides that play in any browser without software
  • You need to share or embed decks without requiring sign-in

When it is not worth it

  • You collaborate heavily in real-time — traditional tools have stronger co-editing
  • You only make slides monthly — the free tier may suffice
  • You need advanced animations — interactive HTML has limits compared to dedicated presentation software
