How this stack is chosen

Agencies buy seats, not subscriptions. The evaluation axis is different from solo use: team workspaces, permission controls, brand-voice consistency, output rights you can transfer to clients, and per-seat cost against billable value. Consumer plans usually break here — check commercial-use terms before client delivery.
