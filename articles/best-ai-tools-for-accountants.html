Accounting AI needs verification built in

Never let AI output reach filings, returns, or client advice unchecked. Treat every generated figure, citation, and rule reference as a draft requiring review by a qualified professional.

Best first workflow

Standardize a recurring close workflow: checklist generation, variance commentary drafts, client query letters, meeting summaries, and status reports assembled from templates.

Suggested first workflow

  1. Choose one repeated job that currently takes more than 30 minutes.
  2. Run the same task through two tools from this shortlist.
  3. Measure review time, quality, privacy risk, and total cost.
  4. Document the winning process so it can be repeated.

FAQ

Can AI do my accounting work?

No. AI can draft, summarize, and speed up research, but professional judgment, sign-off, and compliance remain the accountant’s responsibility.

Is client data safe in AI tools?

Review each vendor’s data processing and retention terms, disable training on your data where possible, and avoid pasting identifying client information into consumer plans.
