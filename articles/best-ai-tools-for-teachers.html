How this stack is chosen

Teachers should start on official free education tiers, not a $20 chatbot, unless a general assistant already owns a weekly task. MagicSchool publishes a $0 individual teacher plan and Plus at $12.99/month or $8.33/month billed annually. Khanmigo’s official teacher page states teacher tools are free; learner and family plans are $4/month or $44/year, and classroom student access is a district implementation, not a teacher-account toggle. Vendor FERPA/COPPA marks are claims to verify with your school. Do not paste identifiable student records into a personal chat.
