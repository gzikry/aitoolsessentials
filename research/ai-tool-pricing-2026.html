What the data shows

Freemium is the default. 31 of 40 tools (78%) offer a genuinely usable free tier alongside paid plans. Only 8 tools in our directory are paid-only — and most of those are enterprise or API-first products where a self-serve trial doesn't fit the sales motion.

The free tier is a funnel, not a gift. Across reviews, the pattern repeats: free tiers cap the exact dimension that scales with value — messages (ChatGPT), credits (Make, AllVideoAI), words (Jasper), or minutes (Otter). Our advice across guides holds: start free everywhere, upgrade only when limits break your real workload.

Credit-based pricing is spreading. Newer entrants increasingly sell credits rather than seats — Make's operations model, video generators' per-second costs, image tools' per-render costs. Credits shift risk to the buyer: your bill scales with usage, not headcount. Our Zapier vs Make vs n8n comparison breaks down why this matters for automation specifically.

Pricing changes constantly. Every figure here was checked against official vendor pricing pages and dated; we re-verify monthly. See each tool's "official sources checked" block for its verification date.
