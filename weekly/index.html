    Free-tier picks to try before paying

    AI Agents · Free (97 templates; no paid tier, sponsorship-funded)

    BotLoadout

    A free library of 97 AI agent templates, each published in separate Claude, ChatGPT and Grok versions with the system prompt, the connectors to switch on, a short setup sequence, an example input and output, and stated limits. The full prompt text is public and there is no hosted runtime: the templates are copied into your own account, and the site is funded by newsletter sponsorship rather than subscriptions.

    Best for: Starting a first Claude, ChatGPT or Grok agent from a worked template instead of writing the system prompt, connectors and setup from scratch

    Read review
    AI Agents · $99/month billed monthly (10-day free trial; cancel anytime)

    Catch

    An AI admin assistant that schedules meetings, drafts email, books travel, and places phone calls over Slack, Messages, email, or voice — positioned as action-taking rather than suggest-or-summarize.

    Best for: Busy executives who want an AI admin to schedule meetings, triage email, book travel, and make calls

    Read review
    AI Agents · Free (300 credits/mo) + paid plans from $25/mo; optional live agent seats from $19/seat/mo

    Robylon

    An AI customer support platform whose agents work tickets rather than only answering them: they read the systems an answer lives in, act through tools built from your APIs, and hand anything they cannot finish to a person with the checking already done. It runs inside an existing helpdesk or standalone with its own inbox.

    Best for: Resolving customer support tickets end to end across email, chat, voice and WhatsApp with AI agents that act inside the helpdesk you already run
