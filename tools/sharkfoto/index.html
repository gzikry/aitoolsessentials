    Overview

    SharkFoto is a browser-and-mobile AI media studio that puts image, video, audio and 3D generation on one credit balance, alongside a large layer of genuinely free file utilities. The paid half covers text-to-image and image-to-image editing, background and watermark removal, upscaling, restoration, headshot and avatar generation, text-to-video and image-to-video, lip sync and talking photos, text-to-music and lyrics-to-song, text-to-speech, voice cloning and vocal removal, and image-to-3D - 59 AI tools drawing on 44+ named frontier models (GPT Image 2.6, Seedream 5.5, Midjourney V8.2, Runway Gen-4.5, Luma Ray 3.2, Wan 3.0, MiniMax H3, Happy Horse 1.0, Gemini Omni Flash and others). The free half is 243+ no-sign-up tools - image, video and audio conversion and compression, resize and crop, and embroidery digitizing (DST/PES) - with common image formats processed locally in the browser. It is operated by SharkAI Limited, a Hong Kong company whose registered address, governing law and exclusive jurisdiction are all published, and whose privacy notice names its payment processors, its Cloudflare and AWS hosting, its Google Analytics 4 measurement ID and its Google AdSense publisher ID.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who operates SharkFoto?

    SharkFoto is operated by SharkAI Limited, 'a company incorporated in Hong Kong whose address is Rm B53, 2/F, Kwai Shing Industrial Building Phase 1, 36-40 Tai Lin Pai Road, Kwai Chung, New Territories, Hong Kong'. The Terms of Use are governed by the laws of the Hong Kong SAR and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Hong Kong courts. The same entity name and address appear on the app's Google Play listing (developer: SharkAI Limited). Contact is by email only; no telephone number or street-level registered office is published.

    What does SharkFoto cost?

    The Free plan is $0 with 10 credits. Personal is $10.00/month, or $9.00/month billed annually at $108.00, for 500 credits a month ($0.018/credit). Professional is $30.00/month, or $27.00/month billed annually at $324.00, for 2,000 credits a month ($0.0135/credit). All paid plans list the same features and add no watermark and a commercial license. Cards are billed through Stripe on the web, or through Google Play Billing / the Apple App Store inside the mobile apps.

    Can I get a refund?

    The Refund Policy gives a 7-day window, but only for subscription credits and only if you have used 50 or fewer subscription credits; add-on credit packs are non-refundable, and if you have used certain non-credit paid features (such as embroidery file conversions) after purchase the purchase is not eligible unless the result has a clear quality problem. Approved refunds may carry a processing fee of up to 6% of the initial payment, and purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google under their own policies. Note the /pricing page's '7-day money-back guarantee on all products' is broader than the policy.

    Is my content used to train AI models?

    The Privacy Policy states that SharkFoto does not use your Input or Output to train or fine-tune AI models and that it instructs its model providers to process content solely to generate the requested output and not to train their models, 'to the extent we are able to do so under our agreements'. Your Input and Output are retained until you delete them, which you can do in account settings.

    Does SharkFoto run in the browser?

    Yes. The AI tools and the free file tools run in the browser, and there is also a mobile app (SharkFoto: AI Photo & Video, package com.sharkfoto.app) on Google Play and the App Store. The web free tools process common image formats (JPG, PNG, WebP) locally in the browser; advanced formats are uploaded and deleted shortly after conversion.
