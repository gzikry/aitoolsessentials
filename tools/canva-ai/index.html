Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: The official US annual pricing view lists Canva Free at US$0, Canva Pro at US$180/year for one person, Canva Business at US$250/year per person, and Enterprise through sales. Canva's AI allowance documentation states: Free—up to 200 Standard or 20 Premium uses/month, with no Ultra access; Pro, Teams, Nonprofits, and Education—up to 2,000 Standard, 200 Premium, or 20 Ultra uses/month; Business and Enterprise—up to 4,000 Standard, 400 Premium, or 40 Ultra uses/month. These are alternative estimated ranges against one shared allowance, not additive quotas.

Open verification questions
  • Canva prices are localized and may be personalized by billing region; the summary records the official US annual view observed on the verification date.
  • The official pages do not translate the shared AI allowance into a guaranteed generation count because task complexity changes consumption.
  • Canva does not guarantee that AI output is unique, copyrightable, accurate, or free of third-party rights.
  • Commercial use of output containing Canva library content depends on the separate license for that content.

Canva states that allowance consumption depends on the tool, model, and task complexity; allowances reset monthly. Canva's privacy page says User Content is not used to improve AI-powered features unless consistent with the user's Privacy Settings, which users can review and change. The AI Product Terms say that, as between Canva and the user and to the maximum extent permitted by law, the user owns Output and may use it for any lawful purpose. Output that incorporates or modifies Canva Licensed Content remains subject to the applicable content license. Outputs may not be unique.

Who Canva AI is for

Canva AI is aimed at teams and individuals who need non-designers making social, presentation, and marketing assets. It is designed for creative & marketing workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
