Overview

A credit-based subscription platform that wraps multiple third-party image and video models (Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, Seedream, Seedance, Veo 3.1 Fast/Pro, Wan 2.7, HappyHorse 1.0) behind one workspace with pay-as-you-go or monthly plans.

Key features

  • Credit-based pricing across image and video
  • Multi-model access: Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, Seedream 5.0 Lite, Seedance 1.5 Pro, Veo 3.1 Fast/Pro, Wan 2.7, HappyHorse 1.0
  • Per-task credit cost table (publicly listed, no surprise bills)
  • Real-time affiliate dashboard (30% first-year recurring)
  • API access for programmatic use
  • Built-in unlimited image compression tool

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-28

Current official summary: Official /en/pricing lists Free trial credits, plus monthly Basic $29.9/mo (was $39.9), Pro $49.9/mo (was $69.9), and Ultimate $99.9/mo (was $120) with 800, 1,400, and 2,800 credits per month respectively. Yearly billing is 20–40% off (Basic yearly ~$180; Pro yearly equivalent ~$29.9 × 12 × 0.6 ≈ $215; Ultimate $99.9 × 12 with 40% off ≈ $720, saving $238.8 on the Pro tier). Pay-as-you-go credit packs available. Per the public Credits Cost Table, text-to-image runs 12–46 credits (~$0.05–$0.18) and 5-second video runs 150–380 credits (~$0.60–$1.52). Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, Seedream 5.0 Lite, Seedance 1.5 Pro, and Veo 3.1 Fast run on monthly; Veo 3.1 Pro, Seedance 2.0, and HappyHorse 1.0 require yearly or credit packs.

Frequently asked questions

Is AIBase affiliated with Midjourney, OpenAI, or any model maker?

No — AIBase is an independent platform that routes to third-party models. The brand names appear only to identify which supported model is running.

Do I own what I generate?

Yes, subject to the underlying model/provider terms. AIBase's ToS section 4 says you may use Generated Content for personal or commercial purposes. Outputs are not guaranteed unique.

What's the real cost per task?

Their pricing page is explicit: text-to-image runs ~12–46 credits (~$0.05–$0.18), 5s video runs 150–380 credits (~$0.60–$1.52). At 800 credits/mo Basic, that's ~25 standard images or ~2–5 short videos.

Is there a real free tier?

Yes — free trial credits to test the workspace. Ongoing work costs money; it's not a 'free forever' tool.

Does AIBase have an affiliate program?

Yes — 30% on all subscription payments during the first year, including renewals and upgrades. PayPal payouts NET-15. Sign-up on /affiliate-program.
