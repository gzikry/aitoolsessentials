Frequently asked questions

Is Khanmigo free for teachers?

The official teacher page states Khanmigo is completely free for teachers. Official help documentation describes a Free Teacher Tools subscription for teachers in the United States and other listed locales. Confirm current eligibility in the official signup flow.

How much is Khanmigo for students or families?

The official pricing page lists $4/month or $44/year for learners and for families. Sales tax is not included. District Tools are request-pricing only.

Can a teacher turn Khanmigo on for the whole class?

Official teacher-page FAQ language says teachers cannot grant students Khanmigo access; classroom access is through school or district implementations. Do not treat a free teacher account as a student-tutoring license.
