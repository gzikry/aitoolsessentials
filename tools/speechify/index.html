    Pricing

    Last checked 2026-10-01

    Current official summary: Free: $0, listen at up to 1.5x speed, 10 robotic-sounding voices, text-to-speech features only. Premium: $29/month, includes 1000+ natural voices, 60+ languages, 5x speed, scan and listen, AI summaries and chats, Google Drive/Dropbox/OneDrive integrations, voice typing, AI podcasts, and a voice AI assistant. The official pricing page also offers a yearly billing toggle advertised as 'SAVE 60%'; the captured view showed monthly prices with the note 'Prices shown per month, billed monthly'. No team or enterprise price was exposed on that page.

    Who Speechify is for

    Speechify is aimed at teams and individuals who need text-to-speech reading assistant for documents, web, and learning. It is designed for audio & video workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    How we evaluated

    Job fit 4 - turning documents, web pages, and PDFs into listenable audio serves accessibility and commuting workflows alike. Output quality 4 - natural reader voices with wide format support, though the best voices sit on paid plans. Adoption 5 - install and start listening, with an accessibility-first design. Cost 3 - the free tier's voices are noticeably weaker than paid ones. Scored 4.2 because background-noise handling is limited and the voice gap creates a paywall early.
