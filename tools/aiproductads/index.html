    Overview

    An image-to-video and text-to-video studio for product ads: it renders 3-15 second clips at 480p, 768p or 2K and charges credits per second of output, with a fixed credit cost per resolution. Packs never expire; subscriptions add monthly credits.

    Key features

    • Photo or text brief to 3-15s product video
    • 480p, 768p and 2K output at stated credit costs per second
    • One-time credit packs that never expire
    • Monthly and annual subscriptions
    • 48-hour pro-rata refund on unused credits

    Trial checklist

    Render one 5-second 480p clip from an ordinary catalogue photo and count the credits it actually consumes against the stated 1-per-second rate. Then check the output at 768p on the same source, since resolution is where the cost triples and where product fidelity usually breaks.

    Use cases

    • Making a product ad clip from a catalogue photo
    • Testing several ad angles without a shoot
    • Producing short social spots at scale

    Pros

    • Credit costs are stated per resolution and per second, which makes the real cost of a clip calculable before you buy
    • Packs never expire, and the monthly and annual figures agree with each other and with the pack prices
    • The refund rule is the most specific in this group: unused credits are refundable within 48 hours pro rata, with the formula spelled out as price paid divided by the credits the purchase granted
    • Minimum age is stated as 16

    Cons

    • No registered company, trading name, postal address or named controller appears on any page, and the vendor declined to supply one when asked directly, and no governing-law clause is published
    • Its privacy policy names no analytics at all, while every page loads self-hosted Umami and Microsoft Clarity (project yi8m85ks1u). The operator has acknowledged the gap in writing and it is still not fixed
    • Retention is a phrase rather than a period ('account details stay while the account is in use'), with no deletion timeline
    • Every page carries an always-on 'TODAY'S FLASH SALE' / 'Limited Time Offer' 20% banner with a 30-minute countdown that resets, and all four packs show struck list prices - a discount that is never not available
    • The AI model vendors behind the generation are not named, so it is unclear whose terms cover the output
    • No statement in the policy on whether inputs or outputs are used for training; the operator says no, but it is not published

    Frequently asked questions

    What does AIProductAds cost?

    Starter is $19.90/month and Professional $39.90/month; annual plans are $191.04 and $383.04. One-time packs run from $9.90 for 80 credits to $99.90 for 960 credits and do not expire.

    How are credits charged?

    By resolution and duration: 480p costs 1 credit per second of output, 768p costs 2 and 2K costs 3, with 1 extra credit per input image beyond the first five.

    Can I get a refund?

    Unused credits are refundable within 48 hours on a pro-rata basis, calculated as the price paid divided by the credits the purchase granted.
