Overview

An AI grant-writing workspace with a real free plan. Official pricing lists Starter at $50/month and Pro at $150/month, plus a one-year 50% nonprofit discount for qualifying 501(c)(3) organizations under about $500K operating budget. Docs also describe a Pro+ tier whose public dollar price was not listed on the main pricing page during this check.

Key features

  • Official Free: 5 chat messages/day, file imports up to 10 MB, no per-seat fees
  • Official Starter $50/month and Pro $150/month
  • Official nonprofit discount: $25/month Starter and $75/month Pro for 1 year for 501(c)(3)s under about $500K
  • Official docs: canceling moves the account to Free and keeps workspaces
