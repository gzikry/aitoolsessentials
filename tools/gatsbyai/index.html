    Overview

    A browser video generator that puts four model families behind one prompt box: Seedance 2.0 Mini, Kling, Veo 3.1 and the MiniMax H3/Hailuo routes. You type a scene or upload a first-frame image, set duration, aspect ratio and resolution, see the credit cost for that combination, and generate; an 'AI Optimize' step rewrites a weak prompt and suggests a model. Guests get two generations a day and signed-in accounts get 10 daily credits, then subscription or purchased credits take over, with generation history in the account and MP4 downloads. Failures release the reserved daily credits and return charged credits rather than consuming them.

    Key features

    • Text-to-video and image-to-video (first frame) generation in the browser
    • Seedance 2.0 Mini, Kling, Veo 3.1 and MiniMax H3/Hailuo model routes
    • Per-generation credit cost shown before you submit
    • AI Optimize prompt rewriting and model suggestion
    • Duration, aspect-ratio, resolution and sound controls
    • Two free guest generations a day; 10 daily credits for signed-in accounts
    • Account generation history
    • Failed generations release reserved daily credits and return charged credits
    • One-time credit packs with six-month validity and 50% bonus credits for subscribers

    Trial checklist

    Pros

    • Four separate model families in one workspace - Seedance 2.0 Mini, Kling, Veo 3.1 and MiniMax H3/Hailuo - with the credit cost of the chosen model, duration and resolution shown before you generate, so routes can be compared without opening four accounts
    • The free allowance is real and checkable rather than decorative: the site's own credits endpoint returns two guest generations a day and 10 daily credits for a signed-in account, and the default Seedance Mini run spends 5 of them
    • Prices agree across three surfaces - /pricing, the site's llms.txt and the shipped plan config - and the '50% off yearly' badge is arithmetically exact ($19.98 to $9.99, $39.98 to $19.99, $77.98 to $38.99 a month), which is not the norm in this category
    • Failed generations are handled in the buyer's favour in writing: reserved daily credits are released and charged credits returned, stated on the pricing page instead of buried in a policy
    • The Terms separate the vendor from the model makers explicitly - not 'affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially associated with any underlying third-party AI model provider' - and the pricing FAQ repeats that a purchase here is not a subscription to Seedance, Kling, Veo or MiniMax
    • The acceptable-use section is the substantive kind this product class needs: likeness consent, an absolute CSAM prohibition, deepfake and non-consensual-imagery limits, and a named content-report route with what to include
    • Output rights are stated plainly - paid subscribers may use generations commercially, free generations are personal and non-commercial, and the vendor's rights in the service are not transferred with your video
    • The published contact address works: support@gatsbyai.app accepted mail in an SMTP probe against the domain's Cloudflare Email Routing MX while a random local part was rejected

    Cons

    • No registered company, trading name, postal address, data controller or governing-law clause appears anywhere on the site - the Terms are an agreement 'between you and GatsbyAI', the brand, and the only contact detail in them is the support address
    • The privacy policy names no processor at all: authentication, payments, hosting, video processing, email and analytics appear only as categories, most sentences are hedged with 'may', and no retention period is given for uploaded images, generated videos or account data
    • Google Analytics (G-GDTETMSPS7) loads on every page, including /privacy and /terms, with no consent control and no named analytics provider, while the policy mentions analytics only as one unnamed provider category
    • Which company actually charges the card cannot be confirmed from the site: the policy says only 'third-party payment processors', and the shipped bundle carries Stripe and Creem environment slots alongside a hardcoded Waffo adapter with product IDs, so the live processor needs confirming
    • The domain was registered 2026-10-01, three weeks before it was submitted, so there is no track record for uptime, support response or output consistency
    • The name is already crowded: Gatsby is Netlify's React framework, an unrelated AI life-coaching product used the exact name GatsbyAI at gatsbyai.xyz (that domain no longer resolves), and at least one directory lists a near-identical AI assistant under it. No relationship to any of them is claimed on the site
    • Free-tier copy contradicts the shipped behaviour: the homepage FAQ says you must sign in to get free generations, while the credits endpoint grants two a day to guests
    • Subscription credits reset at each monthly allowance and do not roll over, and one-time pack credits expire six months after purchase
    • Purchases are 'generally non-refundable' and reviewed case by case with no published window, so a video you simply dislike is spent money
    • No affiliate or partner programme is published - /affiliates, /partners and /contact all return 404

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does GatsbyAI cost?

    Lite is $19.98 a month, or $119.88 a year for 2,000 credits a month; Pro $39.98 a month or $239.88 a year for 5,000; Ultra $77.98 a month or $467.88 a year for 10,000. One-time packs are 1,500 credits for $29.90, 3,000 for $49.90, 5,000 for $69.90 and 8,000 for $99.90. The site's llms.txt and its shipped plan config carry the same figures.

    What does one generation cost in credits?

    It depends on the model, duration, resolution and settings, and the generator shows the figure before you submit. The default Seedance 2.0 Mini setup spends 5 credits, so the 10 daily credits cover about two runs, and any available daily credits are applied before paid credits.

    Is there a free tier?

    Yes, two generations a day for guests when capacity allows, and 10 daily credits once you sign in. That is a daily allowance rather than a free plan: subscription credits refresh each month and pack credits last six months, and neither is replaced by the daily amount.

    Can the videos be used commercially?

    Paid subscribers can use their generations for personal or commercial purposes under the Terms; free generations are limited to personal, non-commercial use. You remain responsible for likeness, copyright, trademark and platform rules, and the Terms put that responsibility on the person writing the prompt.

    Who operates GatsbyAI?

    The site does not say. No company, address or jurisdiction is named on any page and the Terms carry no governing-law clause; support@gatsbyai.app is the only published route. The Terms state that the operator is independent of the model providers it resells.
