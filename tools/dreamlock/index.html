    Dreamlock review

    Dreamlock is a private dream journal for iPhone: record a half-asleep voice note, and the app transcribes it, writes a retelling with symbols and interpretation, and paints a dream card. Face ID locks the journal, there is no account to create, and iCloud sync is optional and encrypted. The data-flow disclosure is the standout: the policy names the exact pipeline (a small Vercel relay forwards the transcribed text to Anthropic's Claude for the writing and OpenAI's image model for the card, with only a scene description sent for the painting), states the data is never used for advertising or training, and accurately disclosed the two analytics scripts (cookieless Ahrefs and GA4) that we independently found in the page heads. The App Store page names the individual seller, and the terms set Delaware governing law. What keeps it below the top tier: it is a small new single-platform app, interpretation requires a server round-trip by design, and the seller is a natural person with no company structure.

    Pros

    • The privacy policy names its subprocessor pipeline exactly - Vercel relay, Anthropic Claude for the text, OpenAI image model receiving only a scene description - and states the data is never used for advertising or model training, with the API terms reasoning included
    • The analytics disclosure matches reality: the policy names Ahrefs (cookieless) and Google Analytics 4, and that is exactly what loads in the page head - a rare match between policy and markup

    • iPhone only - there is no Android or web journaling surface beyond the site's free interpreter demo

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Dreamlock send my dreams to a server?

    Yes - and it says so plainly: the transcribed text goes over an encrypted connection to a small Vercel relay, then to Anthropic (writes the card) and OpenAI (paints the image from a short scene description only). The policy states the data is never used for advertising or training, and both vendors' API terms prohibit training on it.

    What does Dreamlock cost?

    Free forever tier: unlimited capture, 3 painted cards a week, 10 questions. Plus is $4.99/month or $39.99/year with a 7-day free trial on annual, billed through Apple - pricing is shown in the app before purchase.

    Do I need an account?

    No. The journal is Face ID-locked on-device, with optional encrypted iCloud sync under your Apple ID.
