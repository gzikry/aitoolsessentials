    Frequently asked questions

    Does BeadPattern cost anything to convert a photo?

    No. Photo-to-pattern conversion is free and does not consume credits. Only AI cartoon generation costs credits, at 10 per successful generation, and failed generations are not charged.

    What does a subscription cost?

    Basic is $59.99/year for 2,400 credits, Pro $99.99/year for 5,040 and Max $199.99/year for 12,000, granted upfront for the full year.

    Are my uploads kept?

    According to the privacy policy, original images are not permanently stored and usage logs are deleted after 90 days.
