Fathom review

An AI meeting assistant for recording calls, generating summaries, and sharing highlights with teams.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

An AI meeting assistant for recording calls, generating summaries, and sharing highlights with teams.

Key features

  • Unlimited free meeting recording + transcription
  • 5-second clip creation for sharing key moments
  • Auto-generated call summaries organized by section
  • Real-time transcription during calls
  • CRM auto-sync on paid plans

Hands-on notes

Fathom's unlimited free tier is the most generous in the category — full recording and summaries at $0 made it our default recommendation. Summary structure (sections by topic with action items) was the cleanest we tested. Paid tiers add sales-team intelligence rather than fixing free-tier gaps.

Pricing

Free$0 — Unlimited recordings, transcripts, AI summaries — rare
Premium$15/mo — AI task extraction, CRM sync, keyword tracking
Team Edition$19/seat/mo — Admin controls, team playlists, Salesforce/HubSpot sync
Team Edition Pro$29/seat/mo — Coaching analytics, deeper CRM integration

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Sales calls
  • Customer interviews
  • Follow-up emails
  • Meeting highlights
  • Reviewing decisions from calls you missed
  • Extracting action items without manual note-taking

Pros

  • Fast meeting summaries which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Good sharing workflow — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Useful for calls with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Consent and privacy policies matter so factor that into your monthly budget
  • Integrations should be checked which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Fathom is for

Fathom fits teams and individuals who need meeting notes, call summaries, and crm-friendly follow-up workflows. It is strongest in meetings work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Fathom scores 4.3/5 in our evaluation. Fast meeting summaries which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: consent and privacy policies matter so factor that into your monthly budget.
