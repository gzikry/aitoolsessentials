Fathom review

An AI meeting assistant for recording calls, generating summaries, and sharing highlights with teams.

Editorial review · Updated 2026-08-24 · Hands-on result not yet published

Overview

An AI meeting assistant for recording calls, generating summaries, and sharing highlights with teams.

Key features

  • Unlimited free meeting recording + transcription
  • 5-second clip creation for sharing key moments
  • Auto-generated call summaries organized by section
  • Real-time transcription during calls
  • CRM auto-sync on paid plans

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving sales calls—not a demo prompt. Verify whether unlimited free meeting recording + transcription materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to consent and privacy policies matter so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: free: $0; unlimited recordings, transcription, and storage; instant AI summaries; clips, playlists, and search. premium: $20/user/month monthly or $16/user/month billed annually; advanced summaries, AI-generated action items, conversational meeting assistant, and custom meeting bot. team: $19/user/month monthly or $15/user/month billed annually, two-user minimum; adds shared search, playlists, folders, comments, keyword alerts, and collaboration features. business: $34/user/month monthly or $25/user/month billed annually, two-user minimum; adds CRM field sync, Deal View, coaching metrics, AI scorecards, and advanced/custom summaries. enterprise: Custom quote; adds organization-wide controls, custom retention, onboarding, and enterprise security options.

Open verification questions
  • The Free plan's unlimited recording, transcription, and storage do not establish unlimited use of every advanced AI, team-search, CRM, API, or automation feature.
  • Exact global API rate limits and plan-specific API entitlements were not stated on the public pricing page.

The repository's Free unlimited-recording/transcription claim remains supported. The repository's plan names and prices are outdated: current individual Premium is $20 monthly/$16 annual; current team tiers are Team and Business rather than Team Edition and Team Edition Pro. The official pricing HTML was published on August 20, 2026, and the privacy policy was last updated August 16, 2026. Fathom may use de-identified data derived from meeting content to train, customize, or improve its in-house models, depending on account settings; users can opt out. Fathom says it does not authorize OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or other third parties to use personal information or meeting content to train their models. Personal-account users retain applicable copyright and proprietary rights in their content. Content in a company Team Account belongs to the company. Fathom uses content as needed to provide the service and through listed subprocessors. The terms require the user to obtain every meeting participant's required consent before each call begins. Official developer documentation at https://developers.fathom.ai/ confirms a public API, OAuth apps, webhooks, and TypeScript/Python SDKs.

Who Fathom is for

Fathom is aimed at teams and individuals who need meeting notes, call summaries, and crm-friendly follow-up workflows. It is designed for meetings workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fathom really free?

Yes — unlimited recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries at no cost. Paid adds team/sales features.

Fathom vs Otter?

Fathom emphasizes a generous free recording-and-summary workflow; Otter emphasizes live transcription and collaborative notes. Try both with the same meeting format.

Which platforms does it support?

Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and RingCentral.
