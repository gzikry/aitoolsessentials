Overview

An AI meeting assistant for recording calls, generating summaries, and sharing highlights with teams.

Key features

  • Unlimited free meeting recording + transcription
  • 5-second clip creation for sharing key moments
  • Auto-generated call summaries organized by section
  • Real-time transcription during calls
  • CRM auto-sync on paid plans

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving sales calls—not a demo prompt. Verify whether unlimited free meeting recording + transcription materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to consent and privacy policies matter so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Who Fathom is for

Fathom fits teams and individuals who need meeting notes, call summaries, and crm-friendly follow-up workflows. It is strongest in meetings work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fathom really free?

Yes — unlimited recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries at no cost. Paid adds team/sales features.

Fathom vs Otter?

Fathom emphasizes a generous free recording-and-summary workflow; Otter emphasizes live transcription and collaborative notes. Try both with the same meeting format.

Which platforms does it support?

Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and RingCentral.
