    Overview

    Change Text in Image does one narrow job and does it in a browser: you upload a JPG, PNG or static WebP up to 10MB / 20MP, drag a box over the words you want to replace, type the original text and the replacement, and the model redraws the wording in the font, colour and style it detected while rebuilding the background behind the old letters. Leaving the replacement empty removes the text instead. There are no font, size or colour controls, by design - the promise is an edit that looks like it was always there - and the neighbouring pages cover the same workflow for screenshots and translations, plus a blur tool that runs entirely on your device. Pricing is one-time credit packs only: $14.90 for 40 edits, $26.90 for 100 and $39.90 for 180, with 10 free credits at sign-up. The paperwork is unusually straight for this class - the operators are named as an individual with a working contact, Cloudflare, fal.ai's Nano Banana and Waffo are named by company and function, prompt screening before generation is disclosed, and the policy refuses to claim zero retention or supplier-wide no-training. What holds it back is that it says tracking is not active while Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and a third-party visitor counter all load on the policy page itself, and that no company, address or jurisdiction appears anywhere.

    Key features

    • Box-select text editing: drag around the words, type the original and the replacement, and only matching text inside the box changes
    • Font, weight, colour and perspective matching, with the background behind the removed characters rebuilt automatically
    • Remove mode - leave the replacement field empty and the AI erases the wording and fills the background back in
    • Screenshot workflow with the surrounding interface left pixel-identical, plus dedicated pages for edit-text-in-photo, remove-text, translate-text and blur-text
    • Translation with your own supplied wording, keeping the original design, with Latin, Cyrillic, Greek and CJK scripts supported
    • Free blur tool that runs in the browser with brush size and strength controls, undo, and a PNG download - no upload, no account
    • Upload limits stated up front: JPG, PNG or static WebP, up to 10MB / 20MP; edits download as PNG
    • Credit meter of 10 credits per generation with per-edit prices on each pack and automatic credit return on failed generations
    • Sign-in with Google (One Tap supported) or email; no account needed to upload and select text, only to generate
    • Pre-generation content-safety screening through Waffo, disclosed in the privacy policy with the statement that images are not sent to that endpoint

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Change Text in Image?

    A browser editor for the words inside a finished picture rather than a text layer on top of it. You upload a JPG, PNG or static WebP up to 10MB / 20MP, draw a box around the text, enter the original wording and the replacement, and the model redraws it in the font, size, colour and style it detected while repairing the background behind the old letters. Leave the replacement empty to remove the text instead. There are no font, size or colour controls - the product's claim is that the edit should look like it was always there - and every generation costs 10 credits.

    How much does Change Text in Image cost?

    One-time credit packs, no subscription: Starter $14.90 for 400 credits (40 edits, $0.37 each), Pro $26.90 for 1,000 credits (100 edits, $0.27 each) and Studio $39.90 for 1,800 credits (180 edits, $0.22 each). Each generation is 10 credits, failed generations return their credits, and purchased credits do not expire while the account remains available. Checkout is hosted by Waffo Pancake. Refunds are a 7-day clause inside the Terms of Service: full refund only if none of that purchase's credits have been used.

    Is there a free tier, and do I need an account?

    New accounts get 10 free credits, which is exactly one generation - enough to test one real file, not enough to judge a font library. You can upload an image and select text without signing in, but generating requires an account (Google or email). The separate blur tool is free, needs no account and runs on your device: our check found no image upload when using it.

    What does it not do?

    It will not change the font, size or colour - only the wording - and it does not guarantee copyright, uniqueness or freedom from infringement in the output. It refuses in its own acceptable-use policy: altered IDs, passports, visas, supporting letters or official seals, falsified bank statements, receipts or payment confirmations, edited chat screenshots, and edited images submitted as authentic evidence. It is also an individual-run service with no company, postal address or governing law published, and no password-reset path.
