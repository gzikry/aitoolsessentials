    Overview

    MiniMax H3 Image is a browser studio that puts a whole model catalogue behind one credit balance. The image tool leads with MiniMax H3 Image and MiniMax H3 Image Highspeed and continues through GPT Image, Nano Banana, Seedream, Qwen Image, FLUX, Ideogram, Recraft and Z-Image for stills, while the video side carries Seedance, Kling, Veo, Wan, Grok Imagine, Gemini Omni and MiniMax H3 / H3 Max / H3 Max Turbo, plus music and audio models, an agent chat and an infinite canvas. You write a prompt or upload a reference, choose a model, resolution and ratio, read the credit estimate, and generate in the browser with nothing to install. Access is a monthly or yearly subscription on three tiers or a one-time credit pack valid 365 days, with API access and an MCP route sold alongside. Credits are metered per generation, shown before you submit, and returned automatically when a task fails.

    Key features

    • One credit balance across roughly 55 models in the platform catalogue - around 19 image, 20 video, 12 chat and 4 audio entries served from a public model endpoint
    • Image side: MiniMax H3 Image and H3 Image Highspeed, GPT Image 2.5/2.6 Flare and Sunburst, Nano Banana 2 / 2.1 / Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro and Flash and 5.5, Qwen Image 3.0, Grok Imagine Image 2.0, FLUX.2 Pro, Ideogram 4.5, Recraft V4 and V4 Pro, Z-Image Turbo
    • Video side: MiniMax H3, H3 Max and H3 Max Turbo, Seedance 2.5 / 2.5 Flash / 2.0 / 2.0 Fast / 2.0 Mini, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0 Turbo, Wan 3.0 and 2.7, Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and Lite, Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 and 1.2, HappyHorse 1.1, FLUX 3 Video, LTX-2.5 Fast and Pro
    • Text-to-image, image-to-image with up to 16 references, image editing, text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video, music generation and audio tools
    • Credit cost shown for the chosen model, resolution and output count before submitting, with failed tasks refunded
    • An agent chat and an infinite canvas for chaining image, video and audio steps in one session
    • Image generation API with keys managed in account settings, plus an MCP route for tool-driven workflows
    • A public community showcase whose prompts can be reused, and 41+ single-effect pages (product, UGC, cinematic, character, trend categories)
    • Monthly subscriptions on three tiers or one-time credit packs valid 365 days, paid by card through Stripe

    Frequently asked questions

    What is MiniMax H3 Image?

    A browser studio that generates images and video from prompts or reference files using a catalogue of current third-party models on one credit balance. It is named after MiniMax's H3-Image model, which its image tool offers first, but it is a multi-model workspace rather than a single-model generator: stills can also run on GPT Image, Nano Banana, Seedream, Qwen Image, FLUX, Ideogram, Recraft or Z-Image, and video on Seedance, Kling, Veo, Wan, Grok Imagine, Gemini Omni or MiniMax H3 and its Max variants. Nothing is installed locally.

    How much does it cost?

    Monthly billing is Lite $12.99 for 3,000 credits, Pro $34.99 for 10,000 and Ultra $129.99 for 30,000 a month. Billed yearly the same tiers are $9.99, $24.99 and $79.99 a month, charged as $119.88, $299.88 and $959.88 a year, with the credits released monthly. One-time packs that stay valid 365 days cost $29.99 for 5,000 credits up to $899.99 for 250,000. Every model draws on the same balance and the cost of a generation is shown before you submit it.

    Does MiniMax H3 generate the images?

    On this site the image tool offers MiniMax H3 Image as its first listed model, costing 11 credits for a 1:1 image at 2K in the tool's default state, alongside an H3 Image Highspeed variant. Note that the site's own homepage and llms.txt still say H3 is used only as a video model here and that stills come from another model - that copy is out of date against the tool itself. The video models named MiniMax H3 are separate entries from the H3 Image ones.

    What happens if a generation fails?

    Credits are deducted when a task is submitted and returned automatically if it fails, including failures caused by content safety review or copyright restrictions. The Terms and the pricing FAQ say the same thing, and the credit history in the account shows the status of each task.

    Do credits expire?

    Subscription credits expire at the next monthly refresh and do not roll over; one-time pack credits stay valid for 365 days. When credits with different expiry dates are on the account the soonest to expire are used first, and a later purchase does not extend earlier credits.

    Who operates the site?

    No company, trading name, postal address or governing-law clause is published on any page; the only identity given is the brand name and a support@ address. The Terms are governed by no named jurisdiction. That is worth knowing before you pay, because it is the operator a buyer would have to pursue rather than a registered entity.
