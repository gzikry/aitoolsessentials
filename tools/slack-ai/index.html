Overview

AI features inside Slack for summarizing channels, catching up on threads, and finding workplace context.

Key features

  • Channel and thread summaries catch you up instantly
  • Huddle transcriptions with action items
  • Search answers questions using your Slack history with citations
  • Daily recap of what you missed
  • Stays within Slack's existing security model

Hands-on notes

Channel summaries are the feature that earns the fee — returning from a week away, recaps replaced hours of scrolling in testing. Search-with-citations answered 'what did we decide about X?' reliably when the discussion lived in Slack. Weakness: only knows what's in Slack.

Who Slack AI is for

Slack AI fits teams and individuals who need team conversation summaries, channel catch-up, and workplace knowledge retrieval. It is strongest in productivity work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Slack AI scores 4.1/5 in our evaluation. Works in team chat which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: value depends on slack adoption so factor that into your monthly budget.
