    Overview

    RapAI is a browser studio that turns one photo of two people, or two separate portraits, into a short AI rap performance. You supply the photos and a background, then choose one of three rap modes - a surprise verse, a theme you describe, or your own lyrics - and pick a ratio, a duration of 4 to 15 seconds and 480p, 720p or 1080p. The site writes the bars when you do not, animates both performers to the generated vocals, and lists finished clips in your studio for download. It sells one-time credit packs at $6.90, $14.90 and $29.90 rather than a subscription, and it is unusual in this category for how specifically it writes down its own limits: photos are deleted from Cloudflare R2 seven days after upload with the period stated on the page rather than buried, SeeAPI is named as the generation provider, and a dedicated AI Technology Disclosure says the underlying model vendor and version 'have not been disclosed in the template documentation available to us' and that it 'cannot currently substantiate' any named foundation model. Commercial-use licensing is explicitly not offered, which is a real limitation for client work, and the site publishes no example video output at all - only still images - so a buyer cannot see what the result looks like before paying.

    Key features

    • One photo of two people, or two separate portraits, into a 4 to 15 second rap performance with generated vocals
    • Three rap modes: a surprise verse written for you, a theme you describe, or your own lyrics pasted in
    • Ratio chosen before generation - 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 and more - so there is no crop afterwards
    • Resolution tiers at 480p, 720p and 1080p with the credit cost of each shown before you generate
    • Four built-in scenes (harbor promenade, recording studio, concert stage, holiday living room) or upload your own background
    • Cost quoted on the button before submission (130 credits for a 5-second 480p render) and failed runs refunded to credits
    • Photos deleted from Cloudflare R2 seven days after upload, with deletion on request inside that window
    • One-time credit packs at $6.90 / $14.90 / $29.90, credits valid 365 days, no subscription and no auto-renewal
    • Rap Battle Generator page and a two-performer workflow where both characters lip-sync to verses written to answer each other
    • Google sign-in with one-tap; finished clips listed in a Recent videos view in the studio

    Pros

    • The product demonstrably runs: /api/rap-video, /api/rap-video/credits and /api/user/credits each return the site's own JSON auth error ({"code":-1,"message":"Unauthorized"}) to an unauthenticated GET rather than a 404, and /api/config/public returns app_name 'RapAI' with rap_video_enabled:'true', google_auth_enabled:'true' and waffo_enabled:'true' - so a generation route, an account route and a live payment provider are all wired, not stubbed
    • The credit arithmetic reconciles against itself, which is rare in this category: /pricing states 390 credits for a 15-second 480p render, 825 for 720p and 2,055 for 1080p, and the studio's own default 5-second 480p quote is 130 - exactly one third of 390, so cost is linear in duration rather than an estimate the two pages disagree about. The pack cards' 'about 1 / about 2 / about 5 videos' understate rather than overstate what 390, 1,000 and 2,200 credits buy
    • The operator is named on the pages that matter: 'Airapvideo.design (RapAI) is operated by Tom Lai, who is responsible for the processing of your personal data' appears in both the Privacy Policy and the Terms, which is more than most submissions in this catalogue manage

    • Processors are named by company and by job: SeeAPI for generation (with the exact template identifier 'rap-video' and the endpoint POST /v1/templates/rap-video/generations), Cloudflare R2 for storage, Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps and session replays - and the policy warns that images are handed to SeeAPI through accessible URLs so anyone holding one could read it
    • A dedicated AI Technology Disclosure states plainly that the underlying model vendor and version are undisclosed and that the site cannot substantiate Veo, Kling, Seedance or any other named model - an honest negative instead of a borrowed brand name

    Frequently asked questions

    What does RapAI cost?

    Three one-time credit packs: Lite $6.90 for 390 credits, Pro $14.90 for 1,000 and Ultra $29.90 for 2,200, with credits valid 365 days. A 15-second clip costs 390 credits at 480p, 825 at 720p and 2,055 at 1080p, and a 5-second 480p clip costs 130 - so the smallest pack buys exactly one 15-second 480p video, or three 5-second ones. Nothing renews automatically and there is no subscription.

    Is there a free trial?

    No. The FAQ says so directly - 'RapAI doesn't have a free plan right now' - and the cheapest entry is the $6.90 Lite pack. Credit cost is shown before you generate and failed runs return their credits, but there is no free render to judge quality with.

    Can I see an example video before paying?

    Not on the site. Every page we fetched serves still images only - there are no video elements and no .mp4 or .webm files referenced anywhere - so the output has to be judged from your own first paid render. Treat the $6.90 pack as the sample.

    Can I use the videos commercially?

    No, not currently. The Terms, the AI Technology Disclosure and the FAQ all state that commercial output licensing is not offered. You keep whatever rights you already hold in your photos and lyrics, and the site says to check the Terms before any commercial use - which for client work or advertising means waiting for a licence that does not exist yet.

    Which AI model makes the video?

    The site will not claim one. It generates through SeeAPI's 'rap-video' template and its own AI Technology Disclosure says the underlying model vendor and version 'have not been disclosed in the template documentation available to us' and that it cannot substantiate Veo, Kling, Seedance or any other named model. That is an honest answer rather than a borrowed brand name, but it also means quality cannot be predicted from the model.

    How long are my photos kept?

    Uploaded photos are stored in Cloudflare R2 and deleted automatically 7 days after upload, with Cloudflare usually completing removal about a day later; re-uploading the same image in that window reuses the copy without extending the clock. You can email for earlier deletion. Finished videos are held through SeeAPI's links, whose retention is set by SeeAPI rather than by RapAI.

    What do I need to supply?

    One clear, front-facing photo of both performers (or two portraits, one per person), plus optionally a background. JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 10 MB each, and you need permission from everyone in the photo - the terms make that your responsibility.

    Who operates RapAI?

    Tom Lai, named as the operator in both the Terms and the Privacy Policy. No company, postal address or governing-law clause is published, and the only contact route is support@airapvideo.design.
