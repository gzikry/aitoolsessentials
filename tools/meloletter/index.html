    Overview

    MeloLetter turns a written story - names, memories, the things you meant to say - into a personalised song gift. You answer guided prompts, get an AI lyric draft you can edit before paying, hear a free 30-second preview of both generated versions, and then buy a song pack to unlock full playback, MP3 downloads and a public shareable gift page. Songs come from Suno v5.5 through the YourMusic API and lyrics from GPT-5.5 through Brioi, both named with model identifiers in the terms. It sells one pack at $19.90 or three a month at $9.90. What stands out for a four-month-old site is documentation: the privacy policy names its analytics stack with retention periods, the terms carry a usable acceptable-use policy with a moderation and appeals process, and the examples page labels its demos 'illustrative' instead of dressing them as customer stories. What is missing is who runs it - no company, individual, postal address or governing law - and there is no refund page, only that clause.

    Key features

    • Guided story prompts, then an editable AI lyric draft that costs nothing and can be rewritten before purchase
    • Free 30-second audio preview of both generated versions, with no card required for the preview step
    • Two complete song versions per pack plus one included regenerate, so a buyer can pick between arrangements
    • Songs generated by Suno v5.5 (chirp-v5.5) through YourMusic; lyrics by GPT-5.5 through Brioi
    • MP3 downloads saved to the account, with account history and a billing log
    • A shareable gift page that can carry the message, a cover image, the selected song and the lyrics
    • One-time $19.90 pack beside a $9.90/month plan for three packs, so repeat gifting is a separate choice
    • English, French and Chinese site trees with hreflang annotations and localized journal guides
    • Occasion-specific landing guides for birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, weddings, Mother's Day and long-distance gifts
    • A public acceptable-use policy covering voice cloning, deepfakes, copyright and child safety, with a reporting address and an appeal route

    Use cases

    • A birthday or anniversary gift song built from names, specific memories and inside details, delivered as a link the recipient can open
    • A proposal or wedding song where the lyric draft is edited before any money is spent, so the wording is right first
    • A thank-you song for a parent or friend, using the free 30-second preview of both versions to pick which arrangement to pay for
    • A small batch of gifts in one month - three recipients under the $9.90 monthly plan instead of three separate $19.90 purchases
    • A songwriter sketching melody and vocal direction from a written brief, with two renders per brief to compare

    Pros

    • Preview before payment is the real design decision: lyrics are generated and editable for free, and both versions can be heard for 30 seconds before a pack is bought - there is no card required for that step and no render is bought sight-unseen
    • The generation models are named with identifiers rather than adjectives - 'Suno v5.5 (model identifier chirp-v5.5) through the YourMusic API' for songs and 'OpenAI GPT-5.5 (model identifier gpt-5.5)' for lyrics, both in the Terms and restated in the Privacy Policy
    • The privacy policy describes the analytics stack instead of gesturing at it: GA4 for visits, ecommerce events and Google Ads attribution, Mixpanel for funnel and server-confirmed payment events in its US data region with IP-derived city/region only, Microsoft Clarity for masked session review, analytics retained no longer than 14 months and Clarity recordings 30 days
    • It goes further than required on a niche point that most sites in this category get wrong: the OpenAI ad-click reference is described as a first-party cookie held up to 30 days, with the exact fields sent on purchase listed, and Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track honored for new checkouts
    • There is a real acceptable-use policy rather than a link to someone else's: it names deepfakes and voice cloning, copyright, child safety and fraud as categories, describes a report-and-review process with priority for child-safety and credible-threat reports, publishes support@meloletter.com as the reporting channel, and states an appeal route with a reconsideration commitment
    • The editorial pages police themselves - the About page states that 'MeloLetter Editorial' is an organizational byline and 'does not represent a named expert, certification, or independent reviewer', and both /about and /llms.txt say the song examples are illustrative and 'not customer testimonials'
    • The share page is described honestly in all three places a buyer meets it - pricing, privacy and About all say published gift pages are public to anyone with the link and are 'not recipient-only or password-protected'
    • The deliverable is stated precisely and consistently: two generated versions per pack, one included regenerate, MP3 downloads, account history, and a gift page that can show message, cover image, song and lyrics
    • Both a one-time $19.90 purchase and a $9.90/month plan exist, so a reader who wants one gift is not pushed onto a subscription, and the monthly plan's three packs are itemised rather than described as 'unlimited'
    • The product runs: /api/checkout and /api/generate answer HTTP 405 to a GET rather than 404, meaning the POST-only routes exist behind them, and the site serves English, French and Chinese page trees from the same build

    Cons

    • No operator anywhere: neither the Privacy Policy nor the Terms names a company, a person, a jurisdiction, a governing-law clause, a venue or a postal address - the only identity is support@meloletter.com, so a buyer with a dispute has no named counterparty
    • No refund policy page: /refund and /refund-policy both 404, and the only refund statement is one Terms sentence - 'Except where required by law, payments are not automatically refundable after generation work has started or a paid credit has been used' - with no cooling-off window, no failed-generation remedy and no partial-refund route published
    • The site loads a full measurement stack and there is no consent gate: Google Tag Manager with GA4 G-1SHKEZYBXF and Google Ads attribution in the served HTML, and a headless session on /pricing fired api.mixpanel.com/track and Microsoft Clarity project xj2y068b60 as well - against zero 'consent' or cookie-banner tokens anywhere in the page, so European visitors are measured before they choose, whatever the policy's rights paragraph offers afterwards
    • The monthly plan's mechanics are delegated rather than described: the Terms say only that 'subscription renewals, cancellations, taxes, and payment processing may be handled by our payment provider', so there is no published cancellation path, no statement of when the renewal is charged, and no price-change notice commitment
    • Gift pages are public by link with no recipient-only or password option, and the privacy policy confirms a page may show recipient name, sender name, message, cover image, audio and a lyrics snapshot - disclosed, but for a gift built out of private memories that is the wrong default for many buyers
    • The free preview is rationed with unpublished numbers - 'subject to rolling 24-hour limits and daily capacity' - so someone drafting songs for several people in one sitting can hit a wall with no stated reset time and no way to see the allowance
    • One of a network rather than a single product: the footer cross-links seven sibling sites (stitchfromphoto.com, launchai.tools, easyvideoai.com, bulin47ai.net, dimedimebaby-aivideo.com, clueffygame.com, tinydeskaivideo.net), all seven resolve on the same Cloudflare nameserver pair aspen/everton, and stitchfromphoto.com links back to meloletter.com in its own footer - so this is one operator running a portfolio of single-purpose consumer sites, with no indication on the listing which entity is behind them
    • Four months of history: the domain was registered 2026-07-07 and there is no independent review or press coverage yet, only directory listings that repeat the vendor's own description
    • There is no free trial of the paid product - the free path stops at 30 seconds per version, and an included regenerate is the only re-render in a pack, with the pricing page saying further regenerates 'should consume another paid pack'
    • The song model is not pinned contractually: the Terms say MeloLetter may 'update pricing, plans, generation providers, or feature availability' at any time, so a later pack may not be rendered by the Suno v5.5 that the current documentation describes
    • Credits and packs have no cash value per the Terms, which combined with the missing refund page leaves an unused pack with no stated remedy

    Who MeloLetter is for

    MeloLetter is aimed at teams and individuals who need people buying a one-off keepsake gift - a birthday, an anniversary, a proposal, a thank-you to a parent or friend - who want to hear and read the thing before they pay for it, and who would rather spend $19.90 once than hold a music-generation subscription they will use twice a year. It is designed for audio & video workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does MeloLetter cost?

    Two published prices: $19.90 for one song pack as a one-time purchase, or $9.90 per month for three song packs a month that renews monthly. A pack includes two generated versions, one included regenerate, MP3 downloads and a shareable gift page. Lyrics and a 30-second preview of both versions are free before you pay. Both figures appear on /pricing and on the Chinese pricing page.

    Can I hear the song before I pay?

    Partly - you can hear 30 seconds of each of the two versions for free, and the lyric draft is free and editable. Full playback and the MP3 downloads unlock with a paid pack. That is enough to judge whether the song feels personal, but not enough to judge the whole arrangement, so treat the preview as a fit check rather than a full audition.

    Is there a free trial or a free plan?

    No trial of the paid product. The free path is the lyric draft plus the 30-second previews, and it is 'subject to rolling 24-hour limits and daily capacity' with no published allowance. If you need to compare several songs in one session, expect the preview budget to be the constraint.

    Who runs MeloLetter, and where is it based?

    Not stated. Neither the Privacy Policy (last updated September 23, 2026) nor the Terms (September 21, 2026) names a company, an individual, a country or a governing law, and there is no postal address. Support runs through support@meloletter.com, which is also the contact for data requests and content-safety reports.

    Can I get a refund?

    There is no refund policy page - /refund 404s - and the Terms say only that payments 'are not automatically refundable after generation work has started or a paid credit has been used'. That leaves a narrow window in principle (a pack bought and not yet used) but no published process, deadline or address for asking, so treat a purchase as final.

    Are the songs on the site real customer examples?

    No, and the site says so. The examples page and llms.txt label every demo 'illustrative' and state they 'are not customer testimonials', and the About page notes its editorial byline is organizational rather than a named expert. That is unusually candid for this category - but it also means there is no independent sample of what a paid song sounds like.

    Which AI models does it use?

    Songs: Suno v5.5, model identifier chirp-v5.5, accessed through the YourMusic API. Lyrics: OpenAI GPT-5.5, model identifier gpt-5.5. Both are named in the Terms and referenced in the privacy policy, which also discloses that generation inputs go to those third parties. The Terms reserve the right to change generation providers.

    Who can see a gift page once I send it?

    Anyone with the link. The site states in pricing, privacy and About that published gift pages are public and are 'not recipient-only or password-protected', and a page may show the recipient name, sender name, message, cover image, audio and a lyrics snapshot. Keep that in mind before writing in private detail.
