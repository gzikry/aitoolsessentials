    Overview

    PoseLoop is an image-to-video studio built around short-lived viral trends rather than a general clip generator. Trends get their own page - a rap duet in a green booth, a lip-synced music video, a cashier meme, a car flipping off a ramp - and each states the photos it needs, its length, its resolution and its credit price before you start. Behind the button a trend runs as a short chain of AI steps: some place you in the scene as a still, some read your photo so the scene fits, and a video model then renders the motion; where a trend lives on its sound the original track is laid under the finished clip. Output is vertical 9:16 MP4 framed for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, with a 66-effect library, video and image prompt galleries and its own prompt-to-image generation alongside. Accounts sign in with Google or email and password, and checkout is Stripe. It is operated by ZheLogic, LLC, a Delaware company, and - after a first pass in which none of that was published - its legal set now names the operator and the governing law.

    Key features

    • Trend pages that each state the photos needed, the clip length, the resolution and the credit price before a render starts - 9 trends live in November 2026 (including Tiny Desk AI, Chat GTT AI, Popstar AI, Bulin 47 AI, Burning Bridges AI and Tesla Roadster AI)
    • A 66-effect library plus video and image prompt galleries, so older formats and prompt-driven generation sit alongside the current trends
    • Multi-step render chains: some trends place you in the scene as a still or read your photo so the scene fits before a video model renders the motion, and the trend's original sound is laid under the clip where the format carries one
    • Credits are quoted on the Generate button before you confirm, and the credits reserved for a failed render return to the balance automatically
    • One-time credit packs with no expiry alongside monthly and yearly subscriptions, on one credit balance
    • 15 free credits on signup, valid 90 days, with a watermark on images made from free credits and clean downloads on paid credits
    • Vertical 9:16 MP4 output framed for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts; finished clips download and stay in My creations, where they can be deleted at any time
    • Sign-in with Google or an email and password; Stripe checkout; AI image generation from prompts as well as video
    • A legal set that names the operator (ZheLogic, LLC, Delaware, US) and the governing law (Delaware), with retention periods, a no-training statement on uploads and a stated 7-day refund window on packs and first subscription charges

    Frequently asked questions

    Who operates PoseLoop?

    poseloop.app is operated by ZheLogic, LLC, a limited liability company established in the State of Delaware, United States, which the Privacy Policy also names as the operator and data controller. That is stated on the About, Terms, Privacy and Refund pages, and the Terms identify the courts of Delaware as the forum for disputes. No street or registered-office address is published, and support@poseloop.app is the only contact channel given.

    What does PoseLoop cost?

    One-time credit packs are $9.99 for 200 credits, $24.99 for 600 and $59.99 for 1,600 (credits never expire). Monthly plans are Basic $19.90 for 500 credits a month, Pro $49.90 for 1,500 and Ultra $99.90 for 3,000. Billed yearly the same plans cost $15.90, $29.90 and $59.90 per month at $190.80, $358.80 and $718.80 a year - exactly twelve monthly payments, with Basic 20% off and Pro and Ultra 40% off. New accounts start with 15 free credits valid 90 days.

    Can I get a refund?

    Within 7 days of buying a credit pack or the first charge of a subscription, if the credits are substantially unspent, write to support@poseloop.app; approved payments take 5-10 business days to return via the original route. Subscription renewals are not refunded - cancelling before the next billing date stops the next charge - and statutory consumer rights are preserved. A render that fails returns its credits automatically without a request.

    Does PoseLoop train on my photos?

    No, not for its own models: the Privacy Policy states that PoseLoop does not feed uploads or finished clips into training for models of its own. The third-party providers that carry out a render operate under their own published terms and privacy notices, which the policy says it does not control. Uploads are erased within 30 days of a verified request, with up to 90 days of backups.

    Is PoseLoop affiliated with the AI models it uses?

    No. The Terms say PoseLoop is an independent product, names the engines it runs on (Seedance, GPT Image, Gemini Flash, Veo and Kling among them), and state that no affiliation with, sponsorship by or endorsement from any model provider should be inferred unless stated in writing.

    How much does one clip cost in credits?

    It depends on the trend or effect, the model, the clip length and the resolution you choose, and the figure is shown on the Generate button before you confirm anything - the pricing page does not publish a per-clip credit table. Failed renders return their credits.
