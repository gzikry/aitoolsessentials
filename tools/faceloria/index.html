The pitch leans on a 0-100 'attractiveness score' alongside the geometry, and the site itself frames results as informational.     Overview

    Faceloria runs an AI face analysis in the browser: you upload a front-facing photo and get a face-shape category, symmetry and proportion measurements, plus an optional one-time Full Report with a styling action plan. The pitch leans on a 0-100 'attractiveness score' alongside the geometry, and the site itself frames results as informational. The operation is unusually transparent about the data path for a face tool: payments are handled by Waffo, source photos sit in Cloudflare R2 and are automatically deleted within 48 hours, and a complete legal set (privacy, terms, cookie and acceptable-use pages) is in place with a recent update date. The weak spots are identity and analytics: no company, controller or governing law is named anywhere, and the pages load a Plausible-compatible analytics script from pvuv.ai while the privacy policy says only that analytics 'run only when enabled by deployment configuration'.

    Key features

    • Free instant face-shape analysis from a single front-facing photo
    • Facial measurements including width-to-height ratio and vertical balance
    • One-time $16.99 Full Face Report with a 4-week styling action plan and PDF export
    • Automatic deletion of uploaded source photos within 48 hours
    • No account required for the free analysis; Google Sign-In for account features

    Pros

    • Source-photo handling is verifiable and specific: uploads are stored in Cloudflare R2 and automatically deleted within 48 hours, stated plainly in the privacy policy
    • The payment processor (Waffo), storage (Cloudflare R2) and auth provider (Google Sign-In) are all named, which most small face-analysis sites never do
    • Genuinely one-time pricing - the Full Report is $16.99 with an explicit 'no subscription' statement, so nothing renews against a card later
    • A complete legal set exists (privacy, terms, cookie policy, acceptable use), with the privacy policy updated 2026-10-26 - days before our check

    Cons

    • No controller, company or jurisdiction is named anywhere: the footer reads only '(c) 2026 Faceloria', the privacy policy names no legal entity, and the terms never state a governing law - a reader has no one to hold accountable
    • Analytics under-disclosed: every page (including the privacy and terms pages) loads a Plausible-compatible tracker from www.pvuv.ai plus plausible.io, while the policy only mentions analytics 'when enabled by deployment configuration' and names neither
    • The headline framing is a 0-100 attractiveness score, which may not suit everyone; the site itself qualifies results as informational rather than objective judgments
    • Refund terms are not published (no refund-policy page), which matters less on a one-time $16.99 purchase but is still a gap

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Faceloria keep my uploaded photos?

    The privacy policy says source photos uploaded for the Full Report are stored in Cloudflare R2 and automatically deleted within 48 hours of upload. We verified that sentence on 2026-11-02; we did not independently verify the deletion itself.

    Is Faceloria a subscription?

    No. The analysis is free, and the Full Face Report is a $16.99 one-time payment - the pricing page states 'One-time payment - No subscription'.

    What data does Faceloria collect?

    Per its policy: upload photos (deleted within 48 hours), email and payment details handled via Waffo, account/session data if you sign in with Google, and analytics. Note the policy names no analytics provider while the pages load a Plausible-compatible script.
