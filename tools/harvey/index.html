Frequently asked questions

How much does Harvey cost?

The official homepage does not publish a self-serve consumer price. Buyers are routed to a demo or sales conversation. Do not treat third-party seat estimates as official list prices.

Can I put privileged client files in Harvey?

Only after your firm’s ethics, conflicts, and IT review. Confirm contracts, retention, training, and access controls in writing. Consumer chat tools are the wrong starting point for privileged matter files.

Does Harvey replace a lawyer?

No. Use it to draft and retrieve. Licensed professionals remain responsible for advice, filings, privilege, and anything under signature.
