Frequently asked questions

Is AllVideoAI free?

There is a free tier. Paid plans are credit-based: Starter ($17/month billed annually), Plus ($39), Max ($59), and Ultra ($159). Verify current prices on the official pricing page — promotional countdowns appear frequently.

How does credit pricing compare to flat subscriptions?

Credits make light users cheaper and heavy users more expensive than flat plans. Model your expected generations per month before choosing.
