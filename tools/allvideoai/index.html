    Frequently asked questions

    Is AllVideoAI free?

    There is a free tier. Paid plans are credit-based: Starter ($17/month billed annually), Plus ($39), Max ($59), and Ultra ($159). Verify current prices on the official pricing page — promotional countdowns appear frequently.

    How does credit pricing compare to flat subscriptions?

    Credits make light users cheaper and heavy users more expensive than flat plans. Model your expected generations per month before choosing.

    AllVideoAI vs Runway or Pika?

    It differentiates on multi-model access under one subscription rather than one proprietary model. See our best AI video tools guide.

    Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

    Run the same job, not a demo.

    Use one representative multi-model ai video generation from text prompts, images, and source footage in one workspace task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

    01 · Same input

    Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

    02 · Measure time

    Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

    03 · Score the result

    Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

    04 · Price the run

    Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
