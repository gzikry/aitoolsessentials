    Overview

    An AI tarot reader on its own 78-card Rider-Waite deck with upright and reversed meanings, four spread types, six reader personas and follow-up questions within a session. Credits are charged per spread and the credit cost of each spread is published.

    Key features

    • Own 78-card Rider-Waite deck with reversed meanings
    • Single, three-card, two-paths and Celtic Cross spreads
    • Six reader personas and in-session follow-ups
    • Published credit cost per spread
    • One-time packs that never expire plus monthly credit subscriptions

    Trial checklist

    Use cases

    • Getting a reading with interpreted cards rather than a lookup table
    • Working through a decision with a multi-card spread
    • Learning spread structure with a defined deck

    Pros

    • The legal set is the strongest in this group: it names its infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers and D1) and its inference provider by company and model - WaveSpeed, google/gemini-3.7-flash
    • It states plainly that self-hosted Umami and Google Analytics 4 load by default without a click-consent prompt, and that there are no analytics preference controls - an admission most sites in this category avoid
    • Deletion is described precisely: removing a reading clears the history entry but leaves a separate session record that is deleted only when the account closes
    • Gives a 30-day response window for rights requests and names Hong Kong as governing law with a two-step dispute path
    • Credit costs are published per spread (single card 10, three cards 20, two paths 30, Celtic Cross 40, first follow-up free, later follow-ups 5) and the packs never expire

    Cons

    • Governing law is named but the party is not: no company, trading name, postal address or controller identity appears anywhere, which is the disclosure a data-protection notice needs most
    • The privacy policy says Google Analytics 4 loads by default, but no G- measurement ID appears in the served pages and the shipped configuration carries an empty gaId - so which is accurate is unresolved
    • Training use is deferred to the provider's terms rather than stated for the service itself
    • The 'independent session records' that survive a history deletion are not described in the policy
