    Overview

    RapVideo AI turns one or two uploaded photos into a short vertical rap video: it writes the lyrics around whatever topic you type, generates the beat and the vocals, and renders the people from your photos lip-synced on a stage you pick. The default Hotel Lobby (orange) stage in 'Original remake' mode recreates the real trend recording and choreography with your faces; the other stages make an original AI rap instead. Everything is paid in credits - 10 for a standard 12-second 720p render, more for the faster or higher-resolution Seedance models - either as one-time packs that never expire or as a monthly plan whose credits reset each month. Samples render in about two to three minutes, download as watermark-free MP4 in 9:16, square or widescreen, and a render that fails or is blocked by a safety filter is not charged. The site is a single-purpose studio built around this one job, with roughly 200 trend and guide pages around it.

    Key features

    • Two-photo intake (one person per photo) or a single photo of both, plus a solo mode
    • Four stages - Hotel Lobby (orange), Luxury Lobby, Studio Booth and Street Cypher - in 'Original remake' (the real trend recording) or 'New song' (an original AI rap) mode
    • AI lyric writing from a typed topic, names and occasion, in whatever language the topic is written in
    • Seedance 2.0 Mini/Fast/Pro and Seedance 2.5 Ultra renders at 480p, 720p and 1080p, 5-15 seconds (up to 30 on Ultra), vertical/square/widescreen
    • Per-render credit cost shown before you commit, with automatic refunds for failed, timed-out or safety-blocked renders
    • Gift links that split paid credits into shares other people claim into their own accounts
    • Watermark-free HD MP4 download, My videos history, credits and billing pages, and zh/es/fr/de locale mirrors
