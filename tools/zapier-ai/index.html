Zapier AI review

AI-assisted automation builder for connecting apps, triggering workflows, and reducing manual operational tasks.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

AI-assisted automation builder for connecting apps, triggering workflows, and reducing manual operational tasks.

Key features

  • AI actions: GPT/Claude steps inside any automation
  • Zap builder from natural-language description
  • 8,000+ app integrations — the widest catalog anywhere
  • Tables, Interfaces, and Chatbots round out the no-code suite
  • Reliable trigger/filter logic with error retry

Hands-on notes

Zapier remains the default for a reason: the app catalog is unmatched and Zaps are dependable in month six, not just week one. AI steps inside Zaps work well for classify-and-route patterns. Pricing scales sharply with task volume — heavy automators should compare against Make.

Who Zapier AI is for

Zapier AI fits teams and individuals who need automating business workflows across apps. It is strongest in automation work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Zapier AI scores 4.4/5 in our evaluation. Huge app ecosystem which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: complex workflows need testing so factor that into your monthly budget.

Frequently asked questions

Zapier vs Make — which is cheaper?

Make is typically 3-5x cheaper at high task volumes but has a steeper learning curve. See our Zapier vs Make vs n8n comparison.

What counts as a task?

Each action execution in a Zap. Filters that stop a Zap don't count.

Can Zapier use AI?

Yes — GPT, Claude, and other AI steps drop into any Zap, plus AI-built workflow drafts.
