Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: Core Zapier plans: Free at $0 with 100 tasks/month; Professional starts at $19.99/month when billed annually; Team is $69/month when billed annually; Enterprise is custom. AI steps, code, MCP, SDK, and Zap workflows draw from a shared task pool. In AI by Zapier, Standard model steps consume 1 task per run, Advanced 3 tasks, and Premium 5 tasks; tool calls add task usage. The pricing page separately lists Agents Free with up to 400 automated activities/month and Agents Pro with up to 1,500 activities/month, but did not expose a dependable Agents Pro price in the retrieved official page text.

Open verification questions
  • The official AI by Zapier help page is internally inconsistent about Free-plan access: its availability panel marks Free unavailable, while a later section discusses the Standard tier on a Free plan.
  • A current official Agents Pro dollar price was not reliably exposed in the retrieved pricing-page text.
  • Zapier's Terms do not promise that AI output is unique, copyrightable, or non-infringing.
  • Third-party model handling depends on Zapier's AI Functionality Subprocessors and, for bring-your-own-model configurations, the selected provider's terms.

AI by Zapier supports built-in models or a connected customer AI-provider account, structured output fields, knowledge sources, and app actions as tools. Zapier's April 9, 2026 Terms define Inputs and Outputs as Customer Content and state that customers retain ownership of Customer Content. Zapier receives a limited license needed to provide, maintain, operate, improve, and support the service. Zapier may derive de-identified data sets from Customer Content and use them to improve products, including through model training. Customers may submit the linked opt-out form; Company and Enterprise accounts are automatically opted out under the current Terms. Usage Information may be used to develop and improve Zapier products, but the current clause does not explicitly say that Usage Information is used for model training.

Frequently asked questions

Zapier vs Make — which is cheaper?

Make is typically 3-5x cheaper at high task volumes but has a steeper learning curve. See our Zapier vs Make vs n8n comparison.

What counts as a task?

Each action execution in a Zap. Filters that stop a Zap don't count.

Can Zapier use AI?

Yes — GPT, Claude, and other AI steps drop into any Zap, plus AI-built workflow drafts.

Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

Run the same job, not a demo.

Use one representative automating business workflows across apps task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

01 · Same input

Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

02 · Measure time

Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

03 · Score the result

Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

04 · Price the run

Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.

Portability · before you subscribe

Make sure you can leave.

Good fit today does not mean low lock-in tomorrow. Before paying for Zapier AI, test the exit path against your real workflow. We do not infer portability from marketing copy; verify the vendor's current export, retention, and cancellation rules.

Export one real result

Separate reusable work

Which prompts, templates, source files, contacts, or data remain usable outside the product?

Find the trapped layer

What history, credits, integrations, team knowledge, or proprietary format might not transfer?

Test cancellation

Confirm the retention period, downgrade behavior, renewal date, and whether cancellation deletes anything.
