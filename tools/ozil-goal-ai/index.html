    Overview

    Ozil Goal AI (ozilgoalai.io) is a single-purpose photo-to-video studio that turns one photo of an adult into a short vertical football clip from fixed presets - a Daylight or Halloween goal, a missed-goal celebration, a Ronaldo-style header jump and a Lamine Yamal rabona. You upload one JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB, tick a consent declaration, choose 480p or 720p, and pay with credits whose cost is shown before you generate; most scenes ask for two photos (jumper and defender). Output is 9:16, 9-15 seconds depending on the scene, and every video carries the template's soundtrack. It is operated by Jimmy Allen in Portland, Oregon, and states plainly on every page that it is 'not affiliated with or endorsed by any football club, league or player'. One personalised reference image is free per verified account; video generation is always paid. Pricing is one-time packs from $4.90, monthly subscriptions from $29.90 and yearly plans from $100 that grant credits once a year. The legal set - Terms, Privacy, Refund Policy and Acceptable Use - is written for this product and dated after the domain was registered (2026-10-15).

    Key features

    • Fixed football presets instead of prompts: a Daylight goal (15s), a Halloween goal (15s), a missed-goal celebration (15s), a Ronaldo-style header jump (13s, two photos) and a Lamine Yamal rabona (9s)
    • One JPEG, PNG or WebP per person, up to 10 MB, with head, legs and clothing visible; the two-photo scenes take one subject per role and allow a person or an animal in either
    • 9:16 vertical output at 480p (10 credits) or 720p (20 credits), with the template's soundtrack included; the credit cost is displayed before the render starts
    • Automatic credit return for a confirmed failed or rejected generation, and the notice that a rejected input before the charge consumes nothing
    • One free personalised reference image per verified account, with sign-in and email verification required, and video generation always paid separately
    • One-time credit packs that never expire alongside monthly and yearly subscriptions, with unused subscription credits expiring at the end of the paid period
    • Seven interface languages (English, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, Arabic) including a right-to-left layout, stated as interface languages rather than dubbing
    • Private storage for photos and finished videos behind the owning account; playback and download require the account and current approval, and a local photo draft lives in browser storage with a 30-minute reuse limit

    Frequently asked questions

    What does Ozil Goal AI cost?

    A video costs 10 credits at 480p and 20 at 720p. One-time packs: 10 credits $4.90, 30 credits $12.90, 50 credits $19.90, 350 credits $99, 900 credits $199 and 3,000 credits $499, none of which expire; a single 720p render is offered at $5.99. Monthly subscriptions: Basic $29.90 (100 credits a month), Pro $49.90 (300) and Studio $99.90 (800), with unused subscription credits expiring at the end of the paid period. Yearly plans grant credits once a year: Basic $100 (400 credits), Pro $500 (4,000) and Studio $600 (6,000). USD, tax excluded at checkout.

    Is there a free trial?

    Partly. Each verified account gets one free personalised reference image, and the site states it is one per account rather than per day or scene - deleting the preview does not reset it, and a failed request does not grant another. Generating a video always requires purchased credits, and the site says plainly that the free image 'does not guarantee identical facial details, clothing or movement in the final video'.

    Which football scenes can I make?

    Five as of 2026-11-20: a Daylight goal and a Halloween goal (15 seconds each, one photo), a missed-goal celebration (15 seconds, one photo), a Ronaldo-style header jump (13 seconds, two photos - one who jumps, one who defends) and a Lamine Yamal rabona (9 seconds). All are 9:16; the free preview covers a still image, not a video.

    Can I use the video commercially, and what about the music?

    The Terms grant you the output, but they also say the purchase does not grant rights to other people's likenesses, trademarks or music, and the Acceptable Use policy says fixed presets do not authorise misuse of club marks, player identities or third-party music. Every video ships the template's soundtrack and no licence for that audio is published, so ask the vendor before using a clip commercially - the same question this cluster's trend-video sites have left open.

    Is it affiliated with Mesut Ozil or the clubs?

    No. The footer of every page reads 'Independent product. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any football club, league or player', and the Terms say the name does not imply endorsement. Note the same-name family around it: ozilgoalai.app and ozil-goalai.org sell the same trend, and large studios (Kapwing, SJinn, EveryGen, Vismuse) resell an Ozil-goal template. The domain is printed in this listing's title so you can tell which one you are buying from.
