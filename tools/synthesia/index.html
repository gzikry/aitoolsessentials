Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: Basic is $0 with 1,200 credits/month. Starter is displayed at $29/month with 1,200 credits/month, or $18/month billed yearly with 14,500 credits/year. Creator is displayed at $89/month with 3,600 credits/month, or $64/month billed yearly with 44,000 credits/year. Enterprise has custom pricing and credits, unlimited video minutes subject to its terms, 240+ stock avatars, and unlimited Personal Avatars subject to reasonable consumption and compute.

Open verification questions
  • The pricing page is internally inconsistent: its annual cards display Starter at $18/month and Creator at $64/month, while its FAQ states $264/year for Starter and $804/year for Creator. Those annual totals do not equal the displayed monthly equivalents, so no corrected value was inferred.
  • The reviewed public terms permit use of Customer Data to improve the services but do not clearly state whether that includes general model training, nor do they identify a self-serve training opt-out. A stronger no-training claim could not be verified.
  • Commercial use is not unrestricted: Stock Avatar advertising and sensitive-context uses can require written consent, an Enterprise plan, or both.

Customer Terms effective July 20, 2026 say the customer owns Customer Data, including Inputs and Outputs, while Synthesia owns Synthesia Content. Customers receive a limited license to Synthesia Content during the subscription and, after termination, to the extent that content was incorporated into generated videos, subject to continuing compliance. The terms license Customer Data to Synthesia only as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the services, address security/support issues, comply with law, prevent fraud, or as expressly authorized in writing. The privacy policy generally describes Synthesia as processor of Customer Data under customer instructions. Personal-avatar documentation requires a live consent recording matching the person shown; stock avatars are based on actors who consented. The Acceptable Use Policy prohibits rights violations, deceptive impersonation, and disabling provenance mechanisms. It restricts paid social advertising with Stock Avatars unless Synthesia gives express written consent; Custom Avatar advertising requires appropriate consent from the depicted individual. Stock-avatar use in sensitive endorsement-like contexts, news, politics, or current-events content is also restricted. Acceptable-use source: https://www.synthesia.io/legal/acceptable-use-policy
