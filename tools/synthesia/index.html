Synthesia review

An AI video platform for creating training, enablement, and explainer videos with avatars and templates.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

An AI video platform for creating training, enablement, and explainer videos with avatars and templates.

Key features

  • 230+ AI avatars with natural gestures
  • 140+ languages from one script
  • Custom avatar clones of yourself
  • PPT-to-video and doc-to-video conversion
  • 1-click translation of finished videos

Hands-on notes

Avatar realism leads the category — lip sync and gestures passed the 'is this real?' glance test for most viewers in our samples. Script-to-video turnaround is minutes. Best for training and comms at scale; not a creative storytelling tool. Custom avatar quality was excellent.

Pricing

Free$0 — 3 min/mo, 140+ avatars
Starter$29/mo — 10 min/mo, 120+ languages, personal avatar option
Creator$89/mo — 30 min/mo, gesture avatars, one-click translation
EnterpriseCustom — Unlimited minutes option, SSO, brand kits

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Training videos
  • Internal enablement
  • Explainers
  • Localization
  • Creating talking-head content without filming
  • Producing localized video variants per market

Pros

  • Enterprise-friendly which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Good training workflows — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Repeatable templates with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Less suited to cinematic creative so factor that into your monthly budget
  • Needs governance for avatar use which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Synthesia is for

Synthesia fits teams and individuals who need enterprise training videos and ai avatar presentations. It is strongest in video work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Synthesia scores 4.2/5 in our evaluation. Enterprise-friendly which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: less suited to cinematic creative so factor that into your monthly budget.
