    TINY DESK AI (tiny-deskai.net) review

    TINY DESK AI (tiny-deskai.net) is a single-purpose browser studio for the 'Outstanding' Tiny Desk meme: it turns one lead portrait plus up to two optional bandmate photos into a 15-second 480p desk-concert video, with audio included, in a vertical or widescreen frame. You pick a room concept (The Soul Session, The Outstanding Moment, The Indie Room), cast a lead singer and optional keyboard and sax players, tick a photo-permission checkbox and a separate processing consent, sign in with Google and spend 1 video credit. Draft preparation is free and local - selecting photos and saving a draft uploads nothing; photos reach kie.ai's file service and Seedance only after you explicitly confirm generation. It sells one-time video packs ($5.90 for 1, $15.90 for 3, $49.90 for 10) through Waffo Pancake, returns the credit on a confirmed failed generation, and caps output at 2 attempts per account per 24 hours. The site is six days old and unusually plain-spoken about its own limits: it disclaims any NPR affiliation, labels its scene concepts 'NOT A GENERATED RESULT', discloses that provider media URLs are accessible to anyone with the link, and names its processors and their retention windows. What it does not yet resolve is who operates it, what audio the finished video actually carries, and why its privacy notice still says generation is disabled while the live status endpoint enables it.

    Overview

    TINY DESK AI (tiny-deskai.net) is a single-purpose browser studio for the 'Outstanding' Tiny Desk meme: it turns one lead portrait plus up to two optional bandmate photos into a 15-second 480p desk-concert video, with audio included, in a vertical or widescreen frame. You pick a room concept (The Soul Session, The Outstanding Moment, The Indie Room), cast a lead singer and optional keyboard and sax players, tick a photo-permission checkbox and a separate processing consent, sign in with Google and spend 1 video credit. Draft preparation is free and local - selecting photos and saving a draft uploads nothing; photos reach kie.ai's file service and Seedance only after you explicitly confirm generation. It sells one-time video packs ($5.90 for 1, $15.90 for 3, $49.90 for 10) through Waffo Pancake, returns the credit on a confirmed failed generation, and caps output at 2 attempts per account per 24 hours. The site is six days old and unusually plain-spoken about its own limits: it disclaims any NPR affiliation, labels its scene concepts 'NOT A GENERATED RESULT', discloses that provider media URLs are accessible to anyone with the link, and names its processors and their retention windows. What it does not yet resolve is who operates it, what audio the finished video actually carries, and why its privacy notice still says generation is disabled while the live status endpoint enables it.

    Key features

    • Photo-to-video desk-concert generation: one required lead portrait plus up to two optional bandmates (keyboard, sax), JPG/PNG/WebP up to 8 MB each
    • Three room concepts (The Soul Session, The Outstanding Moment, The Indie Room) and two frames (9:16 vertical, 16:9 widescreen)
    • 15-second 480p output with audio included, at 1 video credit per generation
    • Free local draft preparation - photos are not uploaded until you explicitly confirm processing and generation
    • One-time video packs (1, 3 or 10 credits) via Waffo Pancake, no subscription; confirmed failed generations return the credit
    • Explicit photo-permission and processing-consent checkboxes before any upload, with processors and provider retention named in the privacy notice
    • Google sign-in, account video history, and a disclosed 2-attempts-per-24h capacity cap

    Frequently asked questions

    What does TINY DESK AI cost?

    Three one-time video packs and no subscription: $5.90 for 1 video, $15.90 for 3 ($5.30 each) and $49.90 for 10 ($4.99 each). Each video uses 1 credit, checkout is through Waffo Pancake, and a confirmed failed generation returns the credit automatically. Adding optional bandmates does not change the pack price. The studio caps generation at 2 attempts per account per 24 hours, so a 10-pack is rendered over several days.

    Is there a free trial?

    Browsing, choosing photos and saving draft settings are free and local - nothing is uploaded until you confirm generation. There is no free finished video: generating requires a Google sign-in and 1 paid video credit. The public status endpoint lists a dailyLimit of 2, which the pricing page describes as a 2-attempts-per-24h capacity cap rather than a free allowance.

    What audio is in the finished video?

    The pricing cards say 'Audio included', but the site does not state what that audio is. The /outstanding page says the project 'does not supply the original song or recording', and the Terms grant 'no rights to third-party recordings, footage, songs, artist voices or trademarks'. Since the meme is defined by The Gap Band's 'Outstanding', ask the vendor whether the included audio is that recording, a soundalike or original music before buying for a public post.

    What happens to my photos?

    Selecting photos and saving a draft does not upload them; they stay in your browser until you tick the processing consent and generate. Then they are sent to kie.ai's file service (kieai.redpandaai.co) and Seedance, cropped and re-encoded without original metadata. The notice discloses that provider uploads and results use accessible media URLs - anyone with the link may reach them - that uploads are deleted after 24 hours and generated files kept 14 days by the provider, and that the site's own R2 copies have no automatic expiry or self-service deletion yet.

    Is this affiliated with NPR?

    No. The footer and FAQ state it is 'an independent AI project, not affiliated with or endorsed by NPR, Tiny Desk Concerts, Charlie Wilson or The Gap Band.' 'Tiny Desk' is NPR's trademark; the site is one of several independent tools riding the same meme, and its rights to the name are not confirmed.

    What do I get back, and in what quality?

    A 15-second 480p video with audio, in vertical 9:16 or widescreen 16:9, downloadable from your account history. There is no resolution above 480p and no length option. Likeness, singing and instrument motion vary, and the scene concepts are labelled 'NOT A GENERATED RESULT', so the preview is creative direction rather than a promise of the output.
