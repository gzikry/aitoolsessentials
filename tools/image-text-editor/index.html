    Overview

    Image Text Editor changes the words that are already part of a finished picture instead of adding a text layer on top. You upload a JPG, PNG or WebP, the editor detects readable text blocks, you select one and type the new wording - or choose the removal mode and let it reconstruct the area - and it returns one flattened result to compare against the original before you download. Nothing has to be re-designed: the pitch is a single headline, price, date or label corrected on a poster, screenshot, banner or product photo whose source file is gone, with the surrounding typeface, colour and background regenerated around the change. The output is deliberately not editable as text, the sample demo runs in the browser with no sign-in and no AI call, editing your own image needs a Google account, and results are saved for a fixed window - 7 days without a subscription, 30/60/90 days on Basic/Pro/Studio - with the expiry date shown in the dashboard.

    Key features

    • Text-block detection with single-region selection, then replacement in place or full removal with the surrounding area reconstructed
    • One flattened result per generation, returned to a dashboard with before-and-after review and 7/30/60/90-day retention by plan
    • 1 credit per saved Standard 1K result and 2 per HD 2K; reserved at generation, charged only on success, released on failure
    • One-time credit packs (12 / 30 / 70 credits at $4.99 / $9.99 / $19.99) that never expire, alongside monthly and yearly subscriptions
    • Concurrency by plan - one generation at a time free or on packs, up to 2 / 3 / 5 for Basic / Pro / Studio - with jobs continuing after the tab closes
    • ZIP export of up to 20 saved results at 100MB per export on Pro and Studio
    • Per-result expiry date shown in the dashboard, so the retention window is visible per file rather than only in the policy
    • Local no-sign-in demo; Google sign-in to edit your own image and use account storage
    • Upload limits stated up front (JPG, PNG and WebP) with one selected text region per edit
    • Privacy choices banner with Reject / Accept and a footer Cookie settings link that changes the choice later

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Image Text Editor?

    A browser editor for the words inside a finished picture rather than a text layer on top of it. You upload a JPG, PNG or WebP, the editor detects readable text blocks, you select one and type the replacement - or use the removal mode to erase it and rebuild the area - and it returns one flattened image to compare against the original. It is built for correcting a headline, price, date or label on a poster, screenshot, banner or product photo whose source file is gone; it is not a design application and the result is not editable as text afterwards.

    How much does Image Text Editor cost?

    One-time packs cost $4.99 for 12 credits, $9.99 for 30 and $19.99 for 70, and never expire. Subscriptions are $9.99 / $19.99 / $39.99 a month for 40 / 90 / 200 monthly credits, or $99.90 / $199.90 / $399.90 a year - exactly ten monthly payments. A saved 1K result costs 1 credit and 2K costs 2; credits are reserved at generation and charged only when a result is saved, and a failed generation releases them.

    Is there a free tier?

    Eligible new accounts get one free credit - one Standard 1K result - plus capacity to scan three images, and the sample demo runs locally without signing in and without any AI call. Editing your own image requires a Google account, which the site says protects AI processing capacity.

    How long are my files kept?

    Original uploads are stored privately for up to 24 hours. Saved results last 7 days without a subscription, or 30 / 60 / 90 days on Basic / Pro / Studio, with the expiry shown in the dashboard; cancelling later does not shorten a result already saved. Private diagnostic copies used for troubleshooting are kept up to 7 days, and you can delete saved images and their diagnostics from Generation history yourself.

    What shouldn't I use it for?

    Anything unlawful or deceptive: the Terms ban impersonation, forged records, misleading product claims, removing required legal disclosures, and the whole document class - IDs, passports, certificates, receipts, bank statements. The Acceptable Use Policy also covers adult and non-consensual content. Separately, the download is flattened with no text layer, so it is not a substitute for keeping your editable source.

    What should I check before paying?

    Three things. Whether you need the longer retention (30/60/90 days) that only subscriptions give, since packs stop at 7 days of saved results. Whether you accept that no company, jurisdiction or named AI provider is published and that refunds are approved case by case. And whether you are comfortable with analytics - Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity session replay and Plausible - running by default until you use the Reject control.
