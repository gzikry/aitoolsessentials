Who DeepSeek is for

DeepSeek fits teams and individuals who need reasoning, coding, and cost-conscious model evaluation. It is strongest in general ai assistant work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.
