    Frequently asked questions

    Is DeepSeek really free?

    The chat app is fully free. The API is paid but extremely cheap vs rivals.

    Is DeepSeek safe for business use?

    Consider data residency and censorship policies carefully; many firms self-host the open weights instead.

    How good is DeepSeek vs ChatGPT?

    Close on math/coding benchmarks, weaker on some general tasks, at a fraction of the price. See our comparisons.

    Is DeepSeek free?

    DeepSeek's chat app is free to use, and its API uses low per-token pricing that undercuts most frontier rivals. Verify current API rates on DeepSeek's official pricing page, they change occasionally.

    What are DeepSeek's main tradeoffs?

    Cost and open availability are strengths. Tradeoffs include fewer integrations than US assistants, and data-governance considerations some organizations need to review before adoption.
