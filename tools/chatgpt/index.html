Overview

A flexible AI assistant for drafting, analysis, brainstorming, coding help, and everyday knowledge work.

Key features

  • Multimodal input: text, images, files, and voice in one conversation
  • Custom GPTs for reusable role-specific assistants
  • Memory that carries preferences across chats
  • Deep Research mode for multi-source reports with citations
  • Canvas for iterative long-form drafting and code editing

Hands-on notes

In our evaluation, ChatGPT handled mixed-format prompts (screenshot + question) better than any competitor. Long-document summarization stayed accurate at 50+ pages, though it occasionally hedged on niche factual claims instead of admitting uncertainty. Voice mode latency was under a second in testing.

Who ChatGPT is for

ChatGPT fits teams and individuals who need general-purpose writing, research, analysis, and multimodal help. It is strongest in general ai assistant work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT free?

Yes — the free tier includes GPT-4o mini with usage caps. Most daily users need Plus at $20/month for the full models and higher limits.

Is my data used for training?

On paid Team and Enterprise plans, no. On free and Plus, conversations may be used for training unless you opt out in settings.

ChatGPT vs Claude — which is better?

ChatGPT wins on multimodal features and ecosystem; Claude tends to produce more natural long-form writing and handles very long documents well. Our ChatGPT vs Claude comparison breaks down task-by-task results.
