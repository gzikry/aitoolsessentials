Overview

A flexible AI assistant for drafting, analysis, brainstorming, coding help, and everyday knowledge work.

Key features

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving content drafts—not a demo prompt. Verify whether multimodal input: text, images, files, and voice in one conversation materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to needs review for factual claims so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Current official summary: OpenAI officially lists ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Free is $0; paid self-service plans are billed per user per month, while Enterprise pricing is sales-assisted. Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT free?

See the official pricing summary and source links above. Plan names, limits, and regional prices can change.

Is my data used for training?

On paid Team and Enterprise plans, no. On free and Plus, conversations may be used for training unless you opt out in settings.

ChatGPT vs Claude — which is better?

ChatGPT wins on multimodal features and ecosystem; Claude tends to produce more natural long-form writing and handles very long documents well. Our ChatGPT vs Claude comparison breaks down task-by-task results.
