Overview

A grant operating system for finding, drafting, and managing awards. Official pricing starts at $299/month when paid annually for Discover, with higher Pre-Award and Full Lifecycle tiers. A 14-day free trial is advertised. Vendor ROI marketing figures are not independently verified here.

Key features

  • Official Discover: $299/month paid upfront annually, or $349 paid monthly, up to 3 users
  • Official Pre-Award: $499/month annually or $579 monthly, up to 5 users
  • Official Full Lifecycle: $999/month annually or $1,159 monthly, up to 15 users
  • Enterprise is custom pricing; 14-day free trial advertised on all self-serve plans
