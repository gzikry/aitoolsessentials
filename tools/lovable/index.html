  • GitHub sync two-way — own your code

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Free includes 5 daily build credits, capped at 30 per month, plus monthly grants of 20 Cloud credits and 4 credits for AI features in user-built apps. Pro is $25/month per workspace, shared across unlimited users, with 100 monthly credits and additional daily grants. Business is $50/month per workspace, shared across unlimited users, with 100 monthly credits and added SSO, roles, internal publishing, security, and workspace controls. Enterprise uses a company-size-based platform fee and volume-based credit pricing. Credit use varies by feature and task complexity; monthly plan credits expire two months after issue, while daily grants do not roll over.

Open verification questions
  • As of August 21, the effective Terms already grant training rights with a prospective opt-out, while the product documentation says Free and Pro customer-data training begins September 9, 2026. The sources do not fully explain operational use during the intervening period.
  • Lovable does not guarantee that AI Output is unique, free of third-party rights, accurate, or suitable for a particular purpose.
  • Enterprise pricing is not published as a fixed amount.

Official documentation describes Lovable as generating editable frontend, backend, database, authentication, and integration code from natural language, with GitHub synchronization and shared workspaces. Terms effective August 15, 2026 state that users own Customer Data, including applications, websites, and projects, and own AI Output generated for them, subject to third-party rights in underlying models, training data, or outputs. The Terms permit personal or internal-business use, including creating, deploying, operating, and making built applications available to end users. The Terms grant Lovable a broad license to use Customer Data, including for AI/ML development and training, but users can opt out prospectively for free regardless of plan. The official data-control documentation says Free and Pro customer-data training is scheduled to begin September 9, 2026 and users may opt out before or after that date. Business and Enterprise workspace data is excluded from training by default under organization agreements and DPAs. The April 14, 2026 Privacy Policy says raw or identifiable Personal Data is not used for training, while anonymized or aggregated data may be used.

Who Lovable is for

Lovable is aimed at teams and individuals who need ai app generation for product prototypes and front-end experiences. It is designed for development workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
