Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-09-01

Current official summary: NotebookLM has been renamed to Gemini Notebook. The official about page states it is free to use for individuals. The page advertises uploading PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, audio files, Google Docs, and Google Slides; summarization with citations; and an Audio Overview feature. Who NotebookLM is for

NotebookLM is aimed at teams and individuals who need document qda, study guides, and audio overviews from uploaded sources. It is designed for research workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

Run the same job, not a demo.

Use one representative document qda, study guides, and audio overviews from uploaded sources task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

01 · Same input

Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

02 · Measure time

Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

03 · Score the result

Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

04 · Price the run

Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
