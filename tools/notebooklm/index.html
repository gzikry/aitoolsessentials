    Pricing

    Official sources checked 2026-10-01

    Current official summary: NotebookLM is now Gemini Notebook (the official FAQ states the rename took effect in July 2026). The product remains free for individuals with standard usage limits and up to 50 sources per notebook. Gemini Notebook is also included in Google AI Plus (double the standard usage limits, up to 100 sources), Google AI Pro (four times the standard limits, up to 300 sources), and Google AI Ultra (5-20x the Pro limits, up to 600 sources). The plans page does not publish US-dollar prices; the paid tiers are billed through Google One and are available only in listed regions. Organizations, schools, and enterprise access route through Google Workspace or Google Cloud.

    NotebookLM is aimed at teams and individuals who need document qda, study guides, and audio overviews from uploaded sources. It is designed for research workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    01 · Same input

    Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

    02 · Measure time

    Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

    03 · Score the result

    Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

    04 · Price the run

    Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
