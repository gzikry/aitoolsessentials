    Overview

    AIVane turns described or recorded browser and desktop tasks into editable automation workflows. It offers a browser app, Windows and Mac downloads, Hosted AI via DeepInfra and optional bring-your-own model configuration. Sam Luo publicly identifies as its independent operator; Creem separately sells purchases. Workflow execution, installers and checkout were not independently tested.

    Key features

    • AI-assisted creation and editing of browser/desktop workflows
    • Browser cloud execution and scheduled runs on eligible plans
    • Windows and Mac desktop downloads with optional local execution
    • Optional Hosted AI credits or own model provider
    • MCP access to saved automations
    • One-time credit and cloud-time packs

    Frequently asked questions

    Who operates AIVane?

    The About page names Sam Luo as independent developer and operator. AIVane Labs is a product brand; Creem (Armitage Labs OÜ) acts as merchant of record and contractual reseller for purchases, not as the software operator.

    What does AIVane cost?

    Free includes three saved automations and one-time trial allowances. Starter is $12/month or $120/year, Pro $29/month or $290/year, and Business $39/seat/month or $390/seat/year with a three-seat minimum. Check the official pricing page for packs and taxes.

    Where do Hosted AI tasks go?

    AIVane says its current Hosted AI inference provider is DeepInfra and that its own service retains request and response content for 30 days. Own-provider configurations follow the chosen provider’s terms.

    Can I use my workflows elsewhere?

    The Terms say users retain rights to recordings, inputs and workflows; portability and execution outside AIVane were not tested.

    Which law governs the product terms?

    The Terms specify the laws of the People’s Republic of China and competent courts in mainland China where permitted, while preserving mandatory consumer protections. Creem purchases are governed separately by Creem Buyer Terms.
