The paid centre of it is an image-to-image tool: upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB, pick an inked, halftone, cel-shaded, storybook or game-art treatment, compare the result against the original with a slider and download.     Frequently asked questions

    What does StylMorph cost?

    One free Seedream 4.5 image per guest identity, then one-time credit packs with no subscription: $6.99 for 40 credits ($0.175 each), $12.99 for 90 ($0.144) and $24.99 for 200 ($0.125). Purchased credits never expire and nothing renews automatically. A Seedream 4.5 image is 1 credit, a Nano Banana Pro image is 4 purchased credits and the two-model comparison is 2 purchased credits, so the largest pack buys about 200 Seedream images or 50 Nano Banana Pro images - roughly $0.06 and $0.50 an image respectively.

    Is there a free trial?

    There is one free successful Seedream 4.5 image per guest browser identity, available without an account and shared across the site's image tools. Nano Banana Pro and the comparison tool cannot use it - those need purchased credits. Signing in with Google is required only to buy and to keep a balance across devices.

    How long are my images kept?

    Originals and results are designed to expire after 24 hours on every plan, paid packs included, and the download button is the only way to keep one. Technical task records may be retained beyond that, but the policy does not state for how long.

    Can I get a refund?

    There is no refund page; the Terms and the pricing FAQ both say delivered digital credit purchases are generally final, and ask you to contact support@stylmorph.com within 14 days for a duplicate charge, an unauthorized payment, or credits that never arrived. Confirmed generation failures return the reserved credits automatically.

    Do the free tools upload my photos?

    The site says the browser-local tools - crops, borders, watermarks, converters, colour and metadata utilities - do not upload your images, and that only the AI transformation sends a file to its processing provider. The AI-generated results and originals are then held in private storage on a 24-hour expiry.
