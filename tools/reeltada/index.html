    ReelTada review

    ReelTada turns one or two photos into a ready-to-post vertical video using trend-ready AI templates for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. It sells only one-time credit packs - no subscription - and returns credits when a generation fails, which the refund policy states in writing. The legal set exists (privacy, terms, refund) and the content rules in the terms are unusually specific. The weaknesses trail the trend-microsite class: no operator identity or jurisdiction anywhere, the privacy policy names no processors, no retention period and no training-use statement, three analytics scripts load on every page (Plausible, Cloudflare Insights, and a self-hosted script from analysis.gaolong.site) with none of them named in the policy, and its own refund policy still calls the product 'Bby Wow' - a template leftover from another brand.

    Overview

    ReelTada turns one or two photos into a ready-to-post vertical video using trend-ready AI templates for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. It sells only one-time credit packs - no subscription - and returns credits when a generation fails, which the refund policy states in writing. The legal set exists (privacy, terms, refund) and the content rules in the terms are unusually specific. The weaknesses trail the trend-microsite class: no operator identity or jurisdiction anywhere, the privacy policy names no processors, no retention period and no training-use statement, three analytics scripts load on every page (Plausible, Cloudflare Insights, and a self-hosted script from analysis.gaolong.site) with none of them named in the policy, and its own refund policy still calls the product 'Bby Wow' - a template leftover from another brand.

    Key features

    • Photo-to-vertical-video generation from trend templates for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
    • One-time credit packs only (10/40/100 credits), no subscription
    • Standard template at 10 credits per generation; premium templates at 30
    • Credits returned automatically on failed generations
    • Content moderation rules and reporting flow in the terms

    Use cases

    • Turning a single photo into a trending-format vertical video for TikTok/Reels/Shorts
    • Producing a themed short (e.g. lip-sync or dance template) without filming
    • One-off seasonal or reaction clips where a credit pack beats another monthly subscription

    Pros

    • Strictly one-time pricing - no subscription exists, and packs are clearly priced ($9.90/$29.90/$59.90 for 10/40/100 credits)
    • Failed generations return credits, stated in the refund policy rather than implied
    • The terms' content prohibitions are specific (non-consensual imagery, exploitation, synthetic sexual content), which is more than most trend sites publish
    • A complete legal page set (privacy, terms, refund) exists with a recent update date

    Cons

    • No operator identity, company or governing law appears anywhere on the site
    • The refund policy still references 'Bby Wow credit packs' - a leftover brand name from the template this legal set was copied from - which undermines confidence in the paperwork
    • Privacy policy names no processors, gives no retention period, and says nothing about training use of uploaded photos
    • Three analytics scripts (Plausible, Cloudflare Insights, a self-hosted tracker from analysis.gaolong.site) load on every page including the legal pages, and none are named in the policy
    • The pricing meta description advertises a $6.89 entry point while the listed Starter pack is $9.90 - a promotional gap worth knowing before checkout

    Frequently asked questions

    Is ReelTada a subscription?

    No. It sells one-time credit packs only: $9.90 for 10 credits, $29.90 for 40, $59.90 for 100. A standard template generation costs 10 credits.

    What happens if a ReelTada generation fails?

    Per its refund policy, credits for a failed generation are returned to your account automatically.

    Who operates ReelTada?

    No company, individual or jurisdiction is named on any page we checked - you are dealing with an unidentified operator, which the listing reflects.
