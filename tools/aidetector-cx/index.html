    Frequently asked questions

    Is AIDetector.cx free to use?

    There is a free entry of five one-time introductory checks (1 guest + 4 signed-in) with no recurring reset. Ongoing use runs on credit plans from $12/month.

    Does AIDetector.cx train on the text you submit?

    No. The Privacy Policy's Model Training section states submitted analysis text is not used to train or fine-tune detection models on any plan. Analysis history is retained 12 months by default and can be deleted from the dashboard.

    How accurate is the AI-text detection?

    The vendor publishes its own benchmark - precision 0.97, recall 0.94, F1 0.955 on a 400-document internal corpus. Treat any AI-detection score as probabilistic, and note there is no independent third-party evaluation of this tool.
