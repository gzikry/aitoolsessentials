Open WebUI is aimed at teams and individuals who need a self-hosted chatgpt-style interface over local and compatible model backends. Frequently asked questions

Is Open WebUI free?

The project remains available as self-hosted software, but its current license and branding terms should be checked before redistributing or operating larger deployments.

Does Open WebUI include a model?

No. It is primarily an interface and orchestration layer; connect it to Ollama or another supported backend and evaluate that backend separately.

Is it private?

Self-hosting can keep the application and connected data on infrastructure you control, but provider connections, logs, uploads, extensions, and network exposure still need review.
