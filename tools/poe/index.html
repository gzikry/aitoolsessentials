Open verification questions
  • Monthly-billing prices were not included in the extracted official pricing page, so only verified annual prices and their displayed monthly equivalents are reported.
  • No blanket commercial-use assurance was found because Poe grants a personal right to use the service and underlying model-provider policies may impose additional restrictions.
  • Privacy and training treatment varies by each bot or app's privacy-shield status.

Poe provides one interface for third-party text, image, audio, and video bots, custom bots/apps, group chats, and shared subscriptions. Fully shaded privacy-shield bots share data with model providers but interactions are not used by those providers for training; half-shaded bots/apps may share data with developers or external resources and may use interactions for training. Poe's terms define prompts and bot-generated results as 'Your Content' and state that users retain ownership, while granting Poe a perpetual license for stated service purposes. Use also remains subject to policies of the underlying model providers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Poe worth it?

If you switch between GPT-4o, Claude, and image models, yes — it consolidates subscriptions.

What are compute points?

Per-model usage currency; bigger models cost more points per message.

Can I make my own bot?

Yes — custom bots with custom prompts are shareable via link.

What is Poe?

Poe is Quora's multi-model chat app: one subscription gives access to many AI models (including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others) in a single interface.

Is Poe cheaper than paying for separate AI subscriptions?

For casual multi-model exploration, often yes — Poe bundles access that would otherwise require several $20/month plans. Heavy users of one specific frontier model may still prefer that vendor's native app for full features.

Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

Run the same job, not a demo.

Use one representative trying multiple ai models and bots from one interface task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

01 · Same input

Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

02 · Measure time

Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

03 · Score the result

Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

04 · Price the run

Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
