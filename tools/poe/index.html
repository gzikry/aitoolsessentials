Overview

A multi-model chat platform for comparing assistants, using specialized bots, and testing different model behaviors.

Key features

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving model comparison—not a demo prompt. Verify whether access dozens of models in one app materially reduces editing or setup time. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Poe offers free daily points and annual subscriptions of $49.99/year for 10,000 points/day, $199.99/year for 660,000 points/month, $499.99/year for 1.65 million points/month, $999.99/year for 3.3 million points/month, and $2,499.99/year for 8.25 million points/month. Poe displays annual equivalents of $4.17, $16.67, $41.67, $83.33, and $208.33 per month. Taxes, currency conversion, regional availability, and app-store pricing may vary.

Open verification questions
  • Monthly-billing prices were not included in the extracted official pricing page, so only verified annual prices and their displayed monthly equivalents are reported.
  • No blanket commercial-use assurance was found because Poe grants a personal right to use the service and underlying model-provider policies may impose additional restrictions.
  • Privacy and training treatment varies by each bot or app's privacy-shield status.

Poe provides one interface for third-party text, image, audio, and video bots, custom bots/apps, group chats, and shared subscriptions. Fully shaded privacy-shield bots share data with model providers but interactions are not used by those providers for training; half-shaded bots/apps may share data with developers or external resources and may use interactions for training. Poe's terms define prompts and bot-generated results as 'Your Content' and state that users retain ownership, while granting Poe a perpetual license for stated service purposes. Use also remains subject to policies of the underlying model providers.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Who Poe is for

Poe is aimed at teams and individuals who need trying multiple ai models and bots from one interface. It is designed for model hub workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
