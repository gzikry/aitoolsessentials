    Overview

    Unblurfy rebuilds detail in photos that are blurry, pixelated, grainy or simply too small, rather than just enlarging them. You pick a file (JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC up to 20MB), choose 1K or 2K, press Enhance photo, then compare the result with the original on a slider before downloading as PNG, JPG or WebP - no watermark on any plan. Each page covers one job people actually search for: unblur a photo, unblur text in a screenshot, unpixelate, sharpen a face, restore a scanned print, colourise a black-and-white photo, prepare a file for 300 DPI printing or pull a still from a video, all drawing on the same credit pool. The site is unusually direct about the trade-off - AI redraws missing detail, so small text, faces and logos can come out slightly different, results are 'not verified reproductions' and not for evidence - and it refuses the jobs it cannot honestly do: removing mosaics or censorship someone else applied, unlocking paywalls or blurred previews. Free use is one 1K enhance a day without an account (two when signed in); paid credits buy 2K output and batches of up to 20, and uploads are deleted about 24 hours after they arrive.

    Key features

    • 1K (about 1MP) and 2K (about 4MP) output with a before-and-after slider and 4x zoom for comparing detail
    • Single-job pages covering the common searches: unblur text, unpixelate image, fix pixelated image, unblur face, restore old photos, colourise photo, image upscaler, photo enhancer, fix grainy photos, video stills, DPI and print-size helpers
    • Batch mode for members: up to 20 photos at once, progress tracking, ZIP download, and jobs that continue if the tab closes
    • One credit per 1K enhance and two per 2K across every tool; credits are charged only on success and returned if a fix fails, times out or is rejected
    • Free daily allowance of 1 fix signed out / 2 signed in at 1K, with no watermark and no card
    • Card payments through Stripe Managed Payments (merchant of record Link, a Stripe company); Google sign-in ties purchases to an account
    • Uploads and results deleted about 24 hours after they arrive (48 hours in rare cases), with a job record kept that contains no image
    • JPG, PNG, WebP and HEIC input up to 20MB, with HEIC conversion and downscaling done in the browser before upload

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Unblurfy?

    A browser photo-enhancement service that rebuilds detail lost to blur, pixelation, grain or low resolution. You upload an image (JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC up to 20MB), pick 1K or 2K, and it returns a sharper version to compare against the original before you download. Neighbouring pages apply the same engine to specific jobs - blurry text, pixelated images, faces, old prints, colourising, print preparation and pulling a still from a video - and it will not attempt to strip censorship, mosaics or paywall blur.

    How much does Unblurfy cost?

    Free use is one 1K enhance a day without an account, or two a day when signed in, with no watermark. Paid options are monthly subscriptions - Basic $5.99/mo for 80 credits, Pro $9.99/mo for 200, Max $24.99/mo for 600 - annual versions at $47.88, $79.99 and $199, or one-time packs that never expire: $4.99 for 20 credits, $14.99 for 80 and $29.99 for 200. A 1K enhance costs 1 credit and 2K costs 2, and credits come back if a fix fails.

    Is there a free tier, and do I need an account?

    There is a real daily free tier and you do not need an account for it: 1 enhance a day at 1K, 2 if you sign in, with no watermark. Purchase does require signing in with Google, so credits attach to an account, and usage before signing in counts toward the same daily total.

    What happens to my photos?

    Uploads and results are deleted automatically about 24 hours after upload, 48 hours in rare cases, and a job record without the image is kept for accounting. The policy states the images are never made public, are not sold, and are never used to train models, and that IP addresses are stored only as a one-way hash. Before processing, images are screened by OpenAI's moderation service, and the image AI provider may keep a temporary copy under its own terms.

    What can't it do?

    It will not remove blur that was applied on purpose - censorship, mosaics, paywalled text or blurred previews - and it stops at 2K, with no 4K output and no API. Because enhancement redraws detail, small text, faces and logos can differ from the original, and the Terms state results are not verified reproductions and must not be used as evidence or for fake documents.

    What should I check before paying?

    Two things. First, whether you need 2K at all, since 1K covers screen use and 2K doubles the credit cost. Second, the refund rule: packs are refundable only while completely unused, within 7 days, and the first-subscription exception is 7 days and untouched credits only. Also note that no company, address or jurisdiction is published and there is no governing-law clause.
