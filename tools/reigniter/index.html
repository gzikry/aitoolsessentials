    Overview

    Reigniter combines a branded AI video studio, prompt-led app builder, interactive training and marketing agents. Flittor Ltd (England and Wales company 17255586) operates it. Public pricing and legal pages describe credits, content rights and UK governing law; an actual generation and social posting were not tested.

    Key features

    • Branded AI product, UGC-style and commercial video formats
    • Prompt-based app and website builder
    • Agent-assisted campaign planning and approval-gated posting
    • Interactive training exports including SCORM 1.2 and xAPI on eligible plans
    • Shared credit balance across product lines

    Who Reigniter is for

    Reigniter is aimed at teams and individuals who need small teams making branded ai video adverts, interactive training and simple app prototypes from one shared credit balance, with human approval before social publishing. It is designed for creative workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who runs Reigniter?

    Flittor Ltd, registered in England and Wales as company 17255586, identifies Reigniter and Reignitor as trading names. The Terms specify English and Welsh law and courts.

    What does a plan cost?

    Starter offers 5 free signup credits. Pro starts at $29/month for 75 credits, Interactive Training at $149/month for 385, and Agency at $199/month for 515; check the current pricing page for higher credit quantities and tax.

    Do credits roll over?

    The Terms say credits from plans and top-ups expire two months after being added and the oldest are used first.

    Can I use the outputs commercially?

    The Terms say users own their uploads and generated media insofar as law permits; obtain rights and consent for uploaded faces, voices and third-party brand assets.
