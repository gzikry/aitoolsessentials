Who Claude is for

Claude fits teams and individuals who need long-form writing, careful analysis, document review, and coding assistance. It is strongest in general ai assistant work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.
