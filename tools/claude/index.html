    Overview

    A thoughtful assistant especially useful for editing, long-context analysis, and turning messy notes into structured outputs.

    Key features

    • Projects that keep context, files, and instructions organized per workstream
    • Artifacts for live-rendering documents, code, and prototypes beside the chat
    • 200K-token context handles entire books or codebases
    • Computer Use for operating a browser and desktop apps
    • Consistently strong long-form writing tone

    Who Claude is for

    Claude is aimed at teams and individuals who need long-form writing, careful analysis, document review, and coding assistance. It is designed for general ai assistant workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    Frequently asked questions

    What makes Claude different from ChatGPT?

    Claude emphasizes long documents and natural writing; ChatGPT emphasizes a broader product ecosystem. Many teams run both. See our ChatGPT vs Claude comparison.

    Does Claude have a free plan?

    See the official pricing summary and source links above. Plan names, limits, and regional prices can change.

    Can Claude read my whole codebase?

    With Projects and API use, yes, 200K tokens fits most small-to-mid repos in one context.
