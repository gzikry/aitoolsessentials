Overview

A thoughtful assistant especially useful for editing, long-context analysis, and turning messy notes into structured outputs.

Key features

  • Projects that keep context, files, and instructions organized per workstream
  • Artifacts for live-rendering documents, code, and prototypes beside the chat
  • 200K-token context handles entire books or codebases
  • Computer Use for operating a browser and desktop apps
  • Consistently strong long-form writing tone

Hands-on notes

Claude produced the most publish-ready first drafts of any assistant we tested — fewer AI-isms, better instruction-following on tone. Its 200K context genuinely held a 600-page PDF query in testing. Message caps on Pro are the main friction point for heavy days.

Who Claude is for

Claude fits teams and individuals who need long-form writing, careful analysis, document review, and coding assistance. It is strongest in general ai assistant work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Bottom line: Claude scores 4.7/5 in our evaluation. Excellent writing quality which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: can still miss details so factor that into your monthly budget.
