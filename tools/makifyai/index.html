    Makify AI review

    Makify AI is a browser workspace for prompt-driven image editing and generation with a product-photography focus: upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB, describe the change in plain English, and get product scenes, listing images, ads, mockups and infographics, exported at 1K or 2K and up to 4K on models that support it. The same credit balance buys short video, and the pricing page publishes the credit cost for each model and each 5-second clip rather than leaving it to be discovered at checkout. The site is a rebrand of AI GPT Image - it announces the change in-app and keeps 'AI GPT Image' as an alternate name in its structured data - and it runs on third-party models it names and disclaims affiliation with, including OpenAI's GPT Image line, Google's Nano Banana family, ByteDance's Seedream and Seedance, FLUX, Qwen, Kling and MiniMax. Pricing is unusually consistent for this category: the subscription ladder, the one-time pack ladder and the per-model credit costs reconcile with each other, and the 17%/33%/50% annual discounts match the monthly equivalents. What the site does not answer is who is accountable and how long uploads live. The footer names ARKELUMA LLC, but no privacy notice names a controller, address, jurisdiction or retention period, the Terms point governing law at 'the jurisdiction in which makifyai.com operates', training use is not mentioned anywhere, and analytics plus Microsoft advertising tags fire on first load with no consent banner rendered.

    Overview

    Makify AI is a browser workspace for prompt-driven image editing and generation with a product-photography focus: upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB, describe the change in plain English, and get product scenes, listing images, ads, mockups and infographics, exported at 1K or 2K and up to 4K on models that support it. The same credit balance buys short video, and the pricing page publishes the credit cost for each model and each 5-second clip rather than leaving it to be discovered at checkout. The site is a rebrand of AI GPT Image - it announces the change in-app and keeps 'AI GPT Image' as an alternate name in its structured data - and it runs on third-party models it names and disclaims affiliation with, including OpenAI's GPT Image line, Google's Nano Banana family, ByteDance's Seedream and Seedance, FLUX, Qwen, Kling and MiniMax. Pricing is unusually consistent for this category: the subscription ladder, the one-time pack ladder and the per-model credit costs reconcile with each other, and the 17%/33%/50% annual discounts match the monthly equivalents. What the site does not answer is who is accountable and how long uploads live. The footer names ARKELUMA LLC, but no privacy notice names a controller, address, jurisdiction or retention period, the Terms point governing law at 'the jurisdiction in which makifyai.com operates', training use is not mentioned anywhere, and analytics plus Microsoft advertising tags fire on first load with no consent banner rendered.

    Key features

    • Prompt-based editing of an uploaded JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB
    • Image models in one workspace: GPT Image 2.5/2.6/2/1.5, Nano Banana 2/2.1/Pro/Pro Lite, Seedream 5.0 Pro/Lite, FLUX 3, Qwen Image 3.0, Ideogram 4.0, Grok Imagine Image 2.0, Z-Image
    • Video generation from text, image or reference: Seedance 2.5/2.0/Fast/Mini, MiniMax H3, Kling 4.0, Wan 3.0, FLUX 3 Video
    • Image-to-image with reference images, object and background replacement, and text-aware generation
    • Task presets for product/ecommerce, marketing and ads, text and layout, portrait, and quick fixes
    • Upscale to 1K or 2K and up to 4K on supported models, plus expand, restore, background removal and vector conversion
    • Prompt library and example gallery with copyable prompts
    • Credit-based billing with a published per-model cost table on the pricing page
    • Five locales: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and partial Korean
    • Chrome extension ('Image Makify') for editing from the browser toolbar

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Makify AI?

    A browser-based AI image editor and generator aimed at product and marketing visuals. You upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB, describe the edit in plain English, and it returns product scenes, listing images, ads, mockups and infographics using third-party models including OpenAI's GPT Image line, Google's Nano Banana family, Seedream 5.0 and FLUX 3. The same credit balance also buys short videos from Seedance, MiniMax, Kling and Wan. It was previously sold as AI GPT Image, and the in-app notice confirms existing accounts and credits moved across.

    How much does Makify AI cost?

    Free to start with 30 sign-up credits and additional 7-day check-in credits. Subscriptions run $11.90/month (Basic, 100 credits/month) and $29.90 (Standard, 300), with a Pro slider from $59.90 (750) to $159.90 (2,300); billed annually those become $118.80, $238.80 and $358.80-$958.80 a year, which is 17%, 33% and 50% below the monthly equivalents. One-time packs run from $9.90 for 80 credits to $199 for 2,500 and expire after 6 months, and subscribers can buy top-ups from $29.90 for 300 credits to $899 for 12,500. A one-week Starter Pass of 40 credits costs $4.99.

    How are credits used up?

    Per generation, at a rate published on the pricing page for each model and setting: 3 credits for a GPT Image 2 image, 4 for GPT Image 2.5/2.6 variants, 8 for Nano Banana Pro, and 25 to 125 credits per 5-second video clip depending on model. The pricing page states the same billing engine charges the account, so the table and the deduction should match - our reading is that the ladder is consistent, but nothing here has been tested hands-on.

    Can I use the images commercially?

    The marketing copy says yes on paid plans: the homepage says paid plans 'include a commercial license for your store, ads and client work' and the pricing FAQ repeats it. The Terms do not carry that promise - section 6 says whether an output can be used 'depends on applicable law, your inputs, the rights of third parties, and the terms of the third-party models involved' and that 'we do not guarantee that any output is eligible for copyright protection or can be used commercially in every situation'. Treat the commercial licence as unconfirmed in writing until the vendor says which document governs, especially for client deliverables.

    Who runs Makify AI, and how long are my uploads kept?

    The footer of every page says the site is 'operated by ARKELUMA LLC'; no other identity appears. The privacy policy names no controller, postal address or governing law, states no retention period for uploads or outputs, and never says whether submitted images are used for training. Contact is support@makifyai.com (the submission also used contact@makifyai.com, and the domain's Cloudflare mail routing accepts both). If you are uploading client product photography, get retention in writing before you start.
