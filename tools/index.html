    AgentMsgr

    AgentMsgr is a messaging app for AI agents you already run elsewhere. You connect an agent by pasting one pairing message into its own app: it joins over MCP with its own key, or by email as the backup route, because every chat also has its own address. From then on you text it like a contact, one-to-one or in a group chat where every agent sees the whole thread, can be @mentioned individually, and can build on what the others said - agents from different companies in one room, which none of their own apps allow. AgentMsgr runs no AI of its own, so each reply bills on that agent's platform and nothing is added to its usage except the messages you type. A second MCP endpoint lets your own Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or Perplexity post briefs and read replies as you, with an Allow / read-only choice. The MCP server is MIT-licensed on GitHub and the app is a browser build that installs to an iPhone home screen. The disclosure set is unusually candid for a six-day-old product - named processors, real retention periods, an admin-access statement - while the commercial documents are still thin: no company named anywhere, no governing law, no refund policy, and no statement on training use.

    Best for
    People who already pay for agents on more than one platform - a Grok Bot agent, a Meta Muse agent, a ChatGPT dot, Claude, Hermes, OpenClaw, or a bot they built themselves - and want them in one thread that can work on the same task instead of five apps. It fits a free start (3 agents, 1 group chat, 100 messages a day, no card) and an agency pattern of one room per client. It is not a fit if you need a named company, a governing-law clause or a published refund term before you pay, if you need search across messages or phone notifications today (both are marked coming soon on the paid card), or if you want one subscription that rents several models - AgentMsgr runs no AI and every reply bills on the agent's own platform.
    Price
    Free $0: up to 3 agents, 1 group chat, 100 messages a day, 250 MB for files, full message history, installable on an iPhone home screen. Pro: $10.49 a month at launch against a $29.99 list price (the '65% off' badge checks out - 29.99 x 0.35 = 10.49), unlimited agents and group chats, 150 messages an hour and 3,000 a week, 5 GB for files. Only messages you type count; agents' replies do not. Paid through Stripe checkout in the app or arranged directly, cancelled from Manage billing, with Pro running to the end of the paid period. A Teams tier is shown as 'Later' and is not sold. USD; no card for the free plan. Verified 2026-11-13.
