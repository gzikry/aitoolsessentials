    Overview

    RapVideoGo turns one or two uploaded photos into a short vertical rap video: you pick a template or write the lyrics, the site arranges the scenes and renders the people from your photos rapping to a generated beat and vocal. The default stage is a recording-studio duet, with a Hotel Lobby (golden lobby) effect, a rap-battle generator, birthday pages for a mother, friend, daughter, son, sibling or partner, plus anniversary, farewell and team-celebration templates. Output is 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts - the studio's default render is 12 seconds at 480p for 24 credits, and the sample clips on the page are 720p. Everything is bought as one-time credit packs that never expire rather than a subscription, and the site is explicit that only photos of consenting adults may be uploaded. It is a single-purpose studio with 70 indexed URLs (English plus a Chinese mirror) and eight how-to guides around it.

    Key features

    • One or two reference photos in, a 12-second vertical rap video out (9:16, 480p by default, 720p examples)
    • Editable lyrics with template starts, including named birthday, anniversary, farewell and roast lyrics
    • AI scene and shot arrangement from the lyric and template
    • Hotel Lobby effect using a selected reference clip and two photos
    • Rap battle generator with labelled A/B roles plus roommates, couple, gaming-teammate and friends templates
    • Cost of the exact render shown before generation (24 credits for the default 9:16 / 12s / 480p render)
    • One-time credit packs, credits never expire, no auto-renewal
    • Chinese language mirror of the studio, pricing and policies (/zh)
    • Eight how-to guides covering photo preparation, birthday lyrics, rap-battle scripts and TikTok publishing
