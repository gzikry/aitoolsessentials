    Overview

    Marigold Pet makes one thing: a 15-second Halloween video of your pet from a single photo. You upload a clear JPG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MB, pick one of four preset looks - pumpkin, witch, vampire or friendly ghost - and either 9:16 or 16:9, pay $6.99 once, and get an MP4 with a three-beat structure (arrival in a Halloween scene, magical costume reveal, hero pose) plus AI-generated synchronised audio. There are no prompts to write and no editing step. Costume stills run on Google Nano Banana 2 and the video and audio on MiniMax H3 Max Turbo, both through fal.ai; each finished video passes the provider's safety checker before delivery. The site is bilingual (English and Spanish) and the interface, pricing and legal pages are served in both languages.

    Key features

    • One photo to one 15-second MP4, in portrait 9:16 or landscape 16:9
    • Four preset Halloween looks - pumpkin, witch, vampire, friendly ghost - each with its own scene and costume reveal
    • No prompt writing and no editing controls; the three story beats and shot timings are fixed
    • AI-generated synchronised audio included with the video
    • Costume stills by Google Nano Banana 2, video and audio by MiniMax H3 Max Turbo, both served through fal.ai
    • Provider safety checker on every finished video, with re-render or refund if a render fails or is blocked
    • Waffo Pancake hosted checkout, one-time USD 6.99, tax shown before payment
    • English and Spanish across the landing page, form, pricing and all three legal pages
    • Order progress survives closing the tab; the video is delivered into the page with a download link

    Pros

    • The consent implementation is real, not decorative: the shipped script sets Google consent defaults with analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization all denied, loads GA4 only after Accept, switches to granted on accept and clears the _ga cookies on reject, and exposes a working "Privacy choices" control in the footer to change the decision later
    • Analytics data minimisation is coded rather than promised: page_location is rebuilt from origin plus path with only the approved utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign/utm_term/utm_content parameters kept, referrers get the same treatment, and send_page_view plus allow_google_signals are both off
    • Processors and models are named with what each one receives - fal.ai for generation, Waffo Pancake as merchant of record and payment processor, Google Nano Banana 2 for the costume stills, MiniMax H3 Max Turbo for video and audio - and the Terms add an explicit non-affiliation line for MiniMax, Google and fal.ai
    • Retention is stated as periods rather than adjectives: the photo sits in the operator's own database from checkout, is deleted when generation starts or within 7 days if the order is never paid, and is held at fal.ai for up to 30 days so a failed render can be repeated without re-uploading
    • Output rights are addressed directly - "you may use the delivered output for lawful personal purposes", with the honest caveat that no promise is made that an output is unique or eligible for exclusive rights
    • Failure handling is spelled out: a technical failure or safety rejection triggers a re-render or refund through the payment processor and never a second charge, with a 7-day support window
    • A separate Acceptable Use Policy exists, the pre-purchase consent is explicit that the photo must show a pet with no recognisable people and that the buyer owns or has permission to use it, and the video provider's safety checker is described as always on
    • No subscription, no auto-renewal and no upsell ladder - the price is one number on the homepage, on /pricing and behind the checkout config

    Cons

    • No operator identity anywhere: the seller is the brand "Binglin Studio" and nothing else - no company, no individual, no country, no postal address, no company number - and the Terms contain no governing-law, jurisdiction, dispute or arbitration clause at all, so a buyer cannot see who they are contracting with or which law applies
    • The legal pages are effective-dated 2026-10-20, one day before the domain was registered (2026-10-21), so the notice governing the service predates the site it governs
    • The domain publishes no MX records, so no @marigoldpet.com mailbox can receive mail; the only support route is the free-provider Gmail address printed in the footer, Terms, Privacy Policy and Acceptable Use Policy
    • The domain was registered two days before it was submitted, and the brand behind it has no footprint a buyer can check
    • Paid up front with no free tier or trial, and the only previews are the vendor's own sample renders, so a buyer cannot see output quality on their own animal without paying
    • A delivered video is generally final sale: refunds are limited to non-delivery or technical failure within 7 days, and there is no standalone refund page - the policy lives inside the pricing page and Terms section 5
    • Everything runs off a single order identifier kept in browser local storage instead of an account area, and rights requests are exercised by emailing support rather than through self-serve export or deletion
    • The product is a single seasonal effect: no second look, style or length beyond the four Halloween costumes and the 15-second runtime
    • The photo is collected before payment - it is uploaded to the operator's server when checkout starts so generation can begin automatically on confirmation - which the consent text does disclose, but it means an image of your pet leaves your device before any money changes hands

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does Marigold Pet cost?

    USD 6.99 once, for one 15-second video. There is no subscription, no auto-renewal and no credit system - one payment, one finished video, portrait or landscape. The homepage, /pricing and the site's public config endpoint all report the same 6.99 USD one-time price, and applicable tax is shown at checkout before payment.

    Is there a free trial or a free tier?

    No. There is no free video and no card-free trial; the sample clips on the page are the vendor's own renders. That makes this a pay-first product, so judge it on the four preset looks rather than on a test you can run yourself.

    What happens to my pet's photo?

    From checkout the photo is held in the operator's own database so the video can start automatically once payment clears, then deleted when generation begins or within 7 days if the order is never paid. It is sent to fal.ai only after payment is confirmed and kept there up to 30 days so a failed render can be repeated without re-uploading. The Terms limit uploads to a pet photo with no recognisable people that you own or have permission to use.

    Who owns the video, and can I post it?

    The Terms allow use of the delivered output for lawful personal purposes, subject to applicable law, and say plainly that no promise is made that an output is unique or eligible for exclusive rights. There is no commercial licence granted, so treat it as a personal-use clip.

    Can I get a refund?

    Only in narrow cases: if a technical failure or safety rejection means nothing was delivered, the site re-renders or refunds through the payment processor and does not charge again, and support must be contacted within 7 days. A video that was delivered successfully is generally final sale. There is no separate refund page - the terms are inside /pricing and section 5 of the Terms.

    What should I check before paying?

    Three things the site itself does not resolve: who the seller is (only the brand "Binglin Studio" is named, with no company, address or governing law), that the support address is a Gmail rather than a domain mailbox (the domain publishes no MX), and that the legal pages are dated a day before the domain was registered. None of those change what the product does, but they are the reason this listing is scored below the top of the new-entrant band.
