    Overview

    An AI admin assistant that schedules meetings, drafts email, books travel, and places phone calls over Slack, Messages, email, or voice — positioned as action-taking rather than suggest-or-summarize.

    Key features

    • Schedules meetings and resolves calendar conflicts across calendars
    • Drafts and triages email, including follow-ups
    • Books flights and hotels
    • Places outbound phone calls
    • Chat via Slack, Messages, email, or a call
    • CC schedule@ea.catchagent.ai to hand off a scheduling thread
    • Focus-time / calendar protection, tasks, reminders, and claimed unlimited integrations

    Trial checklist

    Do not buy from the homepage headline alone. Attempt the advertised 10-day free trial with one real scheduling thread, one real inbox follow-up, and one calendar conflict. Confirm whether signup is open or waitlist-gated. Record what mailbox, calendar, and phone permissions Catch requests. Compare the same three tasks against your current assistant (human EA, Shortwave, or a general chat tool) before paying $99/month. Named testimonials on the site are vendor-supplied — do not treat them as independent proof.
