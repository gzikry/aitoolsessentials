Overview

Adobe's browser podcast tools for Enhance Speech cleanup and Studio recording. The official plans page publishes a Free plan and a Premium plan with a 30-day free trial. Exact Premium US-dollar pricing was not listed on that page during this check, so no unofficial third-party dollar figure is recorded.

Key features

  • Official Free Enhance Speech: audio only, 30 minutes / 500 MB, 1 hour per day, no bulk or strength adjustment
  • Official Free Studio: 30-minute projects, 2 downloads per day, no original-recording download, branded audiograms
  • Official Premium: video support, bulk upload, strength adjustment, 4 hours/day, files up to 1 GB / 2 hours, unlimited Studio downloads
  • Official page offers a 30-day Premium trial

Trial checklist

Run one noisy 20-minute voice file through Free Enhance Speech. Record whether the 30-minute / 1-hour daily caps block a weekly show, whether branded audiograms are acceptable, and whether Premium's 30-day trial is required for video or bulk work.

Is Adobe Podcast free?

Yes. Official Free includes Enhance Speech with a 30-minute file / 1-hour daily cap and Studio downloads of 2 projects per day. Those limits are the real constraint, not a missing free plan.

Can it replace Descript or Riverside?
