    Overview

    PawMambo is a single-purpose browser studio that turns one dog photo plus a dance video into a short vertical clip, using Kling 2.6 motion control through Kie AI rather than a text prompt. You pick one of two built-in dance references - Denim Shuffle at 16 seconds, Varsity Strut at 10 - or upload your own 3 to 30 second MP4 or MOV under 10 MB, add a JPG or PNG of one dog up to 8 MB, sign in with Google and generate. Output is 720 by 1264 portrait, and the two example MP4s the studio plays are real files of 16.1 and 10.03 seconds showing the finished anthropomorphic-dog result rather than the reference clips. It sells one-time credit packs at 2 credits per second of video with a 10-credit minimum, gives new accounts 20 credits once, returns reserved credits when a generation fails, and lets you download the MP4 or create a public share page. What sets it apart from the rest of the pet-video wave is how much it prints about its own uncertainty: the features section says sample results are still being tested, the motion section says the operator still needs to test how closely output follows the reference, the FAQ says cat photos can be selected but have not been tested - and after a review exchange on 18 November 2026 the Terms and Privacy notice put the refund, commercial-use and provider-training limits in writing instead of leaving them out.

    Key features

    • Reference-guided motion transfer: a built-in dance clip or your own 3 to 30 second MP4/MOV under 10 MB drives the movement, instead of a text prompt
    • Two built-in references - Denim Shuffle (16 seconds, side steps and paw swings) and Varsity Strut (10 seconds, bouncy steps and raised paws) - each previewed in the studio by a finished example clip
    • One dog photo in, 720x1264 portrait video out, up to 30 seconds per request
    • Credit pricing at 2 credits per second with a 10-credit minimum, quoted before you generate
    • Failed generations cost 0 credits; reserved credits return when a task is marked failed
    • Download the MP4 or create a public share page, with a private copy kept in account history; submitted jobs and share pages are listed in the profile
    • Google sign-in, Stripe checkout on one-time packs, and analytics held behind an explicit consent choice

    Frequently asked questions

    What does PawMambo cost?

    Three one-time credit packs and no subscription: $6.99 for 60 credits, $14.90 for 160 and $29.90 for 340, sold at 2 credits per second of finished video with a 10-credit minimum per request. That makes a 10-second clip 20 credits, the built-in 16-second Denim Shuffle 32 and a 30-second custom reference 60, so the smallest pack buys 30 seconds of video and a 10-second clip costs $2.33 on that pack against $1.76 on the largest. Purchased credits do not expire and nothing renews automatically.

    Is there a free trial?

    New accounts receive 20 credits once, which the site describes as enough for one 10-second video. That does not cover the 16-second Denim Shuffle at 32 credits. Existing accounts get no retroactive grant, and the welcome grant is tied to a Google sign-in because there is no other way to create an account.

    Do I need my own dance video?

    No. Two built-in dances ship with the studio - a 16-second Denim Shuffle and a 10-second Varsity Strut. If you want a specific move, switch to My video and upload an MP4 or MOV of 3 to 30 seconds under 10 MB; longer or larger files are rejected, so trim before uploading. A 30-second custom reference is a single 60-credit request.

    What do I get back, and in what resolution?

    A portrait video at 720 by 1264, up to 30 seconds depending on the reference you chose. You can download the MP4 or use Copy video link to create a public share page that anyone with the link can watch. Sound from the reference may carry through, but the FAQ does not guarantee it.

    What happens when a generation fails?

    Failed generations cost 0 PawMambo credits and reserved credits return automatically when a task is marked failed, per the pricing FAQ and the Terms. That is separate from a cash refund: the Terms add a 14-day refund for an unused pack after an accidental purchase, a refund for a verified duplicate charge and a choice of credits or refund where a paid order delivered none (/terms#refunds). A partly used pack is not refunded automatically.

    Can I use a photo of my cat?

    The interface lets you select one, but PawMambo's own FAQ says cat results have not been tested and that the product is focused on dogs. Treat a cat result as an experiment rather than a supported use.
