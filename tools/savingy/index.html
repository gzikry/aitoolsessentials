    Overview

    Savingy is an India-first expense tracker that turns what you log into one number - what is safe to spend today - plus a runway to month end, a monthly plan with budgets and goals, and a Sunday check-in it calls the Weekly Reset. Entry is manual by design: one line such as 'chai 40 upi', a receipt photo, a PDF, CSV or Excel statement import you review before anything is saved, or voice on Pro. There is no bank linking and it never asks for a banking password or PIN. The free core is not a trial - unlimited records, budgets, goals, the Weekly Reset, tracker history, reports and export cost nothing - and Pro is sold as fixed access, not a subscription: Rs 149 for 30 days or Rs 1,188 for 365 days, one payment each, with nothing renewing automatically. A one-time 45-day Pro trial opens after 30 real expense or income entries, at the user's choosing, with no card. The web app is the same product as the Android app on Google Play (com.rhnmaheshwari.savingy, updated 11 October 2026), which prints the same two prices and the same 'no subscription and no automatic renewal' line. The operator is Savingy Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (CIN U62013WB2025PTC282100), Tower House, 2A Chowringhee Square, Kolkata 700069, with Rohan Maheshwari named as director, and a Security & Trust Center that names its subprocessors - Microsoft Azure and MongoDB Atlas in Central India, Razorpay for payments, OpenAI for the assistant. The gap a buyer should know about is documentation drift rather than the product: the Privacy Policy and Terms still call the counterparty 'Savingy, Inc.', the December 2024 refund policy describes a renewing subscription the site does not sell, and the Terms still advertise Basic, Pro and Expert plans that do not exist.

    Key features

    • Safe-to-spend: one daily figure with the arithmetic shown when you tap it, plus a runway to month end and bills still ahead
    • Manual-first logging: one-line entry ('chai 40 upi', 'uber 230 yesterday'), receipt photos, multiple accounts and pots, and bulk statement import from PDF, CSV or Excel with a review step before saving
    • Ask Savingy: natural-language affordability questions answered from your own records, with anything saved subject to your approval
    • Pro money moves: a ranked list of savings found in your own spending, priced per month and per year, with commit/snooze, 'What drove it' evidence, and verification after the month closes
    • Ahead and Statement: bill guardian for 30 days, duplicate-service and price-rise warnings, tight-day flags, a five-year forecast, and a line-by-line monthly receipt with a health score
    • Weekly Reset: a Sunday review and checklist; goals with target dates; recurring purchases; budgets and pacing alerts
    • Reports for any period with CSV, PDF and print export; Strategic Planner with debt-payoff and plan-vs-actual tracking on Pro
    • Data management: back up, restore and export everything; account deletion via an in-app request with a 7-day window and a documented retention carve-out
    • Web app plus Android app (Google Play, com.rhnmaheshwari.savingy), rupee-first formatting and Indian number grouping; English, Hindi and Bengali

    Frequently asked questions

    What does Savingy cost?

    The free tier is free for good with no card: unlimited income and expense records, budgets, goals, the Weekly Reset, history, reports and export. Pro is sold as fixed access, not a subscription - Rs 149 for 30 days or Rs 1,188 for 365 days, one payment each, nothing renewing automatically - and the same two prices appear on the Google Play listing. When a paid period ends you drop back to Free and keep all your data. A one-time 45-day Pro trial opens after 30 real expense or income entries, at your choosing, with no card.

    Is the trial automatic, and does it need a card?

    No card and no automatic charge, per the pricing FAQ. The trial unlocks after 30 real entries and you start it when you choose; the copy says it opens with the first thing Pro notices in those entries or three weeks later at the latest. If you do nothing when it ends, Pro stops and the account returns to Free with the data intact. Buying afterwards is a single Razorpay payment for a fixed 30- or 365-day period.

    Do I have to link my bank account?

    No. The site states on the homepage, the security page and in the FAQ that it does not link to internet banking and does not ask for banking passwords or PINs. Tracking runs on what you enter, on receipt photos, and on statement files you upload and review yourself. That also means the numbers are only as complete as your logging - the product's own copy is careful to call the output estimates, and logging alone does not guarantee savings.

    Which company am I actually buying from?

    The footer names Savingy Solutions Pvt. Ltd., CIN U62013WB2025PTC282100, Tower House, 1st Floor, 2A Chowringhee Square, Kolkata 700069, with Rohan Maheshwari as director and founder, and the Terms set governing law to West Bengal with Kolkata courts. The Privacy Policy and Terms, however, name 'Savingy, Inc.' as the counterparty. We asked the vendor which entity is authoritative; until that is corrected, treat the Indian company in the footer and on the Google Play developer block as the operator.

    What happens to my data if I stop paying, and can I get it out?

    Nothing is deleted when Pro ends - the help page says you keep entries, budgets, goals, bill reminders and your history of closed months, and only the Pro screens lock. Data management offers backup, restore and export on every account, and the delete-data page documents an in-app deletion request with a 7-day confirmation window, after which the account, records, receipts and profile are erased, with payment records kept without name or email for the period tax law requires.
