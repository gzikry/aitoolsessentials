    Overview

    Auteo is a multi-model AI creative studio: one workspace and one credit balance for video (Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3, MAGI-2, Seedance 2.0 and Seedance 2.0 Fast), images (GPT Image 2.6 and 2.5 in Flare and Sunburst variants, Seedream 5.5 and 5.0 Pro, Nano Banana 2.1 / Pro / 2, Z Image, FLUX.2, SenseNova U1.6, GPT Image 2), voice (text-to-speech) and music (Suno V6, YuE2). It is operated by Sigmify LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company whose registered address, governing law and venue are published, and whose privacy notice names its payment (Stripe, PayPal), email (Resend), sign-in (Google, Apple) and hosting/analytics (Cloudflare) providers - plus DeepSeek, which it says helps it follow up on feedback - as companies. The notice states that prompts, lyrics and attached files are sent to third-party AI model providers to generate results, carries an explicit 'we do not use your content to train our models' statement, describes retention per data category, and discloses its own first-party analytics plus a page session-recording tool. The named engines are attributed as third-party suppliers rather than presented as Auteo's own.

    Key features

    • One credit balance across video, image, voice and music rather than per-medium billing
    • Video models: Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3, MAGI-2 (Sand AI), Seedance 2.0 and Seedance 2.0 Fast

    • A published legal set - Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and a Music License page - naming the operator, the governing law and the processors

    Who Auteo (auteo.ai) is for

    Auteo (auteo.ai) is aimed at teams and individuals who need solo creators and small teams who want one subscription that turns a prompt into video, images, voice and music instead of buying each medium from a different site, and who want the credit cost of every render shown before they commit. it suits a buyer who values a named operating entity (sigmify llc, wyoming) with a real governing-law and venue clause, processors named as companies, an explicit no-training statement, and a storefront that advertises no false urgency. it is a weaker fit for someone who needs an operating (not registered-agent) address, retention committed to fixed periods, free access to premium models without a subscription, or a track record - the domain is about two months old and the legal set was rewritten the same week. It is designed for creative workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who operates Auteo?

    Auteo is 'operated by Sigmify LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company with its address at 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA', per the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the footer reads 'Powered by Sigmify LLC'. The Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming and any dispute is resolved in the Wyoming state or federal courts, with consent to their jurisdiction. Contact is by email only (support@auteo.ai); no telephone number is published, and the address is a registered-agent / virtual-office address rather than an operating location.

    What does Auteo cost?

    Subscriptions: Starter $15/month or $12/month billed annually (200 credits a month), Plus $29/month or $23/month billed annually (500 credits a month), Ultra $129/month or $103/month billed annually (3,000 credits a month); annual billing is 20% off. One-time credit packs (valid 1 year) are Small 200 credits $26, Medium 500 credits $56 and Large 3,000 credits $276, with Plus/Ultra members seeing lower pack prices (up to 36% off). A Black Friday offer (through Dec 1 2026) takes 40% off the first payment of any plan and 30% off credit packs. Checkout is Stripe or PayPal.

    Is my content used to train AI models?

    The Privacy Policy states 'We do not use your content to train our models.' It also states plainly that your prompt, any lyrics or text, and any attached files are sent to third-party AI model providers to generate your result, and it names the payment, email, sign-in, hosting and feedback providers as companies. It adds that Auteo does not 'currently commit to fixed retention periods', describing instead how long each category is kept.

    Can I get a refund?

    The Refund Policy allows a refund request within 3 days of payment, provided none of the credits from that purchase have been used. Requests go to support@auteo.ai with the order information. Card payments run through Stripe or PayPal, so the refund channel follows the original payment processor.

    Does Auteo run in the browser?

    Yes - it is a browser studio (the site shows a portrait-mode notice for phones) covering AI video, image, voice and music from one workspace and one credit balance. There is no separate desktop app listed; sign-in is by email, Google or Apple.

    Does Auteo offer a free plan?

    Its free music generator page offers a free account, no card and unlimited full-length songs on the default YuE2 model, with MP3 downloads. Premium models and image/video tools use credits, and commercial licensing requires a paid subscription according to that page. We did not test generation behind sign-in.
