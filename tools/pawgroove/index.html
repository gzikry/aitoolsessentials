    Overview

    PawGroove turns one photo of one dog into a short vertical video, using a browser studio rather than an app. Its 11 templates are mostly solo routines and group scenes - Dog + Backup Dancers, Dog Trio Dance, Kung Fu Dog, a 90s Christmas movie, a Halloween zombie dance, a New Year dance, a five-second silent 'Scientists Reveal the Hi' clip - and the same balance also buys themed photo packs (6-8 images, including stickers and an animated GIF), custom transparent WebP stickers with your own words, and looping reaction GIFs. Most clips target about ten seconds at 9:16, start silent, and take an original PawGroove music track afterwards for free on supported templates, which the Terms license for use inside the video you post but not as a separate download. The library's examples are labelled 'Actual generated example' and are served as real MP4s; each template page prints its own input requirements and limits (one dog, paws visible, JPG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MB) rather than a shared marketing paragraph. It is a single-purpose consumer creative studio built on the ShipAny starter, with no subscription, no watermark claim and no account wall before you can see the price.

    Key features

    • Eleven dog video templates including group scenes (Dog + Backup Dancers, Dog Trio Dance), a kung fu routine, seasonal clips and a five-second silent 'Hi' clip
    • One photo in, a 9:16 clip out - about ten seconds for most templates, silent by default, with a free original PawGroove music track added afterwards on supported templates
    • Themed photo packs of 6-8 images (stickers and an animated GIF included) from the same credit balance, plus a 2-image preview option
    • Custom transparent WebP sticker sheets with your own words, and six looping reaction GIFs generated from one dog photo
    • A published prompt library with settings, exclusions and the site's own dated format checks rather than quality rankings
    • Stripe checkout on one-time credit packs, with 480p trial renders and 720p for accounts that hold a paid order
