    Overview

    CustomGPT.ai sells no-code agents built on a customer's own documents. The pricing page checked on 2026-10-27 lists Standard at $99/month ($89 billed annually) and Premium at $499/month ($449 billed annually), plus a custom Enterprise plan. Both published tiers start with a 7-day trial that requires a credit card and charges the selected plan when the trial ends. This listing records those published limits. It does not treat the affiliate approval as a product score, and no hands-on result is published.

    Key features

    • Standard: 10 agents, 1,000 queries/month, 5,000 documents per agent, 60 million words, 3 team members, RAG API
    • Premium: 25 agents, 5,000 queries/month, 20,000 documents per agent, 300 million words, 5 team members, website auto-sync, remove CustomGPT.ai branding
    • Enterprise: custom limits, with SSO, role-based access, and a data processing agreement listed on that tier
    • 7-day trial of the selected Standard or Premium plan
    • Citations listed on every published plan
    • Add-ons on the same page: 2,500 extra queries for $375/month, 300 million extra words for $300/month, 100,000 extra documents for $100/month, 25 extra agents for $100/month, 5 extra seats for $100/month

    Trial checklist

    Use the 7-day trial on one real document set you already trust, not a demo corpus. Before the card is charged, check whether answers cite the document you uploaded, what the agent says when the documents do not cover the question, and whether 1,000 Standard queries covers a normal week. Cancel from the billing page before day 7 if you stop, because the pricing FAQ says the selected plan is charged automatically when the trial ends. Do not assume the $99 plan includes branding removal or website auto-sync; the comparison table puts those on Premium.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does CustomGPT.ai cost?

    The pricing FAQ checked on 2026-10-27 says Standard is $99 a month (10 agents and 1,000 queries) and Premium is $499 a month (25 agents and 5,000 queries), with 10% off when billed annually ($89 and $449). Enterprise is custom. Both published tiers start with a 7-day trial.

    Does the free trial charge a card?

    Yes. The pricing FAQ says a credit card is required to start the trial, and that the selected plan is charged automatically when the 7 days end. Cancel from the billing page before then if you stop.

    What happens at the query limit?

    The pricing FAQ says agents stop responding to new queries when the monthly query limit is reached. A query already in progress still completes. Standard includes 1,000 queries a month and Premium 5,000. The same page lists 2,500 extra queries at $375/month.
