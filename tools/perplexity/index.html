Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Official support lists Standard/Free, individual Pro, individual Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max. Max includes everything in Pro plus the highest access to advanced models, Comet Max Assistant, Create files and apps, and Brain. The reviewed public consumer-plan pages did not expose current Pro or Max dollar prices. An official organization-onboarding article lists Enterprise Pro at $40/seat monthly or $400/seat annually.

Open verification questions
  • Current public prices for individual Pro and Max were not verifiable from the reviewed official pages.
  • A blanket commercial-use or output-ownership claim was not made because consumer outputs may be governed by third-party model licenses.
  • Enterprise Max pricing was not publicly stated in the reviewed sources.

Perplexity is documented as an AI-powered answer/search product with citations, premium models, file uploads, Research, Create files and apps, Comet, and Computer features depending on plan. AI Data Retention is enabled by default for Free, Pro, and Max users and can be turned off for future collection; previously collected training data is not removed by a later opt-out. Enterprise data is not used for AI training, and Perplexity says its third-party model-provider agreements prohibit provider training on Perplexity data. Consumer terms warn that output may be subject to third-party model licenses and use restrictions.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Who Perplexity is for

Perplexity is aimed at teams and individuals who need source-backed web research and quick market scans. It is designed for research workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

Frequently asked questions

Is Perplexity better than Google?

For research questions where you need sources, yes — it synthesizes instead of listing links. For local searches and shopping, Google still wins.

Does Perplexity use ChatGPT?

It offers multiple models including GPT-4o and Claude alongside its own Sonar models — you choose per search.

Can I trust the citations?

Mostly. Every claim links to a source so you can verify, but we found it occasionally picks low-authority pages on obscure topics.
