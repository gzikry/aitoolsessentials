Overview

A research-first AI search engine that keeps sources visible while answering questions.

Key features

  • Every answer includes numbered source citations
  • Pro Search runs multi-step research instead of one-shot answers
  • Focus modes: academic, Reddit, YouTube, news
  • Spaces for organizing research threads by project
  • Model switching inside a single product

Hands-on notes

Perplexity is the fastest route from question to cited answer — in testing it replaced 'Google + open 6 tabs' for most factual work. Citation accuracy was strong, though it occasionally cited low-authority sources on niche topics. The free tier alone justifies installing it.

Who Perplexity is for

Perplexity fits teams and individuals who need source-backed web research and quick market scans. It is strongest in research work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Perplexity better than Google?

For research questions where you need sources, yes — it synthesizes instead of listing links. For local searches and shopping, Google still wins.

Does Perplexity use ChatGPT?

It offers multiple models including GPT-4o and Claude alongside its own Sonar models — you choose per search.

Can I trust the citations?

Mostly. Every claim links to a source so you can verify, but we found it occasionally picks low-authority pages on obscure topics.
