Perplexity is documented as an AI-powered answer/search product with citations, premium models, file uploads, Research, Create files and apps, Comet, and Computer features depending on plan. AI Data Retention is enabled by default for Free, Pro, and Max users and can be turned off for future collection; previously collected training data is not removed by a later opt-out. Enterprise data is not used for AI training, and Perplexity says its third-party model-provider agreements prohibit provider training on Perplexity data. Consumer terms warn that output may be subject to third-party model licenses and use restrictions. 2026-09-02 official blog “Optimizing On-Device Inference for Apple Silicon” (https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/optimizing-on-device-inference-for-apple-silicon): Lily is a lightweight local inference engine built specifically for Apple silicon and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B so on-device compute does not bottleneck Hybrid Compute / Perplexity Computer tasks. Frequently asked questions

Is Perplexity better than Google?

For research questions where you need sources, yes — it synthesizes instead of listing links. For local searches and shopping, Google still wins.

Does Perplexity use ChatGPT?

It offers multiple models including GPT-4o and Claude alongside its own Sonar models — you choose per search.

Can I trust the citations?

Mostly. Every claim links to a source so you can verify, but we found it occasionally picks low-authority pages on obscure topics.
