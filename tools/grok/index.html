Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Grok Free is $0 per month, SuperGrok is $30 per month, and SuperGrok Plus is $100 per month. Enterprise offers custom rate limits, dedicated infrastructure, SSO/SCIM, compliance options, data residency, support, and volume pricing through sales.
