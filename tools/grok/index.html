Who Grok is for

Grok fits teams and individuals who need conversational ai with real-time culture, news, social context, coding, and brainstorming. It is strongest in general ai assistant work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.
