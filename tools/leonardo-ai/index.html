Overview

A creative AI platform for image generation, asset production, style control, and visual experimentation.

Key features

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving concept art—not a demo prompt. Verify whether fine-tune custom models on your own image sets materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to requires visual direction so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Official individual pricing lists Free with 150 Fast Tokens daily; Essential at US$12/month with 8,500 Fast Tokens/month and a 25,500-token rollover-bank cap; Premium at US$30/month with 25,000 Fast Tokens/month and a 75,000-token rollover-bank cap; and Ultimate at US$60/month with 60,000 Fast Tokens/month and a 180,000-token rollover-bank cap. Concurrent-generation limits are 2, 3, and 6 for Essential, Premium, and Ultimate; queue limits are 5, 10, and 20. Premium and Ultimate include unlimited relaxed generation on specified image and video models, subject to fair-use queues and supported-model restrictions.

Who Leonardo AI is for

Leonardo AI is aimed at teams and individuals who need game assets, concept art, product mockups, and controlled image generation. It is designed for creative workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
