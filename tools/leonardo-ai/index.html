    Pricing

    Official sources checked 2026-08-25

    Current official summary: Official individual pricing lists Free with 150 Fast Tokens daily; Essential at US$12/month with 8,500 Fast Tokens/month and a 25,500-token rollover-bank cap; Premium at US$30/month with 25,000 Fast Tokens/month and a 75,000-token rollover-bank cap; and Ultimate at US$60/month with 60,000 Fast Tokens/month and a 180,000-token rollover-bank cap. Concurrent-generation limits are 2, 3, and 6 for Essential, Premium, and Ultimate; queue limits are 5, 10, and 20. Premium and Ultimate include unlimited relaxed generation on specified image and video models, subject to fair-use queues and supported-model restrictions.

    Leonardo AI is aimed at teams and individuals who need game assets, concept art, product mockups, and controlled image generation. It is designed for creative workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter.     Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

    Run the same job, not a demo.

    Use one representative game assets, concept art, product mockups, and controlled image generation task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

    01 · Same input

    Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

    02 · Measure time

    Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

    03 · Score the result

    Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

    04 · Price the run

    Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
