Leonardo AI review

A creative AI platform for image generation, asset production, style control, and visual experimentation.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A creative AI platform for image generation, asset production, style control, and visual experimentation.

Key features

  • Fine-tune custom models on your own image sets
  • Realtime canvas with live generation while drawing
  • Strong game-asset and product-shot pipelines
  • Elements: mixable style modifiers
  • Motion: short image-to-video clips

Hands-on notes

Leonardo's custom model training is the differentiator — a model fine-tuned on 20 product shots produced consistent branded imagery in testing that generic prompting couldn't match. Realtime canvas makes it feel like drawing with AI rather than gambling with prompts.

Pricing

Free$0 — 150 tokens/day (~15-30 images)
Apprentice$12/mo — 8,500 tokens/mo, image upscaling
Artisan$30/mo — 25,000 tokens, fine-tuned models
Maestro$60/mo — 50,000 tokens, priority generation

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Concept art
  • Game assets
  • Product visuals
  • Style exploration
  • Producing concept art variations for client pitches
  • Creating on-brand social visuals without a designer

Pros

  • Strong creative controls which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Good for asset workflows — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Useful presets with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Requires visual direction so factor that into your monthly budget
  • Advanced workflows take practice which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Leonardo AI is for

Leonardo AI fits teams and individuals who need game assets, concept art, product mockups, and controlled image generation. It is strongest in creative work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Leonardo AI scores 4.3/5 in our evaluation. Strong creative controls which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: requires visual direction so factor that into your monthly budget.
