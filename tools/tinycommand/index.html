    Overview

    TinyCommand is one platform holding eleven connected products - Discovery and Enrichment, Forms, Tables, Workflows, AI Agents, an email designer, Email & campaigns, Portals, SEO and AI visibility - so a form, the record it creates, the workflow that reacts to it, the agent that researches it and the email that follows all live in the same place instead of in six subscriptions. Forms are dynamic rather than static: they can verify a field, look up a person mid-fill, branch, and take payment through Stripe, Razorpay or UPI, and forms and responses are unlimited on every plan including the free one. Tables behave like a light database with six views, 37 field types and AI columns that fill themselves; Workflows run on a visual canvas with 60+ node types, seven trigger types and human sign-off steps; AI agents use six built-in tools and can be embedded on a site or run inside a workflow; email design and broadcast campaigns reuse the same table data. Credits are the meter for automated work rather than for data collection - a workflow step costs 1 credit, an AI agent step about 10, a company lookup 20 and a person lookup 10, with no charge when a lookup misses. Vision and AI agent features run on TinyCommand's own self-hosted models, and the Privacy Policy says customer data processed by those features is not sent to third-party AI providers. The company is TinyCommand LLC of Sheridan, Wyoming, the terms are governed by Wyoming law, and the legal set includes a DPA covering the EU and UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act 2023 and CCPA/CPRA with SCC and UK IDTA transfer mechanisms. Buying runs through the vendor's own signup and checkout; the free tier needs no card.

    Key features

    • Eleven products on one platform and one credit pool: TinyDiscovery, TinyEnricher, TinyForms, TinyTables, TinyWorkflows, TinyAgents, TinyEmails, TinyCampaigns, TinyPortal, TinySEO and TinyAnswers
    • Dynamic forms that verify, look up, branch and take payment as people fill them in - unlimited forms and responses on every plan including Free
    • Tables with six views (grid, kanban, calendar, gallery, Gantt, form), 37 field types, 14 rollup functions, linked records and AI columns
    • Workflows on a visual canvas: 60+ node types, seven trigger types (form, table, schedule, webhook, app event, manual, date field), branching, loops, error handling and human sign-off before an action runs
    • 458 native integrations, including Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Stripe, Shopify, Jira, Linear, Airtable and GitHub; private-beta MCP server advertised with 14 planned tools
    • AI agents with six built-in tools (Scout, Composer, Search, Enrichers, Email Finder), a knowledge base built from your own files, BYOK for GPT, Claude, Gemini or Mistral, and embeddable widgets
    • Email designer plus broadcast campaigns sent from table data, from your own verified domain, with suppression, one-click unsubscribe and open/click reporting
    • Credit accounting printed on the pricing page: 1 credit per workflow step, ~10 per AI agent step, 20 per company and 10 per person found by Discovery (no charge on a miss), everything else quoted before it runs
    • A free-forever tier that needs no card, and a documented rate for what is and is not metered (data collection is never charged)

    How we evaluated

    Job fit 4.3/5 - genuinely broad and genuinely shipped: eleven products sharing one data model and one credit pool, a free tier that is usable rather than a crippled demo (unlimited forms and responses and all 458 integrations), a workflow canvas with 60+ node types and seven trigger types, agents that can act on the customer's own records, and a published credit rate card (1 per workflow step, ~10 per agent step, 20 per company and 10 per person found, with no charge on a miss) that lets a buyer forecast cost instead of guessing.     Frequently asked questions

    What is TinyCommand?

    One platform that replaces a stack of separate tools with a single login and a single data model. Forms collect and verify data, Tables store and enrich it, Workflows automate what happens next across 458 integrations, AI agents read and act on the records, and Email campaigns send from the same table - so a submission, its record, the automation it triggers and the follow-up all live in one place instead of being glued together across several subscriptions.

    How much does TinyCommand cost?

    There is a free-forever plan with 1,000 credits a month, 1 seat, unlimited forms and responses and all 458 integrations, with no credit card. Paid plans are Basic $19/mo (10,000 credits, 3 seats), Professional $49/mo (50,000 credits, 10 seats) and Agency $149/mo (250,000 credits, 50 seats); billed annually the plans are $199, $499 and $1,499 a year, which the cards show as $17, $42 and $125 a month. The site also sells one-payment lifetime tiers on its own page - Solo $199, Growth $499, Agency $999, Founder $1,499 - which are not listed beside the subscriptions, and there is a Black Friday offer that makes months two to twelve free on the yearly plans until 7 December 2026.

    What uses credits?

    Only automated work. Building forms and tables and collecting form responses never cost a credit on any plan. A workflow step costs 1 credit, an AI agent step about 10, a company lookup 20 and a person lookup 10, with no charge when a lookup returns nothing, and heavier steps such as SEO or scraping are priced where they run. Credits refill with the plan each month and lifetime tiers refill monthly for as long as the product exists.

    Do I get the software or a service?

    It is a hosted platform you build in - there is no implementation service attached. Signup creates a workspace with forms, tables, workflows, agents and email ready to use, and the vendor's own case studies describe customers wiring systems themselves. A buyer who wants someone else to build it should look at the partner programme instead.
