    GTA6 AI Video turns one photo into a finished GTA-inspired video clip. You choose a prepared scene from a gallery of more than a hundred templates - GTA 6 Night Drive, GTA 6 Police Chase, GTA 6 Car Wash, GTA 6 Apocalypse, Grand Theft India, the GTA 5 goodbye and thirteen-year montages, plus dance and pet trends - load its roles, add a clear portrait for each one, then check the mode, duration and credit estimate before generating; finished clips land in My creations for review and download.     • A prepared-scene gallery of 100+ GTA-inspired templates (night drive, police chase, car wash, apocalypse, Grand Theft India, GTA 5 goodbye montages, dance and pet trends) where browsing and selecting costs nothing

    • An image generator on the same credit balance (GPT Image 2, Nano Banana Pro and others), priced at 6 credits per image

    Frequently asked questions

    What is GTA6 AI Video?

    An independent web app that creates GTA-inspired fan videos from your photos. You browse a prepared GTA 6 or GTA 5 scene, add a photo for each required role - or start from your own prompt, still or reference clip - review the model, duration and credit estimate, and generate. Finished clips appear in My creations for review and download. It is not an official game product and the site says so itself.

    How much does GTA6 AI Video cost?

    The free plan gives 20 one-time signup credits plus a weekly check-in worth 60 credits across a seven-day cycle, with watermarked downloads and roughly three images or one video before you pay. Subscriptions are Starter $22.90/mo for 840 credits, Creator $48.90/mo for 1,800 and Studio $98.90/mo for 3,650, or yearly at $218.90, $410.90 and $593.40 (each exactly twelve monthly payments). One-time packs that never expire run from $8.99 for 300 credits to $918.90 for 44,000. Usage is quoted at 6 credits per image and 18 per video on the cheapest models.

    Do the credits expire?

    It depends on how you bought them. Subscription credits expire at the end of their billing period and renew with an active plan; credits from a one-time pack never expire. Promotional credits follow whatever expiry is shown when they are granted, and the free plan's 20 signup credits are issued with a 365-day expiry in the site's own configuration.

    Is this an official GTA 6 tool?

    No. The site's own FAQ states that GTA6 AI Video is an independent service, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or the owners of Grand Theft Auto, and that it is not an official game, release, game editor or access to unreleased gameplay. GTA 6 itself launched on 2026-11-19; this domain was registered the same day.

    Can I use the videos commercially?

    The Terms say you own the outputs generated for your account, but commercial-use rights are not settled model by model - the pricing FAQ answers the question by telling buyers to review the Terms, applicable law and 'restrictions for the chosen model'. Treat commercial use as unresolved until the vendor says which models permit it.

    Who operates the site, and where is the company?

    The site names no company, trading name, postal address or jurisdiction anywhere - the Privacy controller block and the Terms contact clause both list only 'GTA6 AI Video' with a support@gta6ai.video address, and the governing-law clause refers to 'the jurisdiction where GTA6 AI Video is established' without naming it. That is an open item, not a settled fact.

    What do I need to sign up?

    A Google account. Google is the only sign-in the shipped configuration enables (credential and GitHub login are off), and purchase requires signing in so credits attach to that account. There is also no self-service account deletion - deletion is requested by email to support@gta6ai.video.
