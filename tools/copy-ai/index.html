Copy.ai review

Overview

An AI platform for marketing and sales content workflows, from copy generation to GTM process support.

Key features

  • GTM-focused: sales email sequences, prospecting workflows
  • Workflow builder chains AI steps into repeatable processes
  • Infobase stores company facts for consistent output
  • Unlimited words even on the entry paid plan
  • Strong short-form marketing copy templates

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving sales copy—not a demo prompt. Verify whether gtm-focused: sales email sequences, prospecting workflows materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to quality depends on inputs so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Official pricing page lists Chat at US$29/month or US$24/month billed US$288 annually, with 5 seats and unlimited words and projects in Chat. Growth is US$1,000/month billed annually with 75 seats and 20,000 workflow credits/month. Expansion is US$2,000/month billed annually with 150 seats and 45,000 workflow credits/month.   • Enterprise pricing is not publicly specified.

The official pricing page was published on 2026-08-17 and was checked directly. Copy.ai's official security page, https://www.copy.ai/security, says it does not train its models on individual customer data, share prompts with other customers, or sell customer data. The Terms say Copy.ai does not claim ownership of User Content; they grant compliant users a non-exclusive worldwide sublicensable license to use, copy, modify, sell, create derivatives from, distribute, display, and perform Generated Content for lawful business purposes. The Terms also grant Copy.ai and its licensors a broad license over User Content for providing, operating, securing, and improving their services.

Who Copy.ai is for

Copy.ai is aimed at teams and individuals who need go-to-market workflows, sales copy, and repeatable marketing content systems. It is designed for marketing workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
