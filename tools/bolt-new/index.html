Bolt.new review

A browser-based AI development environment for quickly creating and iterating on web apps from prompts.

Editorial review · Updated 2026-08-23 · Hands-on result not yet published

Overview

A browser-based AI development environment for quickly creating and iterating on web apps from prompts.

Key features

  • Prompt-to-full-stack app in browser (no local setup)
  • Runs real Node/DB environment via WebContainers
  • Deploy straight from the builder
  • Supports React, Next.js, Astro, Svelte stacks
  • Error-aware AI fixes its own runtime errors

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving rapid prototypes—not a demo prompt. Verify whether prompt-to-full-stack app in browser (no local setup) materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to production hardening required so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Free: $0, public and private projects, 300,000-token daily limit, 1 million tokens/month, 10 MB uploads, Bolt-branded hosting, up to 333,000 web requests, and unlimited databases. Pro: $25/month, no daily token limit, starts at 10 million tokens/month, 100 MB uploads, token rollover, custom domains, private sharing, and up to 1 million web requests. Teams: $30/member/month with Pro features plus centralized billing and administrative controls. Enterprise is custom-priced.

Official pricing ↗Product documentation ↗Privacy / data use ↗Rights / terms ↗
Open verification questions
  • Bolt's security page says code and prompts are never used to train foundation models, while the Privacy Policy permits aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified AI content to improve AI performance and describes plan-dependent training controls. The exact boundary between 'foundation-model training' and other model/service improvement is not defined.
  • The public Privacy Policy does not enumerate which plans receive training opt-out controls.
  • The commercial-use statement does not provide a non-infringement warranty for generated code.

Official documentation says Bolt offers Standard and Max agents; Standard is available on Free, while Max requires a paid plan. Bolt's commercial-use documentation states that all code created with Bolt and StackBlitz is the user's code and may be used for any legal purpose, including commercial purposes. StackBlitz's Terms state that it does not claim ownership of data, content, or other material submitted, published, or transmitted through the service. The Privacy Policy says Bolt may use AI Inputs and Outputs to operate, maintain, and improve the service, including AI performance, reliability, and safety, using aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified data. The Privacy Policy says training/improvement opt-out controls may depend on account type or plan, while third-party AI providers process Inputs to provide services to Bolt under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Who Bolt.new is for

Bolt.new is aimed at teams and individuals who need prompt-to-app prototyping in the browser. It is designed for development workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
