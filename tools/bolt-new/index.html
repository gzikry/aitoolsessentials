    Bolt.new review

    A browser-based AI development environment for quickly creating and iterating on web apps from prompts.

    Editorial review · Updated 2026-11-27 · Hands-on result not yet published

    Overview

    A browser-based AI development environment for quickly creating and iterating on web apps from prompts.

    Key features

    • Prompt-to-full-stack app in browser (no local setup)
    • Runs real Node/DB environment via WebContainers
    • Deploy straight from the builder
    • Supports React, Next.js, Astro, Svelte stacks
    • Error-aware AI fixes its own runtime errors

    Trial checklist

    Use a real task involving rapid prototypes, not a demo prompt. Verify whether prompt-to-full-stack app in browser (no local setup) materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to production hardening required so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

    Pricing

    Last checked 2026-10-25

    Current official summary: Free: $0, public and private projects, 300,000-token daily limit, 1 million tokens/month, 10 MB uploads, Bolt-branded hosting, up to 333,000 web requests, and unlimited databases. Pro: $25/month, or $18/month billed annually ($216/year), no daily token limit, 10 million tokens/month with rollover, 100 MB uploads, custom domains, private sharing, and up to 1 million web requests. Teams: $30/member/month, or $27/month billed annually, with Pro features plus centralized billing and administrative controls. Enterprise is custom-priced. Re-verified 2026-10-25 from bolt.new/pricing.

    Official documentation says Bolt offers Standard and Max agents; Standard is available on Free, while Max requires a paid plan. Bolt's commercial-use documentation states that all code created with Bolt and StackBlitz is the user's code and may be used for any legal purpose, including commercial purposes. StackBlitz's Terms state that it does not claim ownership of data, content, or other material submitted, published, or transmitted through the service. The Privacy Policy says training/improvement opt-out controls may depend on account type or plan, while third-party AI providers process Inputs to provide services to Bolt under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

    Who Bolt.new is for

    Bolt.new is aimed at teams and individuals who need prompt-to-app prototyping in the browser. It is designed for development workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Bolt.new free?

    Yes. Checked 25 Oct 2026: Free is $0 with 1 million tokens a month and a 300,000-token daily limit. Pro is $25/mo ($18/mo billed annually, $216/year) for 10 million tokens with rollover. Teams is $30/mo ($27 billed annually).

    Does Bolt host my app?

    It deploys via integrated partners (Netlify etc.) right from the builder.

    Bolt vs Lovable vs v0?

    Bolt for full-stack control in-browser; v0 for UI polish; Lovable for simplicity. See our AI app builders guide.
