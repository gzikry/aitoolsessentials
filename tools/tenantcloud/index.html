    Frequently asked questions

    How much does TenantCloud cost?

    The official pricing page checked 2026-08-26 lists Starter at $25/month or $180/year, Growth at $35/$350, Pro at $60/$600, and Business starting at $100/month as a custom plan. A 14-day trial is advertised.

    Is TenantCloud an AI leasing agent?

    Official pages describe rent collection, maintenance, listings, lease tools, and accounting. Do not treat it as a certified fair-housing or tenant-decision system.

    Can I put tenant Social Security numbers in ChatGPT instead?

    No. Keep tenant identifiers, screening reports, and financials in an approved property system or offline process. Consumer chat is the wrong place for applicant files.
