Frequently asked questions

Is OpenEvidence free?

The official homepage states it is free and unlimited for healthcare professionals. Confirm current eligibility and verification requirements on the official signup flow.

Can I use it for diagnosis?

No. Treat answers as literature lookup to be verified against primary sources. Clinical decisions stay with a licensed professional.

Does a HIPAA badge mean I can paste PHI?

No. Confirm eligibility, contracts, and your organization's policy before entering any patient-identifiable information.
