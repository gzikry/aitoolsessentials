Editorial review · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

Microsoft’s AI assistant for workplace productivity, search, writing, meetings, and enterprise software workflows.

Key features

  • Drafts and rewrites inside Word with document context
  • Excel analysis: formulas, trends, pivot suggestions
  • Outlook email triage and draft replies in your voice
  • Teams meeting recaps with action items
  • Enterprise Data Protection — prompts not used to train

Who Microsoft Copilot is for

Microsoft Copilot fits teams and individuals who need ai assistance across microsoft 365, windows, edge, and enterprise productivity workflows. It is strongest in productivity work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Copilot included in Microsoft 365?

Basic chat yes; the in-app Office experience requires the $30/user/mo add-on.

Is my company data safe?

Microsoft states tenant data isn't used to train models, with Enterprise Data Protection.

Worth $30/user/month?

For heavy Word/Excel/Outlook users, productivity gains justify it. Light Office users won't see ROI. See our review.
