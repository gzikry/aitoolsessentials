Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is available for work or education without an additional Copilot license and supports metered pay-as-you-go agents. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business is displayed at a promotional $18/user/month, paid yearly, versus a $21 list price. Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot is $23.50/user/month paid yearly, and Business Premium with Copilot is $32/user/month paid yearly. Prices are USD, annual subscriptions that auto-renew, and may vary by market.

Open verification questions
  • The general pricing page does not expose one comprehensive price for every Microsoft Copilot product or enterprise licensing scenario.
  • Pay-as-you-go agent consumption depends on the selected agent and metering arrangement; no single universal usage limit or price is stated on the overview page.
  • The $18 Microsoft 365 Copilot Business amount is promotional; the checked pricing page does not state its expiration directly.

This verification is scoped to Microsoft 365 Copilot for business, not every consumer or product-specific service branded Copilot. Microsoft 365 Copilot works in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, Loop, and other Microsoft 365 surfaces; it uses Microsoft Graph data the user is permitted to access and includes Copilot Chat, Copilot Search, agents, and app-specific drafting, summarization, analysis, and meeting capabilities. Prompts, responses, and Microsoft Graph data are not used to train foundation LLMs. Interaction content is stored under the customer's Microsoft 365 contractual commitments, encrypted at rest, manageable through Microsoft Purview, and deletable through Copilot activity history controls. Microsoft does not claim ownership of service output but does not determine whether output is copyright-protected or enforceable. Its Copilot Copyright Commitment can defend qualifying commercial customers against certain copyright claims when required guardrails and content filters were used. The official privacy and overview documentation was updated July 9, 2026.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Who Microsoft Copilot is for

Microsoft Copilot is aimed at teams and individuals who need ai assistance across microsoft 365, windows, edge, and enterprise productivity workflows. It is designed for productivity workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
