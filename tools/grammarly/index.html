Grammarly review

A writing assistant for grammar, tone, clarity, rewriting, and communication support across everyday business apps.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A writing assistant for grammar, tone, clarity, rewriting, and communication support across everyday business apps.

Key features

  • Works everywhere: browser, desktop, Office, Google Docs
  • Tone detection and adjustment suggestions
  • Full-sentence rewrites, not just error flags
  • Plagiarism checker on Pro
  • Generative AI for drafting and shortening

Hands-on notes

Grammarly remains the least intrusive AI assistant — it improves text where you already write rather than demanding a new app. Rewrite quality improved noticeably with the newer models. The free tier covers most grammar needs; Pro earns its price mainly through tone and rewrites.

Pricing

Free$0 — Grammar, spelling, punctuation, 100 AI prompts/mo
Pro$12/mo (annual) — Full rewrites, tone adjustment, plagiarism, across apps
EnterpriseCustom — Style guides, analytics, SSO, admin dashboard

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Email polishing
  • Tone edits
  • Grammar checks
  • Business writing
  • Editing tone and clarity before hitting send
  • Maintaining brand voice across a writing team

Pros

  • Works across many apps which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Low-friction adoption — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Clear writing focus with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Less powerful for deep research so factor that into your monthly budget
  • Can over-standardize voice which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Grammarly is for

Grammarly fits teams and individuals who need business writing, grammar, tone, and communication quality across apps. It is strongest in writing & productivity work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Grammarly scores 4.4/5 in our evaluation. Works across many apps which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: less powerful for deep research so factor that into your monthly budget.

Frequently asked questions

Is Grammarly Pro worth it?

For daily professional writing, yes — tone control and full rewrites are the real value. Occasional writers can stay free.

Does Grammarly work in Google Docs?

Yes, fully integrated including the sidebar and generative features.

Is my text private?

Grammarly states text is not sold; Enterprise adds contractual controls. Free users' text may be processed to provide the service.
