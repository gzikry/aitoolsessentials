Grammarly review

A writing assistant for grammar, tone, clarity, rewriting, and communication support across everyday business apps.

Overview

A writing assistant for grammar, tone, clarity, rewriting, and communication support across everyday business apps.

Key features

  • Works everywhere: browser, desktop, Office, Google Docs
  • Tone detection and adjustment suggestions
  • Full-sentence rewrites, not just error flags
  • Plagiarism checker on Pro
  • Generative AI for drafting and shortening

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving email polishing—not a demo prompt. Verify whether works everywhere: browser, desktop, office, google docs materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to less powerful for deep research so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Free: $0/month and 100 generative-AI prompts per month. Pro: displayed at $12 USD and includes 2,000 AI prompts per member per month, unlimited personalized suggestions, full-sentence rewrites, tone controls, plagiarism checking, and AI-generated-text detection. Enterprise: contact sales and includes unlimited prompts per member per month plus enterprise controls. The extracted pricing page did not unambiguously expose the billing cadence attached to the displayed $12 Pro amount.

Open verification questions
  • The billing cadence represented by the $12 Pro price was not rendered unambiguously in the retrieved official pricing text.
  • Grammarly's exact treatment may differ for negotiated Enterprise agreements, which are governed by a separate Customer Business Agreement rather than the public self-service terms.

Official sources confirm AI drafting, rewriting, tone adjustment, fluency suggestions, grammar assistance, plagiarism checking, and AI-text detection. For Free, Premium, and single-user Pro accounts, Product Improvement and Training is enabled by default, but users can opt out; when disabled, user content is not used for model training, validation, or product improvement. Multi-user accounts bought directly online are also enabled by default, with an administrator opt-out. Enterprise-tier, sales-purchased Pro/Business, and Education accounts have training disabled by default. Grammarly says its external LLM service providers may receive prompts and context to provide generative features but may not train their models on user content. Under the October 29, 2025 Superhuman terms, inputs and outputs are User Content, and users own all right, title, and interest in User Content; the terms also grant Superhuman a broad license for operating, improving, customizing, and developing its services.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Who Grammarly is for

Grammarly is aimed at teams and individuals who need business writing, grammar, tone, and communication quality across apps. It is designed for writing & productivity workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

Frequently asked questions

Is Grammarly Pro worth it?

For daily professional writing, yes — tone control and full rewrites are the real value. Occasional writers can stay free.

Does Grammarly work in Google Docs?

Yes, fully integrated including the sidebar and generative features.

Is my text private?

Grammarly states text is not sold; Enterprise adds contractual controls. Free users' text may be processed to provide the service.
