Descript review

An editor that lets creators edit audio and video by editing text, with AI cleanup and production features.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

An editor that lets creators edit audio and video by editing text, with AI cleanup and production features.

Key features

  • Edit video/audio by editing the transcript text
  • Studio Sound removes background noise dramatically
  • Overdub voice clone fixes flubbed lines by typing
  • Filler word removal in one click
  • Screen recording with auto-transcription

Hands-on notes

The edit-text-to-edit-video model genuinely changes how you work — cutting a paragraph cut the video. Studio Sound rescued several bad-mic recordings in testing. Overdub worked well for small corrections but uncanny for long insertions. Best podcast/video editing value anywhere.

Who Descript is for

Descript fits teams and individuals who need podcast and video editing with transcript-based workflows. It is strongest in audio & video work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Descript scores 4.3/5 in our evaluation. Fast editing workflow which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: advanced video work may need dedicated tools.
