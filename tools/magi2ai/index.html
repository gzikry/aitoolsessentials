    Overview

    Magi2AI is a browser video generator centered on Sand.ai’s MAGI-2, operated by Sigmify LLC, the same Wyoming company behind Auteo. Its Terms say it accesses MAGI-2 through a third-party API; its privacy notice identifies Sand.ai via NanoAPI and names TypeSafe as the prompt-safety provider. NanoAPI independently publishes a MAGI-2 endpoint and model ID magi-2.0. The studio offers text-to-video, first/last-frame and reference-based creation, with video and sound together. This is an independent service, not Sand.ai itself; no paid render was tested.

    Key features

    • MAGI-2 text, first/last-frame and reference-to-video creation with native audio
    • 4–30 second, 1080p clips in six aspect ratios according to the vendor and model provider
    • Credit-metered Generate button with cost before submission
    • 200/500/3,000-credit monthly and annual subscription tiers; one-time top-ups
    • Email or Google sign-in; Stripe or PayPal payments

    Pros

    • The model developer and independent intermediary are named: Sand.ai via NanoAPI, whose own public docs offer MAGI-2 as magi-2.0
    • Sigmify LLC and a Sheridan, Wyoming mailing address are published, with Wyoming law and Wyoming courts named in Terms section 10
    • Privacy identifies Stripe and PayPal payments, TypeSafe prompt screening, Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, PostHog, Google Ads and DeepSeek feedback processing
    • The privacy policy says submitted prompts and references go to third-party providers, and says the service does not use content to train its models
    • The pricing page discloses monthly and annual tiers, one-time packs, credit cost before generation and credit return on failed generations

    Cons

    • There is no free generation allowance shown; a reader cannot independently test MAGI-2 output before payment without buying a plan or pack
    • The privacy policy explicitly declines to commit to fixed retention periods; residual backups may remain after user deletion for an unspecified period
    • The disclosed first-party session recorder captures text entered into fields; PostHog and Google Ads also process visitor activity
    • The mailing address is a widely used registered-agent address, not an operating office
    • Credit packs expire after one year and the refund policy excludes packs and renewals, allows only three days for a first subscription refund with no purchased credits used, and may deduct up to 6%
    • Magi2AI and Auteo are separate products under the same operator, Sigmify LLC; buying one does not establish access to the other

    Frequently asked questions

    Who makes the model and who runs this site?

    Sand.ai developed MAGI-2. Magi2AI is an independent Sigmify LLC service, not affiliated with Sand.ai. Its Terms say it accesses the model through a third-party API provider, and its Privacy Policy names Sand.ai through NanoAPI.

    What does it cost?

    Starter $15/month or $144/year, Plus $29/month or $276/year, Ultra $129/month or $1,236/year; one-time 200/500/3,000-credit packs start at $26, before temporary Black Friday discounts. Credits on packs are valid for one year.

    Can I request a refund?

    The Refund Policy gives three days for a first subscription payment only if none of the credits from that purchase have been used. Packs and renewals are excluded; an approved refund may have a processing fee of up to 6%.

    Who handles my uploads?

    The Privacy Policy says uploaded media and prompts are sent to third-party providers, identifies Sand.ai through NanoAPI for generation and TypeSafe for text screening, and says no fixed retention periods are currently promised.
