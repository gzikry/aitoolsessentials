    DREAMMMER is a browser AI image and video editor built around an agent rather than a model picker: you describe the result, it writes the English prompt, chooses among GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1, Seedream 5.0, Qwen Image 3.0, Grok Imagine, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3, and you refine in the same thread while every step is mapped on a canvas.     Overview

    DREAMMMER is a browser AI image and video editor built around an agent rather than a model picker: you describe the result, it writes the English prompt, chooses among GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1, Seedream 5.0, Qwen Image 3.0, Grok Imagine, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3, and you refine in the same thread while every step is mapped on a canvas. Eleven one-click tools (background remove, upscale, object remover, photo restore, clothes and background changer, expand) sit in front of the same credit meter, and a public showcase publishes real prompts with the model and credit cost behind each result. The commercial terms are unusually clear for a four-month-old product: you own what you create, the site states it does not train its own models on your uploads, failed generations refund credits automatically, and there is a written 7-day refund window with a 20% usage cap. What is missing is identity and data detail rather than honesty: no registered company is named anywhere, there is no governing-law clause, the policy promises to say how long data is kept and never gives a period, and Google Analytics loads on the privacy policy itself while the policy names no analytics vendor.

    Key features

    • Agent chat that writes the prompt, picks the model and iterates on the result in the same conversation
    • Infinite canvas that maps every request, prompt, render and revision as linked cards, with branching from any node
    • Eleven image models and seven video models behind one credit balance, priced per output in a comparison table
    • Eleven one-click tools: background remover, upscaler, object remover, photo restore, background changer, clothes changer, image expander and more, each opening into the chat for further edits
    • Image-to-video at 480p (24 credits) and 720p (48 credits) for 6-second clips, with longer and 1080p clips in the agent chat
    • Reference-image workflows: keep what matters, change what does not, with one or several references
    • Public showcase of published conversations with the full prompt, model and credit cost on each card
    • Templates for product photos, portraits, posters and effects
    • English and Chinese storefronts, both with their own pricing and legal pages
    • Failed and refused generations refunded automatically in credits

    Frequently asked questions

    What is DREAMMMER?

    A browser AI image and video editor where an agent writes the prompt and picks the model. You describe the result, it chooses among models such as GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana 2.1/Pro, Seedream 5.0, Qwen Image 3.0, Grok Imagine 2.0, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and MiniMax H3, returns images or short videos, and you refine in the same thread. Eleven one-click tools handle common edits, and every step is mapped on a canvas you can branch from.

    How much does DREAMMMER cost?

    Subscriptions are Starter $29/mo for 1,500 credits, Pro $99/mo for 6,000 and Studio $299/mo for 18,000 plus 1,000 free 1K/2K images a month; the yearly plans work out at $20.30, $59.40 and $149.50 a month when paid as $243.60, $712.80 and $1,794. There is also a one-time 60,000-credit pack for $999 that never expires. New accounts get 50 credits once, without a card. Subscription credits reset monthly and do not roll over; pack credits do not expire.

    Is there a free plan?

    A one-time grant of 50 sign-up credits, not a recurring free tier. At 7 to 10 credits for most 1K images that is roughly five to seven generations; 2K and 4K output needs a paid plan, and downloads from an account that has never purchased carry a small DREAMMMER mark. Failed generations return their credits.

    Who owns the images I create?

    As between you and DREAMMMER, you do, and the terms allow personal or commercial use subject to the model's own terms and third-party rights. The site takes only the licence it needs to store, process and display your work, and says it does not train its own models on your uploads. It does not say what the underlying model providers do with prompts and images, so check their terms if that matters to you.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, within 7 days of a credit pack, a new subscription or a renewal, if you have used no more than 20% of the credits that purchase added. After 7 days or past that usage it is case by case. Email support@dreammmer.com from your sign-in address; the policy promises a reply within 2 business days and card refunds back through Waffo within 5-10 business days.

    Will my uploads be published?

    If your account has never made a purchase, the work you create with free credits may be featured in the public showcase after review, and a featured case can include the prompts, the reference images you uploaded and the results, under a non-exclusive worldwide licence - with no name or email attached. Once the account has purchased, nothing further is featured without permission, and you can ask for a case to be removed.

    Who operates DREAMMMER?

    The site does not say. No company, trading name or legal entity appears on any page; the privacy policy publishes only a postal address in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China, and the terms contain no governing-law or jurisdiction clause. That is the single biggest gap in an otherwise unusually clear legal set.
