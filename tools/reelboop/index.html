    ReelBoop review

    ReelBoop is a trending-video studio: you pick a template from a catalogue of about sixty dance, duet, transformation, pet and product scenes, upload the one or two photos the template asks for, and get a vertical MP4 back. The catalogue is specific rather than generic - every card prints how many photos and how many seconds it needs, so 'LISA Tarantino AI Dance' reads '1 photo, 15s' and 'JISOO ADDICTED' reads '2 photos, 15s' before you commit. Underneath, different templates run different models, and the pricing page quotes each cost up front: Kling 3.0 Pro at 60 credits per 5-second clip, Seedance 2.5 at 100, Wan 2.2 I2V Fast for the product-film preset, and GPT Image 2.6 or Nano Banana 2.1 for stills. Generated clips land in private Cloudflare R2 storage with playback and download tied to the owning account, there is no public gallery, and a confirmed failed generation returns its reserved credits. Pricing is three metered subscription tiers or three one-time credit packs, with a 365-day expiry on pack credits and a 7-day refund window on an untouched pack. The parts that make it worth listing are the consent gate that actually blocks analytics until you accept, the per-model credit table, and the refund page - the parts a buyer should weigh are the missing operator identity and the absence of any free trial.

    Overview

    ReelBoop is a trending-video studio: you pick a template from a catalogue of about sixty dance, duet, transformation, pet and product scenes, upload the one or two photos the template asks for, and get a vertical MP4 back. The catalogue is specific rather than generic - every card prints how many photos and how many seconds it needs, so 'LISA Tarantino AI Dance' reads '1 photo, 15s' and 'JISOO ADDICTED' reads '2 photos, 15s' before you commit. Underneath, different templates run different models, and the pricing page quotes each cost up front: Kling 3.0 Pro at 60 credits per 5-second clip, Seedance 2.5 at 100, Wan 2.2 I2V Fast for the product-film preset, and GPT Image 2.6 or Nano Banana 2.1 for stills. Generated clips land in private Cloudflare R2 storage with playback and download tied to the owning account, there is no public gallery, and a confirmed failed generation returns its reserved credits. Pricing is three metered subscription tiers or three one-time credit packs, with a 365-day expiry on pack credits and a 7-day refund window on an untouched pack. The parts that make it worth listing are the consent gate that actually blocks analytics until you accept, the per-model credit table, and the refund page - the parts a buyer should weigh are the missing operator identity and the absence of any free trial.

    Key features

    • A catalogue of roughly sixty photo-to-video templates for dance trends, duets, outfit and character transformations, pets and product films, each labelled with the photos and seconds it needs
    • One or two photos in, vertical MP4 out, with the finished file kept privately in account history
    • Per-tool model choice where the template allows it - GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1 for stills, Kling 3.0 Pro and Seedance 2.5 for video, Wan 2.2 I2V Fast behind the product-film preset
    • Credit cost quoted per generation before you start, with a stated policy that confirmed failures return reserved credits
    • Three metered subscription tiers ($9.90 / $29.90 / $59.90 a month, ~17% off annually) and three one-time packs ($15 / $45 / $90) that do not auto-renew
    • Email-and-password accounts with an Accept All / Reject All cookie banner and analytics held until consent
    • Blog and model guides written for the same workflows - clip selection, product demos, multi-reference video - rather than thin SEO filler

    • A real refund policy exists at /refund-policy (updated 2026-11-13): a full refund within 7 days of buying a pack if none of its credits have been used, 365-day pack validity, credits returned for a confirmed failed generation, and a reviewed credit refund if you are unhappy with a delivered clip
    • Per-model credit costs are printed rather than hidden behind a plan: 5 credits per image on GPT Image 2.6 and Nano Banana 2.1, 60 credits per 5-second Kling 3.0 Pro clip, 100 for Seedance 2.5, 10 per product-film generation, with the model named in each tool
    • Prices agree across surfaces: the English pricing page, the /zh/pricing mirror and the refund policy all print $9.90 / $29.90 / $59.90 monthly, $99 / $299 / $599 yearly and $15 / $45 / $90 for the 200 / 800 / 2,000-credit packs, and the stated ~17% annual saving reconciles to the cent on every tier
    • Output is private by default: reference images and finished films sit in private Cloudflare R2 storage, playback and download require the owning account, and the site states there is no public gallery
    • Failures are handled in policy and in copy: rejected inputs are stopped before generation, and the pricing page promises credits back for confirmed failures with ambiguous requests checked before they are resolved

    • The catalogue is large and concrete - roughly sixty templates, each printing its photo count and duration, from Kling and Seedance video scenes to still-image tools

    Cons

    • No registered company, trading name, postal address or named data controller appears anywhere on the site, and the Terms carry no governing-law, jurisdiction, venue or arbitration clause - 'company', 'entity', 'jurisdiction', 'governing', 'laws of' and 'controller' all occur zero times across /privacy-policy and /terms-of-service

    Frequently asked questions

    What does ReelBoop cost?

    Two shapes, both metered in credits. Subscriptions are Creator at $9.90/month for 200 credits ($99/year for 2,400, shown as $8.25/month), Pro at $29.90/month or $299/year for 9,600, and Studio at $59.90/month or $599/year for 24,000; the yearly discount is a flat ~17% and plan credits expire at the end of the period. One-time packs are $15 for 200 credits, $45 for 800 and $90 for 2,000, valid 365 days with no auto-renewal. What a clip costs depends on the model the template runs: 60 credits per 5-second Kling 3.0 Pro clip, 100 for Seedance 2.5, 10 for the product-film preset, and 5 for an image. There is no free trial.

    Is there a free plan?

    No. The pricing FAQ states that video generation requires credits, and no page offers a trial grant. The credit chip in the header reads 0 / 600 before sign-in, but that is a display default rather than a published allowance, and no page explains it - so budget either a $9.90 month or a $15 pack to try the product.

    What happens if a generation fails?

    Reserved credits are returned when a generation is confirmed failed. If a request is still processing or its outcome is unknown, the site says it checks the original request before resolving it, and asks you not to submit duplicates. The refund policy adds that a delivered clip you are unhappy with can be raised with support for a reviewed credit refund.

    Can I use the videos commercially?

    The Terms require you to have permission for the photos you upload and say to review outputs 'before sharing or commercial use', which implies commercial use is contemplated, but no page grants a licence in the finished clip or states who owns it. Ask before using an output in paid advertising.

    Do I need an account, and how do I get back in if I lose the password?

    Yes - email and password accounts, and generation is gated behind sign-in. The public config reports password reset and email verification as disabled, so a forgotten password or a typo in the signup address has to be sorted out with support@reelboop.com rather than through the product.
