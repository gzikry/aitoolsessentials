Overview

A meeting assistant for recording, transcribing, summarizing, and extracting follow-up items.

Key features

  • Live transcription during meetings with highlighting
  • OtterPilot auto-joins and records meetings
  • AI meeting summaries with action items
  • Live notes visible to teammates in real time
  • Integrates with Zoom, Meet, Teams calendars

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving sales calls—not a demo prompt. Verify whether live transcription during meetings with highlighting materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to sensitive meetings need policy review so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: basic: Free; 300 monthly transcription minutes, 30 minutes per conversation, and three lifetime audio/video file imports. pro: $16.99/user/month monthly or $8.33/user/month billed annually; 1,200 in-app recording minutes, up to 90 minutes per meeting, 10 monthly file imports, and unlimited storage. business: $30/user/month monthly or $19.99/user/month billed annually; unlimited meetings and in-app recordings, up to four hours per meeting, unlimited file imports subject to the shared-pool note, three concurrent meetings, and enhanced administration. enterprise: Custom quote; includes SSO, SCIM, domain capture, Otter API/webhooks, enterprise controls, custom integrations, and optional HIPAA compliance.

Open verification questions
  • Otter's official materials describe both automatic de-identified model training and settings-based training permissions. Do not reduce this to a blanket 'no training' or blanket 'opt-out' claim without clarifying which processing path and account tier is meant.
  • The pricing page displayed temporary Business promotional pricing alongside standard monthly and annual rates; promotional figures should not be presented as durable list pricing.

The repository's Basic allowance of 300 minutes per month and Pro limit of 1,200 minutes are still supported. The repository's Business price of $30/user/month is the monthly rate, but the official annual rate is currently $19.99/user/month. Otter's official Privacy & Security page says it automatically trains proprietary models on de-identified recordings and transcripts; the data is encrypted and not manually reviewed as part of that automatic method. A separate Feedback and Training setting allows users to permit or revoke Otter and third-party-provider access to conversations for training and product improvement, including human review. Selecting 'Don't allow sharing' removes access to past conversations and excludes future conversations under that setting. Otter states that its external AI service providers do not train on or retain customer data sent through their APIs. Customers retain ownership of User Content and grant Otter a service-operating license. Workspace or organization administrators may own and control data placed under an organizational account, including migrated pre-existing data. Users are solely responsible under the terms for legally required recording notices and consent. Otter also provides pre-meeting notices, in-meeting announcements, and an Enterprise Microsoft Teams affirmative-permission flow.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Frequently asked questions

Otter vs Fireflies?

Otter leads on live in-meeting transcription; Fireflies on post-meeting intelligence and CRM sync. Both have free tiers worth trying.

How accurate is Otter?

Accuracy varies with microphones, accents, crosstalk, and room noise. Test it on a real team call before relying on transcripts.

Does Otter work with Zoom?

Yes — OtterPilot joins and transcribes Zoom, Meet, and Teams automatically.
