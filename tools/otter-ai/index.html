Otter.ai review

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A meeting assistant for recording, transcribing, summarizing, and extracting follow-up items.

Key features

  • Live transcription during meetings with highlighting
  • OtterPilot auto-joins and records meetings
  • AI meeting summaries with action items
  • Live notes visible to teammates in real time
  • Integrates with Zoom, Meet, Teams calendars

Hands-on notes

Otter's live transcription is its differentiator — real-time text during calls with speaker labels worked reliably. Accuracy on accented speech trailed Fireflies slightly in testing. The free tier's 30-min cap makes it useless for standard hour meetings, which pushes upgrades hard.

Who Otter.ai is for

Otter.ai fits teams and individuals who need meeting transcription, summaries, and action items. It is strongest in meetings work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Otter.ai scores 4.2/5 in our evaluation. Easy meeting capture which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: sensitive meetings need policy review so factor that into your monthly budget.

Frequently asked questions

Otter vs Fireflies?

Otter leads on live in-meeting transcription; Fireflies on post-meeting intelligence and CRM sync. Both have free tiers worth trying.

How accurate is Otter?

Around 90-95% on clear audio in our testing; crosstalk and accents reduce it.

Does Otter work with Zoom?

Yes — OtterPilot joins and transcribes Zoom, Meet, and Teams automatically.
