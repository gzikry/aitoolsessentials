Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: Official support says the chat product has a free plan with unlimited Smart Agent access, limited premium-agent/model queries, and paid plans with greater access, but it does not state current chat-plan prices. The current public pricing page primarily lists APIs: Web Search $5/1,000 calls, Contents $1/1,000 pages, Answer $5/1,000 calls, Research from $12/1,000 calls, and Finance Research from $110/1,000 calls, with a $100 introductory credit.

Open verification questions
  • Current consumer chat-plan names and prices were not published on the reviewed public pricing page.
  • The output assignment does not cover third-party output, whose separate terms may apply.
  • Commercial-use confirmation located in official support expressly concerns the API, not every consumer-chat use case.

Official materials document Smart Agent, premium agents, multiple third-party models, custom agents, file handling, and search/research APIs. The privacy policy says prompts and user-created/shared content sent to third-party AI providers are not used to develop, improve, or train those third-party models. Separately, You.com's terms permit You.com to use Content to provide, maintain, train, and improve its own services and develop products. The terms assign You.com's rights in outputs to the user, subject to applicable law and excluding third-party output. Official API documentation expressly permits commercial applications when the terms are followed and API keys and user data are secured.

Frequently asked questions

You.com vs Perplexity?

They combine cited AI search with chat. Perplexity is more search-first; You.com emphasizes model choice in one subscription. Compare both on the same research question.

Which models does You.com include?

GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Llama and others depending on plan.

Is there a free tier?

Yes, with limited Pro searches daily.

What is You.com best for?

You.com focuses on AI-assisted search with source visibility, plus writing and coding assistance on paid plans. It suits users who want search-first answers instead of a general chat assistant.

Does You.com have a free plan?

Yes. A free tier covers basic searches and limited AI chats; Pro unlocks premium models and higher limits. Check you.com's pricing page for current limits.

Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

Run the same job, not a demo.

Use one representative ai search, research agents, and productivity-oriented web answers task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

01 · Same input

Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

02 · Measure time

Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

03 · Score the result

Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

04 · Price the run

Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
