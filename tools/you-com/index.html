Overview

An AI search and assistant platform for web answers, research, productivity, and agent-style tasks.

Key features

  • AI search with citations like Perplexity
  • Model choice: GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama in one UI

Hands-on notes

You.com blends Perplexity-style search with multi-model chat — both worked adequately but neither led its category in testing. Its niche is wanting search + model choice in one subscription. Citation hygiene was slightly weaker than Perplexity's in side-by-sides.

Who You.com is for

You.com fits teams and individuals who need ai search, research agents, and productivity-oriented web answers. It is strongest in research work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: You.com scores 4.2/5 in our evaluation. Search-first workflow which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: needs source verification so factor that into your monthly budget.
