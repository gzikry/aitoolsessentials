Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: For US personal accounts, Gemini Free is $0 per month, Google AI Pro is $19.99 per month, and Google AI Ultra starts at $99.99 per month, with a $199.99 option offering higher limits. For organizations, Gemini is included as a core service in eligible Google Workspace editions; official US Workspace rates are $8.40/$7 per user per month for Business Starter, $16.80/$14 for Business Standard, and $26.40/$22 for Business Plus on flexible/annual plans respectively. Enterprise pricing is sales-assisted.
