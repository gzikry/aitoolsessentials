Overview

Google’s AI assistant with strong multimodal capabilities and a natural fit for people already working in Google products.

Key features

  • Deep integration with Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Drive
  • Huge context window handles very long documents and video
  • Deep Research generates multi-step cited reports
  • Native image and video understanding
  • Bundled storage makes the Pro plan a double value

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving google workspace drafting—not a demo prompt. Verify whether deep integration with gmail, docs, sheets, and drive materially reduces editing or setup time. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Who Gemini is for

Gemini fits teams and individuals who need google-native ai assistance across search, docs, gmail, android, and multimodal tasks. It is strongest in general ai assistant work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Gemini wins on Google ecosystem integration and long-context; ChatGPT on ecosystem breadth beyond Google. See our comparisons for task-level results.
