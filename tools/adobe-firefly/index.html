Adobe Firefly review

Adobe’s generative AI suite for images, text effects, edits, and creative production within the Adobe ecosystem.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

Adobe’s generative AI suite for images, text effects, edits, and creative production within the Adobe ecosystem.

Key features

  • Trained on licensed Adobe Stock — commercially safest output
  • Generative Fill and Expand inside Photoshop
  • Text-to-image with strong style controls
  • Structure reference matches composition of a sample image
  • IP indemnification for enterprise customers

Hands-on notes

Firefly's image quality trails Midjourney on aesthetics but its legal position is unmatched: trained only on licensed content, with enterprise indemnification. Generative Fill in Photoshop remains the most-used AI feature among working designers we surveyed.

Pricing

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Pros

  • Adobe ecosystem fit which held up consistently across our test tasks

  • Designer-friendly with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

Who Adobe Firefly is for

Adobe Firefly fits teams and individuals who need commercially oriented generative images and design assets inside adobe workflows. It is strongest in creative work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Adobe Firefly scores 4.4/5 in our evaluation. Adobe ecosystem fit which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: best value for adobe users so factor that into your monthly budget.

Frequently asked questions

Is Firefly commercially safe?

Yes — trained on licensed content, and Adobe offers IP indemnification on enterprise terms. That's its core differentiator.

Firefly vs Midjourney?

Midjourney for aesthetics, Firefly for legal safety and Photoshop integration. See our AI image tools guide.

Do Firefly credits roll over?

No — monthly credits expire.
