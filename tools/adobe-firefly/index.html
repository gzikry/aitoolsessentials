Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-21

Current official summary: Official US monthly pricing lists Firefly Standard at US$9.99 with 2,000 generative credits/month; Firefly Pro at US$19.99 with 4,000 credits/month; Firefly Pro Plus regularly US$49.99 with 10,000 credits/month; and Firefly Premium regularly US$199.99 with 50,000 credits/month. On the verification date, Pro Plus and Premium had first-year 30% promotions of US$34.97 and US$139.91 per month, ending 2026-10-21. Standard image features are unlimited; credits cover premium features such as video, audio, and partner-model generations. Promotional unlimited access to selected models applies for the first year under the offer terms.

Open verification questions
  • The exact free-plan credit allocation was not exposed consistently on the public pricing page and is therefore not stated.
  • Promotional unlimited-generation model coverage can change and is governed by Adobe's current promotion page and offer terms.
  • Adobe does not warrant that output is non-infringing, unique, or copyrightable.
  • IP indemnification is plan- and contract-specific, particularly for enterprise offerings, and should not be assumed for every Firefly subscription or beta feature.

Adobe's official Firefly FAQ and policy statements say Adobe does not train Firefly foundation models on customer content; current Firefly models use licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public-domain content. Adobe's General Terms say users retain rights and ownership in their Content and Adobe does not claim ownership. The Generative AI User Guidelines state that output may generally be used commercially; if Adobe specifically designates a beta feature as non-commercial, output from that feature is personal-use only. Credits renew monthly and do not roll over.

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.
