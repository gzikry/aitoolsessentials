    Pricing

    Official sources checked 2026-10-01

    Current official summary: Official US monthly pricing (re-confirmed 2026-10-01) lists Firefly Standard at US$9.99 with 2,000 generative credits/month; Firefly Pro at US$19.99 with 4,000 credits/month; Firefly Pro Plus at US$49.99 with 10,000 credits/month; and Firefly Premium at US$199.99 with 50,000 credits/month and unlimited Firefly Video Model access. All paid tiers include unlimited standard image and vector generations; credits cover premium features such as video, audio, and partner-model generations. The first-year promotional rates recorded on 2026-08-25 (US$34.97 Pro Plus and US$139.91 Premium) were no longer displayed on the official pricing page on 2026-10-01.

    Open verification questions
    • The exact free-plan credit allocation was not exposed consistently on the public pricing page and is therefore not stated.
    • Promotional pricing and unlimited-generation model coverage can change and are governed by Adobe's current promotion page and offer terms.
    • Adobe does not warrant that output is non-infringing, unique, or copyrightable.
    • IP indemnification is plan- and contract-specific, particularly for enterprise offerings, and should not be assumed for every Firefly subscription or beta feature.

    Adobe's official Firefly FAQ and policy statements say Adobe does not train Firefly foundation models on customer content; current Firefly models use licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public-domain content. Adobe's General Terms say users retain rights and ownership in their Content and Adobe does not claim ownership. The Generative AI User Guidelines state that output may generally be used commercially; if Adobe specifically designates a beta feature as non-commercial, output from that feature is personal-use only. Credits renew monthly and do not roll over. Re-verified 2026-10-01: the four regular monthly plan prices and credit allocations are unchanged. The 30% first-year promotional rates recorded on 2026-08-25 were no longer shown on the pricing page, so only the regular list rates are carried in the summary.
