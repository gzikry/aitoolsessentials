    GTA 6 AI Video (gta6aivideo.com) review

    GTA 6 AI Video turns a text prompt or one photo into a 5- or 10-second GTA 6-style clip in the browser. You pick one of eight prepared Vice City-style scenes, adjust who is in the shot, what happens, the camera, the time of day, the format (9:16, 16:9 or 1:1) and the length, or start from your own prompt; then sign in with Google and spend credits to render. Finished clips play in the page and download as 480p MP4 with generated sound and no watermark added by the site, and the page shows the prompt and settings used for the sample it plays. Building, editing and copying prompts, plus a small prompt library with the clip each prompt produced, are free and need no account, and there are guide pages for a fan trailer, a farewell montage and a loading-screen clip. New accounts start with 0 credits, so nothing renders without buying a pack: 100 credits for $9.99, 300 for $29.49 or 600 for $58.49, one-time, with a 5-second clip costing 15 credits and a 10-second clip 30. Uploaded photos are used as the first frame, and the site says they are sent to its AI video provider and deleted from its storage within 24 hours, and that uploads are not used to train AI models; generated clips are unlisted, so anyone with the link can open one. The site states plainly that it is a fan-made tool and not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is GTA 6 AI Video?

    A browser-based generator that makes 5- or 10-second GTA 6-style clips from a text prompt or from one uploaded photo used as the first frame. You choose one of eight prepared Vice City-style scenes or write your own prompt, set the camera, time of day, format and length, then sign in and spend credits to render. Finished clips download as 480p MP4 with generated sound. Building and copying prompts is free and needs no account.

    How much does it cost?

    One-time credit packs only, with no subscription: 100 credits for $9.99, 300 for $29.49 or 600 for $58.49 in USD. A 5-second clip costs 15 credits and a 10-second clip 30, from a prompt or from a photo, so a 5-second render is roughly $1.46-$1.50 depending on pack. New accounts start with 0 credits, so there is no free render, and failed renders are refunded to your balance automatically.

    Is this an official GTA 6 tool?

    No. The site states that it is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, and it tells users not to use official logos, music or character names and to label posts as AI-generated fan content. The domain was registered on 2026-11-19, the day GTA 6 launched. The underlying video model is not named - the policies refer only to 'our AI video provider'.

    What happens to my photo and my clips?

    An uploaded photo becomes the first frame of the clip, is sent to the site's AI video provider, and the site says it is deleted from its storage within 24 hours and is not used to train AI models. Generated clips are unlisted, so anyone with the link can open one, and you can request deletion of your data or delete the whole account from the account page.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, within 14 days of purchase, if none of the pack's credits have been spent, if you were charged twice or bought the same pack by mistake, if you paid but the credits never arrived, or if the generator was broken in a way that stopped you using them. If part of a pack is used, the unused share can be refunded on request. Credits already spent on clips that rendered successfully are not refundable.
