    Overview

    CrowdWisdomTrading aggregates publicly shared views from experienced traders into daily and weekly research setups, each with an entry price, a stop and a target. It positions itself against Discord trading groups by grading every call in public, misses included, and it operates as research only - you execute at your own broker. The corporate disclosure is the best we have seen from a small submission in months: the terms name Tsuroni LTD as the operator, set Israeli governing law with Tel Aviv-Yafo courts, cap liability at a stated amount, and the privacy policy is drafted to GDPR/CCPA expectations with named retention logic for billing records. It loads no third-party trackers. The caution is the usual one for this product class: the 'pro traders' count and the public track record are self-published figures we cannot verify, and the terms themselves say the outputs are educational research, not investment advice.

    Pros

    • Named operator and real legal footing: Tsuroni LTD, laws of the State of Israel, Tel Aviv-Yafo courts, and a stated liability cap - rare among one-person research sites
    • A genuinely usable free tier (weekly outlook plus 20 free setups, no card) and a $9.99 one-time pack route, so the subscription is not forced
    • Finance-risk disclaimers are on the record throughout - substantial-risk warnings, past-performance language, and an instruction to verify outputs with licensed professionals

    Cons

    • The headline '21,576+ pro traders' and the public track record are self-published figures that cannot be independently verified - treat them as marketing until proven
    • It is aggregated research, not advice and not execution: the value depends entirely on the unnamed contributors' inputs, and the aggregation methodology is not detailed
    • Payments run through Whop checkout rather than a mainstream merchant page, which some buyers will find unfamiliar
    • The aggregation uses AI on public posts; the terms themselves direct you to official market data and licensed professionals before acting

    Frequently asked questions

    Is CrowdWisdomTrading financial advice?

    No. Its own terms describe the output as educational research, warn that trading involves substantial risk, state that past performance does not guarantee future results, and instruct users to verify outputs with official market data and licensed professionals. You place your own trades at your own broker.

    What does CrowdWisdomTrading cost?

    Free weekly outlook plus 20 setups after signup. A one-time $9.99 pack buys 10 setup credits with no subscription. Pro is $29.99/month with taxes included, billed yearly at $179.99 (advertised as half the monthly-billed total).

    Who is behind CrowdWisdomTrading?

    The terms name Tsuroni LTD as the operating company, governed by the laws of the State of Israel with exclusive jurisdiction in Tel Aviv-Yafo courts. The domain has been registered since 2014.
