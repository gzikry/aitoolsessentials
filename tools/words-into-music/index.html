    Overview

    A browser song generator: describe an idea or paste your own lyrics, choose genre, instruments, vocal style, language (English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese) and a 60-180 second target length, preview and edit the lyric draft, then generate a recording that stays in a private library and downloads as MP3. Writing tools (lyrics, titles, prompts) cost no song credits, and the same credit balance is exposed through a remote MCP server and an OpenAPI-described REST API plus CLI.

    Key features

    • Song generation from a written brief or supplied lyrics
    • Genre, mood, instrument and vocal-style controls
    • Five song languages (English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese), 60-180 second target length
    • Editable lyric draft before generation
    • Private library with MP3 download and per-recording delete
    • Lyric, song-title and music-prompt writing tools at no song-credit cost
    • Remote MCP server for ChatGPT, Claude and Grok
    • REST API (OpenAPI 3.0.3) and Node.js CLI with 90-day keys

    Trial checklist

    Generate one song from a two-line brief and one from your own lyrics, then check the three things a demo page cannot tell you: whether the vocal pronounces names and non-English words correctly, whether the delivered length lands near the requested duration, and whether editing the lyric draft carries into the recording instead of producing a fresh take you have to pay for.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does one song credit buy?

    One delivered audio generation, up to about three minutes long. Writing a lyric draft or a title costs nothing, and generating a new take costs another credit. The Terms restore a reserved credit once when a confirmed technical failure prevents delivery.

    How much does Words into Music cost?

    Starter is $9.99/month or $59.99/year for 30 songs a month, Creator $19.99/month or $119.99/year for 65, and Studio $29.99/month or $179.99/year for 100. One-time packs are 20 songs for $9.99, 40 for $19.99 and 90 for $39.99. Annual plans are paid upfront and release the allowance monthly.

    Can the songs be used commercially?

    The Terms allow personal or commercial use of your output and assign the vendor's transferable rights in it to you, while warning that AI output may not be copyrightable everywhere, may resemble someone else's output, and is not guaranteed to pass streaming or distribution review.

    Is there a free trial?

    One introductory song for an eligible verified account rather than a recurring free tier, and a claimed trial is recorded so it cannot be claimed repeatedly.

    Who operates the site?

    It is presented as 'a Savuko product', with contact@wordsintomusic.com and contact@savuko.com published. No company, postal address or governing law is named in the product's own terms.
