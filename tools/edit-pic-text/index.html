    Edit Pic Text review

    Edit Pic Text rewrites the words already inside a picture instead of laying new text on top: you upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB, the editor outlines the text it detects, you type the replacement - or leave it blank to remove the words - and the result comes back with the surrounding typeface, weight and background redrawn so the edit does not look pasted on. Instructions in plain English ('keep the logo', 'match the bold style') steer harder edits, and exports go to 1K, 2K or 4K. Four sibling pages cover the jobs people actually search for - screenshot text editing, OCR-style extraction that keeps headings and tables, translation into 110 languages with the original layout, and sharpening blurry text - each with a Chinese mirror. The workflow is honest about cost (5 credits per edit, shown before you send) and about limits (IDs, passports, receipts and payment screenshots are refused). What keeps it below the rest of the catalog is the paperwork around the product: the pricing page's comparison table prices video models this site does not sell, the same table charges 3 credits for an edit the editor bills at 5, the FAQ says refunds stop at 50 used credits while the refund policy says 10, and no company, address or jurisdiction is named anywhere.

    Overview

    Edit Pic Text rewrites the words already inside a picture instead of laying new text on top: you upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB, the editor outlines the text it detects, you type the replacement - or leave it blank to remove the words - and the result comes back with the surrounding typeface, weight and background redrawn so the edit does not look pasted on. Instructions in plain English ('keep the logo', 'match the bold style') steer harder edits, and exports go to 1K, 2K or 4K. Four sibling pages cover the jobs people actually search for - screenshot text editing, OCR-style extraction that keeps headings and tables, translation into 110 languages with the original layout, and sharpening blurry text - each with a Chinese mirror. The workflow is honest about cost (5 credits per edit, shown before you send) and about limits (IDs, passports, receipts and payment screenshots are refused). What keeps it below the rest of the catalog is the paperwork around the product: the pricing page's comparison table prices video models this site does not sell, the same table charges 3 credits for an edit the editor bills at 5, the FAQ says refunds stop at 50 used credits while the refund policy says 10, and no company, address or jurisdiction is named anywhere.

    Key features

    • Automatic text detection that outlines every text block in the upload, with click-to-select replacement and blank-to-remove
    • Font, size, colour and background reconstruction so the new words match the original artwork instead of sitting on top of it
    • Plain-English extra instructions ('keep the logo', 'match the bold style') applied to the selected area only
    • Input JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB; export at 1K, 2K or 4K depending on aspect ratio
    • Screenshot text editor page for app, chat and webpage captures, with the interface and spacing preserved
    • Text extraction that keeps headings, paragraphs, lists and tables intact, copyable or downloadable as .txt
    • Image translation into 110 languages with the picture returned in the original font and layout, then editable line by line
    • Blurry-text enhancer that boxes the words you need and upscales to 2K or 4K before extraction
    • Credit meter printed before each run: 5 credits for an edit, translation or enhance at 1K, 1 credit for extraction
    • Google sign-in with Cloudflare Turnstile, edit history you can revisit, and Stripe checkout on paid plans

    Frequently asked questions

    What is Edit Pic Text?

    A browser AI image text editor that changes the words already inside a photo, screenshot or design instead of adding a new text layer. You upload a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 30MB, the editor outlines the text it detects, you type the replacement (or leave it blank to delete the words), and it redraws them in the original font, size and colour with the background repaired. Paid plans add a commercial licence and watermark-free output; exports go to 1K, 2K or 4K.

    How much does Edit Pic Text cost?

    Monthly plans are Basic $11.90 for 100 credits, Standard $29.90 for 300 and Pro $79.90 for 1,050, with yearly billing at $118.80, $238.80 and $358.80 a year (shown as $9.90, $19.90 and $29.90 a month). One-time credit packs run from $9.90 for 80 credits to $199 for 2,500 and stay valid 183 days, and a $4.99 Starter Pass gives 40 credits for 7 days. An edit, translation or enhance costs 5 credits at 1K and text extraction costs 1, so a $11.90 month is about 20 edits.

    Is there a free tier?

    Yes, and it is small but recurring. Signing in with Google grants 3 credits, and a daily check-in ladder adds 3, 3, 6, 3, 3, 3 and 9 over the first week for 30 in total - which is the number behind the 'Get 30 credits for free' badge. No card is required to start. Extraction at 1 credit is the cheapest way to test whether the OCR holds up on your own files.

    Can I edit text in a screenshot, poster or product photo?

    That is the core use case. The screenshot page keeps the interface, icons and spacing intact while you swap a label or heading; the poster and product-label path is for prices, dates and offers on images whose design file you no longer have. What the site refuses is documents: IDs, passports, certificates, receipts and payment screenshots are excluded in its own documentation, and edits meant to mislead are not allowed.

    Does it work in languages other than English?

    The site says English and other Latin-alphabet languages work best and that Chinese, Japanese and Hindi are supported, and it sells a dedicated translation page that returns the image in the original font and layout across 110 languages. Because the tool redraws type rather than overlaying it, quality depends on the script and the original lettering - very small text, curved text and heavily stylised lettering are the cases it warns may need several attempts.

    What should I check before paying?

    Two things on the site itself. Ask which refund threshold applies, because the pricing FAQ says a refund survives up to 50 used credits while the refund policy says 10. And read the plan you are buying carefully: the comparison table on the pricing page prices video models this product does not sell and puts an edit at 3 credits where the editor charges 5, so the editor's own meter is the number to trust.
