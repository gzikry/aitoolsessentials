Frequently asked questions

Is Heidi Health free?

The official pricing page lists a Free plan. Paid Clinician and Teams plans are also listed. Exact USD amounts can vary by region and were not consistently exposed during this check, so verify the live pricing page.

Is Heidi Health HIPAA compliant?

Do not treat any vendor badge as a substitute for your organization's legal, privacy, and IT review. Confirm contracts, BAA availability, retention, and approved use before putting patient data into the product.

Does Heidi replace a clinician?

No. Use it as a documentation draft assistant. Licensed professionals remain responsible for diagnosis, treatment, coding, and the medical record.
