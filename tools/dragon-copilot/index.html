    How much does Dragon Copilot cost?

    Microsoft's official product page does not publish a self-serve consumer price. Microsoft Learn describes per-user and Flex licenses sold through Microsoft or a certified partner. Get a quote; do not rely on third-party street prices.

    Is this the same as Nuance DAX?

    Microsoft's official materials present Dragon Copilot as the current clinical assistant product and discuss migration from Dragon Medical One. Treat older DAX price quotes as historical unless Microsoft restates them.

    Can a solo clinic buy it online?

    Not from a public checkout page verified in this check. Official next steps route to a Microsoft representative.
