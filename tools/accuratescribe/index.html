    AccurateScribe.ai review

    AccurateScribe.ai is a transcription and subtitle service that turns audio or video into text in 134+ languages, with speaker identification, SRT/VTT subtitle output and export to DOCX, PDF, TXT and CSV. Its commercial model is unusually simple for this class: a free tier that returns partial transcripts, then one unlimited plan at $9.99 a month billed yearly ($119.99) or $19.99 month-to-month, rather than the minutes-metered ladders most competitors sell. The product has been live since November 2024 and is distributed well beyond the website - a docs subdomain, an iOS app, a Chrome extension, a Microsoft Store listing, a ChatGPT app and a Google Workspace add-on - and its privacy policy is unusually specific about the parts that matter: original uploads are deleted after processing, extracted audio is kept 30 days, transcripts stay under the account holder's control, and it states that recordings and transcripts are not used to train its own or third parties' models and are not human-reviewed. What the site does not say is who runs it: no company, postal address, jurisdiction or governing-law clause appears on any page, the refund policy is 'final and non-refundable, regardless of whether the service has been used', and the pricing page runs a permanent countdown on a 'New Customer Offer' alongside a corporate-logo band it describes as customers.

    Overview

    AccurateScribe.ai is a transcription and subtitle service that turns audio or video into text in 134+ languages, with speaker identification, SRT/VTT subtitle output and export to DOCX, PDF, TXT and CSV. Its commercial model is unusually simple for this class: a free tier that returns partial transcripts, then one unlimited plan at $9.99 a month billed yearly ($119.99) or $19.99 month-to-month, rather than the minutes-metered ladders most competitors sell. The product has been live since November 2024 and is distributed well beyond the website - a docs subdomain, an iOS app, a Chrome extension, a Microsoft Store listing, a ChatGPT app and a Google Workspace add-on - and its privacy policy is unusually specific about the parts that matter: original uploads are deleted after processing, extracted audio is kept 30 days, transcripts stay under the account holder's control, and it states that recordings and transcripts are not used to train its own or third parties' models and are not human-reviewed. What the site does not say is who runs it: no company, postal address, jurisdiction or governing-law clause appears on any page, the refund policy is 'final and non-refundable, regardless of whether the service has been used', and the pricing page runs a permanent countdown on a 'New Customer Offer' alongside a corporate-logo band it describes as customers.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is AccurateScribe.ai?

    A transcription and subtitle service. You upload audio or video (up to 10 hours and 5GB per file), paste a public URL, or record in the browser, and it returns text in 134+ languages with speaker identification and export to DOCX, PDF, TXT, CSV, SRT or VTT. It also converts media to MP3/WAV and includes a screen recorder that transcribes what it captures. It has been live since November 2024 and ships as a web app, an iOS app, a Chrome extension, a Microsoft Store app, a ChatGPT app and a Google Workspace add-on.

    How much does AccurateScribe.ai cost?

    One paid plan, unlimited: $9.99/month billed annually at $119.99/year, or $19.99 month-to-month. There is no minutes allowance to track on either - 'Unlimited transcription usage' and 'Unlimited subtitle generation' are listed on both cards. A free tier returns partial transcripts and basic features without a card. The same three figures ($9.99, $119.99, $19.99) appear on the /ar/pricing locale mirror as on the English page. PayPal is named on the pricing page.

    Is there a free plan?

    Yes, but it is a restricted one rather than a full trial: the FAQ describes it as letting free users 'view partial transcripts and use basic features'. That is enough to judge accuracy on your own audio; it is not enough to produce a finished transcript.

    Who owns my transcripts?

    You keep your rights in the recordings, files and transcripts you upload or generate - the Terms say 'You retain your rights to the recordings, files and transcripts you upload'. But the licence the site takes is thin on the output side: it grants you 'a limited, non-exclusive license' to use the service and to download and store results, and does not state affirmatively that the transcript is yours to license onward. If you need output ownership in writing for client work, ask them before you rely on it.

    Does AccurateScribe train AI models on my recordings?

    It says no, and extends the denial to its processors: 'We do not use this content to train, fine-tune, evaluate or improve our own or third parties' AI models, and we do not permit third-party service providers processing this content on our behalf to use it for those purposes.' It also states there is no human review of recordings or transcripts. What it does not state is what OpenAI - named as its AI service provider - does with the audio under OpenAI's own terms.

    How long are my files kept?

    The policy gives actual periods rather than a phrase: original uploads are deleted automatically after processing completes; MP3 files extracted or generated during processing are retained 30 days by default and then deleted; transcript text and the audio associated with results stay in your account for you to manage and delete, and the policy says the 30-day default does not apply to them. Deletion is described as permanent, with no backups.

    Can I get a refund?

    No. The return policy is explicit that 'all purchases are final and non-refundable, regardless of whether the service has been used', and that 'canceling a subscription does not generate a refund for the current billing period'. You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals. If you are in the UK or EU, a blanket no-refund term on a digital service may not be enforceable against you, but nothing on the site offers a cooling-off period - test on the free tier before paying.

    Who runs AccurateScribe.ai?

    The site does not say. No company name, registered address, jurisdiction or governing-law clause appears on any page, and the footer carries only a copyright line. The operator also runs WhisperTranscribe.ai and WhisperTranscript.ai - the three share a legal template, a tool route set, a mail provider, a hosting address and a registrar, and accuratescribe.com redirects to this domain - and both Whisper brands are held from listing pending verification of their names and of a single-listing-or-several decision.

    Does AccurateScribe have an affiliate programme?

    Yes, and it is published on the site: 40% commission on subscriptions, payouts weekly on Fridays by PayPal or bank, no follower requirement, and real-time conversion analytics. The page also states 'Our partners earn an average of $10,000+ monthly', which is an earnings claim with no basis published behind it.
