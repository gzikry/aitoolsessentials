    Frequently asked questions

    Is Tunii free?

    Yes. 10 free minutes on signup, plus 5 free minutes every week. Purchased minutes never expire.

    What file formats does Tunii support?

    MP3, WAV, M4A, and FLAC up to 100 MB and 10 minutes long.

    Can Tunii separate drums, bass, and piano?

    Yes. The AI Stem Splitter separates vocals, drums, bass, piano, guitar, and other available stems.
