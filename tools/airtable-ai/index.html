Overview

AI capabilities inside Airtable for summarizing records, categorizing data, drafting text, and powering operational workflows.

Key features

  • AI fields generate/enrich data per record automatically
  • Ask across your base: natural-language queries of records
  • Summarize and categorize records in bulk
  • Cobuilder: describe an app, get a working base

Hands-on notes

Airtable AI shines on data enrichment — categorizing 500 records or drafting per-record content ran as a column operation, which no chat tool matches. Cobuilder produced usable app skeletons from descriptions. Credit burn on large bases is real; monitor usage.
