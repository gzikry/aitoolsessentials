    Overview

    AI Genjutsu (aigenjutsu.io) is a template-driven photo-to-video site operated by Springtide Labs, LLC, a Delaware company. You pick one of 41 ready-made scenes - pet dances, glow-up transformations, meme effects, action and seasonal looks - add the photo it asks for, and generate a short clip without writing a prompt; each template shows its credit price, example and required inputs first. Most classic templates produce a 5-second vertical 9:16 silent MP4 at 720p (a few at 1080p); four 'lion story' templates (Gold Card Lion, Private Jet Lion, Neon Lion, Hotel Lobby Human-Lion Duet) keep the original performance's duration and complete soundtrack. An AI Video Recreator mode rebuilds a user-supplied 3-30 second reference clip with up to two character photos. Generation runs on third-party models through the APIMart API - Seedance 2.0 Mini/Fast, Kling 2.6/2.6 Pro and Seedream 5.0 Flash for images, with H3 and Seedance 2.5 on the Recreator. There is no free tier; plans run $5.99-$24.99 a month and one-time packs start at $1.99. It is a different product and operator from the Genjutsu AI workspace at aigenjutsu.app, and the site states it is unaffiliated with Higgsfield and other Genjutsu sites.

    Key features

    • 41 ready-made templates across pets, glow-up/transform, funny/pranks, action/cars, lion stories, music/rap, seasonal, dance and friends/couples, each with an example, required inputs and a visible credit price
    • Classic output: 5-second vertical 9:16 silent MP4 at 720p (Pet to Human and a few others at 1080p); 5 or 8 credits per video
    • Four lion-story templates (Gold Card Lion, Private Jet Lion, Neon Lion at 45 credits; Hotel Lobby Human-Lion Duet at 60) that keep the original performance duration and complete soundtrack
    • AI Video Recreator: a user-supplied 3-30s MP4 reference (up to 100 MB) plus up to two character photos; 20-credit analysis then a model/seconds-based generation quote (H3 up to 15s, Seedance 2.5 over 15s); retains reference duration and soundtrack when present
    • Per-template model disclosure: Seedance 2.0 Mini/Fast, Kling 2.6/2.6 Pro, Seedream 5.0 Flash via the APIMart API
    • Monthly and yearly plans plus one-time never-expiring credit packs; one-month rollover capped at two months' allowance; automatic credit return on failed or blocked jobs
    • Google sign-in, private-by-default outputs in My videos, Stripe Managed Payments / Waffo checkout

    Frequently asked questions

    What does AI Genjutsu cost?

    No free tier. Monthly plans: Basic $5.99 (60 credits), Plus $9.99 (120), Pro $24.99 (320). Yearly is 20% off, billed once ($57.50/$95.90/$239.90) with credits still arriving monthly. One-time packs never expire: 10/$1.99, 40/$5.99, 120/$14.99, 360/$34.99. Classic templates cost 5 or 8 credits per video, lion stories 45 and the human-lion duet 60; the Recreator charges a 20-credit analysis plus a generation quote. Failed or safety-blocked jobs return credits automatically. USD, tax excluded.

    Is there a free trial?

    No. The FAQ states 'AI Genjutsu has no free videos or trial credits.' The smallest purchase is a 10-credit pack for $1.99, which covers two 5-credit videos. A successful video you dislike is not refunded; only failed or moderated generations return credits.

    Is this the same as Genjutsu AI at aigenjutsu.app?

    No. They are different products on different infrastructure. aigenjutsu.io (this listing) is a template-driven photo-to-video site run by Springtide Labs, LLC; aigenjutsu.app is a reference-video motion-transfer workspace we list separately as Genjutsu AI. This site's own FAQ says it is 'not affiliated with Higgsfield or with other sites or apps that use the word Genjutsu.' The domain is printed in the title so readers can tell them apart.

    Do the videos have sound?

    Classic templates produce silent 5-second vertical MP4s. The four lion-story templates keep the original performance's complete soundtrack, and the Recreator retains a reference clip's soundtrack when present. The site says it has not verified the original creator of the Sheru Bhai trend and disclaims clearance of third-party rights in AI output, so ask what licence covers a lion-story soundtrack before posting it publicly.

    Who runs it and what happens to my photos?

    Springtide Labs, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, operates the site; support is support@aigenjutsu.io. Source photos are deleted when the job ends, including failed jobs; outputs are private by default and are not published without permission and review. Account and transaction records are retained for service operation, dispute handling and legal obligations with no stated period. Payment is via Stripe Managed Payments or Waffo; AI processing via the APIMart API.

    What can't I upload?

    The Acceptable Use Policy requires you to be 18+ and forbids photos of minors, of celebrities or public figures, NSFW content, and photos of other people without permission. It prohibits pornography, violence/gore, hate speech, child-unsafe content, prohibited deepfakes/impersonation and copyright/trademark infringement, with a report-and-appeal route. Safety-filter rejects fail with a neutral message and refund credits.
