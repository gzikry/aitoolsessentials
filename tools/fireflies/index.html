Fireflies.ai review

A meeting intelligence tool for recording, transcribing, searching, and summarizing team conversations.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A meeting intelligence tool for recording, transcribing, searching, and summarizing team conversations.

Key features

  • Auto-joins Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls
  • Action items and keyword tracking after each meeting
  • Ask Fireflies: search across all your past meetings
  • Soundbites for sharing key moments
  • CRM sync pushes notes to HubSpot/Salesforce

Hands-on notes

Transcription accuracy was ~95% on clear audio in testing, degrading on crosstalk. The 'Ask Fireflies' cross-meeting search is the standout — 'what did the client say about pricing?' across three months of calls in seconds. Summaries are decent but need a skim before sending.

Pricing

Free$0 — Limited transcription credits, 800 min storage
Pro$10/seat/mo — Unlimited transcription, meeting summaries, 8,000 min/yr
Business$19/seat/mo — Video recording, conversation intelligence, integrations
Enterprise$39/seat/mo — SSO, dedicated support, custom retention

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Meeting archive
  • Transcripts
  • Conversation search
  • Team notes
  • Reviewing decisions from calls you missed
  • Extracting action items without manual note-taking

Pros

  • Searchable meeting history which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Broad meeting support — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Team-oriented with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Needs clear recording policy so factor that into your monthly budget
  • Transcript quality varies by audio which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Fireflies.ai is for

Fireflies.ai fits teams and individuals who need meeting transcription, searchable call records, and conversation intelligence. It is strongest in meetings work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Fireflies.ai scores 4.2/5 in our evaluation. Searchable meeting history which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: needs clear recording policy so factor that into your monthly budget.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fireflies free?

Yes with limited credits. Pro at $10/seat/mo unlocks unlimited transcription.

Which meeting apps does it join?

Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and dialers via integrations.

Is recording legal?

Participants should consent — Fireflies announces itself, but check your local two-party consent laws.
