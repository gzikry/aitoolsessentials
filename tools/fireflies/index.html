Overview

A meeting intelligence tool for recording, transcribing, searching, and summarizing team conversations.

Key features

  • Auto-joins Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls
  • Action items and keyword tracking after each meeting
  • Ask Fireflies: search across all your past meetings
  • Soundbites for sharing key moments
  • CRM sync pushes notes to HubSpot/Salesforce

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving meeting archive—not a demo prompt. Verify whether auto-joins zoom, meet, and teams calls materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to needs clear recording policy so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: free: $0; unlimited transcription and AI summaries; 400 minutes of storage per team; 2-hour recording limit. pro: $18/seat/month monthly or $10/seat/month billed annually; 8,000 storage minutes per seat; video recording, downloads, AI Skills, task management, and unlimited integrations. business: $29/seat/month monthly or $19/seat/month billed annually; unlimited storage; 3-hour recording limit; conversation intelligence and team analytics. enterprise: $39/seat/month, annual only; unlimited storage; 4-hour recording limit; SSO/SCIM, HIPAA support, private storage, and custom retention.

Open verification questions
  • Do not claim that the visible bot alone supplies legally sufficient consent; Fireflies places responsibility for obtaining legally required consent on the user.
  • The pricing page lists API access on Free, but exact endpoint entitlements and rate limits should be verified in the API documentation before publication.

The repository's Free-plan claim of limited transcription credits is outdated: the official page now says unlimited transcription and unlimited AI summaries. The repository's 800-minute Free storage claim is outdated: the current official page says 400 minutes per team. Fireflies states that it does not use meeting content or personal data to train internal or external AI models and contractually prohibits vendors from training on that data. Users retain rights in User Content but grant Fireflies a broad service-operating license. The terms expressly limit use of that license to providing the service and prohibit using User Content to train generative AI models. The account holder must ensure every meeting participant consents to recording. Fireflies documents both platform-dependent opt-in and opt-out flows, advance email notices, in-meeting notices, pause controls, and bot removal. Developer API documentation is separately available at https://docs.fireflies.ai/getting-started/introduction.

Who Fireflies.ai is for

Fireflies.ai is aimed at teams and individuals who need meeting transcription, searchable call records, and conversation intelligence. It is designed for meetings workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
