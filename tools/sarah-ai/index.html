    Overview

    Sarah AI is a text-message assistant from NextGen Fusion AI in Chicago for real estate agents, small brokerages, property managers and small landlords. A buyer or renter texts a listing question and Sarah replies, says in her first message that she is an AI, asks a fixed set of pre-qualifying questions (neighbourhood, budget, bedrooms, pets, move-in timing), answers listing questions only from facts the customer keeps in a Google Sheet, books the showing on Google Calendar, and texts the agent or owner an alert with the lead's answers. Price and legal questions go back to the agent, and the customer can take over the conversation by texting from a dashboard. It is inbound SMS only: no voice, no CRM connectors and no listing inventory of its own - the customer's sheet is the inventory. The company is unusually explicit about what it will not claim: its llms.txt and llms-full.txt instruct automated readers not to cite customer counts, accuracy rates, tour multipliers or review scores because 'those figures are not published because they are not backed up', and no customer story is published yet. The site is a 137-URL Next.js build (Vercel) with vertical pages for agents, property managers, landlords and Chicago, a large blog, five dated competitor comparisons, and a second invitation-only pilot called Sarah Lite whose replies the vendor says are 'not written by a generative model'. The document a buyer is asked to agree to does not match the product: Terms section 4.1 describes Sarah AI as a Microsoft Teams / Jira project-management assistant.

    Key features

    • 24/7 inbound-SMS replies from a dedicated number, with an AI disclosure in the first message and a STOP opt-out on every page
    • A fixed pre-qualification script - neighbourhood, budget, bedrooms, pets and move-in timing (monthly budget for property-manager and landlord leads)
    • Listing answers drawn only from the customer's Google Sheet: address, bedrooms, bathrooms, rent or price, neighbourhood, square footage and availability, plus pet policy or parking when filled in, with anything not on the sheet routed back to the agent
    • Showing booking on the customer's own Google Calendar, with reminders and reschedules
    • Agent or owner text alerts carrying the lead's answers, so a human can take over
    • A dashboard login where the customer sees leads and hot leads, checks the calendar and takes over a conversation by texting
    • Vertical pages and dated competitor comparisons (agents, property managers, landlords, Chicago) plus an invitation-only Sarah Lite pilot with a Fair Housing notice and an Owner Pilot Agreement

    Frequently asked questions

    What does Sarah AI cost?

    The published ladder is a free two-week supervised pilot, then free setup and $1 for the first month, then the plan price: one agent $79/month with 500 messages and 1 number; a small brokerage $159/month for 3 agents and 2,000 texts, plus $31 per extra agent; property managers with 11 or more units $1.40 per unit per month with a $119 monthly minimum; landlords with 1 to 10 units $39/month; extra texts $12 per 500. The page says there are no other discounts and no commissions. Billing is arranged after the pilot rather than at a self-serve checkout, and the Terms - not the pricing page - say subscriptions auto-renew and need 30 days' written notice to cancel.

    What does Sarah AI do with a lead's text?

    She replies within the same conversation, says she is an AI, asks the same pre-qualifying questions every time, answers listing questions from the facts in the customer's Google Sheet, books the showing on the customer's Google Calendar, and texts the agent or owner the lead's answers. The site states that price and legal questions go back to the agent rather than being answered, and that the assistant never negotiates a price or guesses a listing fact.

    Where do the listing facts come from?

    From the customer's own Google Sheet. NextGen Fusion AI supplies a Sheet template, the assistant matches listings from that sheet, and the contact page states that anything not on the row - whether a unit is still available today, move-in dates - goes to the agent to confirm. There is no separate listing inventory.

    Does it work with a CRM, or handle voice calls?

    No on both counts, and the vendor says so in the submission as well as on the site: it is inbound SMS only, with Google Sheets and Google Calendar as the only integrations. A lead who calls is not handled, and no CRM connector is published.

    Who runs Sarah AI, and where are they?

    NextGen Fusion AI Inc. The About page names Shubham Mane (founder and AI architect) and Tyler Dmoraes (co-founder and CEO), both with LinkedIn links, and the site publishes a Chicago and a company social presence. The Terms call it a Texas corporation with AAA arbitration in Austin while every page footer and the structured data say Chicago, Illinois, and no postal address is published; the listing carries that conflict rather than resolving it. Contact is [personal address withheld].

    Is there a free trial, and how does a buyer pay?

    The published starting point is a free two-week supervised pilot on one or two of the customer's own listings, reviewed by the founders, followed by free setup and $1 for the first month and then the plan. The pilot is booked through Calendly or the contact form, and no self-serve card checkout is published, so the paid step is arranged with the company.
