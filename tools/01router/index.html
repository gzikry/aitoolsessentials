    Overview

    01Router puts one API key in front of several model vendors. You post a model id and optionally a provider to /api/v1/generations, it forwards the job to whichever upstream serves that model - its own first-party route, AtlasCloud, APIMart, EvoLink, KIE AI, WaveSpeedAI, Replicate, fal.ai, Vercel AI Gateway or OpenRouter - and bills credits at 100 to the dollar. What makes the site worth reading is that it prints every upstream's price side by side for each model, so a buyer can see the spread before routing: the same Seedance 2.5 job costs $0.055/second on APIMart and $0.126/second on fal.ai at 480p. The pricing page lists 18 video, 18 image, 4 audio and 12 chat models while the model library page counts 37. Alongside the API there is a browser studio and playground, a bilingual English/Chinese build, and a Projects feature that lets you resell the same models to your own users at a markup you set. The commercial documents are thin: no company named anywhere, no governing law, legal pages dated six weeks before the domain existed, and no statement on training use of uploaded media.

    Key features

    • One generation endpoint (POST /api/v1/generations) across video, image, audio and chat, with model and optional provider in the request body
    • Per-provider routing: pin a provider per model or let auto-routing choose the cheapest that supports your parameters, with the provider's own limits shown per model
    • Per-model gateway pages that print each provider's price, duration, resolution and input support side by side
    • Projects: pick which models to offer, set a price ratio (130 = 30% markup), read priced model lists by project id and keep platform cost separate from resale price
    • Quote API (POST /api/v1/quote) so an app can price a job before running it
    • Browser studio, prompt library and playground for trying models and seeing credit cost before integrating
    • One credit balance at $1 = 100 credits, one-time packs from $10 to $5,000 with up to 5% bonus, valid 365 days
    • Project key controls: credit quota with reset period, expiry date, IP allow-list and a task log filterable by project, key, model and status
    • Bilingual English and Chinese build with mirrored pricing and docs

    01Router is aimed at teams and individuals who need developers and small product teams adding ai video or image generation to an app who would otherwise need an account, api key and billing setup with several vendors, and who want to see each upstream provider's price before choosing a route. it also suits creators who want one credit balance across video, image, audio and chat in a browser studio, and agencies that want to resell the same models to their own users at a markup they set. it is not a fit if you need a named company, a governing-law clause, a refund policy or a training-use statement before you pay, if you need to buy more than $5,000 of credit at once, or if client media has to be covered by a processor you can name in a data-processing agreement.     Frequently asked questions

    What is 01Router?

    An API gateway that puts one key in front of several AI model vendors. You send a model id, optionally a provider, and your input to /api/v1/generations; 01Router forwards the job to one of ten upstream routes and bills the credits at 100 to the dollar. The same key covers video, image, audio and chat models, and a projects feature lets you resell those models to your own users at a markup you set.

    How much does 01Router cost?

    No subscription. Credits cost $1 per 100, sold in one-time packs of 1,000 credits for $10 up to 525,000 credits for $5,000 (a 5% bonus), valid 365 days. What a job costs depends on the model and provider: Seedance 2.5 at 480p runs from $0.055 to $0.126 a second depending on which provider serves it, and the same model at 1080p from $0.508 to $0.702 a second. Signup grants starter credits, and failed tasks return theirs.

    Is 01Router affiliated with the model makers or with OpenRouter?

    The site states no affiliation either way. It resells ByteDance, Alibaba, Google, Kuaishou and xAI models with no non-affiliation line anywhere, and OpenRouter - the larger router its name sits one word away from - appears as one of the ten upstream providers in its own price tables. Treat the model names as the model makers' trademarks and ask 01Router directly if the relationship matters to your contract.

    Can I use 01Router outputs commercially?

    Yes. Terms section 5 grants full commercial and non-commercial usage rights to media generated on your account, subject to the acceptable-use rules, and the FAQ repeats it. You remain responsible for having the rights to whatever you upload - including reference images and video you use in edit or extend tasks.

    What should I check before buying credits?

    Four things, all of them open on 2026-11-13: no company or legal entity is named anywhere, the Terms name no governing law, there is no refund policy page behind the non-refundable clause, and no page says whether uploaded media is used for training. Also reconcile the credit figure against the dollar rate before a large pack - the two disagree by up to 17.6% at the cheapest tiers because credits round up.
