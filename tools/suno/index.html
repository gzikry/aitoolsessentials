    Overview

    AI music generation with vocals, lyrics, and full song production.

    Key features

    • Text-to-song generation with vocals, lyrics, and full arrangements
    • Free tier with 50 credits per day
    • Pro and Premier plans with commercial use rights
    • Suno Studio: a web-based generative audio workstation with MIDI, effects, and automation
    • Stem separation (2 types on Pro, 3 on Premier)
    • Custom lyrics, personas, and voice recording on paid tiers

    Trial checklist

    Generate one full song in the genre you actually need and check whether the vocal and mix are usable without rework — that is the whole value proposition. Confirm the download allowance and commercial-use terms on the plan you are considering, because both changed recently. Then check whether stem separation matters for your workflow, since that capability differs by tier, and note that unused credits expire each cycle.

    Pricing

    Official sources checked 2026-10-01

    Current official summary: Monthly plan pricing published on the official pricing page (checked 2026-10-01): Free $0/month with access to free models, 50 credits per day, standard features only, up to 8 minutes of audio upload, a shared creation queue, no monthly song downloads, no commercial rights, no add-on credit purchases and no stem separation. Pro $8/month with the best models, 2,500 credits/month, 20 song downloads/month, commercial use rights, 2 stem-separation types and a priority queue of up to 10 songs; yearly billing saves $24. Premier $24/month with Suno Studio, 10,000 credits/month, 60 song downloads/month, 3 stem-separation types and up to 30 minutes of audio upload; yearly billing saves $72. The page advertises saving 20% on annual billing and states taxes are calculated at checkout.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Suno free?

    Suno offers a free tier with limited daily song generations. Paid plans unlock higher quality, more generations, and commercial use rights.

    Can I use Suno songs commercially?

    Commercial use rights depend on your plan tier. Free tier songs are for personal use; verify current terms on the official pricing page.

    Does Suno support custom lyrics?

    Yes — Suno lets you input custom lyrics or generate songs from a text prompt describing the style and mood.
