Who ElevenLabs is for

ElevenLabs fits teams and individuals who need ai voice generation, dubbing, narration, and voice agents. It is strongest in audio work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.
