    Overview

    A camera-specific inventory and studio-operations app: every body, lens, filter, drone and accessory gets a record with serial number, purchase details, condition, storage location, photos, receipts and warranty files, plus loan status and service history. On top of that sit shoot planning with sun and weather, packing and readiness checks, QR labels and scan check-out, value trends and an insurance-ready PDF, and - on Studio - a client CRM, booking, quotes, contracts and invoices. An assistant called Iris answers questions about your own kit, including lens-to-camera mount compatibility, and a free browser tier covers 25 items with export on every plan.

    Key features

    • Camera-specific records with per-type spec fields (mount, sensor, megapixels for bodies; focal length, aperture, filter size for lenses; FAA registration and Remote ID for drones)
    • Serial numbers, purchase details, receipts, warranties and item photos attached to each record, with warranty expiry alerts
    • Loans and check-outs with QR labels, scan in and out, due and overdue reminders, and signed loan agreements
    • Maintenance history, recurring service schedules and issue flags
    • Shoot planning with shot lists, call sheets, sun and moon times, weather and backup plans
    • Packing and production-readiness checks built from gear you actually own
    • Value tracking, six- and 24-month trends, depreciation method choice, and a one-click insurance PDF
    • CSV import with per-type templates and row-level validation; ZIP, JSON or CSV export on every plan
    • Iris assistant for plain-English questions about the kit, including lens-to-camera mount compatibility
    • Studio tier: client CRM, pipeline inbox, online booking, quotes, contracts with e-signature, invoices via your own payment link, 10-seat team workspace, and the Studio Agent API with webhooks and an MCP adapter

    Trial checklist

    Start on Starter with ten real items rather than a full import: include two similar lens names, one adapter, one borrowed item and one body in for repair, then check that Smart Add keeps model, quantity and ownership distinct and that a deliberately ambiguous model name gets a question rather than a guess. Export once and re-import to confirm serials, purchase dates and documents survive the round trip. Then plan one real shoot and compare your own packing list against GearDex's - mark omitted batteries, wrong mounts and anything flagged unavailable - and ask Iris one compatibility question you can verify against the manufacturer. Only after that does the paid question arise: whether Pro's unlimited items, own domain and 150 weekly AI actions remove work you are actually doing, or whether 25 items was enough.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does GearDex cost?

    Starter is free with up to 25 gear items in 10 categories, 3 shoot plans, one 50 MB client gallery, one gear document and 25 AI actions a week. Pro is $4 a month or $39 a year; Studio is $19 a month or $179 a year. A year of Pro saves $9 against twelve monthly payments and a year of Studio saves $49. The pricing page's structured data, the plan cards and the billing policy all carry the same figures.

    Is there a free trial on the paid plans?

    No. The billing policy states plainly that GearDex does not offer a free trial on paid plans, and that the free Starter tier is the way to evaluate it. Refunds are also not automatic once a billing period has begun, and the money-back guarantee clause is conditional rather than advertised on the pricing page.

    Can GearDex replace a gear spreadsheet?

    For a working kit, largely yes: the record holds serial, receipt, value, condition, location, documents, loan status and service history, and a CSV import brings an existing list across with row-level validation. What it adds over a spreadsheet is gear-specific structure - mounts, warranty expiry, loans, maintenance - and what it does not do is appraise your gear: recorded values are your own figures, and the site says plainly that it does not provide insurance or guarantee a claim.

    What does GearDex do with the photos and serial numbers I upload?

    Inventory data, including serials, purchase prices, values and receipt files, is stored in the account in the United States with Supabase. AI features send a request to NVIDIA only for Iris chat, Smart Add, AI descriptions, receipt reading, and a scheduled suggestion job on Pro and Studio; the policy lists what each one includes, that existing serials are not sent for receipt matching, and that NVIDIA's terms allow it to improve its models with request content. Starter inventory is not sent unless you start an AI feature.

    Does GearDex take a cut of client payments?

    No. Invoices and deposits are paid through the photographer's own payment link with Stripe, PayPal, Venmo, Square, Cash App or any https link, and the Terms state that GearDex is not a party to the client agreement, so disputes and chargebacks sit between the photographer, the client and that provider.
