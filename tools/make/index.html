Overview

A visual automation platform for building app-to-app workflows, including AI steps and operational routing.

Key features

  • Visual scenario builder with branching logic
  • Operations are far cheaper than Zapier tasks
  • Iterators/aggregators handle arrays natively
  • AI modules from OpenAI, Anthropic, and more
  • Error handlers with retries and fallbacks

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving operations automation—not a demo prompt. Verify whether visual scenario builder with branching logic materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to more complex than simple zaps so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Who Make is for

Make fits teams and individuals who need visual automation scenarios and multi-step operational workflows. It is strongest in automation work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.
