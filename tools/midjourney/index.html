Pricing

Official sources checked 2026-08-25

Current official summary: Official plans are Basic at US$10/month or US$96/year with 3.3 Fast GPU hours/month; Standard at US$30/month or US$288/year with 15 Fast GPU hours/month and unlimited Relax image generations; Pro at US$60/month or US$576/year with 30 Fast GPU hours/month, unlimited Relax images and SD video, and Stealth Mode; and Mega at US$120/month or US$1,152/year with 60 Fast GPU hours/month, unlimited Relax images and SD video, and Stealth Mode. Annual billing is a 20% discount. Unused subscription Fast time does not roll over.

Open verification questions
  • The official Privacy Policy discusses training-data collection and service improvement but does not expressly state, in the reviewed text, whether every user's prompts or generated Assets are used for model training.
  • Midjourney does not guarantee that generated Assets are copyrightable, unique, or free of third-party rights.
  • Commercial ownership for organizations above US$1 million annual revenue depends on maintaining the required Pro or Mega subscription when creating the Assets.
  • Relax Mode is unlimited subject to queues and operational limits; it is not a guaranteed throughput rate.

The current Terms are effective 2026-05-27. They say users own Assets they create to the fullest extent possible under applicable law, subject to third-party rights and the Agreement. Companies, and employees of companies, with more than US$1 million in annual revenue must use Pro or Mega to own Assets. Midjourney receives a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, sublicensable, royalty-free, irrevocable license over submitted Content and generated Assets. Content is publicly viewable and remixable by default; Stealth Mode is limited to Pro and Mega. The Privacy Policy covers prompts and uploaded content and says Midjourney may use personal data to provide, maintain, and improve the service.

Midjourney is aimed at teams and individuals who need high-quality image generation and concept visuals. It is designed for creative workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

Yes on any paid plan; free-trial images are not commercially usable.

Are my images public?

On Basic and Standard, yes — generations are visible in the community gallery. Pro adds stealth mode.

Midjourney vs DALL-E/Firefly?

Midjourney leads on aesthetics and concept art; Firefly integrates with Photoshop and is commercially safer for enterprise.

Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

Use one representative high-quality image generation and concept visuals task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

01 · Same input

Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

02 · Measure time

Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

03 · Score the result

Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

04 · Price the run
