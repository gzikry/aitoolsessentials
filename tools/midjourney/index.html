Midjourney review

A premium image-generation tool for brand visuals, concept art, moodboards, and campaign creative.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A premium image-generation tool for brand visuals, concept art, moodboards, and campaign creative.

Key features

  • Best-in-class aesthetic quality for stylized and concept imagery
  • Style references and character references for consistency
  • Web editor with inpainting and outpainting
  • Strong prompt adherence improvements in v6+
  • Commercial rights on all paid plans

Hands-on notes

Nothing matched Midjourney's default aesthetic in our side-by-sides — 'good on the first prompt' happened more often than with any competitor. Photorealism is its relative weak spot versus dedicated photoreal models. Stealth mode matters: on Basic/Standard your generations are public by default.

Pricing

Basic$10/mo — ~200 generations, no unlimited relaxed mode
Standard$30/mo — 15h fast GPU + unlimited relaxed generations
Pro$60/mo — 30h fast, stealth mode so your images stay private
Mega$120/mo — 60h fast GPU for heavy production use

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Ad concepts
  • Moodboards
  • Product visual exploration
  • Social imagery
  • Producing concept art variations for client pitches
  • Creating on-brand social visuals without a designer

Pros

  • Beautiful default aesthetics which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Great exploration speed — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Strong community prompts with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Workflow differs from standard design tools
  • Requires human art direction which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Midjourney is for

Midjourney fits teams and individuals who need high-quality image generation and concept visuals. It is strongest in creative work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Midjourney scores 4.5/5 in our evaluation. Beautiful default aesthetics which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: workflow differs from standard design tools.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

Yes on any paid plan; free-trial images are not commercially usable.

Are my images public?

On Basic and Standard, yes — generations are visible in the community gallery. Pro adds stealth mode.

Midjourney vs DALL-E/Firefly?

Midjourney leads on aesthetics and concept art; Firefly integrates with Photoshop and is commercially safer for enterprise.
