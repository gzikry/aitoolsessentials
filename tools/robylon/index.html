    Overview

    An AI customer support platform whose agents work tickets rather than only answering them: they read the systems an answer lives in, act through tools built from your APIs, and hand anything they cannot finish to a person with the checking already done. It runs inside an existing helpdesk or standalone with its own inbox.

    Key features

    • AI agents for email and ticketing, web and in-app chat, phone voice, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, SMS and app-store reviews
    • Tool-building from your own APIs, plus native connections to Freshdesk, Zendesk, Zoho Desk, Salesforce, Vtiger, Intercom, Freshchat, SalesIQ and Gorgias
    • Knowledge base with gap detection and clustering, and a bulk testing suite that grades agent answers from a CSV of questions
    • Model selector across OpenAI GPT-4o/4.1/5 and o-series, Anthropic Claude Sonnet and Haiku, and Google Gemini Pro and Flash, with LLM fallback
    • Credit-based plans (Starter free / Pro from $25 / Business from $180 / Enterprise quoted) that scale by credits rather than seats
    • Optional live-agent seats at $19 (Basic), $49 (Advanced) and $89 (Expert) per seat per month, with a shared inbox and AI Copilot
    • Analytics dashboards for chat and voice, AI insights, RBAC, and Enterprise add-ons including SSO, audit logs and custom retention

    Trial checklist

    Connect one helpdesk or the standalone inbox and one knowledge source, then replay a week of real resolved tickets through the agent and read the ones it closed without a human. Check the credit burn at your actual conversation volume rather than the plan's estimate, since voice calls use credits at a different rate to chat and email. Ask what is retrievable from the trust centre before you rely on the SOC 2 Type II or HIPAA claims, and confirm the deletion window you need.

    Open verification questions
    • SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance are stated on /about and in /llms.txt, but the trust centre cited as the evidence surface (trust.robylon.ai) returns HTTP 403 to a standard browser request. The claims are vendor-stated and were not verifiable during this check; the listing's cons say so and no compliance claim is repeated as fact.
    • Training use on customer data is not addressed directly. The Privacy Policy permits the use of "non-identifiable information, including anonymized, aggregated, or de-identified data, for lawful purposes" but does not state whether submitted prompts or support conversations are used to train models. Robylon's robots.txt notes that blocking "training" crawlers "would also block Googlebot, which is multi-purpose" and allows named AI crawlers - a crawler policy, not a statement about customer data.
    • International transfers rest on consent ("By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of such information") rather than a named mechanism. No Standard Contractual Clauses or Data Privacy Framework basis is referenced.
    • Retention is two-tier and partly open-ended: customer and user information is deleted 30-60 days after termination or on request, but usage data is kept only "as long as needed" with no period. Custom retention policies are an Enterprise-only feature.
    • The Privacy Policy's last-updated date (7 April 2024) predates the current product surface, including voice, WhatsApp and the browser-automation section of the Terms, so parts of it may describe an earlier service.
    • Enterprise is quote-only ("Let's talk") and per-conversation cost depends on which model answers, so no single headline price exists for the top tier.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does Robylon cost?

    Starter is free with 300 credits a month (roughly 40 conversations). Pro starts at $25/month for 500 credits, rising through $75, $113 and $158 at 1,500, 2,500 and 3,500 credits. Business starts at $180/month for 4,500 credits up to $300 for 10,000. Yearly billing takes 10% off. Enterprise is quoted. Live-agent seats are optional and cost $19 to $89 per seat per month. Check the official pricing page before buying.

    Do I need live agent seats to use Robylon?

    No. Many customers run AI agents only. Seats are for teams that want to answer escalations inside Robylon's own inbox; on Business and Enterprise the agent hands unfinished work to Freshdesk, Zendesk or Zoho Desk instead.

    What happens when credits run out?

    AI agent replies pause until credits are added, while the live-agent inbox keeps working. Robylon notifies you at 50%, 20% and zero balance. You can buy extra credits at $25 per 1,000, or move to a higher credit tier.

    Robylon vs Intercom or Zendesk?

    Robylon lists a conversation at roughly $0.20-$0.38 against Intercom's reported $0.99 per Fin resolution and Zendesk's roughly $1.50-$2.00 per committed resolution. Robylon's figure is per conversation attempted rather than per resolution, so it is not a like-for-like comparison. Model your real volume before choosing.
