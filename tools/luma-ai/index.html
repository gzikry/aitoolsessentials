Frequently asked questions

Is Luma AI free?

Luma AI offers a free tier with limited generations. Paid plans unlock higher quality, longer clips, and commercial use.

What does Luma AI do?

Luma AI generates and animates 3D content and video from text prompts or images, targeting creators who need motion without 3D modeling skills.

Repeatable evaluation · before you pay

Run the same job, not a demo.

Use one representative product demos, social video, 3d prototyping task and run it through every finalist. The point is not to produce a fake benchmark; it is to expose the time, quality, retries, review work, and cost your workflow actually creates.

01 · Same input

Use the same source material, prompt, files, constraints, and success criteria for each tool.

02 · Measure time

Record setup time, time to first usable result, and time spent fixing or editing the output.

03 · Score the result

Rate quality, consistency, control, and reviewer effort from 1–5. Keep the notes, not just the average.

04 · Price the run

Record credits, seats, limits, retries, and any human review cost required to finish the task.
