    Luma AI review

    Luma AI is a 3D and video generation platform that creates photorealistic 3D models and AI-generated video from text prompts or reference images, targeting product demos, social video, and 3D prototyping workflows.

    Editorial review · Updated 2026-10-17 · Hands-on result not yet published

    Overview

    Luma AI is a 3D and video generation platform that creates photorealistic 3D models and AI-generated video from text prompts or reference images, targeting product demos, social video, and 3D prototyping workflows.

    Key features

    • Ray3 and Ray3.14 text-to-video and image-to-video models
    • Video-to-video and reframe actions at multiple resolutions
    • Luma Agents for multi-stage creative work with shared context
    • Third-party image and video models in the same platform
    • 3D capture and scene generation
    • Credit-based cost per generation published by model and resolution

    Trial checklist

    Generate a draft-resolution version of a clip you would actually ship before committing to a high-resolution render, because draft costs a fraction of 1080p and answers the quality question cheaper. Record the credits one finished clip consumes at your target resolution and compare it with the 10,000 monthly credits on Plus. If you need brand consistency across assets, test one agent workflow rather than isolated generations.

    Use cases

    • Product and brand video prototyping
    • Social video and campaign assets
    • 3D asset and scene generation
    • Iterative creative exploration with an agent

    Pros

    • Published per-generation credit costs make budgeting possible
    • Multiple resolution tiers including a cheap draft mode
    • Broad model access beyond Luma's own models
    • Commercial use included on individual plans

    Cons

    • Plans start at $30/month with no free generation tier
    • Credit consumption scales steeply with resolution (1080p costs multiples of draft)
    • Video-to-video is billed per second, so long clips get expensive
    • Team controls and SSO require contacting sales

    Who Luma AI is for

    Luma AI is aimed at teams and individuals who need product demos, social video, 3d prototyping. It is designed for video workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.

    Is Luma AI free?

    Luma AI offers a free tier with limited generations. Paid plans unlock higher quality, longer clips, and commercial use.

    What does Luma AI do?

    Luma AI generates and animates 3D content and video from text prompts or images, targeting creators who need motion without 3D modeling skills.
