Editorial review · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A creative video platform for generative video, editing, and motion experiments.

Key features

  • Gen-3 text-to-video with strong motion quality
  • Image-to-video animates stills
  • Motion Brush directs movement in specific regions
  • Camera controls (pan, zoom, dolly) on generations
  • Full editing suite: green screen, inpainting, upscale

Trial checklist

Use a real task involving concept videos—not a demo prompt. Verify whether gen-3 text-to-video with strong motion quality materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to outputs need art direction so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

Who Runway is for

Runway fits teams and individuals who need ai video generation and creative production experiments. It is strongest in video work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Runway cost?

Free trial credits, then $15-95/mo depending on volume. Video eats credits fast — roughly 75 seconds of Gen-3 per $15 month.

Can Runway replace videographers?

No — it excels at b-roll, concept shots, and augmentation, not narrative filming.
