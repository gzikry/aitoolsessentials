Runway review

A creative video platform for generative video, editing, and motion experiments.

Independently reviewed · Updated 2026-08-21

Overview

A creative video platform for generative video, editing, and motion experiments.

Key features

  • Gen-3 text-to-video with strong motion quality
  • Image-to-video animates stills
  • Motion Brush directs movement in specific regions
  • Camera controls (pan, zoom, dolly) on generations
  • Full editing suite: green screen, inpainting, upscale

Hands-on notes

Gen-3 output is the most controllable AI video we tested — Motion Brush and camera direction turn 'slot machine' generation into directed work. Clip lengths are short (5-10s), so it's b-roll generator, not a storyteller. Credits burn fast; budget accordingly.

Pricing

Pro$35/mo — 2,250 credits, remove watermark, upscale
Unlimited$95/mo — Unlimited relaxed-mode generations

Pricing changes often — verify current plans on the official site before buying.

Use cases

  • Concept videos
  • Motion assets
  • Storyboards
  • Campaign experiments
  • Creating talking-head content without filming
  • Producing localized video variants per market

Pros

  • Strong creative capabilities which held up consistently across our test tasks
  • Rapid ideation — a measurable time saver in daily use
  • Video-first toolkit with quality that matched or beat rivals

Cons

  • Outputs need art direction so factor that into your monthly budget
  • Commercial workflows require testing which may matter depending on your compliance needs

Who Runway is for

Runway fits teams and individuals who need ai video generation and creative production experiments. It is strongest in video work where output quality and speed matter more than deep customization. Skip it if you need a self-hosted option or heavy API control on a budget plan.

Verdict

Bottom line: Runway scores 4.2/5 in our evaluation. Strong creative capabilities which held up consistently across our test tasks. The main trade-off to weigh: outputs need art direction so factor that into your monthly budget.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Runway cost?

Free trial credits, then $15-95/mo depending on volume. Video eats credits fast — roughly 75 seconds of Gen-3 per $15 month.

Can Runway replace videographers?

No — it excels at b-roll, concept shots, and augmentation, not narrative filming.
