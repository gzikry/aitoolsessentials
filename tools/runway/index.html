    Runway review

    A creative video platform for generative video, editing, and motion experiments.

    Editorial review · Updated 2026-11-26 · Hands-on result not yet published

    Overview

    A creative video platform for generative video, editing, and motion experiments.

    Key features

    • Gen-3 text-to-video with strong motion quality
    • Image-to-video animates stills
    • Motion Brush directs movement in specific regions
    • Camera controls (pan, zoom, dolly) on generations
    • Full editing suite: green screen, inpainting, upscale

    Trial checklist

    Use a real task involving concept videos, not a demo prompt. Verify whether gen-3 text-to-video with strong motion quality materially reduces editing or setup time. Record output quality, time to a publishable result, and total plan usage. Pay particular attention to outputs need art direction so factor that into your monthly budget. Repeat the same task in at least one alternative before deciding.

    Pricing

    Last checked 2026-11-14

    Current official summary: Creative plans: Free $0 with 125 one-time credits and 5 GB storage; Standard $15 monthly or $12/month billed annually with 625 credits/month; Pro $35 monthly or $28/month billed annually with 2,250 credits/month and 500 GB storage; Max $95 monthly or $76/month billed annually with 9,500 credits/month and up to one month of credit rollover; Enterprise has custom pricing and credits. Standard and Pro subscription credits do not roll over; purchased credits do not expire. The pricing page states Gen-4.5 costs 12 credits per generated second.

    Open verification questions
    • The self-serve terms authorize training on Inputs and Outputs, while Runway separately advertises no training on Enterprise customer data. The precise Enterprise exceptions depend on the separate Enterprise agreement and cannot be generalized to self-serve plans.
    • The pricing page does not provide a fixed Enterprise price or credit allocation.

    Official pricing and Help Center pages agree on the 625/2,250/9,500 monthly credit allocations. Terms last updated May 11, 2026 state that Runway does not claim ownership of Inputs or Outputs and does not restrict commercial use of Outputs, subject to compliance with the agreement. The same terms grant Runway a perpetual license to use Inputs and Outputs for model training, labeling, classification, moderation, and related improvement. The privacy policy says submitted voice and face characteristics may be biometric data and are used only to provide the requested biometric-enabled feature.     Who Runway is for

    Runway is aimed at teams and individuals who need ai video generation and creative production experiments. It is designed for video workflows where output quality, adoption effort, and operating cost all matter. Compare alternatives first if you require self-hosting, unusually deep customization, or tighter policy controls than the official plan provides.
