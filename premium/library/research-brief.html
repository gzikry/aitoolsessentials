What changed in AI tools — September 2026

A public keep/cut digest of recorded vendor launches and directory checks this month. Perplexity open-sourced Lily (2026-09-02 official blog) as a lightweight local inference engine for Apple silicon and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, tied to Perplexity Computer hybrid compute — not a new paid SKU. Catch (catchagent.ai) was listed on 2026-09-02 after the official homepage and /security page advertised $99/month billed monthly (cancel anytime, 10-day free trial); /pricing 404s and the same homepage still shows Closed Beta — Limited Spots. World Labs Atlas remains an early-access watch with no public self-serve pricing.

New listings

  • Catch — Official homepage and /security (checked 2026-09-02) advertise $99/month billed monthly, cancel anytime, 10-day free trial. Same homepage still shows Closed Beta — Limited Spots. /pricing 404. Mailbox/calendar/phone access is required for the advertised jobs. Named testimonials are vendor-supplied.

Vendor launches

  • Lily (Perplexity) — Official blog (checked 2026-09-02): lightweight local inference engine built specifically for Apple silicon and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B so on-device compute does not bottleneck Hybrid Compute / Perplexity Computer tasks. Rust runtime; OpenAI-compatible chat-completions API; custom Metal kernels; neither PyTorch nor MLX in the execution path; standalone demo on GitHub. Official pplx-garden README names the demo at github.com/perplexityai/pplx-garden (lily/), Apache-2.0, greedy generation for the Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MLX affine 4-bit checkpoint, Apple GPU family 10+ (M5 and newer). Not a Perplexity paid plan SKU. Vendor-published throughput figures were not independently reproduced. Perplexity consumer plan dollar prices were not re-verified on this date.
  • Atlas (World Labs) — Official blog (checked 2026-09-01): next-gen omni world model pretrained from scratch on text, images, video, and 3D. Multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer with spatial context. Camera-controlled generation up to 1 minute at 1440p; spatial reconstruction from 1–dozens of images with explicit 3D (point clouds, 3D Gaussian splats); space-time simulation including video reframing and Real-to-Sim for robotics; also text-to-image and 360 panoramas. Will power future versions of Marble. Early access with select partners — request access on the blog. No public self-serve plan prices published.

Watch list

  • Perplexity — Lily is an open-source local engine for Computer hybrid compute, not a new Pro/Max SKU. Do not add a paid Perplexity seat because Lily shipped. Re-check official plan pages before renewing; consumer dollar prices were not re-verified on 2026-09-02.

Keep/cut rule: If a launch has no public pricing, do not treat it as a buy decision. An open-source local engine is not a reason to add a paid seat. If a public price is mixed with closed-beta waitlist copy, treat availability as unresolved until you can actually start a trial. Watch official sources, then re-check before adding a seat.
